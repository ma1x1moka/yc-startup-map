**Website:** https://21st.dev | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/21st

21st.dev is the largest React component registry for AI applications, serving about 2 million developers. It provides UI components and agent templates that let engineers quickly assemble agent‑powered apps for the emerging agentic internet. Based in San Francisco with a three‑person team, the company targets B2B customers in engineering, product, and design.

The platform is positioned as a core infrastructure piece for developers building AI‑enabled interfaces, offering reusable building blocks that accelerate development of agentic experiences.

_Usage:_

"How is 21st different from a regular React component library?"

"Unlike generic React libraries, 21st’s catalog is focused on AI applications, offering components and ready‑made agent templates specifically designed for building agent‑powered apps, and it already powers the workflows of roughly 2 million developers."
