9 Mothers is a defense technology company based in Austin, Texas, that specializes in counter-drone systems. The company has sold $1.6 million worth of systems to the Department of War, delivering multiple units to the agency. Its flagship product, EDDA, is a small, low-power, and low-cost system designed to be deployed on vehicles, bases, or carried by personnel to intercept Group 1 suicide drones.

With a team of 19, 9 Mothers has secured multiple contracts and sales for its autonomous-capable hardware. Beyond its initial product, the company is developing three additional systems that are currently in various stages of development to meet ongoing demand in the defense sector.

_Usage:_

"What makes the EDDA system different from traditional high-end counter-drone solutions?"

"EDDA is designed to be small, low-power, and low-cost, allowing it to be fully autonomous and deployed on vehicles, bases, or carried by individuals.
