ARC Prize builds AI benchmarks that measure general intelligence. Their benchmark, ARC-AGI, has been used by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and xAI.

Founded by Mike Knoop and François Chollet. Through benchmarks, global competitions, research grants, and community, ARC Prize guides researchers, industry, and regulators on the path to AGI. Thesis: AGI requires more than scaling — it demands systems capable of genuine fluid intelligence and adaptation to novel challenges.
