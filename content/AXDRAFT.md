**Website:** https://axdraft.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/axdraft

AXDRAFT analyzes patterns in corporate legal documents and turns the drafting process from error‑prone copy‑and‑paste into a simple Q&A workflow. The platform can draft a document in under four minutes and integrates data from public registers and internal databases, removing the need for manual double‑checking. It also provides data validation, e‑signatures, access control, document IDs, visibility and personal accountability for each draft.

Forty‑five corporations using AXDRAFT report up to a 90 % reduction in drafting time and approval cycles shortened by as much as four days, leading to more deals and better quarterly financial results. The service can be deployed within two weeks of signing a contract.

AXDRAFT was acquired after its Winter 2019 batch, and it now operates with a team of 34 across Kyiv, Frankfurt and Lviv.

_Usage:_

"How does AXDRAFT achieve a sub‑four‑minute drafting time compared to traditional manual drafting?"

"AXDRAFT uses machine‑learning pattern analysis to turn the document creation process into a guided Q&A, automatically pulling data from public registers and internal databases and handling validation, e‑signatures and access control, which eliminates the copy‑and‑paste steps that make manual drafting slow."
