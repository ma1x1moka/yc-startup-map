**Website:** http://ablehealth.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/able-health

Able Health provides a platform that helps physician organizations manage value-based care programs. Calculating HEDIS and Medicare performance measures typically requires significant administrative and IT resources. Able Health simplifies program administration by tailoring workflows to specific reporting requirements and streamlining engagement between clinicians and health plans.

Based in San Francisco and launched in the Winter 2016 YC batch, the Healthcare IT company operated with a team of 11. Able Health has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Able Health handle complex healthcare reporting like HEDIS and Medicare measures?"

"Able Health provides customized program administration that directly calculates HEDIS and Medicare measures. By streamlining communication and data workflows between clinicians and health plans, the platform reduces the heavy administrative burden traditionally required to manage value-based reimbursements."
