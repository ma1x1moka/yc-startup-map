**Website:** https://absurd.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/absurd

Absurd is an online whiteboard for filmmakers working with AI.

It powers the company's Kalshi spot "Election Day" which achieved over 1 million views, and its videos average 400K+ organic views.

The platform serves B2B marketing, media, and advertising, and is based in Los Angeles, CA.

_Usage:_

"How does Absurd differ from other AI video tools?"

"Absurd provides a collaborative whiteboard interface that lets filmmakers storyboard and edit AI-generated video in real time, focusing on rapid iteration rather than a traditional video editor."
