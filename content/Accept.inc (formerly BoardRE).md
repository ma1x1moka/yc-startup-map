Accept.inc (formerly BoardRE) operates as an intermediary buyer that uses its own cash to purchase homes for the majority of buyers who need a mortgage, removing the lender from the transaction. This lets sellers and agents close in as little as two to three days while buyers lock in price before their mortgage closes.

The “Buy Now. Mortgage Later” model gives any qualified buyer an all‑cash deal, distinguishing Accept.inc from iBuyer firms that flip properties. It works with national lenders to assess creditworthiness up front and uses a short‑term credit facility to fund purchases.

Accept.inc was acquired after building a team of about 80 people in Denver and positioning itself at the intersection of fintech and real estate.

_Usage:_

"How does Accept.inc’s “Buy Now. Mortgage Later” model differ from traditional iBuyer services?"

"Accept.inc doesn’t buy, fix, and resell homes itself; instead it uses its cash to purchase a buyer’s chosen property and then offers a short‑term credit facility while the buyer secures a mortgage, allowing the transaction to close in 2‑3 days with an all‑cash offer, unlike iBuyers that profit by flipping properties."
