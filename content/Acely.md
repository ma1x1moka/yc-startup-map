**Website:** https://acely.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/acely

Acely is the leading AI tutor for the digital SAT and ACT, helping students gain confidence and improve their chances of admission to their dream college. Backed by Forerunner Ventures, the company has reached $10M ARR and operates with a 30‑person team based in San Francisco. Acely launched out of beta in January 2024 as a spinout product from Juni, a YC W18 company, and continues to scale its AI‑native tools for high‑school families.

_Usage:_

"How does Acely’s AI tutor differ from traditional SAT/ACT prep services?"

"Acely uses AI‑native tools to personalize study paths for each student, focusing on the digital versions of the SAT and ACT, whereas traditional services rely on static curricula and in‑person instruction."
