**Website:** https://www.actiondesk.io/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/actiondesk

Actiondesk offers a platform that is as easy and flexible as a spreadsheet while delivering the power of a BI tool, letting teams pull live data from multiple sources into a single view.

Designed for B2B engineering, product, and design teams, the SaaS solution runs from New York City and San Francisco and was built by a four‑person team. Actiondesk was acquired, marking its success in the data‑integration space.

_Usage:_

"How does Actiondesk provide live data integration compared to a standard spreadsheet?"

"Unlike a spreadsheet that requires manual data refreshes, Actiondesk connects to live data feeds, automatically updating dashboards and reports, and adds BI‑level analytics on top of that real‑time data."
