Co-founded [Verse Therapy](./Verse%20Therapy.md) with Danielle Yoesep and Shuying Zhang, connecting patients of all ages with licensed speech-language pathologists. Steinle holds an MBA in Entrepreneurship from Chicago Booth and previously worked as a biomedical engineer, a Goldman Sachs banker, and a big-tech PM at Facebook, launching products across content moderation, community growth, and creator monetization over four years. Verse Therapy was later acquired by Beaming Health.
