**Website:** https://www.aercompliance.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/aer

Aer Compliance positions itself as the Rippling for compliance, serving as a central hub for companies to meet their SEC obligations. The platform consolidates various regulatory tasks, including codes of ethics, marketing compliance, calendar management, filings, and workflows, into a single interface.

Based in New York City, the company operates in the fintech and regtech sectors with a team of 18 employees. It was part of the Summer 2021 Y Combinator batch and has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Aer differ from traditional compliance software or spreadsheets?"

"Aer acts as a unified platform that combines disparate compliance functions like code of ethics management, marketing reviews, and SEC filings into one system, rather than relying on isolated tools or manual spreadsheets to track regulatory obligations."
