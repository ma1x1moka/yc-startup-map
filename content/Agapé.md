Agapé is a consumer health and wellness application designed to help users maintain emotional closeness with friends, family, or partners despite physical distance. The platform curates daily questions intended to facilitate deeper, more meaningful interactions between connected individuals.

Operating out of Rochester, NY, and remotely, the company has built a small team of six members to serve a global user base exceeding two million people. By focusing on structured communication prompts, Agapé addresses the challenge of sustaining strong relationships in a digital age.
