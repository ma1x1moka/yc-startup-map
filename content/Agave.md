**Website:** https://www.useagave.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/agave

Agave provides an AI-driven financial operations platform designed specifically for the construction sector, a $12 trillion global industry often reliant on legacy software built 20-40 years ago. The company addresses the fragmentation of this market by building infrastructure to read and write across on-premises systems that typically lack APIs or documentation.

Using these underlying rails, Agave deploys AI to automate critical financial tasks such as invoicing, expense management, compliance, and billing. The San Francisco-based company, which has a team of 40, has secured over 500 customers and has maintained profitability and positive cash flow for more than two years.

Backed by Accel and Y Combinator, with additional support from investors associated with Procore, Autodesk, and Plaid, Agave focuses on modernizing the financial backbone of construction businesses through specialized B2B SaaS solutions.

_Usage:_

"How does Agave handle the lack of APIs in legacy construction software?"

"They spent three years building custom rails to read and write directly across those older, on-prem systems, allowing them to deploy AI for financial tasks like invoicing and compliance without requiring the legacy software to have modern APIs or documentation."
