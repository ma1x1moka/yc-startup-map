Ahazou is a SaaS application designed to assist offline local businesses in Latin America with connecting to customers online, promoting their brands, and building their reputation. The platform aims to help these businesses sell more by bridging the gap between physical operations and digital presence.

Based in São Paulo, Brazil, with a remote team of 27, Ahazou has reached 100k USD in monthly recurring revenue with over 16,000 subscribers. The company is backed by GFC, Monashees, and Y Combinator, and its team includes talent from companies such as iFood, Movile, and PicPay.
