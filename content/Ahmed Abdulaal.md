Co-founded [Mecha Health](./Mecha%20Health.md) with Hugo Fry, Nina Montaña Brown, and Ayodeji Ijishakin, building foundation models that produce accurate draft radiology reports from medical images. Abdulaal trained in medicine at Imperial College London and is a Microsoft PhD scholar at UCL, having previously worked as a Research Scientist at AstraZeneca's Center for Artificial Intelligence. Mecha Health's first model, built in under two months, beat Microsoft, Google, and OpenAI on clinical accuracy while being two orders of magnitude smaller and trained on a quarter of the data.
