Ailit is building the electric energy system for North American freight rail. Its first product, Superwagon, is a battery wagon designed to power existing diesel-electric locomotives, allowing railroads to electrify the fleets they already own.

Superwagon carries swappable battery packs that supply electricity to the locomotive's existing electric motors. Discharged packs are exchanged for charged ones along the route and recharged off-board, providing operators with limitless range.

Ailit delivers energy as a service, owning and operating the batteries and swapping sites. Railroads pay for energy with no upfront equipment purchase, with pricing set at 20% below today's diesel cost.
