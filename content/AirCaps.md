AirCaps brings AI assistance to in-person conversations. Provides live captions, translations, AI meeting notes and insights in real-time — deployed as an app for lightweight AR glasses so you see visual information overlaid on your field of vision.

Processed 16,500 hours of real-world conversations. AirCaps assists with 11% of users' in-person conversations. Did $93K in revenue in October and grew 6.5x from September while spending under $3K on marketing.
