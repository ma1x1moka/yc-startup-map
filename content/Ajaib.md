Ajaib is an online brokerage that lets Indonesians trade stocks, ETFs and mutual funds. Founded in 2018, it has positioned itself as Indonesia's leading fintech platform, driven by a mission to educate the public on the importance of investing for the future.

Guided by principles of ownership, growth mindset and a will to win, Ajaib focuses on expanding financial inclusion by providing safe, trusted and affordable investment instruments. The company claims to have transformed how young Indonesians invest, and it now boasts over one million users on its platform.

With a team of 300 based in Jakarta and remote, Ajaib has become the third‑largest brokerage by transaction volume in Indonesia and is recognized as the fastest‑growing unicorn in Southeast Asia. The firm remains active and continues to grow its user base.
