**Twitter:** @AkshayNarisetti | **LinkedIn:** linkedin.com/in/akshaynarisetti

Co-founded [Pocket](./Pocket.md) with [Gabriel Dymowski](./Gabriel%20Dymowski.md).

Previously built **Omi** — an open-source AI note-taking device, which gave Narisetti direct experience with the hardware AI wearable space before Pocket. He knew the failure modes of previous devices (Humane AI Pin, Google Glass) from having shipped in the same category.

The $27M ARR in 5 months is the metric that defines his founding story: hardware velocity at that rate is essentially unprecedented in recent YC history. Series A-level traction at seed stage.

The prior Omi experience mattered: he didn't walk into hardware AI cold. He understood why previous devices failed (wrong form factor, wrong use case, wrong friction level) and designed Pocket around those lessons — smaller, more passive, solving one specific pain rather than replacing the phone.

_Usage:_

"How does Pocket avoid the failure modes of earlier AI wearables like the Humane AI Pin or Google Glass?"

"Based on lessons from building the Omi note-taking device, Pocket addresses the bad form factors, wrong use cases, and high friction of past hardware by taking a narrower approach: it is smaller, operates more passively, and focuses on solving a single specific pain point rather than trying to replace the phone."
