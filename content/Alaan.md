**Website:** https://www.alaan.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/alaan

Alaan is an AI-native finance platform designed to simplify financial operations for businesses in the Middle East. Launched in August 2022, the company consolidates corporate cards, business bank accounts, international transfers, expense management, and accounting automation into a single interface. The platform integrates intelligence into every finance workflow to streamline processes for its users.

Headquartered in Dubai, Alaan has grown rapidly to serve over 3,000 startups, mid-market, and enterprise customers. The company processes tens of millions of dollars in monthly payment volume and has raised one of the largest Series A rounds in Middle East history, backed by Peak XV. As a fintech company rather than a bank, Alaan issues its business bank accounts through a partnership with ruya.

_Usage:_

"How does Alaan handle the banking aspect if it isn't a licensed bank itself?"

"Alaan operates as a fintech company, not a bank. While it provides the interface for business bank accounts, the actual accounts are issued by a partner named ruya. This allows Alaan to offer banking features alongside its other tools like corporate cards and expense management without holding a banking license itself."
