Aleph Solutions helps automotive aftermarket brands, including companies with thousands of products, increase their revenue in Latin America by listing their inventory on major e-commerce sites like Mercado Libre and Amazon. The platform addresses the challenge of maintaining up-to-date catalogs, prices, and inventory across digital channels, a task that is difficult for brands and resellers to manage without dedicated tools.

By creating high-quality product catalogs, Aleph allows brands to replicate their offline reseller networks online. This infrastructure enables any authorized seller to access the brand's product data and list items on marketplaces, streamlining the shift to digital sales. The company reports that its customers sell over U$D 4 million worth of product every month using its software.

Based in Buenos Aires, Argentina, and operating remotely, Aleph Solutions is an active B2B e-commerce company with a team of approximately 60 people. It serves the retail and marketplace sectors by providing the necessary tools for brands and wholesalers to manage complex product data at scale.

_Usage:_

"How does Aleph handle the complexity of managing thousands of SKUs across different marketplaces like Amazon and Mercado Libre?"

"Aleph acts as a central hub where brands create and maintain high-quality product catalogs. Instead of each reseller manually updating prices and inventory for thousands of items, they access the brand's centralized catalog through Aleph's software. This ensures that when a reseller lists a part on a marketplace, the data is accurate and synchronized, allowing brands to scale their online presence without the overhead of managing each individual listing."
