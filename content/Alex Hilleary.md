Part of the founding team at [Gather](./Gather.md), which builds virtual spaces for online meetings, styled as a 2D game map with spatial, proximity-based conversations, alongside [Kumail Jaffer](./Kumail%20Jaffer.md) and [Phillip Wang](./Phillip%20Wang.md).

_Usage:_

"What is Gather's core interaction model?"

"A 2D game-map interface where conversations happen based on proximity — walk near someone and you start talking, just like in a physical space."
