Founded [Scale AI](./Scale%20AI.md) at 19 after a brief stint studying machine learning at MIT, dropping out to join Y Combinator and build a data-labeling platform that became core infrastructure for OpenAI, Meta, Microsoft, autonomous vehicle companies, and the U.S. government. Wang became the world's youngest self-made billionaire running Scale. In June 2025, Meta acquired 49% of Scale AI at a roughly $29B valuation, and Wang left to become Meta's Chief AI Officer.
