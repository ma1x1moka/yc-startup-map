Alfie offers a virtual medical program built on research from Cleveland Clinic and Harvard that determines the most effective weight loss regimen and medications for its members, optimizing exercise, nutrition, and medications in complex care pathways. The program has been shown to be 2x more effective than standard prescription medication and 5x more effective than habit change alone, and it has raised capital from Goodwater Capital, Nina Capital, Air Force Ventures, Phoenix Club, and super angels including the founder of OpenTrons. Alfie was acquired, marking its success in the digital health and telemedicine space, and it operates from New York City with a team of six.
