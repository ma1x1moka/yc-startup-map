The Copenhagen‑based startup reports $20 k in monthly recurring revenue and a 10 % week‑over‑week growth rate over the past eight weeks. With a team of five, it targets a core market opportunity of over $10 b within the nearly $10 trillion global education market, betting on AI‑driven disruption and product virality.
