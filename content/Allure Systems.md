Allure Systems was acquired by Farfetch (NASDAQ: FTCH) in Dec 2021. It uses generative AI to produce stunning apparel images for eCommerce, allowing brands to generate unlimited images from a single photo on any model instantly. This capability lets retailers show styles on models of every size, improving conversion rates and customer engagement. The platform works with leading fashion retailers, offering a scalable and cost-effective way to create product‑page visuals without on‑site models or photographers. With a team of 18 in New York City, Allure Systems combines computer‑vision and generative AI to deliver images that resonate with shoppers across the B2B retail sector.

_Usage:_

"How does Allure Systems generate product images without using physical models or photographers?"

"Allure Systems feeds a single clothing photo into its generative AI model, which then creates realistic images of the item on any chosen model instantly. Because the AI synthesizes the visual content, brands can produce unlimited variations at scale without arranging on‑site shoots."
