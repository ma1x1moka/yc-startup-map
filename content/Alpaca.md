**Website:** https://alpaca.markets/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/alpaca

Alpaca is an API infrastructure and licensed financial services company that enables developers and businesses to embed stock and cryptocurrency investing into their own products. Its platform supplies the necessary brokerage compliance, settlement, and trading capabilities through a programmable interface.

The company works with more than 100 companies worldwide, aiming to make financial services accessible to everyone on the planet. Alpaca’s globally distributed team of 150 employees, including developers, traders, and brokerage specialists, is backed by investors such as Portage Ventures, Spark Capital, Tribe Capital, Unbound, Social Leverage, Horizons Ventures, and Y Combinator.

_Usage:_

"How does Alpaca being an API‑first brokerage differ from using a traditional brokerage UI?"

"Alpaca provides the full brokerage functionality—order routing, compliance, and settlement for stocks and crypto—through a programmable API, so developers can build custom trading experiences instead of relying on a pre‑built web interface."
