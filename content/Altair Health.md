Altair is an AI-native medical billing service for independent practices. Altair Health takes over a practice's billing end to end and get doctors paid by insurers. AI does the work; its billers handle the exceptions.

That can only work if a practice can trust an agent alone with its revenue. So it formalizeds insurers' billing policies and put a theorem prover under the agent. Before the agent acts on a claim, the prover checks the action against that insurer's policies, and anything that fails goes to one of its billers.

Every claim teaches Altair how each insurer behaves, so the longer it runs a practice's billing, the better it predicts what its insurers will pay, deny or delay.
