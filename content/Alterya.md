**Website:** https://www.alterya.dev/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/alterya

Alterya helps financial institutions, fintechs, and crypto service providers prevent account takeover (APP) fraud by detecting scams that target users and exploit platform vulnerabilities. The company provides a SaaS platform and API for analytics to secure these digital financial ecosystems.

Based in Tel Aviv, Israel, with a team of 30, Alterya operates at the intersection of fintech and crypto security. Its status is listed as Acquired, indicating the company has been bought by another entity, a common trajectory for specialized security startups in the YC ecosystem.

_Usage:_

"How does Alterya differ from standard fraud detection tools?"

"While standard tools often focus on transaction anomalies, Alterya specifically targets Account Takeover (APP) fraud. It is designed for financial institutions and crypto providers to detect scams that exploit platform access rather than just monitoring payment flows."
