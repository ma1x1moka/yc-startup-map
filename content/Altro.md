**Website:** https://altro.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/altro

Altro is a fintech platform that enables users to build their credit history regardless of their background or previous access to financial tools. It targets the 68 million people in the US, primarily women and people of color, who have historically faced bias or lack of access in the credit market. The company operates out of Los Angeles and remotely with a team of 29.

The platform provides members with a $75 line of credit specifically designed to pay for existing subscriptions like Netflix, Hulu, and HelloFresh. By allowing users to make these routine payments through Altro, the company helps them establish a positive credit score using services they are already paying for. This approach aims to empower individuals who were previously denied credit to gain access to broader financial tools such as personal loans and mortgages.

Altro was acquired, marking a significant milestone for the company in the consumer finance space.

_Usage:_

"How does Altro actually help someone with no credit history build a score?"

"Altro gives you a $75 line of credit that you can only use for subscriptions you're already paying for, like streaming services or meal kits. By making these payments through the platform, you generate a positive payment history that gets reported to credit bureaus, effectively building your score without needing a traditional bank card or large loan."
