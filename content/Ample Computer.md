**Website:** https://ample.computer/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ample-computer

Vibecoding has made creating software easy, but launching a real app still means wiring up 5-10 separate cloud services, which stops most new builders. Ample is the one-stop cloud for agents to ship complex web apps.

Add Ample to Claude or ChatGPT, tell your agent to "deploy on Ample", and get a live URL in seconds. Ample reads your codebase, works out what infrastructure it needs, and sets up compute, database, storage, CDN, domain and email, with payments, text and analytics next. It thens monitor, patch and scale your app so you never have to migrate it.

Ravi and Ishan led product and infrastructure engineering for the Shopify App Store, a platform of 25,000 apps used by 5 million merchants and processing $1B in GMV a year. Ample Computer is doing for software what Shopify did for e-commerce, so that anyone with a computer or even a phone can deploy, share and sell ambitious software from any AI platform.
