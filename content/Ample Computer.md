Cloud hosting and infrastructure platform that has everything your agent needs to ship web applications. It sets up compute, frontend, database, storage, CDN, email and (soon) text, analytics, and payments. It thens monitor, patch and scale your app so you never migrate anything again.

Ample Computer is doing for the internet software economy what Shopify did for e-commerce - remove the technical barriers to anyone can launch anything.
