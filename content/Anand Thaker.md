description: Co-founder and CTO of Pulse, a crowdsourced knowledge marketplace for IT executives.
---

Co-founded [Pulse](./Pulse.md) as CTO in 2017 with [Mayank Mehta](./Mayank%20Mehta.md) in San Francisco, part of YC's 2018 batch, building a peer-to-peer network letting IT leaders learn directly from each other. After scaling Pulse through YC and leading it to acquisition, Thaker became managing vice president of engineering at Gartner's Peer Programs.

_Usage:_

"What did Anand Thaker do after Pulse was acquired?"

"He became managing vice president of engineering at Gartner's Peer Programs, continuing in the peer-network space after Pulse's acquisition."
