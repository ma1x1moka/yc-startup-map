Founded [Within](./Within.md) (originally Klarity) with [Nischal Nadhamuni](./Nischal%20Nadhamuni.md), whom he met in an MIT entrepreneurship class. Antos holds a law degree from Harvard. What started as Klarity in YC's Summer 2018 batch has evolved into Within, partnering with Fortune 500 companies like ServiceNow, JLL, and Moody's, and with OpenAI, DoorDash, Uber, and Stripe.
