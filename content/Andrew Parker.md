Founded [Papa](./Papa.md) in 2017 to help older adults and their families stay independent while aging at home, pairing them with "Papa Pals" for companionship and everyday tasks. Parker holds a Bachelor's in Finance from Florida State and a Certified Employee Benefit Specialist certification from Wharton, and previously ran Health Systems Sales and Strategy at MDLive as one of its first 15 employees.

_Usage:_

"What does Papa actually do?"

"It's an on-demand assistance service pairing older adults and their families with 'Papa Pals' for companionship and help with everyday tasks, so people can stay independent while aging at home."
