Co-founded [Artifact](./Artifact.md) with [Corbin Klett](./Corbin%20Klett.md), building what the pair call "the VS Code for hardware engineers" — a collaborative workspace for electrical system design. Samuel is a Georgia Tech-trained electrical engineer with eight years developing avionics and software for complex aerospace systems, most recently as Avionics Lead at Hermeus, where he built the team behind the avionics for the world's fastest aircraft (Mach 5).
