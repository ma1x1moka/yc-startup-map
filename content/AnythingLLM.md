**Website:** https://anythingllm.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/anythingllm

AnythingLLM is an all-in-one AI application designed to make local AI practical for everyday users. It operates across desktop and mobile platforms, allowing individuals to run AI models on their own devices rather than relying solely on cloud services.

The project is notable for its open-source nature, having accumulated 56K stars on GitHub. By prioritizing on-device processing, it targets the consumer market with a tool that prioritizes local utility and accessibility.

_Usage:_

"How does AnythingLLM differ from typical cloud-based AI assistants?"

"AnythingLLM runs locally on your device, meaning it processes data on your hardware rather than sending it to external servers. This makes it an open-source, all-in-one solution for desktop and mobile users who want a practical, local AI experience without the dependency on cloud infrastructure."
