Apero Health is a Y Combinator-backed startup from the Summer 2019 batch that provides transparent medical billing solutions for healthcare practices. It helps practices run their operations by handling eligibility, business intelligence, claims, and patient payments, and integrates with leading electronic health record systems.

Based in San Francisco, the company serves practices nationwide and works with startups that have raised over $1B in venture capital. Its team of 24 engineers and product people focuses on delivering price transparency to both patients and providers.

Apero Health remains active and continues to expand its platform, offering a modern alternative to legacy billing tools that lack integrated EHR support.

_Usage:_

"How does Apero Health's billing platform differ from traditional billing software?"

"Apero Health focuses on transparency and ease, integrating with leading EHRs and handling eligibility, BI, claims, and patient payments, whereas traditional billing software often lacks these integrated features and transparency."
