**Website:** http://www.applydesign.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/apply-design

Apply Design is an AI-powered virtual staging application designed to help real estate professionals showcase the full potential of their properties. By transforming images of vacant or outdated spaces into fully furnished, photorealistic photos, the platform aims to help agents sell homes faster and for higher prices. The company utilizes proprietary software to generate these designs in a single click while allowing for instant customization of specific details.

Based in Tel Aviv, Israel, the four-person team operates within the real estate and construction sector. Apply Design reports virtually staging over 10,000 properties monthly, targeting a market that includes 50 million properties sold or rented annually in North America and Europe. The company positions itself as a unique solution that combines one-click generation with complete user control over design elements.

_Usage:_

"How does Apply Design differ from standard photo editing tools when it comes to staging vacant homes?"

"Unlike manual editing or generic AI filters, Apply Design uses proprietary computer vision to generate photorealistic furnishings for vacant spaces in one click. It is specifically built for real estate workflows, allowing agents to instantly customize individual details within the generated scene, which is intended to make the property more appealing to prospective buyers without the cost of physical staging."
