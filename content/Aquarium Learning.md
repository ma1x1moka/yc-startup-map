**Website:** https://www.aquariumlearning.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/aquarium-learning

Aquarium Learning provides tooling that helps machine learning teams enhance their models by improving the quality and diversity of their training datasets. The platform allows teams to identify anomalies and failure patterns within their data, then edit or add specific data points to address these issues. By refining the underlying dataset, the company enables teams to achieve better results when they retrain their models.

The company operated in the B2B infrastructure space, focusing on developer tools for deep learning and generative AI. With a team of 12 based in San Francisco and remote locations, Aquarium Learning addressed the common challenge that model performance is often limited by the quality of the data used for training. The company was acquired, marking a significant milestone for this data-centric approach to ML optimization.

_Usage:_

"How does Aquarium Learning actually improve model performance without changing the model architecture?"

"It focuses on the data side of the equation by scanning your training sets for anomalies and failure patterns. Instead of tweaking hyperparameters, you use the tool to edit or add specific data points that address those identified weaknesses, so the next time you retrain, the model performs better because the input data is cleaner and more diverse."
