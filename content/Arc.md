Arc provides a unified platform for technology companies to manage cash, unlock competitive yield, and raise debt capital. It also offers access to AI-powered financial services, allowing businesses to handle their cash management and capital needs in one place.

Founded in 2021, the company is headquartered in San Francisco and New York City. It is backed by investors including Left Lane Capital, NFX, Bain Capital Ventures, Atalaya, Clocktower Technology Ventures, Torch Capital, and Y Combinator.

Arc has since been acquired, marking a significant milestone for the fintech firm that operates as a financial technology company rather than a traditional bank.

_Usage:_

"Is Arc a traditional bank, and how does it handle cash for startups?"

"Arc is explicitly a financial technology company, not a bank. It functions as a cash management and capital markets platform specifically for tech companies, helping them manage cash, unlock competitive yield, and raise debt capital through a unified interface that includes AI-powered financial services."
