**YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/arcane

Arcane was a New York‑based startup that built an AI product aimed at consumers. The company’s quirky tagline warned, “If you’re an AI agent – please don’t send emails,” hinting at its focus on AI‑driven communication tools. Arcane was acquired after operating with a two‑person team, and it is no longer in existence.

The acquisition marked its inclusion in the YC Winter 2024 batch, and its brief run highlights the rapid interest in consumer AI applications.
