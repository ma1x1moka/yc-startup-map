**Website:** https://www.arengu.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/arengu

Arengu enables developers to create optimized signup flows in minutes, addressing the resource gap between large tech companies and smaller organizations. While giants like Airbnb, Uber, and Revolut dedicate full engineering teams to this specific function, most companies cannot afford such overhead. Arengu provides a solution that delivers these high-quality flows without the need for extensive internal development resources.

The platform claims to boost conversion rates by 20-30% by removing friction from the user onboarding process. Operating as a B2B developer tool, the company was based in A Coruña, Spain, and remotely, with a team of approximately 10 people. Arengu was acquired, marking its exit from the YC Summer 2021 batch.

_Usage:_

"How does Arengu compare to building a custom signup flow in-house?"

"Building a custom signup flow typically requires a dedicated engineering team to optimize for conversion, a resource level only companies like Airbnb or Uber can easily sustain. Arengu allows smaller teams to implement similar optimized flows in minutes, claiming a 20-30% lift in conversion rates without the ongoing maintenance burden of custom code."
