Armory delivers Continuous Deployment solutions that enable development teams to confidently release every application, every time, while maintaining safety and security. The platform is designed to accelerate time‑to‑market, improve stability, and reduce customer‑impacting issues for B2B infrastructure customers. Armory was acquired after growing to a 90‑person team operating from San Francisco, San Mateo and remotely.

The company positions its technology within the DevSecOps space, offering tools that integrate deployment pipelines with security controls to help organizations treat software as a competitive advantage.
