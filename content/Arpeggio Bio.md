Arpeggio Bio is a pioneering pharmaceutical company that develops drugs targeting transcription factors using AI and high-throughput RNA sequencing. The company focuses on "undruggable" proteins such as NRF2, TEAD, and GPX4, and its lead program is rapidly progressing toward a drug candidate for IO-resistant melanoma. With partnerships with J&J and FORMA, Arpeggio has validated its platform in rare disease and inflammation, achieving a significant Phase I success. The team of 20 works out of Boulder, CO.
