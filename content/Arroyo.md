Arroyo is a serverless stream processing platform built on top of the open-source Arroyo streaming engine. It lets companies transform, filter, aggregate, and join their Kafka streams in real-time by writing SQL, eliminating the need for managing clusters or paying fixed costs.
The platform charges purely based on usage, making it a cost‑efficient alternative for B2B infrastructure workloads. In 2025, Arroyo was acquired by Cloudflare and now powers Cloudflare Pipelines, extending its real‑time data capabilities to a broader audience.

_Usage:_

"How does Arroyo's pricing model compare to traditional Kafka stream processing solutions?"

"Arroyo charges purely based on usage, so you pay only for the compute you consume, unlike traditional setups that require fixed cluster costs."
