Arthena provides a financial product that lets high‑net‑worth individuals invest in the alternative asset class of art through a traditional fund structure. By focusing on the most liquid segment of the market, the company aims to deliver growth that is uncorrelated with traditional investments and characterized by low volatility.

The firm has built a statistically rigorous model that estimates artwork values over time using auction records and repeat‑sale data. Features such as artist name, medium, creation date, origin, and size are used to group similar works, calculate expected ROI and volatility, and run Monte Carlo simulations that produce annualized returns and Sharpe ratios. This data‑driven approach validates the financial viability of its funds.

Arthena was acquired after its Winter 2017 batch, reflecting the market’s interest in quantifying art as an investment. The company operates out of New York City with a team of twelve.

_Usage:_

"How does Arthena’s valuation model differ from a typical art appraisal service?"

"Arthena’s model relies on quantitative data from auction records and repeat sales, using features like artist, medium, date, origin, and size to group similar works and then applying Monte Carlo analysis to estimate ROI, volatility, and Sharpe ratios, whereas traditional appraisals are usually qualitative and based on expert opinion rather than statistical simulation."
