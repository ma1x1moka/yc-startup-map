**Website:** https://www.artifact.engineer/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/artifact-2

Artifact is a collaborative integrated development environment built specifically for hardware engineers. It connects the full engineering lifecycle—from design and procurement through manufacturing, integration, test, and sustainment—within a single UI. The platform includes an AI copilot that automatically generates and verifies design artifacts, linking each output to a single source of truth. Artifact’s approach is intended to make hardware development more rigorous, deliberate, and system‑aware, and the company works directly with customers tackling complex technical problems. Artifact has raised $3.5 million and operates with a four‑person team in Los Angeles. The startup is part of Y Combinator’s Winter 2025 batch and remains active.

_Usage:_

"How does Artifact’s AI copilot differ from the AI features in standard CAD tools?"

"Artifact’s copilot automatically generates every engineering artifact and ties it to a single source of truth across the entire hardware lifecycle, rather than just offering design suggestions within a CAD interface. This end‑to‑end traceability and integration across procurement, manufacturing, and test is what sets it apart."
