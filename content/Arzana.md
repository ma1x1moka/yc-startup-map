Arzana trains custom models on manufacturers' operational data to automate quoting, order entry, invoicing, and other repetitive office work. It has saved customers over $1.5M in avoided staff costs.
The company plans to expand into shop‑floor automation, sending instructions to machinery and robots to produce the orders that its office agents processed. By 2035, Arzana aims to fully automate 10,000 American factories.
Based in New York City, Arzana is a small team of six focused on B2B operations. It is an active startup in the Spring 2026 batch.
