Co-founded [Level](./Level.md) as CTO in 2021 with [Molly Hogan](./Molly%20Hogan.md) and [Vladimir Korshin](./Vladimir%20Korshin.md), part of YC's Summer 2021 batch, before it was acquired by Vouch in 2023. Schachar holds a degree in Applied Mathematics & Computer Science from Harvard, started his engineering career at Microsoft, and later managed engineering teams at Optimizely. He is now co-founder and CTO of Voicepanel (YC W24), which builds AI agents for customer research.
