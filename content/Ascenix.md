Ascenix is the all-in-one incident platform that turns unstructured EHR data into automated reporting, reimbursement recovery, compliance flagging, and clinical insight. Ascenix is already live in 50 homes and scaling across North America. It connects to systems like PointClickCare and analyze the notes nurses already write, adapting to any note structure and any compliance or reporting requirement.

It helps teams identify documentation gaps, uncover missed revenue opportunities and spot emerging resident risks, while reducing time spent reviewing charts and building spreadsheets. Its goal is to improve residents' quality of life by helping care homes reach their full clinical, financial and operational potential.
