**Website:** https://www.ashbyhq.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ashby

Ashby builds intelligent recruiting software designed to boost the productivity of hiring professionals. Its platform delivers clever automation, powerful analytics tools, and customizable workflows wrapped in a user‑friendly interface that aims to be as approachable as a spreadsheet while supporting power‑user extensions. The company counts paying customers such as Opendoor, Airtable, Duolingo, and Notion, with Notion’s Head of Talent calling Ashby the tool the recruiting team relies on most. Backed by Y Combinator, investors like Elad Gil and Lachy Groom, Ashby has grown to a team of 110 across San Francisco and remote locations, positioning itself as a notable B2B recruiting solution.

_Usage:_

"Is Ashby just another applicant tracking system, or does it provide capabilities beyond a standard ATS?"

"Ashby goes beyond a typical ATS by offering spreadsheet‑style flexibility together with automation, analytics, and highly customizable workflows, which many of its customers—including Opendoor and Notion—use daily to streamline hiring processes."
