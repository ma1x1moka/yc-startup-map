**Website:** https://aside.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/aside

Aside is an AI browser that completes real work across the websites you already use — signing in through your browser, working inside email, dashboards, internal tools, docs, and spreadsheets. Continues working when a task takes minutes or hours.

Achieved state-of-the-art on agentic browsing benchmarks, surpassing OpenAI and Anthropic's results — ranked #1 across Mind2Web, Odyssey, and bu-bench-v1. Unlike agents that depend on integrations, Aside uses the web the same way humans do.
