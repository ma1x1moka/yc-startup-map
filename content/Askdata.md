**Website:** http://www.askdata.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/askdata

Askdata lets users interact with their data through natural language, allowing them to connect their apps and data sources and ask questions such as “Show me revenue for 2018.” The platform then returns personalized data insights in response to those queries. It is positioned in the B2B analytics space and is built around artificial intelligence.

Askdata was acquired after its Winter 2019 batch, and it operates out of San Francisco with a team of 17 employees. The acquisition underscores its relevance in the AI‑driven analytics market.

_Usage:_

"How does Askdata’s natural‑language interface compare to a traditional BI tool that requires building dashboards?"

"Askdata lets users simply type a question like “Show me revenue for 2018” after connecting their data sources, and it returns a tailored insight without the need to design charts or write queries. Traditional BI tools typically require users to assemble dashboards or write SQL, whereas Askdata handles the query generation and insight personalization automatically."
