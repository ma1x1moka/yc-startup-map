AssemblyAI provides the speech-to-text and speech understanding models that power top Voice AI companies. It enables developers to launch groundbreaking products quickly while scaling with ease.

The company operates as a B2B infrastructure provider, offering an API-based platform for speech recognition. With a team of 65, it is based in New York City and operates remotely.

_Usage:_

"Why would I use AssemblyAI instead of rolling my own speech recognition pipeline?"

"AssemblyAI offers pre-built speech-to-text and understanding models via API, allowing you to launch Voice AI products faster and scale without managing the underlying model infrastructure yourself."
