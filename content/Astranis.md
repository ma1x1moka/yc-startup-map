Astranis designs, builds, and operates advanced satellites for high orbits, providing dedicated and secure telecommunications networks across the globe. The company caters to customers with stringent requirements for network visibility, uptime, customization, and data security, including large enterprises, sovereign governments, and the US military.

Operating out of a 153,000-square-foot headquarters in Northern California, Astranis handles the end-to-end design, manufacturing, and operation of its spacecraft. The company currently has five satellites on orbit with additional launches planned, supporting a commercial contract backlog of over $1 billion.

Founded in the Winter 2016 Y Combinator batch, Astranis employs a team of 500 engineers and entrepreneurs. To date, it has raised more than $750 million from prominent investors including Andreessen Horowitz, BlackRock, and Fidelity.
