**Website:** https://atlas.kitchen | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/atlas

Atlas functions as a comprehensive operating system for the restaurant industry, providing tools to launch, manage, and expand businesses across digital and physical channels. The company is particularly focused on serving the Southeast Asian market, where it aims to simplify complex operational workflows for food service operators.

The team behind Atlas previously built Grain, a venture-backed online restaurant that reached millions in revenue, bringing significant domain expertise to the platform. With a team size of 12 based in Singapore, Atlas leverages background from major organizations including Accenture, Microsoft, and Y Combinator to build its infrastructure.

_Usage:_

"How does Atlas differ from standard point-of-sale systems or restaurant management software?"

"Atlas positions itself as a full 'operating system' rather than just a POS or inventory tool, meaning it covers the entire lifecycle of a restaurant from startup to growth. It is specifically tailored for the Southeast Asian context and combines both online and offline capabilities, unlike many legacy systems that focus primarily on in-store transactions or separate e-commerce channels."
