---
description: The finite capacity to weigh what is in the context. Distinct from the window's capacity to hold it.
---

The window measures how much fits; the attention budget measures how much can actually be brought to bear. The two diverge badly. A [model](./Model.md) can hold a document it cannot reliably reason across, which is why doubling the window does not double effective capability.

Everything in [context](./Context.md) draws on the budget, including material you added defensively and never needed. This reframes context management as spending rather than storing — the question is not whether something fits, but whether it earns its share of attention.

_Usage:_

"There's plenty of room left."

"Room isn't the constraint. It's already spending attention on things we don't need."
