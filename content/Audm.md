Audm is a mobile app that turns long‑form print journalism into professionally narrated digital audio. Launched quietly in 2016, it uses professional voice actors to read news content, making it enjoyable to listen to. The company was acquired, and it had a team of 10 employees based in Los Angeles, New York, and remote locations.

_Usage:_
