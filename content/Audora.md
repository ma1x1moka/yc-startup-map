**Website:** https://audora.art | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/audora

Audora turns your book or story into an audiobook where every character has their own voice.

The best adaptation of a book is the one in your head. You can see the characters in front of you and hear them talking to each other. Audora wants an audiobook to do those characters justice: a voice that fits each of them, so a conversation actually sounds like a conversation.

Paste a story link or upload a file. Audora works out who's speaking and gives each character a distinct voice alongside the narrator. You can start listening while the rest of the book is still being made.
