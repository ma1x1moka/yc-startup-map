AutoHub is a marketplace for automotive wholesale transactions, offering a transparent, efficient, convenient and cost-efficient digital auction platform. The service targets B2B and retail customers, enabling dealerships to appraise vehicle trade-ins with greater speed and clarity. AutoHub was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does AutoHub’s digital auction platform improve the trade-in appraisal process compared to traditional methods?"

"AutoHub’s platform streamlines the appraisal process by providing a fully digital, transparent marketplace that reduces the time and cost associated with traditional car auctions, allowing dealerships to evaluate trade-ins more quickly and accurately."
