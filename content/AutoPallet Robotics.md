AutoPallet Robotics is building the next generation of warehouse robotics to address the approximately $10 billion annual cost of human labor for moving cardboard boxes in US warehouses. The company focuses on case picking and mixed-SKU palletization, addressing the high installation costs and complexity that have kept manual operations prevalent in the industry.

Its solution involves swarms of small mobile robots designed to install into existing warehouses with significantly lower costs than current automation alternatives. These robots are engineered to be both flexible and robust, providing a practical path to automating tasks that are currently labor-intensive.

Based in San Francisco, the six-person team operates within the industrials, manufacturing, and robotics sectors, leveraging machine learning and swarm robotics to deliver a low-cost automation solution.

_Usage:_

"How does AutoPallet's approach to warehouse automation differ from traditional, expensive robotic systems?"

"Instead of requiring major infrastructure changes or high upfront investment, AutoPallet uses swarms of small mobile robots that can be installed directly into existing warehouses. This design aims to provide a low-cost, robust solution for case picking and palletization that is more flexible and easier to deploy than the expensive, difficult-to-install systems currently on the market."
