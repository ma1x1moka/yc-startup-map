---
description: Compaction the harness triggers on its own when the window fills, without asking.
---

Autocompact keeps a long [session](./Session.md) alive unattended, which is essential for [AFK](./AFK.md) work and invisible while it goes well. The trigger is capacity, not quality, so it fires wherever the session happens to be — often mid-task.

The failure it produces is quiet: work continues on a summary that dropped something load-bearing, and nobody sees the moment it happened. Where fidelity matters more than continuity, a deliberate [handoff](./Handoff.md) at a boundary you chose beats an automatic one at a boundary the window chose.

_Usage:_

"Nobody compacted anything."

"Autocompact did, forty minutes ago. Check the transcript for the boundary."
