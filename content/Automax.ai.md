Automax.ai does property appraisals faster and more transparent. Automax.ai's mobile app uses LiDAR and computer vision to capture property details automatically, and its custom-trained AI agents combine that data with all the analysis and aggregation required to to generate a complete appraisal report in minutes. The result is a clear, grounded valuation, fully Fannie Mae & Freddie Mac compliant — done in under 20 minutes from start to finish.
