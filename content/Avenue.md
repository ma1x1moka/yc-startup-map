**Website:** https://avenue.app/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/avenue

Avenue is an observability platform designed to help business and operations teams create simple, set-and-forget alerts directly from their existing data sources. By allowing users to monitor nuanced triggers that might not fit on a standard dashboard, the tool aims to make teams more proactive in their daily operations.

The platform focuses on improving response times to critical problems, potentially reducing detection windows from several days to real-time. It functions as a specialized alternative to broader monitoring tools, specifically tailored for those who need to alert on the data they already manage in their warehouses or databases.

Founded in Winter 2021 and based in New York City, Avenue was acquired, marking its exit from the active startup scene.

_Usage:_

"How does Avenue differ from standard monitoring tools like Datadog or PagerDuty?"

"Avenue is positioned as a simpler, more direct way to set up alerts specifically on your database or data warehouse. While tools like Datadog or PagerDuty are often broader in scope, Avenue focuses on letting operations teams create set-and-forget alerts on nuanced data triggers that might not be visible on a standard dashboard, aiming to cut response times from days to real-time."
