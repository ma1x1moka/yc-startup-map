**Website:** https://aviator.co | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/aviator

Aviator is a developer tool designed to automate merge workflows and maintain build health, aiming to reduce the time engineers spend on manual integration tasks. By streamlining these processes, the platform helps teams avoid the frustration of broken builds and saves significant weekly hours for engineering staff.

Based in San Francisco, the company serves a roster of notable users including Slack, Square, Benchling, Figma, and DoorDash. Aviator positions itself as a leading brand for developer experience, focusing on improving productivity within B2B engineering and product design environments.

_Usage:_

"How does Aviator handle merge conflicts compared to standard CI/CD pipelines?"

"Aviator automates the merge workflow specifically to keep builds healthy, which allows engineers to save up to five hours a week by avoiding the manual effort and frustration associated with broken builds."
