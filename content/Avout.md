**Website:** https://www.avoutcapital.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/avout

Avout finances equipment for physical AI companies, starting with robot fleets, then data centers and data receivables.

Hardware startups pay for machines with venture equity, the most expensive money they'll raise. Avout packages each deal so lenders can price it, match it to the right fund, and run it to close. Every deal adds to the record of how these machines perform, default and recover, which no lender has today.

It haves $8M in LOIs from borrowers, $10M in LOIs from capital providers, and its first $1.5M term sheet for a data center company.
