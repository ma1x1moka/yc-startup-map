**Website:** https://www.avoutcapital.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/avout

Avout provides non-dilutive financing to companies building in the physical world: robotics, space, drones, autonomy, and AI infrastructure & data.

Hardware companies need capital for equipment, inventory, and production long before revenue catches up. Today they fund it with venture equity, the most expensive money they'll ever raise, because banks don't understand the assets or move at startup speed.

Avout is starting with asset-backed lending and revenue-based financing, using a company's own operating data to make decisions quickly. Its vision is to become the financial rails for the machine economy.
