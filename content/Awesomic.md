Awesomic is a B2B marketplace that connects companies with vetted product talents, including designers, engineers, and no-coders, within 24 hours. The platform guarantees results every 24 business hours through its app, operating on a flat monthly subscription model.

Based in San Francisco with a team of 150, the company has successfully matched over 4,000 companies, including more than 400 YC startups, with talents who have completed over 14,000 projects. It focuses on delivering rapid access to high-quality product resources for businesses.

_Usage:_

"How does Awesomic's matching speed and pricing model compare to traditional recruiting agencies?"

"Traditional agencies often take weeks to source candidates and charge success fees or hourly rates, whereas Awesomic guarantees a match within 24 hours and charges a flat monthly subscription. It positions itself as a faster, more predictable alternative for companies needing immediate access to vetted designers and engineers."
