**Website:** https://axle.insure | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/axle

Axle operates as an AI-native clearinghouse for the property and casualty insurance sector. The company provides infrastructure and AI agents that automate complex workflows for third parties, handling tasks such as verification, monitoring, and policy updates.

Based in New York City with a team of 20, Axle has helped Fortune 500 businesses including Rocket Mortgage, Avis, and Experian accelerate their operations. The platform claims to speed up workflows by 95% while recovering over $220M in potential losses.

Axle is backed by Gradient, Y Combinator, and the founding team of Plaid, positioning it at the intersection of fintech and advanced AI application in the insurance industry.

_Usage:_

"How does Axle differ from traditional insurance clearinghouses?"

"Axle uses AI agents to automate specific, complex steps like verification and policy updates rather than just moving data between systems. This approach is designed to significantly reduce processing time, with the company claiming a 95% speedup for clients like Rocket Mortgage and Experian, while also identifying and recovering potential losses that might otherwise slip through."
