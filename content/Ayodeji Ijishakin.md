Co-founded [Mecha Health](./Mecha%20Health.md) with [Hugo Fry](./Hugo%20Fry.md), [Nina Montaña Brown](./Nina%20Monta%C3%B1a%20Brown.md), and [Ahmed Abdulaal](./Ahmed%20Abdulaal.md), whom he met through UCL's Medical Imaging PhD program. Ijishakin is a Machine Learning & Medical Imaging PhD candidate at UCL, publishing at NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR, and presenting at international medical imaging conferences and Harvard Medical School.
