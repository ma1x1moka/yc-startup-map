**Website:** https://www.getbalance.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/balance

Balance is the easiest way for B2B marketplaces, merchants, and service providers to process payments online. It offers the first business checkout, letting business customers pay how they want—check, card, or bank—and when they want, with net terms, payment plans, or invoice payments. The seller receives funds instantly and easily, similar to a card payment. The company raised its last funding round from Lightspeed, Stripe, and Max Levchin to build a "Stripe for B2B" platform. With a team of 70 based in San Francisco, Balance is positioned at the intersection of fintech and payments, aiming to simplify B2B payment flows.

_Usage:_

"What payment methods does Balance support for business customers?"

"Balance allows business customers to pay by check, card, or bank transfer, and offers flexible payment timing such as net terms, payment plans, or invoice payments."
