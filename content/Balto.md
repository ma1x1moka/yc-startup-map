**Website:** http://playbalto.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/balto

Balto offers a contest automation platform that significantly reduces the time and manual labor required to run contests. The tool gives organizers easy ways to manage, track, and communicate with hundreds or thousands of players.

The company, based in San Francisco with a five‑person team, was acquired by FuboTV, highlighting its relevance in the sports‑tech and gaming space. Its software is used to simplify contest operations for consumer and gaming industries.

_Usage:_

"How does Balto’s contest automation differ from using ad‑hoc spreadsheets or manual processes to run gaming contests?"

"Balto replaces manual spreadsheets with a dedicated platform that lets organizers create, track, and communicate contest details at scale, cutting down the labor needed to handle hundreds or thousands of participants."
