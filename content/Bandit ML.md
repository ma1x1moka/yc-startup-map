**Website:** https://www.banditml.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/bandit-ml

Bandit ML developed machine learning tools specifically designed for e-commerce businesses. The company operated as a B2B SaaS provider, helping online retailers leverage data-driven insights.

The startup was based in San Francisco, CA, and operated with a remote-friendly team of four. It was part of the Summer 2020 Y Combinator batch.

Bandit ML has since been acquired, marking its exit from the independent startup landscape.
