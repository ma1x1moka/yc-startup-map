Baseline AI was a San Francisco‑based SaaS that used AI to automate the creation of clinical trial documents and data workflows. It replaced manual steps in producing study protocols, data collection forms, database designs, error checks, analysis code, and data transformation mappings, cutting headcount spend and months of time, potentially saving up to $27 million in direct costs for a single phase‑3 trial.

The company served the healthcare and health‑tech sectors with a B2B solution that leveraged artificial intelligence to accelerate trial readiness and reduce lost revenue. Baseline AI was founded in Summer 2024, had a team of two, and is now inactive, indicating it has since shut down.
