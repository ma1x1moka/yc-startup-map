**Website:** https://www.basepilot.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/basepilot

Basepilot develops line-of-business-specific AI agents designed to streamline claims operations for insurers, third-party administrators, and managing general agents. The company aims to make these processes more efficient, less costly, and more intelligent through automated assistance.

Headquartered in San Francisco, the two-person team is part of the Winter 2024 YC batch. Basepilot serves major industry players such as Old Republic International and Zurich, applying artificial intelligence to specific insurance workflows.

_Usage:_

"How does Basepilot handle the complexity of different insurance lines of business?"

"Instead of using a generic model, Basepilot deploys AI agents that are specific to the line of business, ensuring the automation is tailored to the particular claims processes of insurers, TPAs, and MGAs."
