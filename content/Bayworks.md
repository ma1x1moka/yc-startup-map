**Website:** https://bayworks.app | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/bayworks

Bayworks is building the intelligence that runs vehicle repair. Bayworks captures how expert technicians diagnose and fix cars, then use that knowledge to guide every technician in real time and run the back office on autopilot.

In four months, it haves gone from zero to almost seven figures in cARR, across 6 dealership groups including a top-50 US dealer ($3Bn annual revenue), and have powered over 50,000 repairs.

This becomes the default system for vehicle repair, where dealerships, car manufacturers, insurers and warranty providers transact. Every repair it captures builds the knowledge to eventually direct robots to perform the repairs themselves.
