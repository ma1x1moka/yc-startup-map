Founded by [Mark Pothen](./Mark%20Pothen.md) and [Obinna Akahara](./Obinna%20Akahara.md). San Francisco. Backed pre-YC by Accel and Sequoia scouts.

Raised **$5.4M** pre-YC. Currently operational with an IPA supporting **40,000 patients**.

AI agents working inside existing EHR (Electronic Health Record) systems to automate administrative tasks that consume 2+ hours of a physician's day: quality gap closures, risk adjustment, patient outreach, prior authorization requests, referral coordination, and follow-up scheduling.

The physician co-founder (Pothen) grew up in his mother's primary care practice and spent 6 months embedded in a clinic implementing AI before founding the company. That depth of clinical workflow understanding is what makes EHR integration decisions that outsider founders miss — and what makes the clinical trust required for adoption possible.

The Accel and Sequoia pre-YC backing signals institutional due diligence had already validated the team on clinical validity before YC added its check.

Works alongside [LunaBill](./LunaBill.md) in healthcare AI: [LunaBill](./LunaBill.md) handles the financial layer (billing calls outside EHR), Beacon Health handles the clinical administrative layer inside the EHR.
