**Website:** https://beeper.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/beeper

Beeper is a consumer application designed to solve the fragmentation of modern messaging by connecting to over 15 different chat networks within a single interface. It offers features such as unified search and advanced filtering, allowing users to manage communications across platforms like iMessage and Android-based services without switching apps. The company operates with a team of 16 people, with locations in San Francisco, Palo Alto, and remote positions.

The platform is available on Desktop, Android, iPhone, and iPad, aiming to eliminate the friction of remembering which specific app was used for a particular conversation or locating shared media. By consolidating these disparate channels, Beeper seeks to boost communication efficiency for users who interact across multiple ecosystems.

Beeper was acquired, marking a significant milestone in the consumer messaging space.

_Usage:_

"How does Beeper handle the lack of native cross-platform support between systems like iMessage and Android?"

"Beeper acts as a bridge by connecting to these separate networks simultaneously, providing a unified inbox where you can send and receive messages across different platforms without needing to install or switch between individual apps."
