Berbix offers best-in-class ID verification and proprietary fraud detection technology, accessible through developer-friendly SDKs.

Founded in Summer 2018, the company serves a broad range of industries, including B2B, engineering, product and design, and has grown to a team of 39 in San Francisco.

Berbix was acquired, and it recently raised a Series A led by Mayfield.
