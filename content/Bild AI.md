**Website:** https://www.bild.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/bild-ai

Bild AI automates Division 8 estimating for doors, frames, and hardware. It reads blueprints and specs, generates opening schedules automatically, prices the bid, and exports it directly into the ERPs that Division 8 shops already run on, including Comsense, Avaware, and ProTech.

Division 8 estimating is a brutal trifecta of nitty-gritty details where typos cost hundreds of thousands and timelines are tight; Bild AI collapses days of takeoff into hours, allowing clients to bid 50% more with smaller estimating teams. The company is backed by Khosla Ventures (YC W25) and operates from San Francisco with a team of nine.
