BillionToOne is a public healthcare company based in San Francisco and Menlo Park that has developed a DNA molecular counter to increase cell-free DNA diagnostics resolution by over 1,000 fold. This technology enables transformative improvements in prenatal screening and liquid biopsy for cancers, allowing for more precise detection and measurement of disease.

The company’s flagship product, Unity Screen, is a commercially available non-invasive prenatal test (NIPT) that assesses fetal risk for both recessive conditions and aneuploidies from maternal blood. It is noted as the only NIPT capable of evaluating both types of risks simultaneously, with publications highlighting its accuracy and advantages over other screening methods. BillionToOne has also applied its molecular counting technology to develop liquid biopsy tests aimed at transforming oncology care.
