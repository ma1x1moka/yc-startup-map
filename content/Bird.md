**Website:** http://bird.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/bird

Bird provides the foundational infrastructure for business communications, enabling companies to interact with customers across channels such as SMS, Voice, WhatsApp, WeChat, Messenger, and Email. The platform ensures that these interactions occur with the correct context, supporting connections to billions of devices globally.

Headquartered in Amsterdam, the company operates through nine global hubs including locations in Singapore, Dublin, and London. It serves nearly 20,000 customers worldwide and maintains a distributed team of 500 employees representing over 55 nationalities.

_Usage:_

"How does Bird handle multi-channel context for customer support teams?"

"Bird acts as a unified layer that aggregates communication streams from various providers like SMS, WhatsApp, and Voice into a single interface. This allows businesses to maintain context across different channels, ensuring that customer interactions are consistent regardless of the platform used for the conversation."
