Bloom is a fintech company based in San Francisco that aims to improve financial outcomes by providing users with the knowledge and tools necessary to achieve prosperity. The platform functions as an investing app that educates users on building wealth through interactive financial lessons and a gamified rewards system, while also offering an account designed for long-term investment.

Since its launch in April 2022, the company has grown to over 1 million users and surpassed $3 million in annual recurring revenue. With a team of eight, Bloom operates at the intersection of education and consumer finance, focusing on making investing accessible and engaging for a broader audience.

_Usage:_

"How does Bloom differentiate itself from standard brokerage apps?"

"Bloom integrates education directly into the investment process by using interactive lessons and a gamified rewards economy to teach users how to build wealth, rather than just providing a trading interface."
