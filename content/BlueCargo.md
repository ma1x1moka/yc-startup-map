BlueCargo automates complex freight transactions, managing all financial transactions between logistics businesses so they can focus on moving cargo. It helps global shippers optimize supply chains with accurate and automated financial and operational audits and a tech‑first user experience.
Founded in the Summer 2018 Y Combinator batch, BlueCargo has raised a $4 million seed round and a $11 million Series A. The company operates from offices in Los Angeles and New York and has a team of 35 employees. It has already achieved product‑market fit, built a working platform, and serves hundreds of daily active users.
BlueCargo is in an early growth phase and is recruiting senior software engineers to expand the team and deliver new features, requiring full‑stack web development experience and AWS expertise.
