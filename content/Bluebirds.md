**Website:** https://www.bluebirds.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/bluebirds

Bluebirds is a San Francisco-based startup that helps go-to-market teams automate outbound targeting using real-time intent signals. By creating custom signals from trusted data sources, the platform continuously scans the market to identify and deliver ready-to-prospect leads directly into a company's CRM.

The tool is designed to save sales representatives significant time on manual list building and prospect research, claiming to save two hours a day per rep. Bluebirds was part of the Winter 2023 Y Combinator batch and has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Bluebirds handle the initial setup for defining who to target?"

"You create custom intent signals from trusted data sources, a process the company states takes about three minutes. Once set up, the system continuously scans your market and pushes leads that match those signals into your CRM, removing the need for manual list building."
