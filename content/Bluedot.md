**Website:** https://thebluedot.co/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/bluedot

Bluedot operates as a unified payment infrastructure for electric vehicle charging, addressing the lack of standardized payment and expense management tools in the growing EV market. The company launched its Bluedot card in December 2022, leveraging a team with strong fintech backgrounds to handle the transactional complexity of EV usage for both individual drivers and commercial fleets.

The startup is based in San Francisco and has a team of 15 members. It secured pre-seed funding from Y Combinator, Axel Springer, Porsche, Ford, TechNexus, DRF, and Pritchard Automotive. The company reported rapid growth, citing a 40-45% weekly increase and $110K in GMV in the week prior to its directory entry.

_Usage:_

"How does Bluedot handle the payment side of EV charging compared to existing fragmented solutions?"

"Bluedot acts as a single payment platform that consolidates the process of paying for EV charging and managing associated expenses. Instead of dealing with multiple networks or manual expense tracking, users utilize the Bluedot card to streamline transactions for both personal driving and fleet operations, effectively filling a gap in the payments management infrastructure for the electric vehicle sector."
