Raised **$6.3M** from General Catalyst (January 2026). **500,000+ minutes of calls monthly**. Supports 10+ Indian languages, 50+ accents, sub-500ms latency.

Automates phone calls across sales, support, collections, and recruitment for Indian enterprises. Builds Voice AI Agents from existing transcripts and FAQs — no complex training pipeline required.

The key distinction: English-first voice AI is useless for most of India's 600M+ smartphone users. Bolna trains on vernacular audio from the start rather than bolting on translation to an English-first system. The quality gap is significant and hard to close retroactively.

The geographic specificity is the moat — a global voice AI provider cannot easily replicate the data infrastructure for each Indian language from scratch.
