**Website:** https://bookoapp.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/booko

Booko helps businesses that sell bookable time slots make more money by dynamically pricing their time. Unlike physical inventory, unsold time slots disappear forever, yet most time-slot-based businesses still rely on static pricing. Booko integrates with existing booking systems to adjust prices and incentives based on historical utilization, availability, and demand. Early customers see ~20% revenue uplift by selling time that would have otherwise gone unsold.
