Boom reaches out to your customers before you lose them.

Boom AI runs proactive outbound conversations at scale: recovering churned users, re-engaging drop-offs, and protecting revenue you thought was gone. AI agents hold real conversations with your customers over SMS, email, WhatsApp, or phone, in their language, on the channel they actually answer.

Every month, companies lose 5-15% of revenue to churn, and more walks out through abandoned signups and failed onboarding. The tools built to fix this don't work. Win-back emails get ignored. Cancel flows only catch people at the door. Hiring humans doesn't scale. Boom does the one thing that works: a real conversation. 1,000x the reach of manual outreach, and the customer can reply.

_Usage:_

"How does Boom AI differ from traditional churn tools like win-back emails and cancel flows?"

"Standard win-back emails get ignored and cancel flows only catch users when they are already at the door. Boom instead runs proactive, two-way AI conversations across SMS, email, WhatsApp, or phone in the customer's language, giving you 1,000x the reach of manual human outreach while letting customers actually reply."
