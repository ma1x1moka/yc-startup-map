Booth AI offers a generative‑AI powered platform that lets brand managers, merchandisers, and marketers produce beautiful lifestyle images in a matter of seconds, replacing the complicated, labor‑intensive, time‑consuming and expensive commercial product photoshoots. The company was acquired, marking its success in the B2B retail space. With a lean team of three, Booth AI operates out of San Francisco, CA, and remotely, focusing on delivering high‑quality visual content for e-commerce marketing.
