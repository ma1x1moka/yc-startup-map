Bouncer is a compact SDK that lets mobile apps scan and verify credit cards and photo IDs. It was launched in Summer 2019 and targets B2B clients in engineering, product, and design.

The company, headquartered in San Francisco and Oakland, had a lean team of two. Bouncer was acquired, marking its success in the identity verification space. Its website is https://www.getbouncer.com/.
