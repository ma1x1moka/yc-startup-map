**Website:** http://www.breadcrumbs.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/breadcrumbs-io

Breadcrumbs is a B2B sales analytics platform that analyzes customer and prospect data to uncover hidden revenue opportunities. It utilizes a no-code scoring engine designed to be implemented in minutes rather than months, allowing sales teams to prioritize the right opportunities at the right time.

The company, which operated with a team of approximately 20 people from San Francisco and remotely, was acquired after its YC Summer 2021 batch. This acquisition marks its notable exit from the startup ecosystem.

_Usage:_

"How does Breadcrumbs differ from traditional CRM analytics tools in terms of setup time?"

"Breadcrumbs focuses on a no-code scoring engine that can be set up in minutes, whereas traditional implementations often take months. This speed allows sales teams to immediately start identifying engaged upsell opportunities without lengthy configuration processes."
