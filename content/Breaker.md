**Website:** https://www.breaker.audio | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/breaker

Breaker is a podcast listening and discovery application launched today in San Francisco. It aims to become the preferred place for podcasters to distribute premium audio by signing exclusive deals and charging users $6 per month. The app offers podcasters tools to connect with their community, gather feedback, and view analytics on content performance. It reported a 67% retention rate after a month among beta users. The market context includes 67 million Americans listening to podcasts monthly and high‑budget productions like Serial reaching 100 million downloads, suggesting a sizable audience for paid premium podcasts. Breaker was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Breaker convince listeners to pay $6 a month for podcasts that are often free?"

"Breaker plans to sign exclusive deals for the best premium podcasts and charge a $6 monthly subscription, while offering podcasters community‑building features and detailed analytics that help creators understand what content performs best, leveraging its strong beta‑user retention to build a paying audience."
