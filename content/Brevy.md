**Website:** https://brevy.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/brevy

Brevy is an AI chatbot designed to empower customer service teams through automation. It utilizes large language models to resolve eCommerce support tickets, claiming to achieve human-level accuracy at a fraction of the cost.

The company operated as a remote team of eight, focusing on B2B SaaS solutions for the marketing and customer support sectors. Brevy was acquired, marking the end of its independent operations.

_Usage:_

"How does Brevy handle eCommerce support tickets compared to traditional manual handling?"

"Brevy uses large language models to automatically resolve eCommerce support tickets. It is designed to match the accuracy of human agents while significantly reducing the cost per resolution, allowing customer service teams to handle volume through automation rather than manual labor."
