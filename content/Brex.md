**Website:** https://www.brex.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/brex

Brex is an AI-powered spend platform that unifies corporate cards, expense management, travel, business accounts, and bill pay into a single system. Operating out of San Francisco with a team of 1,000, the platform is designed to automate manual expense workflows and enable real-time tracking across more countries and currencies than competing spend solutions. Brex was acquired.

The platform features corporate cards offering 10–20x higher limits and business accounts with $6M in FDIC insurance, same-day liquidity, and global payment capabilities. Serving tens of thousands of customers, Brex supports high-growth organizations such as DoorDash, Coinbase, ScaleAI, SeatGeek, and Superhuman.

_Usage:_

"How does Brex integrate business accounts and corporate cards into unified spend management?"

"Brex combines business accounts—offering $6M in FDIC insurance, same-day liquidity, and global payment processing—with corporate cards that feature 10-20x higher limits. Rather than relying on post-purchase expense reporting, the platform automates manual expense work, travel, and bill pay in real time within a single system."
