Co-founded [dili](./dili.md) as CTO with [Anand Chaturvedi](./Anand%20Chaturvedi.md), part of YC's Summer 2023 batch, building AI automation that combines with expert compliance teams to handle prevailing wage and Davis-Bacon requirements for large infrastructure projects. dili has raised $3.5M from investors including Y Combinator, Allianz, and Rebel Fund.

"What kind of compliance does dili automate?"
