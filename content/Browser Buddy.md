**Website:** https://www.browserbuddy.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/browser-buddy

Browser Buddy is a recommendation system that helps users discover high-quality essays across the web. It curates content based on the topics users are interested in.

It was acquired, highlighting its perceived value in the consumer content space. The company operates out of San Francisco with a two‑person team, focusing on AI‑driven content discovery for consumers.

_Usage:_

"How does Browser Buddy’s recommendation approach differ from using a standard search engine to find essays?"

"Browser Buddy uses an AI‑powered recommendation system that surfaces essays tailored to a user’s topic of interest, rather than returning a generic list of search results. It aims to surface higher‑quality writing directly, making the discovery experience more focused than a broad web search."
