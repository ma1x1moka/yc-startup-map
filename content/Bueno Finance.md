Bueno Finance operates as a credit building platform designed for the next billion users in India. The company offers a digital credit card that functions over the UPI payment network, aiming to make credit accessible to a broader demographic. It was founded in Winter 2021 and is based in Gurugram, India.

The company has since been acquired, marking a significant milestone in its trajectory within the Indian fintech sector. With a team of 18, Bueno Finance focused on the intersection of neobanking and credit lending to serve underserved markets.

_Usage:_

"How does Bueno Finance integrate credit with India's existing payment infrastructure?"

"Bueno Finance issues a digital credit card that operates directly over UPI, the Unified Payments Interface. This allows users to build credit history through standard UPI transactions rather than relying on traditional bank-issued cards, effectively leveraging the existing payment rails for credit access."
