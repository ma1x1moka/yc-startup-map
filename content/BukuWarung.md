BukuWarung is Southeast Asia's fastest growing startup, building digital infrastructure for 60 million micro-businesses in Indonesia. Its platform gives MSMEs bookkeeping, an online storefront, and payment capabilities, aiming to make them financially aware and help them grow. The company has raised $80 million from top-tier VCs including Peter Thiel's Valar Ventures, Goodwater Capital, Y-Combinator, Rocketship.vc, AC Ventures, Quona Capital, East Ventures, Golden Gate Ventures, SoftBank Partners, DST Global Partners, and strategic angels from Stripe, PayPal, Plaid, Grab, Gojek, Facebook, AirBnB, Fast, and Mastercard. It employs about 250 people and is headquartered in Jakarta.
