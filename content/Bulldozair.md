**Website:** https://www.bulldozair.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/bulldozair

Bulldozair provides a web and mobile platform designed to help organizations with real estate or industrial assets deliver construction projects efficiently. The company digitally integrates the entire construction process, including compliance and checklists, delivering these tasks directly to contractors and engineering teams. By using a mobile application, field teams collect data such as forms, measurements, and pictures, which are uploaded directly to the collaborative platform.

The system allows users to track project progress via a dashboard and receive alerts for potential delays or technical risks. All communication between stakeholders is archived for traceability, and legal and contractual reporting that previously took weeks is now automated. Bulldozair was acquired.

Based in Paris, France, the company was backed by Y Combinator, Station F, and Kima Ventures. It serves major corporate clients in infrastructure construction, manufacturing, energy, and retail, operating with a team of eight.

_Usage:_

"How does Bulldozair handle field data and reporting compared to traditional methods?"

"It replaces manual, week-long legal and contractual reporting with automated processes by having field teams upload data like forms, measurements, and pictures directly from a mobile app to a central dashboard. This ensures all stakeholder communication is archived for traceability and alerts users to potential delays or technical risks in real-time, rather than relying on delayed manual updates."
