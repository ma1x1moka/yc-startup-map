**Website:** https://bulletin.co | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/bulletin

Bulletin is a small business community that lets independent brands and retailers connect, transact, and grow together. It operates as a B2B marketplace focused on the retail industry.

Bulletin was acquired after its Winter 2017 YC batch. The company is based in New York City and has a team of about 15 people.

_Usage:_

"How is Bulletin different from other B2B retail marketplaces?"

"Bulletin is positioned as a community for small independent brands, emphasizing direct connections and growth opportunities with retailers, rather than just a generic transaction platform."
