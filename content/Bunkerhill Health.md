Bunkerhill Health offers an agentic AI platform designed specifically for health systems, enabling them to turn ideas into operational reality. The company is backed by Sequoia and Khosla, reflecting strong investor confidence in its approach. Based in San Francisco with a team of about 30 engineers, Bunkerhill operates within the healthcare and healthcare IT industries.

The platform’s focus on agentic AI distinguishes it from traditional decision‑support tools by allowing the system to act autonomously within clinical workflows. Its active status and participation in Y Combinator’s Winter 2019 batch underscore its ongoing development and market presence.

_Usage:_

"What does ‘agentic AI’ mean for a health system platform like Bunkerhill?"

"In Bunkerhill’s case, ‘agentic AI’ refers to an artificial‑intelligence system that can not only analyze data but also execute actions within a health‑system workflow, effectively turning a user’s idea into an automated process."
