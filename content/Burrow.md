Burrow is a direct-to-consumer furniture company that reinvents home furnishings by focusing on sustainability and ease of assembly. The company’s products are designed to be more comfortable, functional, and durable than industry standards, shipping within a week and allowing for simple disassembly. Burrow was named one of the 10 most innovative retail brands in the world by Fast Company in 2018, and its sofa was recognized as one of the 50 best inventions of the year by TIME.

Based in New York City with a team of approximately 60, Burrow operates in the consumer and home industries. The company has since been acquired, marking a significant milestone in its trajectory as a notable player in the modern furniture market.
