**Website:** https://www.buymeacoffee.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/buy-me-a-coffee

Buy Me a Coffee is a fast and beautiful way for creators to fund their creative work. It is a fintech marketplace that enables creators to receive monetary support directly from fans.

The service is popular with over a million creators and operates from London with a remote‑friendly team of about 30 people.

_Usage:_

"How does Buy Me a Coffee let creators get paid compared to other creator‑support platforms?"

"It provides a simple, web‑based checkout where supporters can send a one‑time payment, often called a “coffee,” without needing a subscription model; the platform is described as fast and beautiful and is used by more than a million creators."
