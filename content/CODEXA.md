Codexa is an AI agent that manages the full scope of international trade operations for exporters and importers. It handles customs processes with government agencies and regulators, orchestrates cross-border payments, and provides credit solutions for trade finance to move goods and money seamlessly across borders.

Built by a Central Bank–licensed FX bank, the platform is designed to eliminate regulatory friction and reduce costs in global commerce. The company is based in Manaus, Brazil, and maintains a team of 40 people.

The system currently powers over USD 2 billion in annual volume across more than 17,000 transactions, demonstrating significant scale in the fintech and payments sectors.

_Usage:_

"How does Codexa handle the regulatory side of international trade compared to manual processes?"

"Instead of manually navigating different government agencies and regulators for each shipment, Codexa’s AI agent directly handles the customs processes. It automates the interaction with these entities while simultaneously orchestrating the cross-border payments and providing necessary trade finance credit, effectively removing the manual regulatory friction from the workflow."
