**Website:** https://cspa.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/cspa

CSPA provides a comprehensive standardized exam for software engineers that evaluates technical knowledge, skills, and abilities across a broad array of topics. The company builds an exam content management system, a proctoring framework, and an autograding system, and it also offers a SaaS product that lets employers browse a database of candidates. CSPA was acquired and is based in San Francisco with a team of six, operating in the B2B recruiting and talent industry.

_Usage:_

"What does CSPA provide to employers?"

"CSPA offers a SaaS employer product that allows employers to browse a candidate database and access standardized assessment results."
