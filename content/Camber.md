Camber is a healthcare technology company based in New York City that builds software to streamline administrative workflows for behavioral health clinicians. By replacing manual efforts with automated solutions, the platform allows providers to focus more time on delivering high-quality care rather than handling paperwork.

The company operates within the mental health tech sector and was part of Y Combinator’s Winter 2021 batch. With a team of 85 employees, Camber is backed by investors including a16z, Y Combinator, and Craft, reflecting its focus on making behavioral health more accessible.

_Usage:_

"How does Camber differ from general electronic health record (EHR) systems?"

"While general EHRs often require clinicians to manually input data and manage administrative tasks, Camber specifically targets behavioral health by automating these manual efforts. This allows clinicians to spend less time on administrative overhead and more time on direct patient care, addressing the specific inefficiencies in the behavioral health workflow."
