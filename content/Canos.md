**Website:** https://www.canos.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/canos

There are 3 times more dog cancer patients than human cancer patients diagnosed in the US every year. Canos is bringing a human-grade liquid biopsy platform for dogs, starting with a single blood draw. Cancer changes how DNA breaks apart and the methylation patterns. It sequences the fragments in a routine blood draw and use AI to spot those changes across the genome. A suspicious result can prompt a vet to order imaging. Early detections save lives and reduce cancer treatment costs. After diagnosis, repeat draws show whether the cancer signal is rising or falling between scans.

Its AI-empowered bioinformatics model reached 91% sensitivity at 95% specificity. Canos is two Oxford scientists who previously built an AI pathology system at ZYTCA that reached over 10,000 veterinary clinics in China.

It estimates a $4 billion annual US screening opportunity at one $200 test a year for 20 million dogs in higher-income households. By following the same dogs through diagnosis and treatment, it is building a dataset to learn which changes in blood predict relapse in cancers that dogs and people share.
