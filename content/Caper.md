**Website:** https://www.caper.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/caper

Caper builds a smart shopping cart that bundles Amazon‑Go‑style image recognition, sensor fusion, and AI into a compact, plug‑and‑play system. Shoppers can drop groceries into the cart and automatically check out without a cashier, enabling a cashier‑less checkout experience in physical stores.
Founded in Winter 2016, the startup grew to a team of 15. Caper was acquired, positioning its technology for rapid deployment in grocery retailers looking to modernize their in‑store experience.

_Usage:_

"How does Caper’s smart cart enable cashier‑less checkout without a full Amazon‑Go store?"

"Caper’s cart integrates image recognition, sensor fusion, and AI to detect items placed inside, automatically calculating the total and allowing the shopper to leave without a cashier. The system is compact enough to fit in a standard cart, making it a plug‑and‑play solution for existing grocery stores."
