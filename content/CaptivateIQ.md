**Website:** https://www.captivateiq.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/captivateiq

CaptivateIQ builds software to modernize incentive compensation, offering tools that let businesses design, deploy and adapt commission plans with greater transparency. It partners with companies such as Carta, Intercom, Gong and Newfront Insurance to simplify their commissions. Since its 2017 launch, CaptivateIQ has raised $63 million from investors including Sequoia Capital, Accel, S28 Capital, Amity Ventures and Y Combinator, and now employs about 280 people across San Francisco and remote locations.

_Usage:_

"How is CaptivateIQ different from using a spreadsheet to manage commissions?"

"CaptivateIQ replaces manual spreadsheets with a dedicated platform that lets users create, modify and track commission plans in one place, integrating with partners like Carta and Intercom to automate calculations and improve transparency."
