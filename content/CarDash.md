**Website:** https://www.repairsmith.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/cardash

CarDash is a managed marketplace that picks up a vehicle from a customer’s home or workplace, oversees the repair process, and returns the car. It offers upfront, transparent pricing and acts as an advocate so customers are never overcharged.

The company is positioned on the cusp of a transportation revolution driven by connected, self‑driving, electric vehicles and new ownership models. Regardless of who owns or powers a car, all vehicles will require service, and CarDash aims to become the industry’s service platform of tomorrow.

CarDash was acquired.
