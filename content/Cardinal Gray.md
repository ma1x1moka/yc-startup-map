**Website:** https://www.cardinalgray.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/cardinal-gray

Cardinal Gray helps auto lenders automate the process of filing liens with Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) offices. The company addresses the complexity of this task, which varies significantly by state and county, by leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs) to handle the interactions.

Based in New York City, the six-person team focuses on enterprise software for the credit and lending industry. It has processed lien filings for over $1 billion in loans, streamlining a traditionally manual and fragmented administrative burden for financial institutions.

_Usage:_

"Why use an LLM for DMV filings instead of just hardcoding the rules for each state?"

"The filing process varies by state and county, making a static rule-based system difficult to maintain and scale. By using LLMs, Cardinal Gray can handle the nuances and variations in DMV requirements more flexibly, allowing lenders to automate lien filings across different jurisdictions without needing to manually update code for every local change."
