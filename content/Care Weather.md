**Website:** https://www.careweather.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/care-weather

Care Weather operates a unique flat-panel radar satellite designed to capture the highest-accuracy global weather data. The system is vertically integrated, which makes it 1000 times more cost-effective than traditional alternatives, while also generating more solar power and sailing on top of the atmosphere for a closer view of the Earth's surface. This technology enables governments and shipping companies to better adapt to extreme weather events.

The company built and launched the smallest radar satellite ever in just four months. Based in Orem, Utah, Care Weather has secured $35 million worth of letters of intent from clients including the Air Force and various cargo shippers. The startup is backed by investors such as Boost VC, Kickstart Seed Fund, and Y Combinator.

_Usage:_

"How does Care Weather's flat-panel design achieve such significant cost savings compared to standard radar satellites?"

"The satellite is vertically integrated, which reduces manufacturing complexity and costs. Additionally, the flat-panel configuration allows the craft to collect more solar power and operate at a higher altitude, providing a closer and more direct view of the surface without the expense of traditional bulky satellite structures."
