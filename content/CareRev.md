**Website:** https://www.carerev.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/carerev

CareRev operates as a technology platform that connects healthcare facilities directly with local clinical professionals. By removing traditional staffing agencies from the equation, the company enables healthcare workers to choose where and when they work while giving facilities access to a resilient, flexible local workforce.

Founded in the Summer 2016 YC batch, CareRev has grown to a team of 275 employees operating primarily remotely. The company was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does CareRev differ from traditional healthcare staffing agencies?"

"CareRev acts as a direct marketplace between facilities and clinicians, effectively cutting out the middleman. Instead of relying on third-party agencies to source talent, it provides a direct line for healthcare professionals to find shifts that fit their schedules, which allows facilities to access local clinical talent more efficiently."
