**Website:** https://www.joincarma.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/carma

Carma is building an AI-native platform that transforms fleet operations from tedious, manual work into effortless, automated workflows. The company powers some of the largest U.S. fleets, including Fortune 500 clients, and claims to save operations teams thousands of hours by eliminating busywork and keeping vehicles on the road. Backed by Soma Capital, Y Combinator, the founders of Superhuman and other Tier-1 investors, Carma raised a $5.5 M seed round and is planning a Series A in 2026 while remaining the fastest-growing firm in fleet-management software.
Its engineering team of 15 in San Francisco builds features that go live in real time, giving enterprise customers immediate feedback and rapid iteration on the field.

_Usage:_

"How is Carma’s AI platform different from traditional fleet‑management software?"

"Carma’s platform replaces manual, busy‑work‑heavy processes with AI‑driven automated workflows, so operations teams can avoid hours of repetitive tasks and keep vehicles on the road, which the company says saves thousands of hours for large fleets including Fortune 500 customers."
