Cashfree is a licensed payment aggregator in India that helps over 50,000 businesses collect and send money. Headquartered in Bengaluru with a team of 200, the company provides a suite of financial tools including an easy-to-integrate payment gateway with instant settlements, an API banking platform for bulk disbursals, and split payment solutions for marketplaces.

The platform also offers specialized infrastructure such as a dedicated UPI stack, a Bank Account Verification API, and Auto Collect, a virtual account solution that matches inbound payments to customers. Cashfree works with all leading banks to build the core payments and banking infrastructure that powers its products.

Notable customers include major Indian consumer and tech brands like Xiaomi, Nykaa, Zomato, Swiggy, and Shell, as well as donation platforms like Ketto and Milaap. The company is also integrated with major global and regional platforms such as Shopify, Wix, PayPal, Amazon Pay, Ola Money, and Google Pay.

_Usage:_

"How does Cashfree handle the complexity of Indian payment rails compared to standard global gateways?"

"Cashfree acts as a licensed payment aggregator that abstracts the complexity of working with multiple Indian banks by providing a unified API for gateways, payouts, and UPI. It handles the plumbing for instant settlements and refunds, and includes specific utilities like Bank Account Verification and Auto Collect to match virtual account payments to specific customers, which is critical for high-volume Indian e-commerce and marketplace operations."
