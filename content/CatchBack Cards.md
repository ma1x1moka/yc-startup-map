CatchBack Cards is a mobile app and website platform for maximizing the thrill of collecting Pokemon and Sports cards. CatchBack Cards allows collectors to create custom mystery packs with personalized chases and customized odds with cryptographically trusted tooling. Users can rip open digital packs and get physical cards shipped to them, receive buyback offers on cards pulled from their mystery packs sent straight to Venmo and PayPal, and buy/sell cards in its $1 marketplace.
