**Website:** https://centralhq.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/central

Central provides end‑to‑end back‑office services for startups, handling payroll, benefits, state compliance, accounting and taxes. It uses a combination of AI agents and human experts to perform the work, delivering about ten hours of saved time each month. Thousands of founders trust the platform, which operates from San Francisco with a team of fifteen.

Central was acquired, reflecting its notable impact in the HR tech space.

_Usage:_

"How is Central different from a typical HR or payroll software platform?"

"Typical HR software gives companies tools to manage payroll and benefits themselves, while Central actually executes those tasks for them using AI agents and human experts, returning completed payroll, benefits enrollment and compliance work rather than just a dashboard."
