Chaos Genius operates as a DataOps observability platform specifically designed for Snowflake environments. It enables organizations to monitor their data infrastructure, reduce Snowflake costs, and optimize query performance.

The company was acquired, marking its exit from the active startup landscape. It operates with a small team of approximately ten people, distributed across Bengaluru, India, San Francisco, USA, and remote locations.

Tagged with Cloud Workload Protection, Machine Learning, and Data Engineering, the platform serves the B2B analytics sector by providing visibility into cloud data workloads.

_Usage:_

"How does Chaos Genius help manage Snowflake spend?"

"It acts as an observability layer for Snowflake, allowing you to monitor usage patterns to identify inefficiencies. By providing visibility into query performance and resource consumption, it helps teams pinpoint areas where costs can be reduced and performance can be optimized within the Snowflake ecosystem."
