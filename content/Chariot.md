Chariot provides end-to-end financial infrastructure for the donor-advised fund (DAF) market, which represents $326 billion in assets and is the fastest-growing segment of philanthropy. The platform simplifies the process for grantmakers to send payments and for nonprofits to receive, reconcile, and enrich their donation data.

The company powers DAFpay, described as the industry’s first integrated DAF payment option and a TIME’s Best Invention of 2025. This payment method is embedded on over 100,000 donation forms from organizations such as the American Cancer Society, Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center, and the University of Pennsylvania, as well as on major fundraising platforms like GoFundMe and GiveButter.

Based in New York City with a team of 24, Chariot is an active YC Summer 2022 company that has already processed hundreds of millions of dollars through its network.

_Usage:_

"How does Chariot's DAFpay differ from standard credit card processing for nonprofits?"

"DAFpay is an integrated payment option specifically for donor-advised funds, allowing donors to give directly from their DAF accounts rather than using personal credit cards. It is embedded on over 100,000 donation forms and major platforms like GoFundMe, serving as a primary payment method that simplifies reconciliation for nonprofits compared to traditional gift processing."
