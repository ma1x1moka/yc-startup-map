**Website:** https://www.charityvest.org | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/charityvest

Charityvest operates as a modern donor-advised fund (DAF) designed for both individual and corporate donors. The platform allows users to contribute various asset classes, including cash, stock, crypto, and equity, to their charitable accounts. These balances can then be invested in low-cost ETFs to achieve tax-free growth.

The service streamlines the granting process by enabling users to send funds to over 1.4 million charities through a simple interface. It also consolidates tax reporting by providing a single tax receipt for all charitable giving activities. Based in Atlanta, Georgia, the company was part of the YC Summer 2020 batch with a team of nine people.

Charityvest has since been acquired, marking the conclusion of its independent operation as a YC-backed startup.

_Usage:_

"How does Charityvest handle the tax implications of different asset types compared to traditional giving methods?"

"It allows you to contribute cash, stock, crypto, and equity directly to your donor-advised fund, which lets you take a tax deduction on the contribution while the assets grow tax-free in low-cost ETFs. Instead of managing separate receipts for each donation, you get one consolidated tax receipt for all your giving, simplifying the accounting for both individuals and corporations."
