Checkars is an e‑commerce platform that lets buyers purchase certified used cars across Latin America, streamlining the buying and financing process and building trust through certification.

In its first 18 months, Checkars completed more than 1,000 transactions and achieved a net promoter score above 85, indicating strong customer satisfaction. The company combines a fintech layer for financing with a marketplace model that connects sellers and buyers.

Headquartered in Buenos Aires, Argentina, Checkars grew to a 45‑person team before being acquired. The acquisition underscores its traction and the value of its trusted used‑car marketplace.
