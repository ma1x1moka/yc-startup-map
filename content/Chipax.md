**Website:** https://www.chipax.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/chipax

Chipax operates as a financial operations platform for small and medium businesses, primarily in Mexico and Chile. The company handles payments, invoicing, and reconciliations to deliver real-time visibility into accounts receivable and accounts payable. It serves over 1,100 paying businesses and has reached $1.6M in annual recurring revenue.

Founded by engineers with firsthand experience in SMB financial management, Chipax addresses the complexity of traditional accounting tools. The team is based in Santiago, Chile, and operates remotely, with a current size of 37 employees. As an active YC Summer 2021 company, it focuses on simplifying financial workflows for the Latin American market.

_Usage:_

"How does Chipax differ from traditional accounting software like QuickBooks for SMBs in Latam?"

"While traditional tools often require manual reconciliation and batch processing, Chipax automates the entire cycle of payments, invoices, and reconciliations. This provides real-time AR and AP data rather than static reports, which is particularly useful for businesses in Mexico and Chile that need immediate visibility into their cash flow without manual data entry."
