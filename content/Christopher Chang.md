Co-founded [Lume](./Lume.md) in 2019, a banking app for nurses offering earned wage access, student loan refinancing, and travel nurse jobs. Chang holds a Computer Science degree from Columbia University. After Lume was acquired by Vivian Health in 2022, he joined Vivian Health with co-founder Shantanu Joshi to lead product and engineering for new financial products for healthcare workers.
