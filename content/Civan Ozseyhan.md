Co-founded [refine](./refine.md) as CEO in 2022 with [Omer Aplak](./Omer%20Aplak.md) and [Eren Erkalkan](./Eren%20Erkalkan.md), part of YC's Summer 2023 batch, building an open-source, headless React framework for rapidly building enterprise web applications. Ozseyhan is a serial entrepreneur with 15-plus years of experience building enterprise software, and previously co-founded Fixie Digital GmbH from 2016 to 2022.

_Usage:_

"What does refine offer developers?"

"An open-source, headless React framework that eliminates repetitive enterprise app work — CRUD, state management, authentication, access control, and i18n — while giving developers full control of their stack."
