**Website:** http://askclara.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/clara-2

Clara operates as an AI primary care and longevity practice, founded by the team that previously built Circle Medical to $100M in revenue. The company aims to extend human healthspan by deploying an AI system with deep clinical knowledge that analyzes medical data to detect subtle trends and patterns.

Unlike general-purpose chatbots, Clara integrates directly with patient medical records to perform actions such as managing medication refills, ordering labs, and interpreting symptoms. While the AI handles initial interactions and recommendations, a human medical provider reviews and approves all clinical decisions before they are finalized. The nine-person team is based in San Francisco.

_Usage:_

"How does Clara differ from using a general LLM like ChatGPT for medical questions?"

"General LLMs can only provide text-based advice based on your description, whereas Clara has direct access to your actual medical data. This allows it to detect longitudinal trends and execute specific actions like ordering labs or processing refills, though a human doctor still reviews the final recommendations."
