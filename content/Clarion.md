**Website:** https://clarionhealth.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/clarion

Clarion provides an AI communication layer for healthcare, deploying agents to manage the high volume of calls and messages that clinics receive daily. By automating complex workflows end-to-end, the company addresses the issue of clinics missing 30-40% of inbound calls due to staffing shortages, allowing staff to focus on direct patient care.

Based in New York City, the 16-person team serves virtual care companies, health systems, and a $5B health insurance company, handling tens of thousands of patients monthly. Clarion raised $5.4M from Accel, Y Combinator, and Sequoia (scout), with additional support from healthcare founders.

_Usage:_

"How does Clarion handle the complexity of medical workflows compared to standard chatbots?"

"Clarion uses AI agents to automate complex workflows end-to-end, specifically targeting the 30-40% of inbound calls that clinics typically miss due to staffing shortages. Unlike simple chat tools, it manages tasks like scheduling, billing, and prescription refills, allowing providers to reclaim time for patient care while ensuring patients get faster resolutions."
