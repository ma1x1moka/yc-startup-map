Clear Genetics develops patient‑centric software that integrates genetics into routine patient care. Its HIPAA‑compliant platform provides an automated, comprehensive solution for delivering genetic services, allowing health systems, clinics, and commercial labs to scale genomics across the nation. The company, based in San Francisco with a team of eight, was acquired after serving leading health organizations in the healthcare and consumer health and wellness sectors.

_Usage:_

"How does Clear Genetics’ platform differ from a typical genetic testing lab workflow?"

"Clear Genetics replaces the manual, fragmented process with an automated, HIPAA‑compliant software platform that integrates genetic testing directly into patient care workflows, letting health systems and labs deliver results at scale without building separate infrastructure."
