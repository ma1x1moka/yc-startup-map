**Website:** https://clearbrain.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/clearbrain

ClearBrain (YC W18) builds the first self‑serve AI platform for growth marketing. It automatically predicts and retargets users when they are most likely to purchase, letting Fortune 1000 companies personalize experiences for hundreds of millions of predictions, comparable to the scale of Uber or Google.

The platform is powered by engineers from Google Ads and Optimizely’s data infrastructure teams and is backed by investors in Dropbox, Optimizely, and AdMob. With a ten‑person team in San Francisco, ClearBrain was acquired after demonstrating high‑availability, ease‑of‑use, and responsive automated machine‑learning capabilities for growth teams.

_Usage:_

"How does ClearBrain’s automated machine‑learning platform differ from building a custom predictive model in‑house?"

"ClearBrain offers a self‑serve AI service that automatically generates and serves predictions for user purchase intent, handling hundreds of millions of predictions without requiring a team to develop, train, and maintain a custom model. This lets growth teams focus on retargeting and personalization rather than the underlying ML engineering."
