---
description: Discarding the context and starting fresh. The cleanest reset available, and it loses everything not written down.
---

Clearing returns the window to its opening state — [system prompt](./System%20prompt.md) and nothing else. It is the only true fix for a degraded [session](./Session.md), because it removes the accumulated material rather than compressing it.

It is also unforgiving. Every decision reached in conversation, every constraint discovered along the way and every dead end already ruled out disappears unless it exists on disk. Clearing without writing a [handoff artifact](./Handoff%20artifact.md) first means rediscovering all of it.

_Usage:_

"Just clear it, it's gone sideways."

"Write the decisions out first, then clear."
