**Website:** https://clew.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/clew

Clew provides a unified file system for the cloud, designed to help employees view and organize files scattered across various work applications from a single interface. By consolidating access to digital assets, the platform aims to streamline how teams manage their data infrastructure.

The company was based in Toronto, Canada, and operated as a small team of two people. It focused on the B2B infrastructure space, offering a SaaS solution to address the fragmentation of file storage in modern workplaces.

Clew has since been acquired, marking the end of its independent operations.

_Usage:_

"How does Clew handle files that are stored in different SaaS tools like Slack or Google Drive?"

"Clew acts as a unified layer that aggregates these files, allowing employees to see and organize them from one central place rather than switching between individual applications to find specific documents."
