Clicks Health builds AI agents that automate back-office operations for RCM companies and healthcare providers. Agents work across EHRs, payer portals, desktop applications, and legacy systems — following existing processes, handling exceptions, and escalating when human judgment is needed.

SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliant. Helps teams improve accuracy, shorten turnaround times, and reduce operating costs. Team of 8. San Francisco.

_Usage:_

"How do Clicks Health's AI agents handle exceptions in legacy healthcare software?"

"Rather than failing on non-standard inputs, the agents operate directly across EHRs, payer portals, desktop applications, and legacy systems by following existing workflows. They handle process exceptions autonomously and only escalate tasks to staff when human judgment is required."
