**Website:** https://clipdrop.co | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/clipdrop

ClipDrop is a mobile and desktop application that utilizes computer vision to transform standard smartphone photos into high-quality product visuals. The platform targets the millions of independent sellers on marketplaces like Poshmark, Etsy, and eBay who lack access to professional photography or design teams.

Founded by a team of former Google and Facebook employees and machine learning PhDs, the company aims to democratize professional-grade design for small businesses. ClipDrop was acquired, marking the end of its independent operations.

_Usage:_

"How does ClipDrop handle the background removal for product shots compared to manual editing?"

"It uses machine learning to automatically replace backgrounds and enhance images, allowing users to generate professional-looking visuals directly from their mobile phones without needing a designer or professional photographer."
