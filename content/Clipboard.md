Clipboard operates an app-based marketplace that connects healthcare professionals with facilities in need of coverage. Workplaces post open shift calls on the platform, allowing workers to browse and accept shifts directly to secure flexible work across the United States.

The platform is a leader in long-term care staffing and is expanding into home health and hospital settings. Founded in 2016, Clipboard has grown into a remote-first organization with a team of over 1,000 employees, helping hundreds of thousands of workers fill millions of shifts annually.

_Usage:_

"How does Clipboard handle shift matching between workers and healthcare facilities?"

"Facilities post open shift calls directly onto Clipboard's app-based marketplace, where healthcare workers can view and accept available shifts. The platform currently supports long-term care facilities and is expanding into home health and hospital environments."
