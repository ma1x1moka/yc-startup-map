**Website:** https://cloudthread.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/cloudthread

Cloudthread is a San Francisco-based analytics platform designed to help engineering teams build cost efficiently in the cloud. The company focuses on eliminating cloud waste by providing tools to attribute and track savings, ultimately reducing cloud bills and preventing costly re-architectures.

The platform was developed by the team behind Lyft's internal cloud cost management system, leveraging that deep expertise to serve external organizations. By engaging engineers directly, Cloudthread aims to save hours of engineering time through better cost visibility and management.

Cloudthread has since been acquired, marking its exit from the YC Summer 2021 batch.

_Usage:_

"How does Cloudthread differ from standard cloud billing dashboards?"

"Unlike standard billing dashboards that just show total spend, Cloudthread focuses on attribution and tracking specific savings. It is built on the internal system used by Lyft, so it is designed to engage engineers directly in the cost management process rather than just reporting numbers to finance, helping teams prevent expensive re-architectures by identifying waste early."
