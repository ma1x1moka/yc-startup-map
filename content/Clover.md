**Website:** https://cloverapp.co | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/clover

Clover is a productivity tool designed specifically for creatives, offering a new style of text editor built from the ground up to support creative thinking. Instead of forcing users into traditional linear documents, the platform allows them to explode content onto an infinite surface. This visual approach supports note-taking, brainstorming, mind-mapping, and task management in a more intuitive way.

The company, which operated with a small remote team based in New York, was acquired. This acquisition highlights the market interest in novel interfaces for knowledge work that move beyond standard word processing constraints.

_Usage:_

"How does Clover's infinite surface differ from standard linear documents or whiteboard tools?"

"Clover takes a hybrid approach by allowing you to take the text from a traditional document and expand it into a visual, infinite canvas. This lets you brainstorm and mind-map directly within the writing context rather than switching between a text editor and a separate whiteboard tool, making it easier to manage tasks and ideas visually without losing the structure of your writing."
