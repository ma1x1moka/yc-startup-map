**Website:** https://cocrafter.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/cocrafter

CoCrafter is a construction marketplace that matches contractors and subcontractors. It is already used by over 3000 companies in the German SMB sector, helping contractors find and manage both local and foreign subcontractors. The Munich‑based team of nine focuses on streamlining subcontractor relationships for real‑estate and construction firms.

_Usage:_

"How is CoCrafter different from a simple contractor directory?"

"CoCrafter not only lists subcontractors but also provides a matching and management platform, allowing contractors to discover and coordinate both local and foreign partners, which goes beyond the static listings typical of traditional directories."
