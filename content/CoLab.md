CoLab accelerates innovation by enabling engineering teams to deliver better products faster. Their Design Engagement System (DES) turns design conversations into actionable reviews, helping teams make smarter engineering decisions.

In 2025 CoLab launched AutoReview, an AI peer checker that automatically marks up 2D and 3D files. The platform is trusted by Fortune 500 companies such as Ford, Komatsu, Johnson Controls, GE Appliances and Lockheed Martin, and it operates from St. John's, NL, Canada with a team of 150.
