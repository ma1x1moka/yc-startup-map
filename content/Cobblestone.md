**Website:** https://getcobblestone.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/cobblestone

Cobblestone builds software designed to help institutional landlords reduce delinquency, beginning with tools that target fraud reduction. The platform automates processes that traditionally required manual oversight, aiming to lower missed payments and improve cash flow for real‑estate owners.

The company was acquired, reflecting its value in the real‑estate and fintech space. With a small team of three based in New York City, Cobblestone emerged from Y Combinator’s Winter 2023 batch and serves the real‑estate and construction industries.

_Usage:_

"How does Cobblestone’s automation differ from standard property‑management tools?"

"Cobblestone focuses specifically on delinquency and fraud reduction for institutional landlords, automating the detection and handling of risky payments rather than offering a broad property‑management suite."
