**Website:** http://cocoon.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/cocoon

Cocoon provides a private space where a user’s most important groups can gather, offering the tools needed to keep each other close.

It also helps groups continue writing an ongoing story together, reinforcing collaboration over time.

Cocoon was acquired after its Winter 2019 batch, reflecting its impact in the consumer social collaboration space.

_Usage:_

"What makes Cocoon different from typical group chat or collaboration apps?"

"Cocoon focuses on a private gathering space that not only lets groups chat but also provides built‑in tools for co‑authoring a continuous shared story, aiming to keep the group’s narrative together rather than just exchanging messages."
