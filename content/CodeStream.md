**Website:** http://codestream.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/codestream

CodeStream offers IDE plugins that simplify code reviews by allowing developers to request feedback on a snapshot of their repository with a single command. The platform can capture uncommitted and unpushed code, enabling engineers to verify work‑in‑progress before committing. It also supports the traditional workflow of committing and pushing at the end of a sprint.

CodeStream was acquired, highlighting its relevance in the B2B engineering and product design space. The company operates with a team of about ten people across New York and remote locations, delivering a SaaS collaboration tool for developers.

_Usage:_

"How does CodeStream let me get review feedback on code that isn’t yet committed compared to using a standard pull‑request process?"

"CodeStream’s IDE plugin adds a command that captures the current state of your repo—including uncommitted and unpushed changes—and sends that snapshot for review, so you can get feedback without creating a commit or push. Traditional pull‑request workflows require you to commit and push changes before reviewers can see them."
