Cohere provides an AI-driven platform that automates customer support, handling 40‑70% of requests without human intervention. The system works across all support channels and can achieve these results within just a few days of deployment. Cohere was acquired, reflecting the market’s interest in intelligent automation for B2B operations. The company operates out of New York City with a team of seven engineers focused on AI and automation.
