College Pulse is the leading research and analytics company focused on college students. It has built the largest database of college student opinion, drawing on more than 700 000 panelists from 1 500 universities across all 50 states. The company uses its panel to help clients better understand the college demographic, offering granular insights for B2B analytics. With a team of nine based in Frisco, CO, San Francisco, CA, and remote, College Pulse aims to become a unique part of the global internet infrastructure, positioning itself as the 'Pulse' for public opinion, analogous to Google for search or Amazon for retail.

_Usage:_

"How does College Pulse's panel differ from other student survey platforms?"

"College Pulse’s panel includes over 700 000 students from 1 500 universities nationwide, giving it the largest and most geographically diverse dataset for college opinion research, which allows clients to segment by institution, region, and demographic."
