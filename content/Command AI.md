**Website:** https://command.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/command-ai

Command AI provides a user assistance platform designed to make software interfaces more intuitive for visitors and customers. The company offers two primary tools: an AI Agent that answers questions, performs actions, and co-browses with users to teach them the interface, and a Nudge Platform that delivers personalized hints and messages based on in-product behavior rather than static rules.

The Nudge Platform is positioned as a replacement for legacy Digital Adoption Platforms, using data on user behavior and 'rage close' metrics to ensure guidance is helpful rather than annoying. By translating user assistance into concrete outcomes like improved engagement, feature adoption, and retention, the platform also helps decrease low-value support tickets.

Based in San Francisco, the company was acquired after serving a team of 35 employees. Its clients include HubSpot, HashiCorp, Yotpo, Thryvv, and Freshworks.

_Usage:_

"How does Command AI's Nudge Platform differ from traditional Digital Adoption Platforms (DAPs)?"

"Traditional DAPs often rely on static rules to display tooltips or guides, which can feel generic or annoying. Command AI's Nudge Platform uses AI to deliver personalized hints based on real-time in-product behavior. It also actively measures the extent to which users 'rage close' nudges, allowing the system to adjust its delivery to avoid pestering users, effectively replacing the rigid rule-based approach of legacy tools."
