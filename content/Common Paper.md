**Website:** https://commonpaper.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/common-paper

Common Paper is a contracting platform that helps startups close deals with customers faster and with less risk. It creates standard, open‑source contracts for commercial transactions—including NDAs, DPAs, and sales agreements—analogous to the SAFE but for vendor‑customer relationships. The standardized format structures contract data from inception, and the company builds software for managing these contracts, accelerating sales cycles, and making contract data programmatically accessible to other business systems. Tens of thousands of users have downloaded the contracts, and 63 % of the contracts on its system close within 24 hours. The company, part of the Winter 2023 batch, has a team of eight and operates from Philadelphia and remotely.

_Usage:_

"How does Common Paper’s open‑source contract format make a sales deal close faster than a custom‑drafted agreement?"

"Because the contracts are standardized and structured from the start, the platform can automatically fill, track, and exchange the necessary terms, letting both parties sign and execute the agreement without the back‑and‑forth of drafting a custom document, which is why 63 % of its contracts close within 24 hours."
