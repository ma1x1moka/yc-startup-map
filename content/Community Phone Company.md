**Website:** https://www.communityphone.org/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/community-phone-company

Community Phone creates a dedicated phone device that works out of the box with no setup required, aimed at seniors living at home. Its AI‑powered check‑in calls surface how seniors are really doing, and a companion family app replaces daily anxiety with safety and connection. With 55 million Americans over 65 living at home, the company addresses a large unmet need and is already generating eight‑figure annual recurring revenue, backed by Y Combinator, YC Continuity, and SNR.vc.

The product combines telecommunications hardware with SaaS‑based AI and a B2B offering for families, positioning it as a niche consumer‑focused solution in the aging‑care market.

_Usage:_

"How does Community Phone’s AI check‑in differ from a regular phone call to a senior?"

"The AI check‑in automatically calls the senior’s phone, records the conversation, and uses artificial‑intelligence analysis to surface the senior’s true condition, whereas a regular call relies on the caller’s subjective interpretation and requires manual follow‑up."
