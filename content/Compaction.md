---
description: The harness summarising earlier context to free space, replacing original material with a condensed version.
---

When the window approaches its limit, the [harness](./Harness.md) can compress history — typically by having a [model](./Model.md) summarise earlier [turns](./Turn.md) and substituting that summary for the originals. The [session](./Session.md) continues with room to work.

What it buys in space it spends in fidelity, and the loss is unpredictable. Specific constraints, exact file contents and the precise wording of a decision are exactly the details that summarisation smooths away, so behaviour can change subtly right after a compaction. It also rewrites the prefix, which invalidates the cache from that point on.

_Usage:_

"It's been odd since about an hour ago."

"It compacted around then. The constraint we set probably didn't survive."
