ComplyDo is a flexible enterprise compliance engine that extracts requirements from any file, maps them to controls and policies, shows gaps, monitors changes, and advises on maturity and next steps.
Designed to make GRC teams ten times more efficient, it is already trusted by global leaders and the largest EU enterprises.
Based in Berlin with a team of ten, ComplyDo serves B2B and legal sectors, leveraging AI to streamline compliance workflows.
