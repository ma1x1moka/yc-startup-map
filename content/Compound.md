**Website:** https://withcompound.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/compound

Compound offers a wealth management platform designed for employees of technology companies. It empowers users to make better financial decisions, giving them the freedom to focus on what matters most.

Based in San Francisco, the fintech grew to a team of 51 and was acquired after its Summer 2019 Y Combinator batch, highlighting its relevance in consumer finance.

_Usage:_

"How is Compound different from a generic robo‑advisor?"

"Compound targets people who work at tech companies, tailoring its financial advice to the compensation structures and career paths common in the tech industry, whereas generic robo‑advisors serve a broader audience without that specific focus."
