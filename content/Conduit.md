**Website:** http://helloconduit.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/conduit

Conduit builds software that systemizes the planning and execution of work on loading docks and in the yard, giving operators real‑time visibility, accountability, and automation across every site they run. It serves more than 100 customers, reports 172% net revenue retention and zero churn, and operates with a team of fewer than 20 people in San Francisco and Seattle. Conduit is backed by Innovation Endeavors and Y Combinator and emphasizes rapid development, direct customer access, minimal internal process, and full use of AI tools to enhance its logistics platform.

_Usage:_

"Is Conduit just another warehouse management system, or does it provide something different?"

"Conduit is positioned as an operating system for shipping and receiving rather than a traditional WMS. It systemizes dock and yard workflows, delivers real‑time visibility and automation, and incorporates AI tools, all built with direct customer feedback, which distinguishes it from typical inventory‑focused solutions."
