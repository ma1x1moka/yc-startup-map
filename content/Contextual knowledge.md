---
description: What the model knows because it is in the context right now. Precise, current, verifiable — and it costs tokens.
---

This is the counterweight to [parametric knowledge](./Parametric%20knowledge.md). Anything in the window is exact rather than remembered, and dated rather than undated, which is why pasting the actual file beats asking the [model](./Model.md) what it thinks the file contains.

The trade is that it consumes budget and competes for attention. Contextual knowledge is strong but not absolute — material buried under a mountain of other content can still lose out to a confident parametric memory. Relevance and position matter as much as presence.

_Usage:_

"I gave it the config and it still guessed."

"Where in the context? It's forty thousand tokens back under the test output."
