**Website:** https://contrario.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/contrario

Contrario is an AI recruiting and hiring platform that pairs expert recruiters with vertical AI agents to build generational teams. It has placed over 150 candidates and generated more than $6M in annualized revenue within six months, making it one of the fastest-growing AI recruiting platforms. The platform is used by 200+ fast-growing companies such as Wispr Flow, Listen Labs, and Slash, and is backed by Y Combinator, Nexus Venture Partners, Goodwater Capital, and Inventum Ventures. With a team of 20 headquartered in San Francisco, Contrario focuses on B2B recruiting and talent acquisition. Contrario’s approach blends human expertise with purpose-built AI to speed hiring and improve quality, positioning it as a leading solution in the recruiting industry.
