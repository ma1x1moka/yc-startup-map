Convex builds software aimed at the commercial services industry, delivering a SaaS platform that supports B2B sales and proptech workflows for enterprise customers. The company is based in San Francisco and employs about 110 people. Convex was acquired, reflecting its relevance in the SaaS and enterprise space.
