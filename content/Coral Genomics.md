Coral Genomics creates new clinical laboratory tests that predict optimal treatments for autoimmune diseases by integrating molecular, functional, and clinical factors. The company has identified a unique subpopulation of patients who do not respond to current therapies and is working to address this unmet medical need. Coral Genomics was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Coral Genomics' test differ from standard autoimmune diagnostics?"

"Unlike standard diagnostics that measure only biomarkers, Coral Genomics' test integrates molecular, functional, and clinical data to predict the best treatment for each patient."
