**Website:** https://coris.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/coris

Coris is an AI platform designed for payments risk and operations, serving companies like GoFundMe, Kajabi, Clio, and Cherry. The system automates critical workflows including merchant onboarding, monitoring, and general payment operations. By leveraging AI agents, Coris delivers capabilities that risk teams previously considered aspirational.

Based in San Francisco, the company operates as a B2B infrastructure provider with a team of 10. It was part of Y Combinator's Summer 2022 batch and has secured backing from Lux Capital and Pathlight Ventures. Coris focuses on applying artificial intelligence to streamline compliance and risk management within the fintech sector.

_Usage:_

"How does Coris handle merchant onboarding compared to manual risk review processes?"

"Coris uses AI agents to automate the entire merchant onboarding and monitoring lifecycle, which allows risk teams to scale operations without linearly increasing headcount. Instead of relying on manual checks for every new merchant, the platform processes these tasks automatically, enabling companies to handle higher volumes of payment operations with greater consistency."
