**Website:** http://coursedog.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/coursedog

Coursedog offers an academic operating system that integrates core administrative functions such as scheduling and enrollment for colleges and universities. It streamlines academic operations into a single SaaS solution.

The company raised a $100 million investment from JMI and grew to a team of about 100 employees located in New York City and remotely. Coursedog was acquired, reflecting its traction in the higher‑education software market.

_Usage:_

"How is Coursedog different from a traditional student information system?"

"Coursedog is marketed as an academic operating system that combines multiple operational tools—scheduling, enrollment, and other admin workflows—into one integrated SaaS platform, rather than offering separate, siloed modules typical of many legacy student information systems."
