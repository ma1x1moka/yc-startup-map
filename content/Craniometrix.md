Craniometrix is a New York‑based digital health company that leverages AI to provide a care navigation service for dementia patients. Launched in July 2025, the service offers non‑clinical coaching and support designed to reduce hospitalizations.

The company reports that appropriate coaching can avoid about 30% of hospitalizations, and Medicare has begun reimbursing for this type of support. By July 2025 the business reached $8 million in annual recurring revenue and has raised over $21 million total, including a $15 million Series A round.

With a team of 64, Craniometrix aims to alleviate provider burden while helping families keep loved ones at home.
