**Website:** https://cron.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/cron

Cron is a next‑generation calendar app for professionals and teams designed to streamline workflows and embed collaboration into the core experience. It integrates with other productivity tools, aiming to help users spend time wisely.

The company was founded in Winter 2020, has a team of three, and is based in San Francisco with remote employees. Cron was acquired by Notion, marking its notable growth in the B2B productivity space.

_Usage:_

"How does Cron differ from traditional calendar apps like Google Calendar?"

"Cron embeds collaboration and integrates other productivity tools directly into the calendar, whereas Google Calendar is primarily a scheduling tool without built‑in collaboration features."
