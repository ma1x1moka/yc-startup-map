Crosslayer Labs detects impersonation attacks on websites and APIs. Crosslayer Labs provides "outside-in" monitoring of customer infrastructure, comprehensively discovering and monitoring all Internet dependencies, including DNS, BGP, TLS certs, Javascript. It correlates signals from different layers of the Internet stack to identify attack patterns. Its alerts are actionable and contain root cause analysis/remediation recommendations. Its founding team is world renowned for previously inventing the MPIC standard adopted by all the major CAs including Google, Apple, Amazon. The MPIC standard is securing over 700 million websites via the issuance of over 3 billion digital certificates, and is now a global internet standard that protects every single HTTPS connection.
