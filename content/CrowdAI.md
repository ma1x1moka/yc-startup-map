**Website:** https://crowdai.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/crowdai

CrowdAI provides a no-code platform that enables enterprises to leverage deep learning for real-time visual decision-making. By treating data as the new code, the company offers infrastructure for the entire AI lifecycle, allowing users to systematically label data, train models efficiently, and scale them iteratively.

The platform is designed to be accessible to a wide range of technical abilities, from business operators to data scientists. CrowdAI was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does CrowdAI differ from standard ML frameworks for computer vision tasks?"

"CrowdAI focuses on a no-code interface that covers the full AI lifecycle, including data labeling, model training, and deployment, rather than just providing a coding framework. This allows non-technical business operators to build and manage vision AI models without writing code, whereas standard frameworks typically require significant engineering effort to set up the data pipeline and training infrastructure."
