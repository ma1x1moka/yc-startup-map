**Website:** https://crowdforce.io/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/crowdforce

CrowdForce is an app that enables any local merchant in Africa, beginning with Nigeria, to become a bank branch within minutes. The service turns merchants into banking points, expanding financial access through a mobile platform.

The company was acquired after its Winter 2020 batch, highlighting its impact in the fintech and banking exchange space. With a team of 41 based in Abuja, Nigeria, CrowdForce demonstrated rapid growth in the African financial ecosystem.

_Usage:_

"How can a merchant become a bank branch in minutes using CrowdForce?"

"CrowdForce provides a mobile app that lets merchants sign up and instantly access banking functionality, effectively turning their storefront into a bank branch without the traditional setup time."
