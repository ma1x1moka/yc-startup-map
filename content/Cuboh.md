Cuboh builds software that helps restaurants manage third‑party delivery online orders. The company is a B2B SaaS business based in Victoria, BC, Canada, with a team of about 50 employees.

Cuboh focuses on the food tech space, streamlining order flow for restaurants and integrating with multiple delivery platforms. The company was acquired, marking its success in the competitive restaurant technology market.

_Usage:_

"How does Cuboh help restaurants manage online orders?"

"Cuboh provides a software platform that consolidates orders from multiple third‑party delivery services into a single dashboard, allowing restaurants to track and fulfill orders efficiently."
