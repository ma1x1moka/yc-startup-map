**Website:** https://www.cuckoo.so/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/cuckoo-labs

Cuckoo Labs offers a real-time AI translation platform that lets sales, marketing, and support teams converse across more than 20 languages. Companies such as Snowflake and PagerDuty embed Cuckoo in Zoom and in‑person meetings to translate even highly technical discussions.

The system instantly learns the technical details of a conversation from keywords and supplied documents, then tailors translations to the specific industry and organization. It is designed for B2B use and is operated out of Seoul, South Korea by a two‑person team.

_Usage:_

"How does Cuckoo handle technical jargon compared to generic translation tools?"

"Cuckoo extracts keywords and relevant documents from the conversation, learns the technical context on the fly, and then produces translations that are customized for the specific industry, which generic tools don’t do."
