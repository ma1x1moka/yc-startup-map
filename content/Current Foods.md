**Website:** http://currentfoods.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/current-foods

Current Foods develops plant-based and biotech seafood products, including raw tuna and smoked salmon, designed to match the taste and health profile of traditional seafood while offering better price and consistency. The San Francisco-based company, which had a team of 18, focused on industrial bio and healthcare applications to address climate concerns through sustainable food production.

The company was acquired, marking a significant milestone in the alternative protein sector.

"How does Current Foods approach the production of seafood compared to traditional fishing?"
