D-ID is a Tel Aviv-based startup established in 2017 that specializes in patented video reenactment technology using AI and deep learning. The company enables creators and developers to generate realistic, high-quality AI personas through its platform and APIs, aiming to facilitate what it terms Creative Reality.

Its product suite ranges from animating still photos to facilitating high-quality video productions and creating viral user experiences. Notably, D-ID created the first facial image de-identification solution designed to protect images and videos from facial recognition software, reflecting its focus on ethical AI usage alongside creative tools.

With a team of 27, the company operates in the B2B and security sectors, leveraging generative AI to serve both entertainment and privacy-focused applications.

"How does D-ID's video reenactment differ from standard face-swapping tools, and what is the de-identification feature for?"
