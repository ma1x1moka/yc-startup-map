---
description: Developer experience: how easy a codebase and its toolchain make it for people to do good work — docs, feedback speed, error quality.
---

DX is measured in friction. How long from a change to knowing whether it worked, how clear the failure messages are, how much undocumented knowledge you need before you can contribute safely.

It has always mattered for hiring and morale, and it now has a second payoff: most of what makes a codebase pleasant for humans makes it tractable for [agents](./Agent.md) too. The overlap is large but not total, which is why [AX](./AX.md) is worth naming separately.

_Usage:_

"Setup took me two days."

"That's a DX problem, and it's costing the agents too."
