**Website:** https://dashdive.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/dashdive

Dashdive provides observability‑based tooling that attributes AI/LLM and cloud usage costs to individual customers, teams, and products at the endpoint invocation level. By breaking down expenses in this granular way, software companies can optimize pricing and assess the profitability of each product offering. Dashdive was acquired after its Winter 2023 batch, reflecting its value in the B2B SaaS and FinOps space. The company operates out of San Francisco with a two‑person team.

_Usage:_

"How is Dashdive different from standard cloud cost‑management platforms?"

"Dashdive focuses specifically on AI/LLM and cloud costs at the endpoint invocation level, tying spend directly to customers, teams, and products, whereas typical cloud cost tools aggregate usage more broadly without that granular AI‑specific attribution."
