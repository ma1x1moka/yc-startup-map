**Website:** http://www.dashworks.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/dashworks

Dashworks serves as a centralized hub where work begins, bringing together documents, messages, tickets, and other knowledge scattered across various company applications and people. By consolidating this disparate information, the platform helps teams get on the same page and execute tasks more effectively.

The San Francisco-based startup operated as a B2B productivity tool with AI capabilities. It was acquired, marking the conclusion of its independent operation.

_Usage:_

"How does Dashworks handle data that is currently siloed in different SaaS tools?"

"It acts as an intelligent start page that seamlessly aggregates documents, messages, tickets, and other knowledge from across your company's various apps and people into a single interface, allowing teams to access all relevant context in one place rather than switching between multiple platforms."
