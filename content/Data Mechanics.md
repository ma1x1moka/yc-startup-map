**Website:** https://www.datamechanics.co | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/data-mechanics

Data Mechanics built a managed Spark-on-Kubernetes platform that simplifies large-scale data processing. It was acquired by NetApp in 2021 and became part of Spot.io's product portfolio, where the platform is now sold as Ocean for Apache Spark.
The company focused on B2B infrastructure solutions, operating from Paris, France, with a team of 25 engineers. Its SaaS offering leveraged open-source technologies to deliver scalable data engineering capabilities to enterprise customers.

_Usage:_

"How does Data Mechanics' Ocean for Apache Spark differ from a standard Spark on Kubernetes deployment?"

"Ocean for Apache Spark is a managed service that runs on Kubernetes and is integrated into Spot.io's portfolio. It provides a ready-to-use, scalable Spark environment without the need for customers to set up and maintain the underlying infrastructure."
