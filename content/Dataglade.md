**Website:** https://dataglade.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/dataglade

Dataglade provides AI‑generated analyses and financial models for public companies, drawing on a proprietary financial data warehouse that backs every number with citations. The service aims to eliminate the manual effort of digging through financial filings, saving investors hours each day. It informs over $1 billion of trades daily and serves customers who trust its reports for comprehensive research needs.

The company operates as a fintech SaaS platform out of New York City with a two‑person team. Since its Winter 2023 Y Combinator batch, Dataglade has positioned itself as a smart, AI‑driven alternative for stock research.

_Usage:_

"How does Dataglade’s AI‑generated research compare to using a traditional financial data platform?"

"Dataglade’s AI automatically writes analyses and builds financial models for any public company, and each figure is linked to citations from its own data warehouse, so users don’t have to manually extract numbers from filings. This contrasts with traditional platforms where analysts must pull data and craft reports themselves, which can be time‑consuming."
