Dataland is an applied AI lab that focuses on complex operations and customer support, creating AI agents that streamline support workflows. In just eight months, the company reached multi‑million dollar annual recurring revenue and remains highly profitable, growing from a small founding team to a four‑person crew in New York City. It partners with some of the fastest‑growing startups and public companies worldwide, cementing its role in the B2B operations space.
