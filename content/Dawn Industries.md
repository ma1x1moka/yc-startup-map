Dawn Industries develops VIM, a system that connects to entire industrial automation cells including PLCs, robots, CNC machines, and sensors. When a fault occurs, the platform identifies the cause and stages the fix, allowing operators to approve the solution with a single click. It also monitors part drift, detecting deviations seconds after a cut and recommending immediate corrections within the machine.

The company is notable for significantly reducing recovery times in large factories, dropping them from approximately 30 minutes to under two minutes. This efficiency gain saves more than $15,000 per fault. Dawn Industries is a two-person team based in San Francisco, operating in the Industrials, Manufacturing, and Robotics sectors.

_Usage:_

"How does VIM handle faults compared to traditional manual troubleshooting?"

"VIM connects directly to the cell's PLCs, robots, and sensors to automatically find the root cause of a fault and stage the fix. Instead of an operator spending 30 minutes diagnosing the issue, they simply review the proposed solution and approve it with one click, reducing recovery time to under two minutes."
