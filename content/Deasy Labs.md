**Website:** https://www.deasylabs.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/deasy-labs

Deasy Labs provides metadata orchestration specifically designed for AI workflows, enabling teams to create and embed customized, high-quality metadata into systems like RAG and agentic frameworks. The company focuses on removing the need for domain experts to generate this metadata, aiming to improve the accuracy and speed of generative AI applications in production environments.

Founded by a team previously involved in building ML data governance tools at McKinsey, Deasy Labs emerged from the observation that high-quality metadata is a critical factor for successful GenAI deployment. The startup was backed by General Catalyst, Y Combinator, and RTP Global, and operated out of New York City with a small team of eight people.

Deasy Labs was acquired by Collibra, a global leader in enterprise data governance, in July 2025.

_Usage:_

"How does Deasy Labs handle metadata generation for RAG pipelines compared to manual curation?"

"Deasy Labs automates the creation of high-quality, customized metadata without relying on domain experts, which is a key differentiator for teams needing to scale GenAI applications to production. By embedding this metadata directly into workflows like RAG and agentic frameworks, it aims to boost both the accuracy and speed of the AI outputs."
