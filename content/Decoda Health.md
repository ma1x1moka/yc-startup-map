**Website:** https://decodahealth.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/decoda-health

Decoda Health provides a unified electronic medical record and practice management system designed specifically for med spas, wellness clinics, and aesthetics practices. The platform consolidates fragmented tools for scheduling, charting, payments, and patient communication into a single interface, addressing the inefficiencies and errors associated with managing multiple disconnected systems.

The company embeds artificial intelligence throughout its core workflows, including an AI front desk that handles calls and bookings around the clock, an ambient scribe for real-time clinical note generation, and automated patient follow-ups to maintain calendar utilization. Decoda is based in Toronto, Canada, and supports a team of 15 people.

The platform is currently live in over 150 clinics and processes more than $100 million in annual transactions, serving as a comprehensive operational hub for elective care providers.

_Usage:_

"How does Decoda handle the administrative burden that usually requires separate software for scheduling and billing?"

"Decoda replaces the typical stack of five to ten disconnected tools by integrating scheduling, payments, memberships, inventory, and analytics into one platform. It also automates key administrative tasks using AI, such as answering phone calls and booking patients 24/7, which reduces the need for manual front-desk management and helps keep the clinic's calendar full."
