**Website:** https://www.joindeed.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/deed

Deed provides a platform that simplifies the process for employees to donate to charities and volunteer with causes, aiming to enhance employee retention and happiness. The company focuses on delivering a user experience comparable to popular consumer applications, ensuring that workplace giving is intuitive and engaging for staff members.

Notable users of the platform include Airbnb, Stripe, DoorDash, Box, and Lob. Deed experienced significant growth after its public launch in January, increasing its customer base by fourfold. The company operated with a team of 40 people based in New York City.

Deed was acquired, marking the end of its independent operations as a YC-backed startup.

_Usage:_

"How does Deed differentiate itself from traditional corporate giving programs?"

"Deed focuses on matching the user experience of consumer apps to make workplace giving more engaging for employees. By prioritizing ease of use and a polished interface, it aims to increase participation and employee satisfaction, which companies like Airbnb and Stripe have leveraged to improve retention."
