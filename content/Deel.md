Deel is an all‑in‑one payroll and HR platform built for global teams. It combines HRIS, payroll, compliance, benefits, performance, and equipment management into a single seamless service. The platform also includes AI‑powered tools and a fully owned payroll infrastructure, supporting every worker type in more than 120 countries.

Deel helps businesses scale smarter, faster, and more compliantly by handling the complexities of international employment. With a team of about 5,000 and operations based in San Francisco and remote locations, it serves B2B customers in finance and accounting sectors.

_Usage:_

"How does Deel’s AI‑powered payroll differ from using a standard payroll provider for a multinational workforce?"

"Deel uses AI‑powered tools together with its own payroll infrastructure to automate compliance, benefits, and equipment management across 120+ countries, so companies can manage all worker types from a single platform rather than integrating multiple providers."
