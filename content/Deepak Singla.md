Co-founded [Fini](./Fini.md) with [Hakim K](./Hakim%20K.md), part of YC's Winter 2022 batch, leading product strategy for AI agents that autonomously resolve up to 80% of enterprise customer support tickets. Singla holds a degree in Mechanical Engineering with a minor in Business Management from IIT Delhi. At Uber, he built a cross-sell strategy on the Rides platform that generated over $100 million in revenue, and he and Hakim led product and growth teams there at the scale of 200 million-plus support tickets a year before founding Fini.
