---
description: The shared understanding of what is being built, held in common by user and agent, separate from any artifact.
---

Before there is a [spec](./Spec.md) or code there is an idea of the thing: what it does, who it serves, which constraints are real and which are assumed. When this is genuinely shared, the [agent](./Agent.md)'s proposals land in the right space and the work moves quickly.

When it is not shared, everything downstream is wrong in ways that look like small misunderstandings and are not. Most disappointing agent output traces back to a design concept that only one party actually held.

_Usage:_

"It built the wrong thing, competently."

"We never had a shared design concept — it filled in the gaps itself."
