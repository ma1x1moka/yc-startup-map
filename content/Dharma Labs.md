**Website:** https://dharma.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/dharma-labs

Dharma Labs, a San Francisco-based fintech company, built the only Ethereum wallet capable of seamlessly moving money between any US bank account and decentralized exchanges like Uniswap. The product was designed to look and feel like a mainstream exchange but with the power of DeFi, allowing users to invest in a vast universe of tokens and yield opportunities with a simple tap.

The company operated with a team of 14 people and focused on simplifying the complex process of interacting with decentralized finance. By connecting traditional banking infrastructure directly to the Ethereum network, Dharma removed significant friction for users wanting to access crypto assets without navigating multiple wallets or bridges.

Dharma Labs was acquired, marking the end of its independent operations as a YC-backed startup.

_Usage:_

"How did Dharma handle the transfer of funds from a traditional bank to a decentralized exchange like Uniswap?"

"Dharma acted as a bridge, allowing users to plug in their US bank account and move money directly to decentralized exchanges. This eliminated the need for users to manually manage gas fees or switch between multiple wallets, creating a seamless experience that felt like using a centralized exchange but with access to DeFi liquidity."
