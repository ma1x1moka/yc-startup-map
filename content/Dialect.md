Dialect is on a mission to organize the internet’s experiences and make them distributable and actionable everywhere. It uses blockchain links, called blinks, a new technology that lets businesses unbundle their product experiences from their apps and sites.

The blinks enable users to take instant, no‑redirect actions wherever they are. This technology is unique to blockchain today and is positioned to redefine how we interact with the internet as the world moves on‑chain.

Dialect was acquired after its Summer 2020 batch. The B2B crypto/Web3 startup is based in New York City and employs eight people.

_Usage:_

"How do Dialect’s blinks differ from traditional deep links?"

"Dialect’s blinks are blockchain‑based links that let a business separate its product experience from any specific app or website, so a user can act instantly without a redirect. Traditional deep links rely on the target app being installed and typically route through the app, whereas blinks work on‑chain and avoid those redirects."
