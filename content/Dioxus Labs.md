**Website:** https://dioxuslabs.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/dioxus-labs

Dioxus makes it easy for developers to quickly build, test, deploy, and manage apps on every platform. They support building and deploying apps for the web, desktop, mobile, AR/VR, and many other exotic targets.

The company is a small team of four based in San Francisco and focuses on B2B engineering, product and design. Dioxus was acquired, marking its success.

_Usage:_

"How does Dioxus compare to React Native for mobile app development?"

"Dioxus uses a single Rust codebase to build and deploy apps across web, desktop, mobile, and AR/VR, offering a cross‑platform solution similar to React Native but with the performance and safety benefits of Rust."
