**Website:** https://doomersion.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/doomersion

Doomersion lets you learn languages by doomscrolling. Watch TikToks in your target language matched exactly to your current level — as you scroll, they get slightly harder. Gen-Z doomscrolls 2.5–3 hours a day; if you bake immersion into that with viral videos in another language matched to their level, you get the optimal language learning method.

Gained ~15,000 downloads in the first two weeks. Power users spend 3+ hours daily on the app.

_Usage:_

"What makes this different from a standard short-form video feed?"

"Standard feeds aren't calibrated to your vocabulary. Doomersion serves viral videos matched precisely to your current language proficiency, gradually increasing the difficulty as you scroll to turn existing 2.5–3 hour daily doomscrolling habits into language immersion."
