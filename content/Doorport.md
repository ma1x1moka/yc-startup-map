**Website:** https://www.doorport.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/doorport

Doorport builds technology that upgrades existing apartment building access control systems into smart, networked solutions, enabling remote management and integration with smart home ecosystems. The company’s platform focuses on the real estate and construction sectors, bringing IoT‑enabled access to multi‑unit housing.

Doorport was acquired by Alarm.com, underscoring its relevance in the smart‑home and IoT market. It operates out of San Francisco with a three‑person team.

_Usage:_

"How is Doorport different from installing a regular smart lock on each apartment door?"

"Doorport doesn’t replace the whole lock; it adds a smart layer to the building’s existing access hardware, allowing centralized control and integration with Alarm.com’s smart‑home platform, which is something a standalone consumer smart lock can’t provide."
