**Website:** https://doppler.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/doppler

Doppler is a developer‑focused secrets management service that acts as a single source of truth for configuration across every language, stack, and infrastructure. It automates security, eliminates configuration drift, and helps teams deliver reliable software quickly. The company, founded in the Winter 2019 batch, has a team of 25 engineers based in Austin, Texas and remote locations, serving B2B customers in engineering, product, and design.

Doppler’s platform is positioned as a DevSecOps tool, simplifying secret handling from local development through production, which reduces the risk of breaches and keeps development teams moving.

_Usage:_

"How does Doppler prevent configuration drift compared to manually managing environment variables?"

"Doppler centralizes all secrets in a single source of truth that syncs across local development and production environments, so changes are propagated automatically and inconsistencies are eliminated."
