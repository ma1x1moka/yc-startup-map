Downtobid, founded in Summer 2019, uses AI to parse construction plans, automatically identify scopes of work, and connect general contractors with qualified local subcontractors. Its platform sends personalized bid invites, streamlining the procurement process for construction projects.

The company was acquired. It has a team of five and is based in Los Angeles, CA, with remote employees.
