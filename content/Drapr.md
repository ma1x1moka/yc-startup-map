Drapr is a San Francisco and Berkeley-based startup that allows shoppers to virtually try on clothing online. The company provides this technology to apparel brands, helping them increase their online revenue.

Founded in the Summer 2020 batch, Drapr operated as a small team of seven people focused on consumer retail tech. It was ultimately acquired, marking the conclusion of its independent operations.

_Usage:_

"How does Drapr help apparel brands perform better online?"

"Drapr integrates with e-commerce platforms to let customers visualize how clothes will look on them before buying. By reducing the uncertainty of online shopping, it helps brands convert more traffic into sales."
