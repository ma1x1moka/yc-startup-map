DreamCraft Entertainment, Inc. offers a code‑free game creation platform that bundles high‑quality art assets, allowing creators to build, publish, and monetize games without writing code. Founded in 2018, the company has a global team of more than twenty people, split between remote and Silicon Valley offices. DreamCraft is backed by prominent investors including March Capital, Makers Fund, Tencent Corporation, and Y Combinator, underscoring its position in the consumer gaming space.
