Dropprint Genomics writes software that decodes the activity of individual cells instead of averaging signals across populations. By focusing on single-cell resolution, the company can pinpoint rare cell states that are invisible to bulk assays. The platform was built by a team that was the first to process and analyze millions of immune cells, and it now hosts a database of over one million immune cells spanning healthy, cancerous, and autoimmune states. Dropprint Genomics cut the cost of single-cell sequencing tenfold and built scalable computational infrastructure to handle the data deluge. Its machine‑learning software turns the growing database into a data‑centric engine for target discovery, delivering more precise, unbiased, and automated insights for drug discovery. Dropprint Genomics was acquired.
