**Website:** https://dyte.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/dyte

Dyte offers a real-time audio and video SDK that enables developers to integrate live video calls using fewer than ten lines of code. The product is positioned as a B2B infrastructure solution for telecommunications and API-driven video services. It supports rapid deployment, allowing teams to add video functionality without building a custom stack.

The company, founded in YC’s Winter 2021 batch, grew to a team of 45 engineers across Bengaluru and remote locations. Dyte was acquired, reflecting its relevance in the video communications market.

_Usage:_

"Can I really add a live video call feature with under ten lines of code using Dyte’s SDK?"

"Yes. Dyte’s Video SDK is built for quick integration, so a developer can embed a full‑featured real‑time audio and video call with fewer than ten lines of code, handling signaling, media streams, and UI components out of the box."
