**Website:** https://easyplan.in/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/easyplan

Easyplan is an AI-driven investment platform designed for beginner investors in India, functioning similarly to US-based services like Betterment and Qapital. The company launched with money market funds and utilized conversational AI to help users build diversified mutual fund portfolios tailored to their specific goals. It also offers a credit card secured by user investments, allowing customers to maintain liquidity while earning returns.

The service targets the large segment of middle-income Indian workers who save significantly but lack access to simplified investment tools, as existing apps are often too complex for beginners. Easyplan achieved approximately 40,000 customers with a cost of acquisition that was 10% of the industry average, growing its assets under management threefold since April 2020.

Despite its early traction and focus on a $325B potential market, Easyplan is now inactive, indicating the company has since shut down.

_Usage:_

"How does Easyplan simplify investing for beginners compared to traditional Indian brokerage apps?"

"Instead of presenting users with over 16,000 mutual funds and 5,000 stocks, Easyplan uses conversational AI to generate diversified portfolios based on user goals. It also offers a credit card secured by these investments, which allows users to spend against their portfolio value without selling assets, effectively combining investment returns with liquidity."
