**Website:** http://www.eclipsefoods.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/eclipse-foods

Eclipse Foods has built the world’s first plant‑based dairy platform that uses micelles – the microscopic structures found in milk – to produce products that taste, feel, and perform like traditional dairy. The company says its approach lets it transform the roughly $500 billion dairy industry with sustainable, healthy, and humane alternatives.

The San Francisco‑based startup has raised over $60 million from investors including Y Combinator, the chairman of Beyond Meat, the founder of Reddit, the creator of Gmail, Forerunner Ventures, Sozo Ventures, and Initialized Capital. Its mission is to create a more sustainable food system by offering plant‑based foods that require no sacrifice.

_Usage:_

"How does Eclipse Foods get plant‑based milk to taste and behave like real dairy without using any animal ingredients?"

"Eclipse Foods leverages micelles, the tiny structures that give milk its characteristic properties, in a proprietary plant‑based platform. By replicating these micelles with plant ingredients, the company can produce products that match the taste, texture, and functionality of conventional dairy."
