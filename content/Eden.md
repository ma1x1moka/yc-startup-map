Eden offers a cloud‑native Diagnostic OS that combines artificial intelligence with medical imaging to provide accurate and timely diagnostics. The platform is used by more than 2,600 medical institutions across 18 countries, reaching millions of patients each year. By focusing on AI‑driven tools, Eden aims to raise the level of care that hospitals can deliver.

The company, based in Mexico City with a team of 55 employees, positions itself as a leading digital‑health solution in the healthcare IT space. Its focus on a unified Diagnostic OS differentiates it from fragmented imaging workflows, helping institutions scale high‑quality diagnostics.

_Usage:_

"How does Eden’s Diagnostic OS differ from traditional on‑premise imaging systems?"

"Eden runs entirely in the cloud and embeds AI models that analyze images in real time, whereas traditional systems require on‑site hardware and manual interpretation. This cloud‑native, AI‑first approach lets hospitals across many countries deploy the same tools without large upfront infrastructure, delivering faster and more consistent diagnostic results."
