EdgeBit provides a platform that automates dependency update analysis, turning weeks of manual review into minutes. By doing so, engineering teams can ship security updates up to ten times faster while maintaining productivity.

The platform helps teams find and fix security vulnerabilities, keeping the software supply chain secure and up‑to‑date. EdgeBit was acquired by FOSSA in July 2025.
