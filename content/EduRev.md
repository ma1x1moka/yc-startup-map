**Website:** http://edurev.in | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/edurev

EduRev is a streaming platform for educational content, akin to Netflix but for learning, with a social networking layer that lets students share and collaborate. It targets Indian students, aiming to make formal schooling unnecessary.

The platform has grown to over 7 million users, adding about ten new users per minute. It has attracted investment from Y Combinator, Artiman Ventures, and angel investors such as Anand Chandrashekaran, Austen Allred, and Martin Basiri.

EduRev has earned recognition, winning Google’s Best App award for learning, the mBillionth award from Bill and Melinda Gates, and Startup of the Year from TieCon. With a team of 24 based in Chandigarh, the company continues to expand its educational ecosystem.
