**Website:** https://www.eduflow.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/eduflow

Eduflow provides a platform designed to host active online learning experiences. Rather than relying on passive video playlists, the software enables instructors to build interactive learning activities that make students active participants in their own education process.

Based in Copenhagen, Denmark, Eduflow was used across thousands of institutions worldwide, spanning from middle school classrooms to corporate training programs at companies like Google. The company participated in the Y Combinator Summer 2017 batch with a team of 12 and has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Eduflow differentiate its platform from traditional video-based learning systems?"

"Eduflow avoids static video playlists in favor of active learning. Its platform provides interactive learning activities designed to engage students directly, making it adaptable for environments ranging from middle school education to corporate training at Google."
