Founded [finbar](./finbar.md), an AI investment analyst automating financial modelling, research, and data analysis for hedge funds, later joined by co-founder [Robin Gan](./Robin%20Gan.md). Huang holds a CFA charter and an Oxford degree, and worked as a hedge fund analyst and earlier at Goldman Sachs — founding finbar in 2023 to eliminate the "grunt work" of his own former role, writing the MVP himself up to first revenue.
