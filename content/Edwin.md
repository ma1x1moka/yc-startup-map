**Website:** https://edwin.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/edwin

Edwin combines adaptive learning and natural language understanding technology with pedagogical content and on‑demand human instructors to offer affordable, personalized 1:1 English tutoring. Based in San Francisco, the startup was backed by General Catalyst, Y Combinator, the Google Assistant Investments Program and other investors, and has helped over 800 000 students improve their English.

In early 2020 Edwin merged with MyBuddy.ai, and the combined entity kept the Buddy.ai name. The new company focuses on a voice‑based English conversation partner for the roughly 500 million children worldwide who need practice speaking the language, extending the mobile Buddy app that blends solutions from both original firms.

Edwin’s status is listed as Acquired, reflecting its consolidation into Buddy.ai.
