**Website:** https://tryegress.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/egress-health

Egress Health builds AI agents that automate revenue cycle management for dental offices, handling eligibility checks, claims scrubbing, payment posting, and denial management. By automating these workflows, its customers typically eliminate one to two full‑time employees and see a 5‑15% increase in monthly revenue. The company was acquired, reflecting the market’s interest in AI‑driven healthcare finance solutions.

_Usage:_

"How does Egress Health’s AI‑driven RCM differ from traditional revenue cycle software?"

"Egress Health replaces manual steps with AI agents that automatically run eligibility checks, clean claims, post payments, and manage denials, which lets dental offices cut one to two staff positions and boost revenue by up to 15% per month, whereas conventional software usually requires more human oversight to perform those tasks."
