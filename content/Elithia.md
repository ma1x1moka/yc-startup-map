US workers' compensation paid out $64.1 billion in 2023. Personal injury, malpractice, and other insurance claims add billions more. Behind every one of these claims sit thousands of pages of medical records, and a clinician spends hours or days reading them to reconstruct what happened to the patient.

Elithia is building agents to do this work.

Elithia's agents reconstruct patient histories, reason across conflicting evidence, and produce defensible analyses, with every finding cited to the original record.
