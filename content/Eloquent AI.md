Eloquent AI automates complex, regulated operations in financial services without requiring APIs or engineering. The platform learns by observing existing workflows and SOPs, then securely controls browser and desktop applications to automate up to 96% of tasks. In just four weeks of deployment, customers achieved $500K ARR, demonstrating rapid adoption and high automation potential.

Based in San Francisco, the five‑person team focuses on delivering enterprise‑grade AI solutions for B2B financial services. With its AI Operator, Eloquent AI provides a plug‑and‑play automation layer that reduces manual effort and mitigates compliance risk for regulated financial institutions.
