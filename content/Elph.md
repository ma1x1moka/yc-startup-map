**Website:** https://www.elph.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/elph

Elph builds digital payments infrastructure aimed at B2B engineering, product, and design teams. The San Francisco‑based startup operated with a five‑person team and focused on streamlining payment workflows.

The company’s technology was deemed valuable enough to be acquired by Brex, highlighting its relevance in the payments infrastructure space.

_Usage:_

"What makes Elph’s payments infrastructure worth acquiring by a larger fintech like Brex?"

"Elph offered a ready‑to‑use digital payments platform built by a small, focused team, which allowed Brex to integrate proven B2B payment capabilities without developing the stack from scratch."
