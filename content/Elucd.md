Elucd is a GovTech SaaS platform that uses mobile ad polling to give cities near real-time insight into citizen sentiment about their government.

Founded in the Summer 2017 batch, the company operated out of New York City with a team of eight.

Elucd was acquired, marking its success in the GovTech space.

_Usage:_

"How does Elucd collect citizen sentiment data?"

"Elucd polls citizens through mobile ads, gathering responses in near real-time."
