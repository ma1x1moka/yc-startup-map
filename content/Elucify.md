**Website:** https://getelucify.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/elucify

Elucify developed an API designed to plug directly into CRM systems to automatically clean contact data. Based in San Francisco, the company built software infrastructure to ensure sales organizations had accurate underlying data, allowing sales teams to focus on selling.

Part of the Y Combinator Winter 2016 batch, the B2B SaaS startup grew to a team of 11. Elucify was acquired in 2018 by Circleback.

_Usage:_

"How does Elucify fit into an existing sales stack?"

"Elucify functions as an API that plugs straight into a CRM platform. It handles data cleaning automatically inside the database so salespeople do not have to manually fix or update records."
