Embark Trucks builds self-driving semi truck software, based in San Francisco, with a team of around 300 engineers. It pioneered autonomous coast-to-coast drives, logged 100,000 miles on public roads, and opened a network of transfer points, establishing it as a leader in autonomous trucking. The company went public on NASDAQ in 2021 and was acquired by Applied Intuition in 2023, cementing its position in the automotive and industrial sectors.
