**Website:** https://embrace.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/embrace

Embrace delivers a user-focused observability platform built on OpenTelemetry, giving SRE and DevOps teams insight into what matters most—user experience—by tying performance data to backend observability insights. The solution illuminates real customer impact rather than just server impact, enabling teams to deliver the best app experiences. Customers such as The New York Times, Marriott, Warby Parker, Masterclass, Home Depot, and Cameo rely on Embrace to make complex, voluminous data actionable.

Based in Los Angeles with a remote workforce, Embrace employs 62 people and operates as a SaaS, open-source, data engineering company in the B2B, engineering, product, and design industries.
