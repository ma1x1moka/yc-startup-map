**Website:** https://www.emburse.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/emburse

Emburse is a San Francisco-based fintech platform offering expense management and accounts payable automation. The system gives businesses greater visibility and control over corporate spending by enabling them to deploy customizable payment cards.

Businesses can issue virtual or physical MasterCard-network branded expense cards directly to employees or contractors. This setup allows companies to authorize only pre-approved business expenses. The company participated in the Winter 2016 Y Combinator batch and has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Emburse enforce corporate spending controls prior to purchase?"

"Emburse allows companies to issue physical or virtual MasterCard-network cards directly to employees and contractors. Authorization is tied to the card itself, ensuring transactions are limited to pre-approved business expenses rather than relying entirely on post-purchase reconciliation."
