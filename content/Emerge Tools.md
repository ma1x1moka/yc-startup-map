**Website:** https://www.emergetools.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/emerge-tools

Emerge Tools provides a suite of developer tools aimed at enhancing mobile applications and supporting the engineering teams behind them. The company operates as a B2B service with a focus on engineering and product design, serving major mobile platforms.

Notable users of the platform include prominent tech companies such as DoorDash, Square, Airbnb, Duolingo, and Stripe. Emerge Tools was acquired, marking the conclusion of its independent operations.

_Usage:_

"What specific problem does Emerge Tools solve for mobile development teams?"

"Emerge Tools offers a suite of developer tools specifically designed to supercharge mobile apps and the teams building them. It is used by large-scale mobile organizations like DoorDash and Airbnb to improve their development workflows."
