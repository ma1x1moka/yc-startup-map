**Website:** https://www.emobi.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/emobi

Emobi provides a single point of integration for electric vehicle charging, combining legal, financial, and technical infrastructure to power apps, fleets, and automakers. Its core technology, JustPlug, enables fully automated and secure charging that works instantly with existing hardware, requiring no special software or setup.

The company has integrated over 122,000 EV chargers across the U.S. and Canada, positioning it as the largest EV charging ecosystem in North America. Emobi serves more than 15 enterprise customers and is trusted by the U.S. Department of Energy and the Department of Transportation.

Headquartered in San Francisco, the nine-person team holds two provisional patents and focuses on maximizing charger utilization through its API-driven platform.

_Usage:_

"How does Emobi's JustPlug technology handle the complexity of connecting to different charging networks without requiring new hardware?"

"JustPlug acts as a unified layer that abstracts the legal, financial, and technical differences between various EV charging networks. Instead of requiring drivers or fleet managers to manage multiple accounts or install specific software, it allows for instant, secure, and automated charging sessions using existing infrastructure, effectively standardizing the experience across the 122,000+ chargers in its ecosystem."
