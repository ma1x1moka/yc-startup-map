**Website:** https://www.enableus.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/enable-us

Enable Us provides a centralized workspace for B2B sales, marketing, and customer-facing teams to deliver personalized and interactive buying and onboarding experiences. The platform allows teams to capture various assets, including video demos, sales decks, contracts, and onboarding materials, within a single location.

It automatically organizes and contextualizes this content based on factors such as industry, persona, deal stage, and deal size. By leveraging AI-targeted content and simplified workflows, the tool enables the creation of personalized deal rooms that facilitate one-to-one communication with prospects and other stakeholders.

Enable Us was acquired by MindTickle, marking the company's exit from the YC Winter 2021 batch.

_Usage:_

"How does Enable Us handle content organization for different deal stages?"

"It automatically organizes and contextualizes captured assets like demos and decks by industry, persona, deal stage, and deal size, allowing sales teams to build personalized deal rooms with AI-targeted content rather than manually sorting files for each prospect."
