**Website:** https://encord.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/encord

Encord serves as the data infrastructure for physical AI, enabling teams to transform messy, multimodal datasets into production-ready systems. It supports a wide range of applications, including humanoid robots, autonomous vehicles, and smart infrastructure.

The platform focuses on curating, managing, and aligning the specific data required for model training. Over 300 teams, including Toyota, Skydio, and Maxar, rely on Encord to handle their data workflows.

Headquartered in London with offices in San Francisco and New York, the company has raised $110M and employs a team of 150 people.

_Usage:_

"How does Encord differ from general-purpose data labeling tools when working with physical AI projects?"

"Encord is specifically designed as the data layer for physical AI, meaning it handles the curation and alignment of multimodal data needed for complex systems like humanoid robots and autonomous vehicles. Unlike general tools, it is built to manage the messy, high-dimensional data these specific use cases require, which is why teams like Toyota and Skydio use it to feed their models."
