End Close builds automatic reconciliation for payments companies: fintechs, marketplaces, banks, and payroll companies. Reconciliation tracks every dollar and resolves every exception — ensuring no money loss, audit-ready financials, and compliance.

The CEO spent 6 years building the reconciliation org at Modern Treasury (YC S18), processing a trillion dollars in payments across 40 banks with 99.995% automation. End Close provides that system as a product, managed by AI agents. Exception handling — where 99% of manual work happens — is the core focus.
