Engage Bio has created Tethosomes, a new class of non‑viral DNA that enables potent, durable, redosable, and scalable genetic medicines. The technology unlocks genetic therapies without using viral vectors, offering a reusable platform for drug discovery.

Based in San Francisco with a team of about ten people, Engage Bio operates in the healthcare and therapeutics sectors. In 2026 the company was acquired by Eli Lilly, highlighting the strategic value of its synthetic biology approach.

_Usage:_

"How does Engage Bio’s Tethosome platform differ from traditional viral‑vector gene therapies?"

"Tethosomes are non‑viral DNA constructs, so they avoid the safety and manufacturing constraints of viral vectors while still delivering potent and durable genetic medicine that can be reused across multiple treatments."
