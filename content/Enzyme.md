**Website:** https://www.enzyme.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/enzyme

Enzyme develops online quality management system (QMS) software and provides supportive quality assurance and regulatory affairs consulting for life science companies. The platform and service combination helps organizations navigate FDA approval processes and maintain ongoing compliance standards.

To support its software platform, Enzyme relies on a team with over 50 years of combined experience in the medical device industry, spanning both Fortune 500 corporations and startups.

Part of the Y Combinator Summer 2017 batch, the company operated from San Francisco, California, alongside a remote team, and has since been acquired.
