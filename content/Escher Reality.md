**Website:** http://www.escherreality.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/escher-reality

Escher Reality builds backend infrastructure and developer APIs designed specifically for augmented reality applications. Operating out of San Francisco and Sunnyvale, California, the company focuses on developer tooling tailored for AR and gaming experiences.

The company participated in the Summer 2017 Y Combinator batch with a team of two. Escher Reality has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"What core service does Escher Reality offer to augmented reality developers?"

"Escher Reality provides a dedicated backend API that handles the infrastructure required to run augmented reality features, saving developers from building custom spatial and AR backend services from scratch."
