**Website:** https://eternal.gg/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/zelos

Eternal built a platform for gamers and viewers to capture, edit, and retain ownership of highlight clips from live streams. By focusing on the consumer and gaming entertainment space, it offered tools for users to keep and monetize their favorite moments. The company, based in Los Angeles with a team of 15, was acquired after its Winter 2020 batch.

Its service stood out in the crowded video media market by emphasizing ownership of streamed content rather than just sharing, positioning it as a notable player in the live entertainment ecosystem.

_Usage:_

"How is Eternal different from the standard clip features on platforms like Twitch?"

"Eternal let users actually own the captured moments from a stream, giving them control and potential reuse, whereas typical clip tools on other platforms only let you share a short video without any ownership rights."
