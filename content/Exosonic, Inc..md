**Website:** https://exosonic.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/exosonic-inc

Exosonic, an early-stage aerospace startup based in Los Angeles, developed supersonic adversary air UAVs to support adversary air training missions for commercial and government customers. The company aimed to mature these unmanned vehicles into loyal wingmen or collaborative combat aircraft, with the long-term goal of applying lessons learned to develop quiet supersonic airliners.

Founded in 2019, the team of 14-15 FTEs raised over $4.5M in private capital and $2M in AFWERX funding. They were developing a subscale flight test vehicle designed to demonstrate autonomy and crewed-uncrewed teaming, with a first flight targeted for Q2 2023. Exosonic has since become inactive.

_Usage:_

"How does Exosonic's approach to supersonic flight differ from traditional jet development?
"

"Instead of jumping straight to passenger aircraft, Exosonic focused on building supersonic UAVs for military training first. This strategy allowed them to generate revenue and refine autonomous flight and teaming technologies before attempting the more complex engineering challenges of quiet supersonic airliners."
