EzDubs is a real-time speech translator that uses proprietary models to process audio without waiting for complete sentences. It distinguishes itself by cloning voices on the fly without requiring user enrollments and replicating emotional tone in the target language. The company was acquired, marking a significant milestone for its technology.

The product is available as an iOS and Android app, enabling users to translate live phone calls, dub voice messages, and auto-translate text conversations. With a team of six based in San Francisco, EzDubs serves thousands of users who use the platform to communicate with friends, family, and offshore teams across language barriers. The company also offers integration options for embedding its live multilingual conversation capabilities into existing communication stacks.

_Usage:_

"How does EzDubs handle voice identity and latency compared to standard translation tools?"

"Unlike standard tools that often require voice enrollment or wait for full sentence completion, EzDubs uses proprietary models to clone voices on the fly and translate in real-time. It also attempts to preserve the speaker's emotional tone in the translated output, which is useful for maintaining natural flow in live phone calls or voice messages."
