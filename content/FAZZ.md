**Website:** https://fazzfinancial.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/fazz

FAZZ operates as a B2B neobank providing an all-in-one finance platform for businesses in Indonesia and Singapore. The company serves underbanked segments such as warungs and startups while building a super-app for mid-market and enterprise clients. Its core features include payment acceptance via e-wallets, bank transfers, and credit cards, alongside a business cash account that earns 4% interest.

The platform also offers transfer and pay capabilities through payment APIs, corporate cards, and invoice processing, as well as lending services with instant settlement and invoice financing. FAZZ integrates value-add SaaS tools for inventory and expense management to support comprehensive business operations.

With a team of 500 based in Jakarta, FAZZ processes over $6 billion in annualized gross transaction volume and has achieved $25 million in annualized revenue. The company has raised over $74 million from investors including Tiger Global, DST, Y Combinator, B Capital Group, and Insignia Ventures.

_Usage:_

"How does FAZZ differentiate itself from standard payment processors for small businesses in Southeast Asia?"

"FAZZ goes beyond simple payment acceptance by acting as a full B2B neobank that combines payments with a cash account earning 4% interest, lending products like invoice financing, and operational SaaS tools for inventory and expense management. This all-in-one approach is specifically designed for underbanked segments like warungs and startups in Indonesia and Singapore, allowing them to manage their entire financial stack within a single platform rather than relying on multiple disjointed services."
