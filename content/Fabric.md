**Website:** http://www.fabric.me | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/fabric

Fabric was a consumer application designed to serve as a personal, location-based journal. It automatically preserved records of users' places, paths, and activities, allowing them to add friends and browse associated photos. The company operated out of San Francisco with a small team of two.

Fabric was acquired, marking the end of its independent operation. Its focus on privacy and automatic cataloging of physical experiences distinguished it within the social and consumer tech space of its 2016 batch.

_Usage:_

"How did Fabric handle data collection compared to manual journaling apps?"

"Fabric automated the process by using location data to preserve a record of places, paths, and activities without requiring users to manually log every experience. It also supported social features like adding friends and browsing photos, creating a passive archive of physical interactions rather than a curated, text-heavy diary."
