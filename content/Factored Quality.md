**Website:** https://www.factoredquality.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/factored-quality

Factored Quality provides a digital QC management tool that consolidates quality and compliance operations into a single platform. It enables brands to execute quality control, supplier audits, and compliance testing globally. The company has worked with over 100 brands, including Brooklinen, The Honest Company, Dr. Squatch, and Canopy Humidifiers.

Founded in the Winter 2020 batch, the New York City-based team of 20 operates remotely and serves the B2B supply chain and logistics sectors. The platform is designed to streamline these operational tasks for companies managing complex global supply chains.

Factored Quality was acquired, marking the conclusion of its independent operation as a YC-backed startup.

_Usage:_

"How does Factored Quality handle supplier audits and compliance testing across different regions?"

"It brings all quality and compliance operations together in a single unified platform, allowing brands to run QC, supplier audits, and compliance testing anywhere in the world without needing separate tools for each region or task."
