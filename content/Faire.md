**Website:** https://www.faire.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/faire

Founded in the Winter 2017 YC batch, Faire operates a global wholesale marketplace that connects independent retailers with brand products tailored to their specific storefronts. The platform leverages machine learning and a data-driven approach to help small shops discover inventory while unburdening them from traditional retail obstacles.

To help independent stores compete with big-box chains, Faire provides straightforward financial terms that eliminate inventory risk and offer direct access to capital. For wholesale brands, the platform delivers built-in sales, marketing, and analytics tools to simplify back-office operations and customer reach.

Based in San Francisco with a team of around 900 people, Faire has built a platform supporting hundreds of thousands of retailers, resulting in over 7 million new brand-retailer connections to date.

_Usage:_

"How does Faire reduce financial risk and streamline product sourcing for local retailers?"

"Faire uses machine learning to match local store profiles with relevant brands and products. To lower the risk of trying new inventory, the platform provides access to capital and structured financial terms that eliminate inventory risk for small storefronts—offering terms that were historically only available to major big-box retailers."
