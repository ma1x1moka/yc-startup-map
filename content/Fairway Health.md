Fairway Health offers an AI‑assistant that speeds up the authorization of patient treatments for health insurers. The system uses large language models to analyze long, messy medical records—often over 70 pages—and determine whether a patient is eligible for a specific treatment. By automating this analysis, insurers can process authorizations faster than manual review.

The company was acquired, reflecting the market’s interest in AI‑driven health‑insurance workflow automation. Fairway Health operates with a small team of three people across New York City, Philadelphia, Boston, and remote locations, focusing on the healthcare and healthcare‑IT sectors.

_Usage:_

"How does Fairway Health’s AI co‑pilot handle 70‑page medical records compared to a traditional manual review process?"

"It runs a large language model over the full record to extract relevant clinical details and eligibility criteria, producing a decision recommendation without a human having to read every page, which speeds up the authorization workflow."
