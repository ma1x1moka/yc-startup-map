Fancy is a UK-based on-demand grocery delivery service that provides snacks, drinks, and other essentials in under 30 minutes. The company operated out of London with a team of approximately 50 people, focusing on the consumer and food and beverage sectors.

Fancy was acquired by Gopuff, marking its exit from the independent startup scene. This acquisition highlights the consolidation of the quick-commerce space, where speed and convenience are key differentiators for consumers seeking immediate access to daily necessities.
