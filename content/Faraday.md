**Website:** https://faradaycompute.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/faraday

Industrial enterprises still rely on people to coordinate disconnected software and machines, with critical knowledge held by a few experienced employees.

Faraday builds self-improving systems that take on this operational work. Faraday connects to existing software and machines and build training and evaluation environments around each company's workflows and data.

Continuous learning turns each company's expertise into a compounding asset. Completed work, corrections, and employee feedback improve the agents, and those improvements carry into future jobs. The business preserves its knowledge while becoming more capable over time.
