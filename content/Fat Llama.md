**Website:** https://fatllama.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/fat-llama

Fat Llama is a fully insured peer-to-peer rental marketplace that lets people rent spare items to others nearby, offering a cheaper and more efficient alternative to buying outright or hiring from rental shops.

The platform enables users to monetize rarely-used possessions while providing a safer experience through full insurance coverage for all rentals.

Fat Llama was acquired and now operates with a team of 41 based in London, England, United Kingdom, focusing on consumer and consumer electronics markets.

_Usage:_

"How does Fat Llama handle insurance for rentals?"

"Fat Llama provides full insurance coverage for every item rented through its platform, protecting both the borrower and the lender."
