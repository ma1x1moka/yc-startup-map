**Website:** http://livefeather.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/feather

Feather offers a furniture rental service for city dwellers and businesses, allowing customers to pay monthly fees that can be applied toward ownership or to swap out pieces as needs change. The company’s refurbishment process gives returned items a new life, and its reverse‑logistics platform lets users add or replace furniture on demand, keeping more pieces in homes and out of landfills. Feather was acquired, bringing its 125‑person team based in New York City and San Francisco under a larger umbrella.

_Usage:_

"How does Feather’s reverse‑logistics platform differ from traditional furniture rental models?"

"Unlike many rental services that only offer a fixed inventory, Feather’s platform lets customers swap or add new pieces at any time; returned items are refurbished and re‑sent to new homes, which reduces waste and keeps furniture circulating."
