Fieldguide is a vertical AI platform that digitizes the end-to-end engagement workflow for modern assurance and advisory firms. Built by former Big Four practitioners and experienced technology leaders, the platform is trusted by top CPA firms to improve margins, win new business, and build stronger client relationships.
Fieldguide is headquartered in San Francisco, CA, but operates as a remote-first organization, allowing teams to work from anywhere. The company has raised backing from prominent investors such as Goldman Sachs, Bessemer Venture Partners, 8VC, Y Combinator, Floodgate, and others, and employs around 200 people.
