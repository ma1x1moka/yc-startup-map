**Website:** http://fig.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/fig

Fig re-imagines the terminal by making it more accessible to beginners and more productive for advanced engineers. The company’s initial product focuses on autocomplete, with plans to expand into a full App Store ecosystem for the terminal.

Based in San Francisco, the five-person team operates in the B2B engineering space with a focus on developer tools and DevSecOps. Fig has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Fig differ from standard terminal shells?"

"Fig enhances the standard terminal experience by adding visual apps, shortcuts, and autocomplete, aiming to lower the barrier for new users while boosting productivity for experienced engineers."
