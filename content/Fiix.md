Fiix offers on‑demand auto repair covering batteries, brakes, diagnostics, inspections, doors, windows, engines, exhaust systems, filters, fluids, fuel systems, heating, lights, sensors, suspension, steering, tires, and transmission. The service is performed at the client’s home or office, allowing customers to avoid visiting a shop.

Founded in 2015 and based in Toronto, Ontario, Fiix operates with a small team. The company was acquired, reflecting its relevance in the consumer transportation services space.

_Usage:_

"How is Fiix different from taking my car to a traditional garage?"

"Fiix sends a qualified mechanic directly to your home or office and handles a wide range of repairs on‑site, so you don’t need to drive the car to a shop or wait for an appointment."
