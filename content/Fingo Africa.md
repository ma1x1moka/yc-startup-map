Fingo Africa operates as a neobank designed to serve the continent's 500 million youth, providing a full suite of financial services including personal, business, investment, and savings accounts. The company addresses the high cost of money transfers in Africa, where users typically pay 5-15% fees, by offering rates that are 90% cheaper than existing options.

Based in Nairobi, Kenya, the company allows users to access these services within 240 seconds. Fingo Africa was acquired, marking a significant milestone for its pan-African digital banking model.
