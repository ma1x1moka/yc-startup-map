**Website:** https://www.usefini.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/fini

Fini creates AI agents that autonomously resolve up to 80% of support tickets for enterprises in fintech, e‑commerce, gaming and fitness tech. The agents understand complex workflows, can take real actions such as refunds or KYC checks, and improve in real time thanks to a proprietary knowledge engine that delivers over 98% accuracy. They integrate deeply with tools like Zendesk, Salesforce and internal APIs.

Enterprises such as Bitdefender and Training Peaks use Fini to cut support costs by about half while raising customer satisfaction scores by roughly 10%. The company operates with a 14‑person team out of Amsterdam, Netherlands, and serves B2B operations customers through a SaaS model.

_Usage:_

"How is Fini’s AI agent different from a regular chatbot that’s hooked up to Zendesk?"

"Fini’s agents go beyond simple text responses: they can execute real actions like processing refunds or performing KYC checks, and they continuously learn to improve accuracy, which the company reports exceeds 98%. By using a proprietary knowledge engine and deep integrations with Zendesk, Salesforce and internal APIs, the agents can resolve most tickets end‑to‑end without human hand‑off, whereas a typical chatbot would usually need escalation for those tasks."
