**Website:** https://finlens.app | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/finlens

Finlens is an AI co-pilot for QuickBooks that automates categorisation, GAAP schedules, and collaboration, giving founders and accounting firms faster closes, real-time visibility, and clean books without spreadsheet chaos. It works on top of the existing QuickBooks stack, requiring no migrations or change management.

Backed by Y Combinator W20 and Accel, Finlens was built by second-time founders who have seen finance backends break and decided to rebuild them. With a team of six based in San Francisco, the company delivers a SaaS solution that blends AI with familiar accounting workflows.

_Usage:_

"How does Finlens integrate with QuickBooks without requiring migrations?"

"Finlens plugs into the existing QuickBooks setup, adding AI-driven categorisation, GAAP scheduling, and collaboration tools on top of the current data, so users can keep their current books and workflows intact without any migration steps or change-management overhead."
