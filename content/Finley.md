**Website:** https://www.finleycms.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/finley

Finley simplifies debt capital raise and management by offering a platform that automates due‑diligence, ensures regulatory compliance, and streamlines ongoing reporting with capital providers. The service is used by firms such as Ramp, Arc, and Parafin to reduce manual effort and improve transparency.

The team draws experience from Goldman Sachs, Nova Credit, and Palantir Technologies, and the company is backed by Y Combinator and Bain Capital Ventures. Finley, based in San Francisco with a 32‑person team, was acquired after demonstrating its value in the fintech and credit‑lending space.

_Usage:_

"How does Finley's platform automate the debt‑capital raise process compared to traditional spreadsheet‑based methods?"

"Finley's software replaces manual spreadsheets by providing built‑in workflows that collect required documents, run compliance checks, and generate standardized reports for capital providers, which lets customers like Ramp and Arc complete due‑diligence faster and with fewer errors."
