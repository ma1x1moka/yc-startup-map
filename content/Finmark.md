**Website:** https://finmark.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/finmark

Finmark provides financial planning software designed specifically for startups, simplifying the management of runway, hiring plans, and cash flow. The company operated as a B2B SaaS provider with a team of 14 based in Raleigh, NC, and remote locations.

Finmark was acquired, marking the conclusion of its independent operations. This acquisition is the primary reason for its inclusion in the atlas, as it represents a successful exit for a YC-backed fintech company focused on startup finance operations.

_Usage:_

"How does Finmark differ from general-purpose accounting tools for early-stage companies?"

"Finmark focuses on forward-looking financial planning rather than just backward-looking accounting. It helps startups specifically manage their runway, hiring forecasts, and cash management, which are critical decision-making areas for early-stage teams that standard accounting software often doesn't prioritize."
