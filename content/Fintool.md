**Website:** https://www.fintool.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/fintool

Fintool.com acts as a financial copilot for institutional investors, layering a ChatGPT‑style interface on top of financial documents. The platform is engineered to surface insights that are beyond the reach of traditional human analysis or standard search tools. Fintool was acquired, highlighting its relevance in the AI‑driven finance space. The company operates out of San Francisco with a six‑person team and serves B2B finance and accounting customers.

_Usage:_

"How is Fintool different from just prompting ChatGPT with a financial report?"

"Fintool integrates a specialized layer on top of ChatGPT that is tuned to parse and correlate financial documents, allowing it to surface insights that generic search or manual analysis would miss, rather than relying on a generic language model alone."
