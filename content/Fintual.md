Fintual is a wealth manager that allows people to invest. It manages more than USD 2B for 200,000 customers and is regulated in Mexico and Chile.

Founded in Summer 2018, the company has a team of 100 and is headquartered in Santiago, Chile. It operates in the fintech and consumer finance sectors.
