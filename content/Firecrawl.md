**Website:** https://www.firecrawl.dev | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/firecrawl

Firecrawl offers an API that transforms any URL into clean, LLM-ready markdown or structured data, solving the complex problem of making the web accessible to AI agents. The company positions itself as essential infrastructure for the AI ecosystem, enabling developers and other AI companies to gather data at scale.

Operating out of San Francisco with a team of just three people, Firecrawl has achieved significant traction, reportedly reaching 8 figures in ARR in its first year and doubling that figure in its second. The platform has gained substantial developer adoption, evidenced by over 150,000 GitHub stars, and is used by various AI companies to power their data gathering needs.

_Usage:_

"How does Firecrawl handle the messy HTML of the web for LLM consumption?"

"Firecrawl takes a URL and converts it into clean, LLM-ready markdown or structured data via a single API call. This abstracts away the difficult process of parsing and cleaning raw web content, allowing AI agents to interact with the web without dealing with the underlying HTML complexity."
