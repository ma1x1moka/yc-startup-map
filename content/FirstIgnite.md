**Website:** https://firstignite.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/firstignite

FirstIgnite builds AI-powered business intelligence tools designed specifically for scientific organizations. The company focuses on streamlining business development processes within the B2B sector, leveraging artificial intelligence to enhance operational efficiency.

The team operates remotely with a size of nine people. FirstIgnite was part of the Summer 2021 Y Combinator batch. The company has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does FirstIgnite differ from general-purpose CRM or sales intelligence platforms?"

"FirstIgnite is tailored specifically for scientific organizations, meaning its AI business intelligence tools are built to handle the unique workflows and data structures of that sector rather than serving as a generic B2B sales tool."
