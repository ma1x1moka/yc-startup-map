**Website:** https://firstquadrant.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/firstquadrant

FirstQuadrant was a San Francisco-based startup that provided AI-driven sales enablement tools designed to help revenue teams streamline behind-the-scenes work and close more deals. The platform focused on keeping sales organizations organized to ensure that no opportunities slipped through the cracks.

The company, which operated with a small team of two, was part of the Y Combinator Summer 2021 batch. It specialized in B2B sales and utilized artificial intelligence to enhance the efficiency of sales processes.

In October 2025, FirstQuadrant became inactive after completing a confidential agreement with a leading AI technology company. This partner recognized the long-term strategic value of specific components of FirstQuadrant's platform and data infrastructure, leading to the sunset of the independent platform.
