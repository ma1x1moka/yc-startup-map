FitnessAI is an app that leverages artificial intelligence to create personalized workout plans for users. It also offers anytime access to coaches, allowing subscribers to get guidance whenever they need it.

Based in New York City, the service targets consumers seeking home‑based fitness solutions and falls within the consumer, home and personal industries.

FitnessAI was acquired, marking its recognition within the AI‑driven fitness space.

_Usage:_

"How does FitnessAI’s AI‑generated workout plan differ from a standard fitness app that uses fixed routines?"

"FitnessAI’s AI builds a plan tailored to each user rather than offering a one‑size‑fits‑all routine, and it pairs the plan with on‑demand coach access, so users get both personalized programming and real‑time support."
