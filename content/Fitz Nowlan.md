Co-founded [Reflect](./Reflect.md) with [Todd McNeal](./Todd%20McNeal.md), building tools to automatically capture and organize meeting notes and follow-up work. Nowlan holds a PhD in Computer Science from Yale. Reflect was later acquired by SmartBear.
