**Website:** https://www.flexdesk.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/flexdesk

FlexDesk deploys intelligent agents to replace fragmented, manual back-office tasks for corporate real estate teams. By automating the workflows required to manage and optimize portfolios, the platform addresses rent, which is often the largest non-payroll expense for companies.

The company is used by internal real estate teams at enterprises such as Reddit and Confluent. It is positioned similarly to how Navan serves travel teams and Zip serves procurement teams, offering a dedicated solution for proptech operations.

Founded in the Winter 2023 batch, FlexDesk operates with a team of eight based in New York City and remotely.

_Usage:_

"How does FlexDesk differ from general property management software?"

"FlexDesk focuses specifically on corporate real estate back-office workflows using AI agents to execute tasks rather than just tracking data. It is designed for internal teams at large enterprises to optimize their portfolios, functioning as a specialized tool for real estate operations in the same way Navan handles travel or Zip handles procurement."
