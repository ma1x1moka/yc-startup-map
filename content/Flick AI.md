Founded by Ray Wang and Zoey Zhang. Raised $6M. Builds an AI-native video creation interface — a canvas-based editor where users can generate, edit, and combine video clips using natural language and visual drag-and-drop, rather than traditional timeline-based editing.

The positioning targets the creator and marketing space: video content is the highest-engagement format but has the highest production barrier. Professional video editors work in Premiere or Final Cut — tools that require years of skill investment. Flick AI targets the gap between "I want to make a video" and "I can afford/learn professional tools."

The canvas metaphor (rather than timeline) is the key UX bet: Figma proved that canvas interfaces unlock creative workflows that linear tools miss. Flick AI is testing whether that insight transfers to video.

Competes in the same creator tooling space as [Cardboard](./Cardboard.md), which takes a browser-native agentic approach to video editing rather than a canvas approach.

_Usage:_

"How is this different from CapCut or Adobe Express?"

"CapCut is template-based; Premiere is professional-grade. Flick AI is AI-first — the editing operations themselves are expressed in natural language, not manual timeline cuts. Different workflow, not a feature upgrade."
