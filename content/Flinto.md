**Website:** http://flinto.app | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/flinto

Flinto provides a digital wallet that enables users in Mexico to send money to friends, pay at merchants, and take care of bills directly from their phones. The service works even for people without a traditional bank account, positioning it as an inclusive fintech solution. Flinto was acquired after participating in Y Combinator’s Winter 2018 batch and operates out of Mexico City, serving the Latin American consumer payments market.

_Usage:_

"How can Flinto let users pay bills without having a bank account?"

"Flinto’s mobile wallet stores funds digitally and uses alternative payment rails that don’t require a linked bank account, so users can load money into the app and then pay merchants or settle bills directly from the phone."
