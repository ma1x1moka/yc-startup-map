**Website:** http://www.flocksafety.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/flock-safety

Flock Safety provides a public safety operating system that empowers private communities and law enforcement to collaborate in eliminating crime. The company offers a Safety-as-a-Service model featuring affordable, LTE and solar-powered devices that can be installed anywhere to detect and capture objective details. These devices decode evidence in real-time and deliver investigative leads directly to relevant public servants.

Headquartered in Atlanta with a team of approximately 1,000 employees, Flock Safety operates nationwide. The company emphasizes ethical design to protect human privacy and mitigate bias in policing. It raised $150M in a Series E round led by Tiger Global at a $3.5B valuation.

_Usage:_

"How does the hardware deployment work compared to traditional surveillance setups?"

"The system uses standalone devices powered by LTE and solar energy, which allows for installation in almost any location without the need for complex wiring or infrastructure. Once installed, the devices use machine learning to detect events and capture objective details, decoding evidence in real-time to send investigative leads to law enforcement."
