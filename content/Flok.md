**Website:** https://goflok.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/flok

Flok provides end-to-end planning for corporate retreats by integrating a digital dashboard with human retreat designers. The service handles the full scope of logistics, from booking hotel room blocks to planning and executing the ideal itinerary for teams.

The company operated out of New York City with a small team of five people. It was acquired, marking the conclusion of its independent operations.

_Usage:_

"How does Flok differ from just using a travel agency or a project management tool for offsites?"

"Flok combines a planning dashboard with human designers to handle the entire process, meaning you don't have to manually coordinate hotel blocks and itineraries yourself or rely on a generic travel agent who doesn't understand the specific team dynamics of a company retreat."
