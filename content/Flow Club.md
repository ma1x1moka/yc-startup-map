**Website:** https://www.flow.club/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/flow-club

Flow Club operates as a virtual co-working space where members engage in high-intensity sprints designed to double their productivity. The platform aims to make deep, focused flow accessible to anyone, addressing the isolation often felt by those working independently or remotely.

Based in San Francisco, the company presented at Y Combinator's Summer 2021 Demo Day and has raised $5M. It is backed by Worklife Ventures and a network of over 100 investors, including Paul Graham and founders of companies such as Dropbox, Mercury, Quora, Outschool, and Sendbird.

With a small founding team of six, Flow Club focuses on building software that powers an inspiring and productive work environment for knowledge workers, emphasizing that great things are built incrementally.

_Usage:_

"How does Flow Club actually help with remote work isolation?"

"It functions as a virtual co-working space where you join high-intensity sprints with other members. The goal is to create a shared environment that makes working remotely feel less lonely while pushing you to achieve deep, focused flow states, effectively doubling your productivity during those sessions."
