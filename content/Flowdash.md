**Website:** https://flowdash.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/flowdash

Flowdash enables teams to quickly construct internal tools for operational workflows that require a mix of automation and human judgment. The platform addresses the gap between fully manual processes and rigid, fully automated systems by allowing users to build custom, semi-automated interfaces without writing code.

The company operated as a small five-person team based in San Francisco, focusing on B2B engineering and product design. It was acquired, marking the end of its independent operation.

_Usage:_

"How is Flowdash different from standard low-code platforms like Retool or Airtable?"

"Flowdash focuses specifically on workflows that cannot be fully automated because they require human intuition, allowing you to weave manual steps into automated pipelines. Unlike platforms that try to replace human input entirely or just provide static UIs, it is designed for semi-automated processes where a person needs to make a judgment call within the workflow."
