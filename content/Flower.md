**Website:** https://flower.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/flower

Flower Labs provides Flower, an open-source framework and ecosystem designed for training artificial intelligence on distributed data and compute resources. The platform utilizes federated learning to allow organizations to improve their AI models on sensitive data that remains securely distributed across organizational silos or user devices, rather than being centralized.

The company argues that most current AI relies on centralized public datasets, which represent only a fraction of available data. By unlocking access to private data, Flower aims to drive the next breakthroughs in artificial intelligence while maintaining privacy and collaboration.

Flower Labs is a Y Combinator W23 graduate with a remote team of 20. It is trusted by industry leaders including Mozilla, Banking Circle, Samsung, Temenos, and Bosch, and is backed by investors such as Felicis, First Spark Ventures, and Mozilla Ventures.

_Usage:_

"How does Flower handle data privacy compared to standard centralized training pipelines?"

"Flower uses federated learning to keep sensitive data distributed across organizational silos or user devices instead of aggregating it in a central location. This allows models to be trained on private data without exposing the underlying information, which is particularly useful for industries like banking or healthcare where data cannot be centralized due to security or regulatory constraints."
