**Website:** https://flutterwave.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/flutterwave

Flutterwave provides technology, tools, and infrastructure for businesses and banks seeking to offer seamless and secure payment experiences. Founded in 2016, the company aims to build the necessary payment rails to integrate African markets into the broader global financial system.

The company has grown to become one of the fastest-growing payments firms in the world, operating across more than 33 African countries. Since its inception, Flutterwave has processed close to $20 billion in payments and 100 million transactions, supported by a team of 500 employees based in Lagos and Los Angeles.

Flutterwave has attracted significant investment from experienced fintech investors, including Y Combinator Continuity Fund, Greycroft, and the Omidyar Network. In 2017, it was named Africa’s best fintech company at the Apps Africa Innovation Awards.

_Usage:_

"How does Flutterwave differ from global payment processors like Stripe in terms of regional coverage?"

"Flutterwave focuses specifically on enabling businesses to make and accept payments across 33 African countries, providing the local infrastructure needed to connect these markets to the global economy. While global players like Stripe offer broad international coverage, Flutterwave is notable for its deep integration and scale within the African region, having processed nearly $20 billion in transactions since 2016."
