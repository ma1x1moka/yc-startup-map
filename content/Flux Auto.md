**Website:** https://fluxauto.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/flux-auto

Flux Auto develops physical AI solutions that automate material handling in industrial settings by retrofitting existing legacy vehicle fleets. The company’s technology allows businesses to reduce reliance on human drivers while simultaneously boosting productivity and lowering operating costs. By leveraging autonomous driving capabilities for internal logistics, Flux addresses the inefficiencies inherent in manual material movement within large-scale facilities.

The company is currently deploying its systems with major industrial clients including Walmart, Daimler, Bajaj, Cummins, and Mahindra Logistics. These deployments span various environments such as warehouses, fulfillment centers, and factories. Based in Bengaluru, India, Flux Auto operates with a team of 56 people focused on the intersection of robotics and artificial intelligence.

_Usage:_

"How does Flux Auto handle the transition from manual to autonomous driving in existing facilities?"

"Flux Auto works with legacy vehicle fleets already present in warehouses and factories, applying physical AI to automate their movement rather than requiring customers to purchase new autonomous hardware. This approach allows companies to modernize their material handling operations and reduce dependency on human drivers without replacing their current fleet infrastructure."
