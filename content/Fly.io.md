**Website:** https://fly.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/fly-io

Fly.io is a cloud infrastructure platform that lets developers run full‑stack applications close to their users. It offers a simple deployment model where a Docker image can be pushed to Fly.io and the platform automatically provisions app servers and databases in the regions that matter most. The service is designed to reduce latency and improve performance for end users worldwide.

Founded in Winter 2020, Fly.io has grown to a team of 48 engineers based in Chicago and remote. It focuses on B2B infrastructure and provides a CLI that simplifies the process of launching and scaling instances. Fly.io’s approach is notable for its emphasis on geographic proximity and ease of deployment for full‑stack workloads.

_Usage:_

"How does Fly.io’s deployment model differ from traditional cloud providers like AWS or Azure?"

"Fly.io’s CLI lets developers launch instances in specific regions with a single command, automatically provisioning app servers and databases close to end users, which simplifies the deployment of full‑stack Docker images compared to the more complex orchestration required on AWS or Azure."
