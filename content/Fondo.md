**Website:** https://fondo.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/fondo

Fondo provides an automated platform that handles bookkeeping, tax filing, and tax credit optimization for startups. It claims to help companies receive cash back from the IRS, with the average startup receiving $21,000 and potential refunds of up to $500,000 per year. The service is used by more than 2,000 startups and is positioned as a fintech SaaS solution that reduces manual accounting work.

Based in San Francisco with a team of about 80 employees, Fondo targets B2B customers in the finance and accounting space. Its AI‑driven approach aims to streamline financial operations so founders can focus on growth rather than bookkeeping.

_Usage:_

"How does Fondo generate such large tax refunds for startups?"

"Fondo uses its platform to optimize and file a startup’s taxes, identifying eligible tax credits that can result in refunds; the company reports that the average startup gets $21,000 back and some see up to $500,000 per year."
