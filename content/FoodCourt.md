**Website:** https://www.getfoodcourt.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/foodcourt

FoodCourt, also known as CoKitchen, operates as Africa's leading virtual restaurant company, utilizing a network of ghost kitchens and dark stores to serve over 350,000 meals in the last 12 months. The company is headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria, with a team of 80 employees.

To address the unique challenges of the African on-demand food delivery market, FoodCourt adopts a full-stack approach that controls key areas of the value chain, including technology, production, and delivery. This strategy allows the company to provide users with a seamless and consistent experience while maintaining the flexibility needed for rapid growth.

By focusing on making good food and quick convenience goods more accessible, FoodCourt aims to expand its reach across the continent through its integrated virtual infrastructure.

_Usage:_

"How does FoodCourt's full-stack approach differ from standard food delivery apps that just connect users to existing restaurants?"

"Unlike standard delivery platforms that act as middlemen for existing brick-and-mortar restaurants, FoodCourt controls the entire value chain by operating its own virtual restaurants and dark stores. This means it manages the technology, production, and delivery layers directly, which allows it to offer a more consistent user experience and greater flexibility for scaling without relying on third-party restaurant partners."
