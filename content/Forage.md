**Website:** https://www.theforage.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/forage

Forage partners with leading companies to create free digital learning simulations that mimic a day in the life of a junior employee. Students complete hypothetical tasks, learn from employees, and see which roles match their interests and skills. The platform currently serves over 4 million students.
Employers such as GE, BCG, HSBC, Goldman Sachs, Lululemon, EY, Accenture, JPMorgan Chase & Co, and many others use the simulations to attract high‑intent candidates from diverse backgrounds and to identify talent for hiring. The company’s model is built on the belief that talent is evenly distributed while opportunity is not.
Forage was acquired and now operates with a team of 65 people across San Francisco and remote locations.

_Usage:_

"How are Forage’s job simulations different from a traditional internship?"

"Forage’s simulations are free, online experiences built with partner companies that let students complete mock tasks and receive feedback, providing a “day in the life” view without requiring on‑site placement or compensation. Unlike traditional internships, they are open to any student regardless of university or location, and they serve as a recruiting tool for companies to evaluate and attract talent."
