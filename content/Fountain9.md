Fountain9 is a predictive inventory planning solution that helps food brands and retailers reduce waste and improve product availability by forecasting future inventory needs. It uses a demand sensing and pricing engine that incorporates historical sales trends, seasonality, holidays, markdown events, and pricing changes to predict demand and optimize prices.

The platform aligns predicted demand with supply-side data to identify ideal suppliers and replenishment quantities, minimizing stock outs and waste. Fountain9 was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Fountain9's demand sensing engine differ from traditional forecasting tools?"

"Fountain9’s engine incorporates multiple real‑time factors—seasonality, holidays, markdown events, and pricing changes—to forecast demand and optimize prices, then aligns those predictions with supply‑side data to recommend suppliers and replenishment quantities, a step beyond conventional static forecasting."
