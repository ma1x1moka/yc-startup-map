**Website:** https://checkfragment.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/fragment

Fragment builds software that bridges AI and human workers, based on the premise that every AI‑enabled company will need humans in the loop for handoff tasks. It initially targets operations teams in fintech firms, providing task‑management tools for manual processes such as onboarding and compliance. By layering human supervision on top of these workflows, Fragment makes it easy for companies to increase automation while retaining oversight. The company was acquired, reflecting its relevance in the B2B operations space.

_Usage:_

"Will Fragment fully automate my fintech onboarding process or still require human supervision?"

"Fragment is designed to add a human‑supervision layer rather than replace humans entirely; it manages manual tasks like onboarding and compliance and then lets you incrementally automate them while keeping a human in the loop for oversight."
