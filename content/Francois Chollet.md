**Twitter:** @fchollet | **LinkedIn:** available

AI researcher at Google for a decade. Creator of **Keras** — the deep learning framework used by millions of practitioners globally, later integrated into TensorFlow as its official high-level API.

In 2019 published a landmark paper arguing that AI benchmarks measure memorisation and pattern matching, not genuine reasoning. Developed the **ARC-AGI benchmark** — tasks requiring visual reasoning and systematic generalisation that LLMs consistently fail.

Co-founded the **ARC Prize Foundation** with [Mike Knoop](./Mike%20Knoop.md) in 2024: $1M annual prizes for AI systems that pass ARC-AGI. The prize has driven significant research attention to the non-scaling approach to AGI.

Left Google in 2025 to co-found [Ndea](./Ndea.md) — an AGI lab pursuing program synthesis. The move was significant: Chollet had spent years publicly arguing that scaling was insufficient, then committed to building the alternative.
