**Website:** https://www.functionup.org/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/functionup-fanplay

FunctionUp, also known as FanPlay, operates a skill-tech platform focused on technical placement bootcamps. The company utilizes an Income Sharing Model, allowing it to train top tech talent without charging any upfront fees. Upon completion of the program, graduates are placed across the company's network of partner companies.

Based in Bengaluru, India, FunctionUp combines technical training with a strong social impact mission. The program provides specific assistance to women and unemployed youth in India, supporting them both economically and socially. The company was part of the Y Combinator Winter 2021 batch and has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does the Income Sharing Model work for students joining FunctionUp?"

"Students pay zero upfront fees to join the bootcamp. Instead, the cost is tied to their future earnings, as the company places them at partner companies after training. This structure removes the financial barrier to entry for top tech talent while aligning the provider's incentives with successful job placement."
