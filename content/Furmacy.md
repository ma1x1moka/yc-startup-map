**Website:** http://www.furmacy.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/furmacy

Furmacy offers a digital e‑prescribing platform that lets veterinarians create and transmit prescriptions electronically, streamlining the medication ordering process for animal care. The service sits within the healthcare IT space, targeting veterinary practices that need a modern, paper‑free workflow.

Furmacy was acquired after its Winter 2021 batch. The company operated with a five‑person team across San Francisco, El Dorado Hills, and remote locations.

_Usage:_

"How does Furmacy’s e‑prescribing platform differ from using traditional paper prescriptions?"

"Furmacy replaces handwritten, paper‑based prescriptions with a digital interface that lets veterinarians enter and send medication orders electronically, reducing the need for physical paperwork and simplifying record‑keeping."
