**YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/fuzzbuzz

Fuzzbuzz delivers a managed fuzzing platform that lets businesses automate security testing of software without building their own infrastructure. The service is positioned for B2B customers and fits within the broader security and cybersecurity ecosystem. Fuzzbuzz was acquired, reflecting its value in the infrastructure and security space.

Based in San Francisco, the company operated as part of Y Combinator’s Winter 2019 batch and focused on providing infrastructure‑level security tooling.

_Usage:_

"How is Fuzzbuzz’s fuzzing service different from running open‑source fuzzers on my own servers?"

"Fuzzbuzz provides a hosted platform that runs fuzzing workloads for you, handling the provisioning, scaling, and management of the underlying infrastructure so you don’t need to maintain your own fuzzing environment."
