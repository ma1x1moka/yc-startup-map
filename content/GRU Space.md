Founded solo by [Skyler Chan](./Skyler%20Chan.md), age 22. Currently accepting reservations at $250K–$1M per stay. The only YC company in the W26 batch building commercial lunar hospitality infrastructure.

GRU Space's thesis: the Moon will have permanent human presence within a decade (via NASA's Artemis program, SpaceX, and commercial partners), and the hospitality infrastructure — safe, comfortable, commercially operated habitats — is a separate problem from the rocket and lander problem that governments and large primes are solving.

The business model mirrors early commercial aviation: sell experiences to wealthy early adopters, use that revenue to fund infrastructure that eventually enables broader access. The $250K–$1M price point targets the same customer who buys a Virgin Galactic ticket, not the general public.

At YC demo day, GRU Space had the highest novelty premium of the batch — the kind of audacious bet that generates coverage regardless of near-term revenue. Whether it's viable depends entirely on the pace of lunar infrastructure development from SpaceX and NASA.
