Co-founded [Pocket](./Pocket.md) with [Akshay Narisetti](./Akshay%20Narisetti.md).

Previously served as CEO and co-founder of **DoxyChain** — a blockchain-based document infrastructure company. The DoxyChain background brings enterprise go-to-market experience and the operational discipline of building B2B infrastructure: compliance, security, enterprise sales cycles.

Dymowski's prior CEO experience at DoxyChain is the operational complement to Narisetti's hardware product background. Pocket's challenge post-launch is scaling the supply chain, managing manufacturing, and building the enterprise version of the product — exactly the operational profile Dymowski brings from DoxyChain.

The 30,000 units shipped in 5 months is as much a supply chain achievement as a sales achievement. Hardware at that volume requires supplier relationships, quality control, and logistics infrastructure that takes most hardware startups 12–18 months to build.
