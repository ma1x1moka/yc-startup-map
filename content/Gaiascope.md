**Website:** http://gaia-scope.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/gaiascope

Gaiascope offers a SaaS platform that simplifies electric grid forecasting and operations for hard assets such as wind, solar, and energy storage.
The software delivers timely, unbiased information that helps these assets improve revenues, reduce risk, and maximize the profitability of every megawatt.
In addition to its core product, Gaiascope manages a quant fund that trades electricity, using the fund’s performance to build credibility with clients and accelerate continuous improvement of its software. The company was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Gaiascope’s forecasting differ from traditional grid management tools?"

"Gaiascope’s platform focuses specifically on renewable and energy storage assets, providing timely, unbiased forecasts that directly improve revenues, reduce risk, and maximize profitability for each megawatt, rather than offering generic grid management features."
