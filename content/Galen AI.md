**Website:** https://www.galenai.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/galen-ai

Galen AI aggregates medical records from over 800 healthcare institutions across more than 12,000 locations in the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada, and connects with over 20 popular wearable devices such as Apple Watch, Fitbit, and Dexcom. The platform unifies all medical records and health data into a single space, enabling patients to view and manage their health information in one place.

Using state‑of‑the‑art large language model memory capabilities, Galen delivers personalized insights, proactive health recommendations, and 24/7 on‑demand medical support. The service is designed to empower patients to be active participants in their care teams, addressing the fragmentation and delays that many patients face in the current healthcare system.

Galen AI was acquired in 2025 and is currently a consumer app in beta testing, with plans to launch on the App Store soon.
