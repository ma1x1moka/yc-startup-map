Gamelynx builds competitive games that are easily watchable across platforms, leveraging a team of five with experience from Riot Games, Google, Jane St, and quantum computing research labs.
Gamelynx raised $1.2 million to date from investors such as Riot Games, M Ventures, and Y Combinator, and has since shut down, as indicated by its inactive status.
