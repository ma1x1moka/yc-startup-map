**Website:** https://gamerpay.gg | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/gamerpay

GamerPay operates as a marketplace for trading gaming skins and digital assets, solving the prevalent issue of scams by integrating directly with games to instantly validate all trades. The company was acquired, marking its exit from the YC Summer 2021 batch.

Founded by a team previously behind a major Danish secondhand clothing marketplace, GamerPay addresses a significant pain point: 46% of surveyed gamers have been scammed more than once, with over half being minors who are often restricted from trading for cash on social media. To address this, the company built a fully compliant system for parental consent, enabling a broader range of gamers to trade legally while providing peace of mind to parents.

Based in Copenhagen with a team of six, GamerPay targets a market of 215 million annual trades in supported games. The platform generates revenue through a per-trade fee model, aiming to capture a share of the growing blockchain game economy.

_Usage:_

"How does GamerPay handle the legal issues of minors trading digital assets, and how is that different from just using a standard payment processor?"

"GamerPay integrates directly with the games to validate trades instantly, which prevents the scams that plague other marketplaces. Crucially, it includes a fully compliant system for parental consent, which allows minors to trade legally with their parents' approval. This is a specific compliance layer that standard payment processors or generic marketplaces don't typically offer for gaming assets, especially given that a large portion of the user base is under 18."
