**Website:** https://usegander.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/gander

Gander builds AI‑powered software that lets private jet operators coordinate, maintain, and monetize their fleets from a single platform. The startup, with a team of seven, is based in New York City and serves the B2B aviation operations market. Gander was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Gander's AI coordinate maintenance across a fleet compared to traditional spreadsheets?"

"Gander's AI automatically schedules maintenance tasks, tracks parts inventory, and sends real‑time alerts, eliminating the need for manual spreadsheets."
