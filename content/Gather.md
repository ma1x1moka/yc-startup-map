Gather is a video chat platform that builds a Metaverse‑style virtual layer over the physical world, letting people work, socialize and learn in shared digital spaces. Launched in May 2020, the service has brought millions of users together across its map‑based rooms.

The company has raised a total of $77 million in Series A and B funding from investors including Sequoia Capital and Index Ventures. It operates with a small, friendly team of about 60 people located in New York City, San Francisco and remotely, working from a state‑of‑the‑art virtual office.

Gather’s mission is to create opportunity and connection for everyone, no matter where they are, by making online interactions feel more human.

_Usage:_

"How is Gather different from a standard video‑conferencing tool like Zoom?"

"Gather replaces a single video window with a navigable virtual map where each participant has an avatar that can move between rooms, start private chats, and join group conversations, making interactions feel more like in‑person gatherings rather than a static conference call."
