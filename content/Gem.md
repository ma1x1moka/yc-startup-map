**Website:** https://www.gem.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/gem

Gem is a San Francisco-based talent engagement platform designed to help recruiting teams leverage data and automation for faster, higher-quality, and more diverse hiring. By integrating data from email, applicant tracking systems, LinkedIn, and other social networks, the platform creates a unified source of truth for all talent relationships. This allows teams to gain insights into the entire recruiting process and plan strategically for future hiring needs.

The company serves savvy recruiting teams at major organizations, including Wayfair, Dropbox, Nasdaq, Cisco, and DoorDash. These customers utilize Gem’s cross-functional collaboration tools and automated outreach capabilities to maintain a competitive advantage in talent acquisition. With a team of approximately 200 people, Gem continues to operate as an active B2B SaaS provider in the recruiting and talent industry.

_Usage:_

"How does Gem handle the data fragmentation problem that recruiters face when using multiple tools?"

"Gem solves this by unifying data and context from the various tools recruiters already use daily, such as email, applicant tracking systems, and LinkedIn. Instead of switching between disparate platforms, the system aggregates this information into a single source of truth for talent relationships, enabling teams to automate outreach at scale and collaborate more effectively across functions."
