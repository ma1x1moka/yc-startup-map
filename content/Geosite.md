**Website:** https://www.geosite.io/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/geosite

Geosite is a cloud-based platform that creates a comprehensive repository of geospatial data, including satellite imagery, by aggregating public and commercially available sources. The marketplace pairs integrated data management and collaboration tools, allowing customers to search, select, purchase, assemble, and manage data sets for faster and more accurate planning.

The platform serves both commercial and military customers, with a focus on Oil and Gas companies and U.S. Special Operations. By bringing spatial data together, Geosite enables cutting-edge logistics planning and operational tracking to increase safety and productivity. The company was acquired, marking its success in the geospatial industry.

Based in San Francisco with a remote workforce, Geosite employed around 40 people before the acquisition.

_Usage:_

"How does Geosite’s marketplace differ from other geospatial data platforms?"

"Geosite’s marketplace aggregates both public and commercial satellite imagery and sensor data into a single, searchable repository. It couples this data with integrated management and collaboration tools, so users can not only find and purchase data but also assemble and manage it within the same platform."
