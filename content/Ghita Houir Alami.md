Founded [ZeroEntropy](./ZeroEntropy.md), building high-accuracy search systems for retrieval-augmented generation and AI agents, raising $4.2M led by Initialized Capital. Houir Alami left Casablanca, Morocco at 17 to study engineering in France, earning master's degrees in applied mathematics from École Polytechnique and UC Berkeley. ZeroEntropy was later acquired by Notion, bringing its retrieval technology and team into Notion's product.
