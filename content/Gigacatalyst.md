**Website:** https://gigacatalyst.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/gigacatalyst

Gigacatalyst lets customers build analytics, dashboards, reports, and forms directly inside their SaaS product. The platform adds an embedded AI customization layer that learns the product’s APIs, enabling users to create tailored visualizations that match their workflows and share them across teams with a single click.

By providing bespoke dashboards for each use case, Gigacatalyst helps customers use their SaaS more, retain longer, and expand sales faster. The company is already live with top Series B firms and reports a 90 % repeat usage rate.

_Usage:_

"How does Gigacatalyst's embedded AI layer differ from standard analytics tools?"

"Gigacatalyst’s AI layer learns the APIs of the host SaaS product, allowing customers to build dashboards, reports, and forms that are tightly integrated with their workflows. Unlike generic analytics tools that require exporting data, Gigacatalyst lets users create and share customized visualizations inside the product with a one‑click share feature, improving adoption and retention."
