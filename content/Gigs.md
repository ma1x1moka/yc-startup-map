**Website:** https://gigs.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/gigs

Gigs operates as the leading provider of embedded connectivity, bridging the gap between traditional telecom carriers and modern digital platforms. The company powers global mobile infrastructure for some of the world’s largest enterprises, enabling connectivity for hundreds of millions of people worldwide.

With a team of 150 based in San Francisco, Gigs focuses on B2B engineering and product design to deliver this infrastructure. It is backed by Ribbit Capital, Google’s Gradient Ventures, and Y Combinator, and remains an active company in the Winter 2021 batch.

_Usage:_

"How does Gigs differ from standard MVNOs or direct carrier APIs?"

"Gigs positions itself as an operating system for embedded telecom rather than just a connectivity provider. It specifically bridges the gap between telecom carriers and modern digital platforms, allowing enterprises to embed connectivity directly into their products to reach hundreds of millions of users, backed by investors like Ribbit Capital and Google’s Gradient Ventures."
