**Website:** https://www.glideapps.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/glide

Glide is a web-based app builder that lets non-programmers quickly create internal business applications. Users can start from scratch, from a prompt, or by importing a spreadsheet, and the platform handles UI design, collaboration, and deployment.

The company positions itself as bringing app development out of the Dark Ages, making it web-based, collaborative, accessible, and fun after decades of little change. With the rise of AI-assisted coding, Glide's latest generation offers an AI-first method that removes the need to manage granular control, security, authentication, database infrastructure, or deployment concerns.

Glide's mission is to become the operating system for businesses by turning operational spreadsheets and data into beautiful, intelligent apps. The team of three technical founders, formerly early employees at Xamarin, has grown to 46 people across San Francisco and remote locations.

_Usage:_

"How is Glide's AI-first app builder different from other no-code platforms?"

"Glide lets users generate full-featured internal apps directly from a spreadsheet or a natural-language prompt, and the AI handles UI layout, data handling, security, and deployment, so users don’t have to manually configure those parts as they would on typical no-code tools."
