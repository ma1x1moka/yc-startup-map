**Website:** http://www.glimpsek12.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/glimpse-k12

Glimpse K12 captures everything a district does to impact student outcomes and evaluates those activities in the context of student achievement. By turning otherwise opaque education return on investment (eROI) into measurable data, the company helps schools align time and money with the unique needs of their students.

The platform provides answers to critical questions that traditionally make eROI virtually impossible to track and manage, thereby eliminating ineffective spending in schools. Glimpse K12 was acquired, reflecting the value of its analytics approach in the education sector.

The company operates with a team of six out of Huntsville, Alabama.

_Usage:_

"How does Glimpse K12 actually calculate education ROI compared to the typical manual tracking schools use?"

"It captures all district activities that affect student outcomes, then evaluates those activities against measured student achievement, turning the data into answers to the critical questions that make eROI trackable, which replaces the manual, often impossible, tracking methods."
