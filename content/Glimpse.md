Glimpse is the leading AI platform for CPG brands, automating key back‑office workflows such as deductions management, revenue recovery, and cash application to increase operating leverage and return millions of dollars to a brand's pocket.

Its agents automatically retrieve deduction data, validate charges, automate cash application, and dispute invalid claims, often starting with a lookback of the past year of deductions. For a $1B CPG, one agent reviewed 17,000 deductions in under 24 hours—work that would take an FTE roughly two years—and identified more than $10 million in recoverable revenue, driving meaningful margin expansion.

Glimpse’s pricing is simple and incentive‑aligned, structured to create clear P&L impact from day one. The company is based in New York City, employs 27 people, and serves the B2B retail sector.
