**Website:** https://video.golpoai.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/golpo

Golpo AI is a platform that transforms prompts or documents into fully produced whiteboard explainer videos, offering a style reminiscent of Khan Academy or RSA Animate. It can convert narration into aligned video, support script-to-video generation, produce videos up to ten minutes long, and handle over fifty languages, all while allowing users to edit the final product through simple prompts. Trusted by more than 170,000 users, Golpo is used for training, faceless YouTube, marketing, technical, and educational content, and offers batch production, API access, and over ten visual styles, including custom branding.

Based in San Francisco, Golpo operates as a B2B productivity tool with a team of seven, serving a wide range of industries such as education, eLearning, video, sales enablement, and marketing.

_Usage:_

"How does Golpo handle multiple languages in its video generation?"

"Golpo supports more than fifty languages; users can input a document or prompt in any language, and the AI generates matching audio and visuals, aligning narration with the video timeline."
