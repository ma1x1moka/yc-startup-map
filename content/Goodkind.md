**Website:** https://www.goodkind.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/goodkind

Goodkind provides a student communications platform designed for universities and colleges. It enables institutions to engage with their student bodies through multiple channels, including SMS, video, chat, and mail.

The company operated as a B2B SaaS provider based in Toronto, Canada, with a team of approximately 30 people. It was part of the Summer 2021 Y Combinator batch.

Goodkind has since been acquired, marking the exit of this higher-ed focused communication tool from the independent startup landscape.

_Usage:_

"How does Goodkind differ from standard marketing automation tools for universities?"

"Goodkind is specifically built for higher education, focusing on student communications rather than general marketing. It aggregates multiple channels like SMS, video, chat, and mail into a single platform tailored for the unique needs of universities and colleges, rather than being a generic B2B sales tool."
