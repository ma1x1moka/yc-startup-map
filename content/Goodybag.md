**Website:** http://goodybag.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/goodybag

Goodybag is an online catering platform that simplifies office meal delivery. Based in Austin, Texas, the platform gives businesses a central tool to explore local restaurant menus and arrange food delivery for team meetings and workplace events.

For restaurants and caterers, Goodybag provides listing access with no upfront startup or monthly fees. Partner vendors retain control over their operations by setting their own delivery fees and coverage zones. Originally part of the Y Combinator Winter 2016 batch, Goodybag has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Goodybag charge restaurants to list their catering menus?"

"Goodybag does not charge restaurants or caterers any startup or monthly listing fees. Instead, food providers set their own delivery fees and define their custom service zones directly on the platform."
