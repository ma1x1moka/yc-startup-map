**Website:** https://www.governgpt.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/governgpt

GovernGPT offers an AI‑powered platform that lets money managers answer diligence questionnaires in minutes instead of days, replacing whole teams that would otherwise spend weeks on repetitive content. By generating the required responses quickly, it helps firms raise capital more efficiently.

The service counts prominent asset managers such as Pantheon, Tishman Speyer, Coatue, Bridgewater, and many others among its clients, showing strong adoption in the fintech sector. Based in Montreal with a seven‑person team, GovernGPT is part of Y Combinator’s Winter 2024 batch and focuses on streamlining the fundraising workflow for asset managers.

_Usage:_

"How does GovernGPT generate questionnaire answers faster than a manual team?"

"GovernGPT uses AI to produce the required RFP content in minutes, reusing and adapting existing information so managers no longer need to allocate days of staff time to fill out repetitive questionnaires."
