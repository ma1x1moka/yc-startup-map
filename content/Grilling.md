---
description: A technique for developing a design concept: the agent interviews you Socratically, one decision at a time.
---

Instead of accepting a vague brief and inventing the details, the [agent](./Agent.md) is instructed to interrogate it — asking about edge cases, conflicts and unstated assumptions before writing anything. Each answer narrows the space.

It works because it inverts [sycophancy](./Sycophancy.md). A [model](./Model.md) asked to build will build; a model asked to find what you have not decided will find it, and it is dramatically cheaper to resolve those questions in conversation than in code.

_Usage:_

"Just get it started."

"Have it grill you first. Ten minutes now saves the rewrite."
