Grin provides a micromobility and payments platform across multiple Latin American countries, including Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, Chile, Uruguay, Argentina, and Peru.

Founded in the Summer of 2018, the company grew to a team of 300 employees headquartered in Mexico City.

Grin was acquired, marking its impact on the region's transportation services.
