**Website:** https://www.gromo.in | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/gromo

GroMo is building India's largest financial products distribution company by operating a tech-enabled social commerce platform. The company has assembled a network of 1.2 million independent agents who use the platform to sell financial products to consumers.

The platform is designed to bridge the financial literacy gap, helping agents guide consumers toward the right offerings. GroMo currently distributes over 100 financial products, including insurance, investments, credit cards, loans, and savings accounts, from brands such as IDFC Bank, Jupiter Money, Axis Bank, and Freecharge.

Headquartered in Gurugram, India, the company employs approximately 100 people and focuses on the fintech and insurance sectors.

_Usage:_

"How does GroMo differ from a standard digital insurance marketplace?"

"GroMo operates as a social commerce platform that empowers a network of 1.2 million independent agents to sell products, rather than relying solely on direct-to-consumer digital sales. It specifically targets the financial literacy gap by equipping these agents to help consumers choose the right offerings from a catalog of over 100 products across insurance, investments, and credit."
