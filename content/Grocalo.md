**Website:** https://grocalo.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/grocalo

Grocalo operates as an AI brain designed to manage content workflows for creators. It utilizes video understanding and deep platform analytics to identify specific audience preferences, allowing it to personalize and distribute content tailored to each creator's unique followers.

The company reports generating 2 billion views and gaining 4 million followers for its clients within a two-month period. Its user base includes high-profile figures such as Jimmy O. Yang, King Bach, and Leenda Dong, who collectively possess tens of millions of followers across various platforms.

Based in Los Angeles, Grocalo focuses on the creator economy by developing an AI model capable of replicating viral success for specific audiences through data-driven personalization.

_Usage:_

"How does Grocalo determine what content to push to a specific creator's audience?"

"It uses video understanding and deep platform analytics to model the specific 'taste' of a creator's audience. Rather than using a generic recommendation engine, it personalizes distribution based on these learned preferences to replicate virality for that specific user base."
