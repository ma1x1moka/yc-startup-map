**Website:** https://www.growthbook.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/growthbook

GrowthBook provides an open source feature‑flagging and A/B‑testing platform that keeps all data in a company’s own infrastructure, avoiding the high costs, compliance issues, and vendor lock‑in of services like LaunchDarkly. By leveraging existing data pipelines and business metrics, it lets teams experiment and iterate without building a new system from scratch. With a team of 40 based in San Francisco and remote, GrowthBook serves B2B analytics customers and positions itself as a leader in the growing feature‑flag market.

_Usage:_

"How does GrowthBook avoid vendor lock‑in compared to LaunchDarkly?"

"GrowthBook keeps all data in the company’s own infrastructure, so it doesn’t send data to a third‑party service, eliminating vendor lock‑in and reducing costs."
