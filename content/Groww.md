**Website:** https://groww.in | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/groww

Groww aims to empower people in India by making financial services simple, transparent and delightful. It leverages technology to build reliable and accessible financial services.

The company is India's largest stock broker by active NSE clients and the biggest distributor of mutual fund systematic investment plans. Groww went public, reflecting its scale and market presence. It employs about 1,050 people and is based in Bengaluru, Karnataka.
