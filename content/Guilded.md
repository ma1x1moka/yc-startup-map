**Website:** http://www.guilded.gg | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/guilded

Guilded equips gaming servers with upgraded group chat tools. The platform goes beyond basic messaging by integrating built-in event calendars, forums, and organizational features directly into server environments.

Founded in the Y Combinator Summer 2017 batch, the team operates out of San Francisco and remotely with a team size of 30 across the consumer and gaming industries. Guilded has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Guilded expand on standard group chat platforms for gaming servers?"

"Guilded integrates organizational features directly into the server interface, equipping group chat environments with native event calendars and forums alongside text channels."
