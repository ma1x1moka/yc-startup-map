Co-founded [Fragment](./Fragment.md) as CTO with [Olivier Moindrot](./Olivier%20Moindrot.md), part of YC's Summer 2023 batch, helping operations teams in fintechs and insurtechs integrate AI. Genthial studied at Stanford and previously led the AI team at a KYC provider, with prior experience at Criteo AI Lab, Roam Analytics, and the Stanford Machine Learning Group. He joined Sierra alongside Moindrot after Sierra acquired Fragment in April 2026.
