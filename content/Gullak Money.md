Gullak is a mobile fintech application based in Bengaluru that automates the savings process by converting small, regular contributions into digital gold. The company addresses a significant gap in the Indian market where hundreds of millions of individuals save in bank deposits that fail to outpace inflation, despite gold being a culturally significant asset with higher returns. By leveraging UPI Autopay infrastructure, Gullak enables users to save as little as $1 per day, removing the friction and high minimum ticket sizes associated with traditional offline gold purchases.

Since launching in India, the company has grown to 40,000 transacting users with a monthly gross transaction value of $280K, reporting 25% week-on-week growth. The founding team previously built the BHIM UPI App and large-scale payment systems for major partners, processing over 30 million payments daily. Gullak targets a $1.5Bn market opportunity by combining automated micro-savings with gold-backed lending for the middle-income demographic.

_Usage:_

"How does Gullak handle the technical complexity of automating small, recurring payments for gold investment compared to traditional methods?"

"Gullak utilizes UPI Autopay infrastructure to automate the transfer of small daily amounts, such as $1, directly into digital gold. This approach eliminates the need for users to manually accumulate funds or visit physical stores, which previously required a minimum purchase of $50 and incurred storage charges. The automation relies on payment rails that were not widely available for this specific use case until recently, allowing for frictionless, high-frequency micro-investments."
