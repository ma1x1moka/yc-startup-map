Founded [Mentum](./Mentum.md) in 2021, an AI-powered assistant for procurement and strategic sourcing that went through YC's Summer 2021 batch. Trigos previously worked as an AI quant at BlackRock. Mentum was later acquired by Nuvocargo, where Trigos joined to strengthen the company's R&D organization and AI agent roadmap for logistics.

_Usage:_

"What did Gus Trigos do before founding Mentum?"
