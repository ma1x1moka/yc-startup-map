**Website:** https://www.hud.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hud

HUD is a platform designed for creating high-quality post-training datasets, specifically focusing on reinforcement learning (RL) environments and evaluations. It serves as infrastructure for data entrepreneurs who aim to build, sell, or utilize these datasets to train AI models.

The company operates on the premise that the next generation of tech builders will create infrastructure around data to align AI with specific goals. Over 50 businesses currently use HUD to construct RL environments, either to sell them to AI labs or to train their own models. The team is based in San Francisco and consists of 15 employees.

_Usage:_

"How does HUD differ from standard data labeling services?"

"HUD focuses specifically on building RL environments and evaluations for post-training, rather than just static labeled data. It allows users to construct interactive environments that can be used to train models via reinforcement learning or sold directly to AI labs, targeting the specific need for dynamic, goal-aligned training data."
