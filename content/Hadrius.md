**Website:** https://www.hadrius.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hadrius

Hadrius provides a single AI‑powered platform that lets regulated financial firms of any size consolidate and automate their securities compliance processes. The service is delivered as SaaS and is built for B2B use in the fintech and asset‑management sectors.

The company currently secures compliance for more than $3 trillion of assets under management across its client base, highlighting its scale within the regulated finance space. With a team of about 80 engineers and compliance experts based in New York City, Hadrius positions itself as a regulator‑grade solution for the US financial system.

_Usage:_

"How does Hadrius differ from traditional rule‑based compliance software?"

"Hadrius replaces static rule engines with an AI layer that continuously interprets and automates compliance tasks, allowing firms to manage all their securities compliance in one place rather than stitching together multiple legacy tools."
