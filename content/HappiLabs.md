HappiLabs is a SaaS company that offers a Virtual Lab Manager service, staffed by PhD‑level former scientists who assist with safety and regulatory compliance, product research, lab shopping, negotiating, purchasing, inventory, and bookkeeping. The company’s software tools, including the ETA Dashboard and HappiApp, enable users to request purchases, manage inventory, and check in packages upon delivery, while its extensive database of product prices gives it a competitive edge in cost efficiency.

Targeting newly funded principal investigators, startups, small biotech firms, and larger pharma or biotech purchasing departments, HappiLabs positions itself as an outsourced purchasing department or personal assistant for scientists. Based in Chicago with a presence in San Francisco, the company has built a team of 14 employees.

HappiLabs was acquired in 2018, marking its recognition as a notable player in the healthcare services and assistive technology space.

_Usage:_

"What advantage does HappiLabs offer over a traditional in‑house lab manager?"

"HappiLabs combines PhD‑level former scientists with software tools like the ETA Dashboard and HappiApp, providing a seamless experience for safety compliance, purchasing, inventory tracking, and bookkeeping—features that a typical in‑house manager may not integrate as tightly or efficiently."
