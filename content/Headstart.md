**Website:** https://www.headstart.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/headstart

Headstart is a London-based recruiting technology platform that uses machine learning to streamline talent acquisition. Instead of relying solely on traditional resume qualifications or past work experience, the platform evaluates applicants on personality, behaviors, strengths, and motivations to create an inclusive screening process.

For candidates, the system relies on a reusable 15-minute application form that connects job seekers directly with hiring businesses. Employers leverage these insights to identify candidates who align with their exact job requirements, culture, and values earlier in the hiring funnel.

The Summer 2017 Y Combinator company grew to a team of 44 people and has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Headstart evaluate candidates differently than standard keyword resume screeners?"

"Headstart shifts away from qualification- and experience-based filtering by using machine learning to assess applicants on personality, behaviors, strengths, and motivations. Candidates complete a reusable 15-minute application form, giving employers a way to match applicants to specific job requirements and company culture earlier in the hiring pipeline."
