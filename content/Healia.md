**Website:** https://www.healiahealth.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/healia

Healia is reinventing health insurance for dual‑income families. Its product enables companies to pay the out‑of‑pocket costs of any employee who enrolls in a spouse’s health insurance plan, delivering 100% coverage of a family’s health costs and saving customers roughly $10 000 per enrollee. The company now serves more than 50 customers across the United States, with over 40 000 employees using the service. Healia is a fintech‑insurance startup based in Columbus, Ohio, with a team of about 15 people.

_Usage:_

"How is Healia’s offering different from a typical employer‑provided health benefit?"

"Healia’s service lets an employer reimburse the out‑of‑pocket expenses of an employee’s spouse‑plan enrollment, covering the entire family cost, whereas most traditional benefits only cover the employee’s own plan or provide partial subsidies. This full‑coverage model can save an employer about $10 000 per enrollee."
