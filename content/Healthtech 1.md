Healthtech-1 builds clinical AI that navigates patients to the right place, first time. It automates eligibility checks, triage and appointment booking for more than 1,600 NHS GP practices across the UK.

The company’s platform will soon allow every healthcare appointment—GP, pharmacy, dentist, physio—to be instantly bookable on bookable.health, streamlining access to care.

With a team of 40 based in London, Healthtech-1 addresses the NHS’s low patient satisfaction by automating access to care, reversing a 25-year trend.
