Heart Aerospace designs and manufactures electric airplanes, offering an all‑electric range of 250 miles. The company is based in Los Angeles and employs about 50 people, positioning itself in the Industrials, Aviation and Space sectors with a focus on climate‑friendly transportation.

Founded in Y Combinator’s Winter 2019 batch, Heart’s electric aircraft aim to reduce emissions in regional travel. Its focus on electric propulsion aligns with broader trends in electric vehicles and aerospace, highlighting its role in advancing sustainable aviation.

The company remains active and continues to develop its electric aircraft technology, contributing to the climate and aerospace industries.
