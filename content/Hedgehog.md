**Website:** https://hedgehog.app | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hedgehog

Hedgehog is a crypto robo-adviser designed to manage user funds by investing in broad baskets of digital assets that cover up to 85% of the cryptocurrency market cap. The company, which has since become inactive, operated out of San Francisco and San Jose with a small remote team. At its peak, the platform served 15,000 users monitoring $176 million in crypto assets, with over $548,000 in gross transaction volume on its initial product version. The company completed its exit interview with the SEC in April 2024 and made its custodial product available on the App Store and Google Play.
