**Website:** https://www.helicone.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/helicone

Helicone is a developer-focused observability platform designed to simplify the operational aspects of deploying Large Language Models. Based in San Francisco, the company offers a unified view of performance, cost, and user interaction metrics across various LLM providers, including OpenAI, Anthropic, and LangChain. By centralizing logs and metrics, it allows engineers to track costs, understand user interactions, and optimize outputs from a single interface.

The platform supports advanced features such as prompt management, performance tracing, and debugging, enabling teams to experiment with prompts and track success rates to improve response quality. Helicone also addresses data privacy concerns by offering deployment options for dedicated instances, hybrid cloud integrations, or self-hosted environments, giving clients control over their data while ensuring compliance with privacy standards.

As a YC Winter 2023 company, Helicone has been acquired, marking a significant milestone in the growing market for AI infrastructure tools. Its focus on providing transparency and control for engineers and data scientists has positioned it as a key tool for teams scaling AI applications.
