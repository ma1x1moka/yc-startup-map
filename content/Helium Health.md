**Website:** https://heliumhealth.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/helium-health

Helium Health is a digital health company operating across Africa, providing a suite of tools for healthcare providers, payers, and patients. Its flagship product, HeliumOS, is an electronic medical records (EMR) and hospital management information system (HMIS) designed specifically for emerging markets. The company also offers business loans to healthcare providers through its HeliumCredit product and collaborates with donors and governments on public health strategies.

Notable for building the largest healthcare data repository on Africans, Helium Health has amassed over 1 million health records. The company, which has been active since its Summer 2017 YC batch, maintains a team of 110 people with locations in Los Angeles and Nigeria.

_Usage:_

"How does Helium Health differ from standard global EMR systems?"

"HeliumOS is tailored for emerging markets, addressing specific infrastructure and workflow needs in Africa that standard global EMRs often ignore. It also integrates financial services like business loans for providers and has built a massive local data repository, making it a more comprehensive operational platform for the region than a pure record-keeping tool."
