Hellosaurus is an interactive video platform designed for children, featuring stories that users can play rather than just watch. The company partners with top kids media creators and popular YouTube stars to produce content that transforms passive viewing into an active and fun experience.

The platform provides software tools for creators to build participatory shows and serves as a single distribution channel for families. By empowering creators to make enriching, interactive content, Hellosaurus supports early childhood education principles that emphasize the importance of play for cognitive and social development.

Hellosaurus was acquired, marking a significant milestone for the company's mission to redefine kids' screen time.

_Usage:_

"How does Hellosaurus differ from standard streaming services for kids?"

"Instead of just streaming pre-recorded video, Hellosaurus provides a platform where kids can actively play along with the stories. It offers specific software tools for creators to build these participatory shows, turning the viewing experience from passive consumption into an interactive activity."
