Hera is an AI motion designer that transforms five‑hour animation projects into five‑minute tasks. It was launched in May 2025 and quickly attracted 100 k waitlist sign‑ups within eight weeks. By focusing on the 95 % of motion‑graphics work that consists of manual button clicking, Hera automates the tedious steps while preserving full creative control for users such as YouTubers creating infographic animations. The Berlin‑based team of five has been doubling revenue each month, positioning Hera as a fast‑growing B2B tool for engineering, product, and design teams.

_Usage:_

"Does Hera replace Adobe After Effects for creating infographic animations?"

"Hera doesn’t replace the creative capabilities of After Effects but automates the repetitive button‑clicking steps. For a typical YouTuber, the same animation that would take hours in After Effects can be produced in minutes with Hera, while still allowing the user to control the visual outcome."
