Hey Revia is a voice AI that handles complex phone calls for healthcare providers. The software automates menu navigation, reduces hold times, and takes care of tedious tasks such as verifying provider details, updating insurance information, and securing approvals.

It is offered as a web app, mobile app, and API, allowing integration into existing provider workflows. The company, based in San Francisco and staffed by four employees, was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Hey Revia handle complex menu navigation compared to a traditional IVR system?"

"Hey Revia uses voice AI to automatically navigate complex phone menus, reducing hold times and eliminating the need for manual interaction, unlike a standard IVR that requires users to press numbers."
