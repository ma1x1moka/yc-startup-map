**Website:** https://heydoctor.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/heydoctor

HeyDoctor provides instant online primary care, letting patients get medical care in five minutes with no insurance or video calls. Most visits cost under $20.
The platform offers a range of services, including acne diagnosis, high cholesterol testing, birth‑control refills, UTI testing, vitamin D screening, pre‑diabetes screening, hepatitis C screening, female condom prescriptions, smoking cessation support, and blood‑type identification.
HeyDoctor was acquired, bringing its telehealth platform into a larger portfolio. The company operates from San Francisco and employs about 50 staff.
