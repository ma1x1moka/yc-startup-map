**Website:** https://hilos.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/quid

Hilos provides a platform built on the WhatsApp API that enables startups and small‑to‑medium businesses to profile leads, automate follow‑ups, and integrate with tools such as CRMs, databases, and payment providers. The service is designed to run a business on WhatsApp without writing code, allowing companies to build apps directly inside the messaging app.

The company was acquired after its Summer 2021 Y Combinator batch, highlighting the market interest in low‑code messaging solutions for B2B marketing and productivity. With a five‑person team based in Mexico City and remote workers, Hilos serves the B2B and marketing industries as a SaaS messaging platform.

_Usage:_

"Is Hilos just another wrapper around the WhatsApp Business API, or does it add unique capabilities?"

"Hilos adds a no‑code layer that lets businesses profile leads, automate follow‑ups, and connect WhatsApp conversations to external systems like CRMs, databases, and payment providers, which goes beyond a simple API wrapper."
