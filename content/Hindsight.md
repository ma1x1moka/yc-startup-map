**Website:** https://www.usehindsight.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hindsight

Hindsight offers an AI‑powered platform that helps sales teams understand why they win or lose deals and turn those insights into more wins. The service is used by companies such as Ironclad, LaunchDarkly and Klaviyo to build workflows on top of their go‑to‑market data. Its features include building evergreen battlecards, objection‑handling guides and case studies, generating AI role‑plays from real sales conversations, and analyzing thousands of deals to produce reports and alerts for GTM, product, and strategy teams. The company operates out of New York City with a three‑person team focused on B2B sales enablement and analytics.

_Usage:_

"Do the battlecards and objection guides stay current automatically?"

"Hindsight builds battlecards, objection‑handling guides, and case studies that never go out of date, so the content is refreshed automatically as new deal data is processed."
