**Website:** https://hippo.build | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hippobuild

HippoBuild builds an operating system for the construction industry, automating back office functions and the supply chain for small and medium construction companies. It saves customers hours everyday and thousands of dollars per month on better sourced materials, with onboarding taking only minutes.

The platform charges a small SaaS fee plus a 1% transaction fee on sourced materials. It integrates with multiple marketplaces and providers to automatically source better priced materials, keeping the service affordable for SMBs while generating $20-100K per company in revenue.

Founded in Winter 2022, HippoBuild operates with a team of five from Bogotá, Colombia and remote locations. It positions itself as a SaaS solution in the construction and real estate industry.
