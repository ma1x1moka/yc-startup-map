**Website:** https://hirebolt.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hirebolt

Hirebolt provides access to a global network of verified engineers, AI-augmented teams, and expert AI evaluators. The platform utilizes instant search capabilities to help organizations find and interview talent in seconds.

Based in London, England, the company operates in the B2B and Human Resources sectors, focusing on recruiting and HR tech. It has raised approximately $1.5M from investors including Y Combinator and Entrepreneur First.

_Usage:_

"How does Hirebolt differ from traditional recruiting platforms?"

"Hirebolt integrates AI-augmented teams and expert AI evaluators into the process, enabling instant search that allows users to start finding and interviewing talent in seconds rather than relying solely on manual sourcing or static job boards."
