**Website:** https://www.hitpayapp.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hitpay

HitPay provides a unified payments platform for growing businesses in the Asia-Pacific region, consolidating e-commerce, point-of-sale, and B2B payment methods into a single account. The platform supports a wide range of local rails, including cards, PayNow, QR codes, and digital wallets, allowing merchants to accept, send, and reconcile funds across the region seamlessly.

Headquartered in Singapore and founded in 2016, the company is regulated across six markets and serves more than 15,000 businesses. By streamlining these diverse payment channels, HitPay has reportedly saved small and medium enterprises over US$35 million in fees. The company maintains a team of 42 and is backed by investors such as Tiger Global, Y Combinator, Global Founders Capital, and HOF Capital.

_Usage:_

"How does HitPay handle the complexity of different payment methods across APAC markets?"

"It unifies various local rails like cards, PayNow, QR, and digital wallets into one platform, allowing businesses to manage e-commerce, POS, and B2B payments from a single account rather than integrating each method separately."
