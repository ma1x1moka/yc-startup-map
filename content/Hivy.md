Hivy is a task‑management tool designed for office operations teams and serves as a helpdesk system for employees. The platform lets office managers collect employee requests, manage projects, assign tasks, and automatically notify employees when their requests are completed. Hivy was acquired by Managed by Q in 2017, reflecting its value in the B2B office‑management space.

_Usage:_

"What capabilities does Hivy offer that differentiate it from a standard project‑management tool?"

"Hivy combines typical project‑management features—like task assignment and project tracking—with a built‑in employee helpdesk, allowing office managers to collect service requests and automatically notify staff when those requests are fulfilled."
