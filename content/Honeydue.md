**Website:** https://www.honeydue.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/honeydue

Honeydue is a personal finance application built specifically for couples to manage their money together. The platform allows partners to track upcoming bills, monitor bank balances, and review shared spending in a single place.

By centralizing accounts and tracking, Honeydue encourages partners to have open dialogues about their spending habits and long-term financial goals. Part of the Summer 2017 Y Combinator cohort, the company has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Honeydue help partners manage joint finances?"

"Honeydue aggregates bank balances, bills, and spending activity into a shared app for both partners. It provides visibility into overall household finances so couples can stay coordinated on joint spending and track their financial goals together."
