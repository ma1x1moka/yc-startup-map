**Website:** https://hoss.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hoss

Hoss is a developer experience platform designed to help API-driven teams deliver superior customer experiences. It provides tools for creating high-quality documentation and developer content, while offering deep visibility into how customers utilize their APIs. The platform also includes features for monitoring third-party APIs and building customizable developer dashboards.

The company has been trusted by API-focused businesses such as Xendit, AssemblyAI, and Zentail to improve customer acquisition and support. Backed by investors including Y Combinator, Liquid2, FundersClub, and Soma Capital, Hoss operates out of Austin, TX, and remotely. Hoss was acquired, marking a notable exit for the company.

_Usage:_

"How does Hoss help API companies understand their customers better?"

"Hoss provides deep visibility into how customers are using your API, allowing teams to track usage patterns and monitor third-party dependencies. It also supports the creation of robust documentation and customizable dashboards, which helps in both acquiring new developers and supporting existing ones."
