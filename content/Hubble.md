**Website:** https://hubble.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hubble-ai

Hubble's mission is to unify the patient medical record and remove the barriers to accessing it. Today, a person's health history is scattered across disconnected systems, leaving patients and the people helping them to piece it together. Hubble is building a future where one authorization unlocks a more complete record, regardless of where someone received care.

Its founders previously built healthcare and AI products at Grow Therapy and Amazon One Medical, and it is backed by Y Combinator, Madrona, and Basis Set. It is building its team in Seattle, with the opportunity to work directly with the founders and shape how people access and use their health information.
