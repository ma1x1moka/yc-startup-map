Hubla is the Shopify for creators, enabling anyone to build, manage, and grow their online business. It offers powerful tools to create courses, communities, sell products, manage memberships, and deliver content—all in one platform. For example, a fitness coach can charge her audience for a unique digital weight‑loss program, turning passion into profit.

Hubla’s fully customizable platform lets creators own their strategy, stand out from competitors, and create unique experiences. Its top creator earns over $8M annually, illustrating the platform’s ability to scale creators’ businesses. With a team of 86 based in São Paulo, Hubla operates in the B2B and sales space, combining fintech, SaaS, and payments to power the creator economy.

_Usage:_

"How does Hubla enable creators to monetize their content?"

"Hubla provides integrated payment processing, allowing creators to charge for courses, subscriptions, and products directly through the platform. It also offers tools for membership management and content delivery, making it easier for creators to monetize and scale their businesses."
