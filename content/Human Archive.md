**Website:** https://www.humanarchive.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/human-archive

Human Archive builds data infrastructure for physical AI. Designs hardware, deploys cameras and sensors globally, and trains models that help AI understand how humans interact with the physical world.

Leading frontier AI labs trust them for video, sensor, robot deployment, image, and audio datasets. Team of 100 with offices in SF, China, and India. Researchers from NYU GRAIL, UPenn GRASP, UC Berkeley, Stanford, and Johns Hopkins Robotics.

_Usage:_

"What does Human Archive actually do to capture data for physical AI?"

"Rather than just processing third-party data, they build the full hardware and software pipeline: they design their own hardware, deploy cameras and sensors globally, and train models focused on how humans interact with the physical world. This allows them to supply frontier AI labs with video, sensor, audio, image, and robot deployment datasets."
