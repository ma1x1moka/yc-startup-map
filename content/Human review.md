---
description: A person reading the code the agent produced and forming a judgement on it. Reading the diff counts; reading the summary does not.
---

The distinction is the whole point. An [agent](./Agent.md)'s summary of its work is written by the same process that wrote the work, and shares its blind spots — a confident description of a change that does not do what it says will read perfectly well.

Human review is the most expensive verification available and the only one that catches problems no check encodes and no reviewer agent thinks to look for. Spending it where blast radius is largest, rather than spreading it evenly, is what makes it sustainable.

_Usage:_

"I read what it said it did."

"That's not review. Read the diff."
