---
description: A working pattern where a person stays alongside the agent during a session — reviewing, redirecting and collaborating in real time.
---

The human is part of the loop rather than a reviewer at the end. Course corrections happen within a [turn](./Turn.md) or two of going wrong, which keeps errors cheap and keeps the [agent](./Agent.md)'s [context](./Context.md) aligned with what you actually want.

It costs your attention continuously, which is the real constraint. It suits work where being wrong is expensive or where the target is still moving — and it is largely wasted on mechanical changes a check could verify.

_Usage:_

"Do I need to sit with this one?"

"It's touching auth. Stay in the loop."
