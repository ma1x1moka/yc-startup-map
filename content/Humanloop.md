**Website:** https://humanloop.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/humanloop

Humanloop provides an evaluation platform designed for enterprises building reliable AI products. The company offers tools for prompt management, evaluation, and observability to help engineering teams adopt best practices in machine learning deployment. Notable users of the platform include Gusto, Vanta, and Duolingo.

Based in San Francisco, the company operated with a team of 14 employees before being acquired. It focused on the B2B sector, specifically serving engineering and product design teams working with generative AI and machine learning systems.

_Usage:_

"How does Humanloop differ from general-purpose logging tools when working with LLMs?"

"Humanloop is specialized for LLM-specific concerns like prompt management and evaluation, rather than just generic logging. It allows teams to systematically test and monitor model performance to ensure reliability before shipping, which is distinct from standard observability stacks that might not capture the nuances of prompt engineering or output quality metrics."
