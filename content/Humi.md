**Website:** http://www.humi.ca | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/humi

Humi builds software that combines payroll, human resources, and health benefits into a single platform tailored for Canadian small and medium-sized businesses. Based in Toronto, the company provides essential employment infrastructure designed to simplify how Canadian companies manage their workforces.

The platform serves thousands of Canadian businesses, with a client roster that includes companies such as Ada, Hubdoc, LiveCA, AudioBooks.com, and Inkbox. Humi has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"What operational features does Humi integrate into its platform?"

"Humi combines payroll processing, human resources management, and employee health benefits into a single platform tailored specifically for Canadian regulations and businesses. Instead of managing separate tools for pay, benefits administration, and HR records, Canadian SMBs can handle all three through one interface."
