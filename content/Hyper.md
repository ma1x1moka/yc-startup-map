**Website:** https://hyper.co | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hyper

Hyper provides a SaaS platform that lets Discord server owners sell memberships to their community. By handling payments and subscription management, it simplifies monetization for creators without requiring custom integration. The company was acquired after its launch in the Summer 2021 batch.

With a team of six engineers based in San Francisco and Palo Alto, Hyper operates at the intersection of fintech and community platforms, offering a streamlined payment experience for Discord‑based businesses.

_Usage:_

"Does Hyper replace a traditional payment processor like Stripe for Discord membership sales?"

"Yes, Hyper acts as a turnkey solution for Discord server owners, handling the payment processing and membership management so creators don’t need to integrate a separate processor such as Stripe themselves."
