**Website:** https://hyperdx.io/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hyperdx

HyperDX is an open-source observability tool designed to help engineers resolve production bugs quickly by correlating logs, metrics, traces, and browser session replays in a single interface. The company, based in San Francisco, focuses on simple workflows that move users from alert to resolution, allowing for setup in a matter of minutes.

Built on top of ClickHouse and S3, HyperDX offers a cost-efficient alternative to traditional vendors like Datadog, claiming ten times the savings for customers. The startup was part of Y Combinator's Summer 2022 batch and has since been acquired.

"How does HyperDX compare to Datadog in terms of cost and architecture?"
