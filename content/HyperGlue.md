**Website:** https://www.hyperglue.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hyperglue

HyperGlue provides machine intelligence for large text datasets, allowing operators, product managers, and analysts to access insights without the engineering overhead of deploying natural language AI in-house. The platform addresses the challenge of sifting through massive volumes of feedback and text to identify signal amidst noise. The company was acquired, marking a notable exit for this San Francisco-based team focused on B2B analytics.

_Usage:_

"How does HyperGlue handle the engineering burden of NLP deployment?"

"It lets users access machine intelligence on large text datasets instantly, removing the need for the engineering resources or additional headcount typically required to deploy natural language AI within a business."
