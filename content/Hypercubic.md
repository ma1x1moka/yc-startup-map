Hypercubic is an AI-native platform for mainframe operations and modernization. About 70% of Fortune 500 companies still rely on mainframes built in the 1960s–90s to run core business applications in banking, insurance, telecom, airlines, and retail.

These systems have become increasingly opaque as original developers retire. Hypercubic pioneers a new class of intelligence capable of understanding, reasoning about, and rebuilding the world's foundational infrastructure.
