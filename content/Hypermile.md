**Website:** https://hypermile.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hypermile

Hypermile develops an AI driver assistance system designed to improve the efficiency, sustainability, and safety of trucking operations. The company addresses the high cost of fuel, which accounts for over a third of logistics operating costs, by deploying a retrofittable AI cruise control called Hypermile Co-Pilot. This system learns optimal driving techniques, such as anticipating traffic flow and maximizing vehicle coasting, to reduce diesel consumption by 11% and extend the range of battery-electric vehicles by 15%.

Based in London, the team of 11 engineers and product specialists builds hardware and software solutions for heavy goods vehicles. The company has received R&D grants from Innovate UK, the Department for Transport, and Horizons 2020. Its long-term vision includes building higher levels of autonomy, with the next milestone being a Level-3 autonomous trucking solution.

Hypermile was acquired, marking a significant exit for the company founded in May 2020.

_Usage:_

"How does Hypermile Co-Pilot differ from standard adaptive cruise control systems in terms of fuel savings?"

"Unlike standard adaptive cruise control, which primarily maintains a safe distance from the vehicle ahead, Hypermile’s AI system actively optimizes speed based on road gradient, traffic flow anticipation, and coasting opportunities. This approach allows the system to reduce diesel consumption by 11% and extend the range of electric trucks by 15%, addressing the specific inefficiencies in heavy goods vehicle operations that standard systems do not target."
