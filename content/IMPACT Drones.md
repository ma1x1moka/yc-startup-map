IMPACT Drones develops autonomous interceptor drones designed to defend critical civilian infrastructure, including data centers, power grids, airports, and military bases. The system operates without on-site crew; interceptors are stored in sealed containers at the protected site and launch automatically when sensors detect a hostile drone. The drones use onboard vision for guidance, allowing them to function effectively even when GPS signals are jammed.

The company positions its solution as a cost-effective alternative to legacy air defense systems, which can cost over $1 million per shot against threats that cost only $30,000. IMPACT’s interceptors are designed to cost less than the threat they neutralize and scale with swarm tactics, similar to systems currently downing Shahed drones in Ukraine. By offering a subscription model that includes hardware, reloads, and monitoring, the company provides Air Defense as a Service rather than requiring sites to purchase expensive missile systems.

Based in San Francisco, the company has secured over $180 million in signed letters of intent. It is a two-person team operating in the defense and industrials sectors, focusing on autonomous hardware and swarm robotics.
