Icarus is building the modern-day U-2 spy plane. Autonomous solar aircraft fly at 60,000 feet for weeks at a time — above weather and below satellites — delivering persistent intelligence and connectivity for the military at a fraction of the cost of existing systems.

The stratosphere is the forgotten frontier: above commercial air traffic, below satellites, and currently essentially unoccupied. Team of 50. Los Angeles. The defense application is ISR (Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance) at dramatically lower cost than traditional satellites.

_Usage:_

"Why operate in the stratosphere instead of using traditional satellites?"

"Flying autonomous solar aircraft at 60,000 feet places them in the largely unoccupied stratosphere—above commercial air traffic and weather, but below satellites. This allows them to stay airborne for weeks at a time to provide persistent military ISR and connectivity at a fraction of the cost of traditional satellite systems."
