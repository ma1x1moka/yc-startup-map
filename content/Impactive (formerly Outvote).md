**Website:** https://www.impactive.io/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/impactive

Impactive, originally founded in 2017 as Outvote with support from Y Combinator and Higher Ground Labs, developed a distributed organizing platform for political and civic campaigns. Initially created as a get-out-the-vote tool for Democrats, the product grew into a full-service suite that allows progressive causes, campaigns, and nonprofits to engage supporters directly.

The platform demonstrated significant scale during major election cycles. In the 2018 midterms, volunteers contacted over 1 million voters through the platform, and by 2020, volunteers had sent over 100 million text messages. Impactive served over 500 clients nationwide, including Planned Parenthood, the DCCC, SEIU, Community Change, When We All Vote, and Greenpeace.

Operating with a remote team based out of Boston, Impactive has since been acquired.
