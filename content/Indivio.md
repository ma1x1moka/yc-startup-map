**Website:** https://www.indiv.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/indivio

Indivio provides video creative automation capabilities designed for modern marketing teams. Its platform enables users to easily deliver dynamic, personalized video experiences tailored to improve business outcomes and customer engagement.

Part of the Y Combinator Summer 2017 batch, the company operated across San Francisco, Redwood City, and remote environments, focusing on video personalization and marketing analytics. Indivio has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Indivio automate dynamic video creation for marketing campaigns?"

"Indivio uses creative automation software that allows marketing teams to generate personalized, dynamic video assets without manually editing every variation. The platform combines video automation with analytics to help marketers deliver targeted content and measure performance."
