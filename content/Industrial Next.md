**Website:** https://www.industrialnext.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industrial-next

IndustrialNext develops the AI brain for the factory floor, focusing on autonomous robotic systems designed for contact-rich manufacturing tasks where conventional automation struggles. The company leverages a proprietary foundation model to bring modern autonomy to industrial settings, aiming to create factories that are cost-competitive at any scale.

Founded by veterans from Tesla, Waymo, Ford, and GM, the team applies their experience in building autonomous systems to the manufacturing sector. Based in San Francisco, the nine-person startup is backed by Y Combinator, Khosla Ventures, Lenovo, AlphaX Partners, and Plug and Play.

_Usage:_

"How does IndustrialNext handle manufacturing tasks that traditional automation can't do?"

"It uses a proprietary foundation model to power robotic systems capable of performing difficult, contact-rich tasks. This approach allows for autonomous factory operations that remain cost-competitive regardless of scale, addressing gaps left by conventional automation methods."
