Inflow is a consumer health application designed to help individuals manage ADHD. It provides support through a combination of psychoeducation, habit development tools, and community features. The company operates with a team of approximately 20 people across offices in London and New York.

Inflow was acquired, marking a significant milestone for the mental health tech sector. Its approach focuses on practical self-management strategies rather than clinical intervention alone, targeting users seeking structured support for their attentional challenges.

_Usage:_

"How does Inflow differ from general productivity apps for ADHD?"

"Inflow is specifically tailored for ADHD management, focusing on psychoeducation and habit development within a supportive community context, rather than just offering generic task management or focus timers."
