Innov8 is a Y Combinator-backed workspace and community startup based in India that provides premium workspaces, including managed private offices and co-working spaces. It positions itself as a "Thought space" designed to encourage community engagement among founders, investors, freelancers, and creators who seek a highly productive environment.

The company has expanded its presence with centers in Delhi, Noida, Gurgaon, Mumbai, Bangalore, and Chandigarh. Innov8 was acquired, marking a notable exit for this YC-backed real estate and construction venture.

"Innov8 focuses on creating a community-driven "Thought space" rather than just providing square footage. It targets a mix of founders, investors, and freelancers, emphasizing a premium work culture and ergonomic design to foster engagement among like-minded individuals across its multiple Indian locations."
