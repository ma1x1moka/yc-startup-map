**Website:** https://instaagent.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/instaagent

InstaAgent helps B2C companies scale a single campaign across hundreds of specific audiences on Meta and TikTok, each with its own content, voice, and targeting.
It builds personalized creatives from a single brief, distributes them through paid channels and niche social accounts, and uses performance data to learn what works, scale winners, and improve every campaign.
With a team of 10 in San Francisco, the startup has achieved a $1M annual run rate in just 10 months, demonstrating rapid traction in the marketing tech space.

_Usage:_

"How does InstaAgent create personalized creatives for hundreds of personas from a single brief?"

"It takes a single brief and automatically generates distinct content, voice, and targeting for each persona, then distributes those creatives across paid Meta and TikTok channels and niche social accounts, using performance data to identify winners and refine the campaign."
