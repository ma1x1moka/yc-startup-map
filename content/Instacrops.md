**Website:** https://www.instacrops.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/instacrops

Instacrops operates a dashboard platform that combines data from IoT sensors, satellites, and drones to provide detailed analytics on farm health. The company is headquartered in Santiago, Chile, and employs a team of 34 people focused on bringing data-driven decision-making to the agricultural sector.

The platform is currently live on more than 300 farms across Latin America, where it has helped farmers increase their crop yields by an average of 12%. Instacrops has reached over $200,000 in monthly revenue with growth rates of 2X year-over-year.

The company targets the $50 billion agricultural market in Latin America, which encompasses 2.9 billion acres of farmland. By integrating physical hardware with remote sensing data, Instacrops aims to optimize farming operations across the region.

_Usage:_

"How does Instacrops actually measure crop health in the field?"

"It uses a combination of physical IoT sensors placed directly in the field alongside external data from satellites and drones. This multi-source approach allows the dashboard to provide comprehensive analytics on farm health, which users report leads to an average 12% increase in crop yield."
