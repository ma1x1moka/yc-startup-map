Intellect is the global leader in hyperlocalised mental healthcare, serving 4 million lives across enterprise employers, insurers, healthcare systems, and governments in over 50 languages and 100 countries. It delivers a comprehensive AI‑powered care ecosystem that combines digital therapeutics, virtual therapy, high‑acuity crisis support, and specialty clinical treatment through proprietary digital platforms and its own mental health clinics. Headquartered in Singapore with nine global offices and a team of 200, Intellect is backed by investors such as Tiger Global, Y Combinator, Insignia Ventures and HOF Capital, and is trusted by hundreds of Fortune 500 companies and other leading organisations.
