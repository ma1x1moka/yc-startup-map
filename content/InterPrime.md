InterPrime offers safe and secure cash management for businesses, allowing companies to manage money like a Fortune 500 company whether they are preparing for an IPO or still in a garage. It addresses the pain of dealing with financial institutions and ensures corporate cash is kept safe while earning a reasonable rate of return.

The platform helps companies keep cash safe, earn a reasonable rate of return, and assure investors that capital is responsibly stewarded. With a small team of three based in San Francisco and Menlo Park, InterPrime was acquired, cementing its position in the fintech space.
