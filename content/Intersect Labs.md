Intersect Labs is a collaborative data notebook that lets business teams bring data from various sources, process it, and send it to a destination of their choice, repeating the workflow in a few clicks daily, weekly, or monthly.

The tool targets B2B engineering, product, and design teams and was built by a small team of four in San Diego, CA. Intersect Labs was acquired, marking its success in the analytics space.

_Usage:_

"How often can I schedule data pipelines in Intersect Labs?"

"You can repeat the workflow in a few clicks every day, week, or month."
