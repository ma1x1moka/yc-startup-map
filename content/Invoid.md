Invoid is a B2B SaaS that creates identity verification workflows tailored to each client. The platform processes more than 5 million KYC checks per month and serves over 35 companies across India, including leading banks, fintechs, investment platforms, and shared-economy services. By leveraging machine-learning techniques, it boosts onboarding speed fivefold and reduces verification costs by over 80%.

The company was founded in the Winter 2021 batch and operated from Gurugram, Haryana, India, with a team of 15. Invoid is now inactive, having shut down after its run in the Indian market.
