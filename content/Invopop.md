**Website:** https://www.invopop.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/invopop

Invopop enables global software firms such as SumUp, Fever, and DeepL to manage electronic invoicing across more than 25 jurisdictions through one unified API. By connecting once to Invopop, companies avoid the complexity of building and maintaining separate local integrations for each tax authority, streamlining compliance and saving time. The service is positioned for a market where, in the next five years, over 40 countries—including all EU members—will mandate real‑time electronic invoicing and reporting.

With a team of seven based in Madrid, Invopop targets B2B finance and accounting needs, helping firms meet upcoming regulatory requirements without extensive in‑house development.

_Usage:_

"How does using Invopop compare to building our own country‑specific invoicing integrations?"

"Invopop offers a single API that already supports electronic invoicing and real‑time tax reporting for more than 25 countries, so you only need one integration instead of maintaining dozens of separate connections. This reduces development effort and ongoing maintenance, letting you comply with upcoming EU mandates without building each local solution yourself."
