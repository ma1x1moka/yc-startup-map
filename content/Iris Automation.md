Iris Automation developed AI software designed to enable autonomous operations for industrial drones globally. The company focused on improving safety by implementing intelligent collision avoidance systems. Its team included engineers and PhDs in computer vision with backgrounds from NASA and Boeing.

The company was based in San Francisco and operated remotely. It was a Y Combinator and Silicon Valley investor-backed startup. Iris Automation has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Iris Automation's approach to drone safety differ from standard manual piloting?"

"It uses AI and computer vision to provide intelligent collision avoidance, allowing for safer autonomous operation rather than relying solely on manual piloting or basic sensors."
