**Website:** https://jasper.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/jasper-ai

Jasper.ai launched in February 2021 as an AI content platform that serves creators and companies across industries. It powers over 105,000 active customers to overcome writer’s block, repackage existing material, produce original artwork, and adjust language and tone for different audiences. By teaching users how to treat artificial intelligence as a creative partner, Jasper positions itself at the forefront of the emerging content generation space. The company operates from Austin and Salt Lake City with a team of 195 employees, reflecting its rapid growth in the B2B generative AI market.
