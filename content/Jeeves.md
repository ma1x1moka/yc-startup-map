**Website:** http://www.tryjeeves.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/jeeves

Jeeves is an all-in-one corporate spend and expense management platform designed for businesses operating in North America, Latin America, Europe, and the UK. The company operates on proprietary infrastructure that allows firms to establish their finance functions within minutes, supporting cash, corporate cards, and cross-border payments. It handles multiple currencies for payback and supports operations in regions including Brazil, Canada, Colombia, Mexico, the EU, the UK, and the US.

The platform combines several tools, including Jeeves Pay for international transfers to over 150 countries with same-day arrival capabilities, Jeeves Cards for physical and virtual global spending, and built-in expense management features. These tools are offered without monthly or software fees, and the service is trusted by more than 3,000 companies worldwide.

Jeeves has raised $380 million from investors including Andreessen Horowitz, CRV, GIC, Tencent, Silicon Valley Bank, Stanford University, and Y Combinator to fuel its international expansion. The company currently employs 150 people and maintains offices in Miami and New York, alongside a remote workforce.

_Usage:_

"How does Jeeves handle cross-border payments compared to traditional banking?"

"Jeeves uses proprietary infrastructure to facilitate cross-border payments to over 150 countries, with transfers between North America, South America, and Europe arriving as quickly as the same day. It offers competitive FX rates and allows companies to spend and pay in their preferred currencies, eliminating the need for personal guarantees or long-term contracts."
