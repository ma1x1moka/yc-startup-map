**Website:** https://jemi.so | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/jemi

Jemi is a San Francisco-based startup that helps creators and entrepreneurs grow their online presence by building beautiful websites and online stores. The platform is designed for users with no coding or design background, allowing them to launch professional digital storefronts easily.

The company operated with a small team of three, working remotely and from San Francisco. It focused on the consumer and social sectors, providing a SaaS solution for the entertainment and community spaces.

Jemi was acquired, marking the end of its independent operation as a YC-backed startup.

_Usage:_

"How does Jemi differ from standard website builders like Wix or Squarespace?"

"Jemi specifically targets creators and entrepreneurs who want to build both a website and an online store without any technical or design expertise. While many builders offer similar no-code features, Jemi positions itself around helping these specific user groups grow their online presence through a unified, beautiful interface."
