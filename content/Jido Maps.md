**Website:** https://jidomaps.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/jido-maps

Jido Maps provides a platform that lets teams with little machine‑learning expertise convert visual or scanned data into production‑ready computer‑vision models. It offers a rapid proof‑of‑concept workflow that can be delivered in under a week. The service is used for security‑camera monitoring, automated data entry, interpreting web‑scraped images, validated user photo inputs, augmented reality, and mobile product scanning, positioning it as an infrastructure layer for B2B customers. Jido Maps was acquired and operates from San Francisco with a remote team of six engineers.

_Usage:_

"How is Jido Maps different from building my own computer‑vision model with open‑source tools?"

"Jido Maps handles the entire pipeline—data ingestion, model training, and deployment—so you can get a working model in under a week without needing machine‑learning expertise, whereas using open‑source libraries requires you to assemble and manage each step yourself."
