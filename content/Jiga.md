**Website:** https://jiga.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/jiga

Jiga enables engineers and supply chain managers to source high-quality, cost-competitive custom parts by partnering directly with vetted manufacturers. The platform is designed to accelerate the procurement process for complex components, removing traditional intermediaries to improve speed and cost efficiency.

The company serves a diverse client base that includes major organizations such as NASA, Siemens, Tesla, and Google. By connecting these large-scale industrial and tech companies directly with manufacturing partners, Jiga addresses the specific needs of high-stakes supply chain operations.

Founded in the Winter 2021 batch, Jiga operates as a B2B supply chain and logistics platform. The team consists of 25 members based in Tel Aviv, Israel, and working remotely.
