**Website:** https://jinba.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/jinba

Jinba lets enterprise teams create AI workflows by describing what they need in plain language, replacing traditional drag‑and‑drop tools. The platform supports "vibe‑code" style development, allowing any employee to start using a workflow immediately.

The service includes enterprise‑grade permissions, audit logging, and on‑premises deployment, catering to the security and compliance needs of large organizations. Jinba currently serves 40,000 users at major financial institutions, demonstrating its adoption in the B2B sector.

With a small team of two, Jinba focuses on delivering a seamless, code‑free workflow automation experience for large enterprises.

_Usage:_

"How is Jinba different from a typical drag‑and‑drop workflow builder?"

"Jinba replaces the visual drag‑and‑drop interface with a chat‑based, natural‑language approach, so users can describe a workflow in plain text and have it generated automatically, eliminating the need for engineers to build or maintain the workflow."
