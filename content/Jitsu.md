**Website:** https://jitsu.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/jitsu

Jitsu is an open-source customer data platform (CDP) designed to collect event data from diverse sources, including web, app, email, chatbots, and CRMs, and route it directly into a data warehouse. The company describes its solution as the fastest and most durable method for this data ingestion, emphasizing security and rapid deployment capabilities.

Founded in the Summer 2020 batch, the New York City-based team of four engineers built Jitsu as a 100% open-source alternative to proprietary analytics infrastructure. Its primary focus is on B2B analytics and data engineering, allowing companies to establish a secure, purpose-built data pipeline in minutes.

Jitsu has since been acquired by Helium Ventures, marking a significant milestone for the open-source analytics tool.

_Usage:_

"How does Jitsu handle data ingestion compared to traditional closed-source CDPs?"

"Jitsu operates as a 100% open-source CDP that ingests event data from various sources like web, apps, and CRMs directly into your existing data warehouse. Unlike proprietary platforms, it is designed to be purpose-built and secure, allowing you to set up the pipeline in minutes without vendor lock-in, which is particularly useful for teams that want full control over their data engineering stack."
