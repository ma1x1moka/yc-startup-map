Co-founded [Compound](./Compound.md) with [Jacob Schein](./Jacob%20Schein.md), whom he met at Washington University in St. Louis, building a wealth management platform for tech employees to manage equity, invest, and get financial advice — part of YC's 2019 class. Compound later merged with Alternativ Wealth to form Compound Planning, a digital family office managing roughly $1.1B in assets.
