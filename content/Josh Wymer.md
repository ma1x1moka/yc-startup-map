Co-founded [Central](./Central.md) as CEO in April 2023 with [Pranav Kashyap](./Pranav%20Kashyap.md) and [Nilay Modi](./Nilay%20Modi.md), part of YC's Summer 2024 batch, consolidating payroll, benefits, state compliance, accounting, and R&D tax credits for startups. Wymer was the 12th employee at Mixpanel, where he ran product engineering and helped scale it past a $1B valuation, and also worked as a software engineer at Meta and played baseball at Virginia Tech. Central was later acquired by Mercury.

_Usage:_

"What happened to Central?"

"It was acquired by Mercury, folding its back-office automation for payroll, benefits, and compliance into Mercury's platform for startups."
