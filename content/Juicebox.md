**Website:** https://juicebox.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/juicebox

Juicebox is an AI-powered talent sourcing platform designed to help teams navigate the competitive landscape of modern recruiting. By leveraging generative AI, it assists recruiters in identifying and engaging candidates, addressing the scarcity of human ingenuity in the AI era. The company positions itself as a tool for winning the zero-sum game of talent acquisition.

The platform serves a diverse client base ranging from early-stage startups to Fortune 500 companies, with over 3,000 customers. Notable users include Ramp, Perplexity, and leading AI labs. Juicebox has achieved significant commercial traction, crossing $10M in ARR with monthly growth rates exceeding 20%.

Headquartered in Los Angeles, the company has a team of 65 members. It has raised $36M in total funding, including a $30M Series A led by Sequoia Capital, with additional investment from Coatue, NFDG, Lux Capital, Y Combinator, and BOND Capital.

_Usage:_

"How does Juicebox differ from traditional applicant tracking systems or manual sourcing tools?"

"Juicebox uses generative AI to automate and power the talent sourcing process, allowing recruiters to run thousands of searches daily. Unlike static databases or manual outreach, it is designed to actively help teams compete for scarce talent, which is why it is used by high-growth companies like Ramp and Perplexity alongside thousands of other customers."
