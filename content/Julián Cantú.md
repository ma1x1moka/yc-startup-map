Co-founded [Eden](./Eden.md) with [Raymundo Gonzalez](./Raymundo%20Gonzalez.md), building the leading AI-and-cloud-native Diagnostic OS in Latin America, serving over 2,600 medical institutions across 18 countries. Cantú studied Physics Engineering at Tecnológico de Monterrey before dropping out to pursue health tech, later becoming a Thiel Fellow; he was inspired to found Eden after living through his mother's breast cancer diagnosis at age 8, which exposed him to the health system's diagnostic shortcomings.
