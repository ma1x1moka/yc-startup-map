**Website:** https://kaagaz.app | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/kaagaz

Kaagaz provides an office suite for mobile‑first businesses in India, enabling users to manage all document requirements on their smartphones without a desktop. It serves a growing user base of over 3 million users, including more than 60 000 paid customers, and is expanding each month. The product targets the 200 million‑plus business market in India, with a $15 per year subscription that represents a $3 billion opportunity domestically and a larger global market.

The company was founded by second‑time entrepreneurs who previously scaled a startup to $1 million in GMV. Kaagaz is backed by Y Combinator, Goodwater Capital, Pravega Venture, Better Capital, and other investors such as Alan Rutledge of TrendMD and Farid Ahsan of Sharechat. With a team of 11 employees based in Gurugram and New Delhi, Kaagaz continues to grow its product and user base.
