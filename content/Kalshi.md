**Website:** https://www.kalshi.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/kalshi

Kalshi is the first federally regulated exchange where people can trade on the outcome of events. It is regulated by the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) as the first legal futures exchange in the United States for a broad category of events.

The company has created a new asset class called “event contracts,” allowing traders to capitalize on everyday knowledge by expanding what can be traded. Kalshi’s platform enables users to buy and sell contracts tied to real‑world events, effectively turning opinions into tradable assets.

Based in New York City with a team of about 125 employees, Kalshi continues to develop its exchange and grow the event‑contract market.

_Usage:_

"How is trading on Kalshi different from trading traditional futures?"

"Kalshi trades event contracts that settle based on the outcome of real‑world events rather than price movements of commodities or financial instruments. Because the contracts are tied to discrete events, the market lets participants bet on things like election results or weather outcomes, and the exchange is regulated by the CFTC as a legal futures market."
