**Website:** https://kapacity.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/kapacity-io

Kapacity.io develops software for smart energy grids, specifically providing an electricity load balancing service designed for buildings. The company focuses on helping property owners and operators optimize their energy usage to lower both operational costs and carbon emissions.

Based in Helsinki, Finland, the team consists of seven members working within the industrials and energy sectors. Their platform addresses the challenge of managing peak demand and integrating renewable energy sources into building infrastructure.

The company was acquired, marking a significant milestone in the growing market for commercial energy optimization and climate-focused technology.

_Usage:_

"How does Kapacity.io's load balancing actually work for a building manager, and is it just another monitoring dashboard?"

"It goes beyond passive monitoring by actively managing electricity consumption to balance loads. Instead of just showing you where energy is being used, it intervenes to smooth out peaks and optimize usage patterns, which directly reduces the bill and the building's carbon footprint. It’s less about seeing the data and more about automating the response to grid conditions."
