**Website:** https://kashin.app/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/kashin

Kashin provides small loans to micro-merchants and individuals operating within the informal economy across Latin America. The company serves a user base of 10,000 people and generates nearly $2 million in annualized revenue through these lending services.

To manage risk in this underserved market, Kashin employs a proprietary AI risk model for underwriting. This system processes over 250 variables derived from user smartphones and credit bureau data to make lending decisions.

The team consists of 45 people and is based in Lima, Peru, with remote operations.

_Usage:_

"How does Kashin handle credit risk for borrowers who likely lack traditional credit histories?"

"It uses a proprietary AI risk model that analyzes more than 250 variables pulled from the user's smartphone and credit bureau data to perform underwriting, allowing it to assess creditworthiness in the informal economy where standard methods might fail."
