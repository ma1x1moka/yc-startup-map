Kernal Bio is a biotech company pioneering mRNA 2.0 immunotherapies. It leverages selective mRNA‑LNP technology to treat cancer and autoimmune diseases with precision, ensuring targeted cell delivery and minimal off‑target effects.

Its KR‑402 program achieves over 90% delivery efficiency with T‑cell‑targeted LNPs, enabling direct CAR‑T programming within the body and resulting in B‑cell depletion within a week. Kernal Bio holds three FDA‑approved therapies and more than 120 patents, and is backed by Hummingbird Ventures, Amgen Ventures, HBM, and NASA.

Based in Cambridge, MA, Kernal Bio’s team of 20 draws on experience from MIT, Harvard, Merck, and BMS. Leadership includes CEO Yusuf Erkul, President Burak Yilmaz, and Sr. VP of R&D Manfred Kraus, all of whom have a track record in drug discovery and immuno‑oncology.
