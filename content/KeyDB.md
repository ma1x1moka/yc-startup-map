**Website:** https://keydb.dev | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/keydb

KeyDB is a high-performance key-value database that unifies in-memory caching and flash persistence into a single package. It was designed to offer a versatile storage solution that eliminates the need to manage separate systems for caching and persistent data.

The company operated out of Toronto, Canada, with a small team of two engineers. KeyDB was open source and focused on the B2B infrastructure market, positioning itself as a comprehensive database solution.

KeyDB was acquired, marking the end of its independent operation as a YC-backed startup.

_Usage:_

"How does KeyDB differ from using separate Redis and disk-based storage?"

"KeyDB combines the speed of in-memory caching with the durability of flash persistence in one system, so you don't have to manage the complexity of syncing data between a cache layer and a persistent database."
