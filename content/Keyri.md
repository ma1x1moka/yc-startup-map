**Website:** https://keyri.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/keyri

Keyri provides a secure fraud prevention and authentication platform designed to help companies keep bad actors off their platforms while delighting users. By tying user identities to trusted devices through mobile application and browser fingerprinting, the company prevents fraudulent activities such as account takeovers, bot attacks, and referral abuse. It establishes an immutable device fingerprint for every device to alert businesses of suspicious activity during sign-up, log-in, or subsequent authentication events.

The platform also includes a passwordless authentication suite that leverages Passkeys, WebAuthn, and QR codes to enable biometric authentication across all platforms and devices. This approach strengthens multi-factor account security and reduces reliance on tedious or unsecure authentication methods.

Founded in the Summer 2021 batch, Keyri was acquired, marking its exit from the YC program.

_Usage:_

"How does Keyri's device fingerprinting differ from standard IP-based fraud checks?"

"Keyri creates an immutable device fingerprint that ties a user's identity to a specific trusted device, rather than relying on mutable IP addresses. This allows it to detect suspicious devices during sign-up or log-in events, which is more effective for preventing account takeovers and bot attacks than IP-based methods alone."
