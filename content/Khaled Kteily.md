Founded [Legacy](./Legacy.md) out of Harvard Innovation Labs in 2018, helping men test, improve, and freeze sperm through mail-in kits analyzed at a CLIA-certified andrology lab, going through YC's Summer 2019 batch. Kteily, a Canadian-Lebanese entrepreneur, holds a Bachelor of Commerce from McGill and a Master in Public Policy from Harvard Kennedy School on a full scholarship. Legacy has raised $45M from investors including celebrities Justin Bieber, DJ Khaled, The Weeknd, and Orlando Bloom.
