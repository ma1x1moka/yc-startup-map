**Website:** https://kidpass.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/kidpass

KidPass is an online platform that helps parents discover and book a wide range of children’s activities, from classes and camps to museum visits and exclusive events. It aggregates both online and in‑person experiences, giving families a single place to unlock new worlds of learning and fun.

KidPass participated in Y Combinator’s Winter 2017 batch, grew to a team of 21, and operated out of New York City with remote staff. KidPass was acquired, marking its recognition within the education sector.

_Usage:_

"Is KidPass just another event‑listing site, or does it provide a distinct service for parents?"

"KidPass is more than a simple list; it curates exclusive events, classes, camps, and museum experiences and lets parents book them directly through its platform, covering both online and in‑person options."
