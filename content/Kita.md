**Website:** https://www.kita.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/kita

In emerging markets, a borrower's financial history lives in documents: e-wallet records, bank statements, utility bills. Because this data is unstructured, credit teams are forced into manual review. Kita automates application completion, document verification, and underwriting from messy financial documents.

A learning engine: links document-level signals to repayment outcomes, allowing models to continuously improve fraud detection and risk assessment. Serves lenders in markets where banking APIs and open finance are still nascent.
