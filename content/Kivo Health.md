**Website:** https://kivohealth.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/kivo-health

Kivo Health provides AI-powered virtual care aimed at seniors managing chronic disease, initially focusing on lung health. The platform keeps patients out of the hospital and saves payers more than $8,000 per patient on average. It partners with major health systems such as Stanford, UCLA and Optum Health, and recently closed a $10 million Series A round while scaling its 40‑person team in San Francisco.

The company’s AI-driven approach targets cost reduction and improved outcomes in the senior population, positioning it as a notable player in digital health and telehealth services.

_Usage:_

"How does Kivo Health’s AI virtual care differ from standard telehealth services?"

"Kivo Health’s solution combines AI-driven monitoring and decision support specifically for seniors with chronic disease, starting with lung care, whereas typical telehealth platforms offer general video visits without that disease‑focused AI layer. This specialization helps keep patients out of the hospital and generates average savings of over $8,000 per patient for payers."
