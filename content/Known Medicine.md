**Website:** http://knownmed.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/known-medicine

Known Medicine combines patient-specific 3D cell culture with AI to translate drugs to clinical success. The company receives patient tumors, breaks them down, and creates thousands of micro-tumors to test various drug responses. By treating each micro-tumor with different drugs, it determines which treatments the patient will respond best to.

Based in Salt Lake City, Utah, the company operates in the healthcare industry with a team of 17. It was acquired, marking a significant milestone in its mission to improve oncology outcomes through personalized medicine.

_Usage:_

"How does Known Medicine's approach to drug testing differ from standard in-vitro methods?"

"Instead of using generic cell lines, Known Medicine takes a patient's actual tumor, breaks it down into thousands of micro-tumors, and tests different drugs on them. This allows them to use AI to predict which specific treatment that individual patient will respond to best, rather than relying on population-level averages."
