Kontigo is a USDC‑smart neobank targeting Latinos in the U.S. and Latin America. It launched a peer‑to‑peer onramp 20 days ago, already attracting $540k in deposits, 10,000 active users and a waitlist of over 300,000. The startup, backed by DST Global, Soma Capital, Pioneer Fund, Transpose and 10 YC alumni, is built by a team that has scaled fintechs across Latin America.

The company tackles the region’s fragmented payments landscape, where cross‑border transfers can cost up to 20% and currency depreciation runs rampant. Kontigo offers a USDC global wallet and a Bitcoin‑backed savings account, letting users send money across 33 countries without limits. Each wallet is linked to an AI‑private banker on WhatsApp that executes international USDC transactions, combining the simplicity of Venmo or Zelle with the stability of a non‑inflationary digital asset.
