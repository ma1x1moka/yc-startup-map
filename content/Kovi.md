Kovi is a Brazilian fintech that offers services to simplify car ownership, delivering flexible financing options that aim to broaden access to vehicles. It operates in the auto commerce and mobility sectors, combining credit and lending expertise to create a more inclusive automotive market. Kovi was acquired, reflecting its significance in the fintech and mobility landscape. The company is based in São Paulo and employs around 890 people.
