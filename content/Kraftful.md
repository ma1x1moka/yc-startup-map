**Website:** http://www.kraftful.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/kraftful

Kraftful helps product builders listen to users at scale so they can create products people want. The platform combines AI and NLP to analyze user feedback, surfacing actionable insights for engineering, product, and design teams.

Founded in Summer 2019, Kraftful operated with a small team of six from San Francisco and remote locations. The company was acquired, marking its impact in the B2B product tooling space.
