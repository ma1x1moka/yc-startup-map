LSK Technologies is a health tech company based in Toronto, Canada, that developed a diagnostic lab in a box. The company focused on the diagnostics industry, aiming to simplify or compact the infrastructure required for medical testing. With a small team of two, LSK Technologies operated as a specialized player in the health tech space. The company was eventually acquired, marking its exit from the independent startup landscape.

_Usage:_

"What exactly is a 'diagnostic lab in a box' and how does it differ from traditional lab setups?"

"It is a compact, self-contained system designed to perform diagnostic tests without the need for a full-scale, traditional laboratory infrastructure. This approach allows for more portable or decentralized testing capabilities compared to standard hospital or central lab environments."
