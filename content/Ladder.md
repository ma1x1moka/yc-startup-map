**Website:** http://meetladder.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ladder

Ladder operates as a specialized recruiting platform designed specifically for the construction trades. By focusing on this vertical, the company addresses the unique hiring challenges within the Real Estate and Construction industries.

The startup was based in Atlanta, GA, and operated with a remote team. Ladder was ultimately acquired, marking its exit from the independent startup phase.

_Usage:_

"How does Ladder differ from generalist job boards for construction roles?"

"Ladder is a vertical marketplace built specifically for the construction trades, rather than a generalist platform. It focuses on the specific recruiting needs of the construction industry, distinguishing it from broad labor marketplaces that cover many different sectors."
