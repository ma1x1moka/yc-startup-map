**Website:** https://www.getlago.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/lago

Lago is an open-source billing platform designed to simplify pricing and metering for SaaS businesses. It allows companies to build flexible pricing models and scale their infrastructure with confidence. The platform has gained significant traction in the developer community, accumulating over 9,000 stars on GitHub.

Notable users of the platform include PayPal, Synthesia, and Mistral.ai. Lago has raised over $22 million in funding from investors such as Y Combinator and FirstMark. The company operates out of San Francisco with a team of 45 people.

_Usage:_

"Why would a SaaS company choose Lago over traditional billing providers?"

"Lago is an open-source alternative that gives teams more control over their billing logic, allowing them to build flexible pricing models without being locked into proprietary systems. It is particularly popular among developer-focused companies that need to integrate custom metering and billing directly into their stack, as evidenced by its adoption by firms like Mistral.ai and PayPal."
