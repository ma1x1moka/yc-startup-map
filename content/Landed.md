**Website:** http://www.landed.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/landed

Landed offers white-labeled shared appreciation down-payment programs that let employers give employees a path to homeownership. It unlocks shared appreciation to create tailored down-payment plans that benefit both funders and buyers, and helps firms attract and keep talent.

By recycling capital as buyers exit the program, Landed builds a self-renewing pool that keeps costs low and expands access. The company was acquired, underscoring its traction in fintech and proptech.

With a lean team of two based in San Francisco and remote, Landed operates at the intersection of credit, lending, and real-estate technology.

_Usage:_

"How does Landed's shared appreciation model differ from a traditional down payment assistance program?"

"Unlike other programs, shared appreciation is mutually beneficial to funders and buyers, creating a self-renewing pool of capital that recycles as homebuyers exit the program."
