**Website:** https://langfuse.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/langfuse

Langfuse is an open‑source platform that gives developers end‑to‑end observability for large‑language‑model applications.

It offers a UI for prompt management, trace collection, analytics, metrics, and evaluation tools, allowing teams to experiment, release, and build datasets.

In January 2026, Langfuse was acquired by ClickHouse, underscoring its impact in the generative‑AI developer‑tools space.

_Usage:_

"What makes Langfuse useful for debugging LLM applications?"

"Langfuse aggregates traces, metrics, and evaluations in a single UI, letting developers see how prompts flow through the model and identify issues quickly, which is harder with separate tools."
