It launcheds eight weeks ago and have grown from $0 to $21K MRR, growing 104% MoM.

Lark is building toward a complete AI-native operating system for distributors, replacing ERPs with one system for inventory, purchasing, orders, sales, and finance. Teams will tailor Lark to their own workflows, business rules, and approval limits, so they can automate operations while staying in control.
