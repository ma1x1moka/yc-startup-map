Latchel is a SaaS platform that automates maintenance coordination and delivers best‑in‑class resident amenities for property managers. The service covers more than 80,000 homes and supports over 400 customers across all 50 U.S. states.

By providing a 24/7 emergency maintenance department, the platform streamlines operations for B2B clients in the property‑management space. The company is based in Seattle with remote staff and continues to grow rapidly.

_Usage:_

"How does Latchel’s maintenance coordination differ from a typical property‑manager’s manual process?"

"Latchel’s platform automates the entire workflow, routing requests, scheduling vendors, and offering 24/7 emergency response, which reduces manual hand‑offs and speeds up resolution compared to a manual process."
