**Website:** https://www.laudable.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/laudable

Laudable is a B2B marketing tool that identifies happy customers and their success stories hidden within a company's existing data sources. It analyzes inputs such as call recordings, NPS scores, and Salesforce logs to automatically generate case study content.

The company, which operated with a small remote team based in San Francisco, focused on streamlining the often manual process of gathering customer advocacy. By leveraging unstructured data, Laudable aimed to help B2B companies scale their marketing efforts without extensive manual research.

Laudable was acquired, marking the end of its independent operations. This acquisition highlights the growing interest in automated content generation tools for B2B marketing teams.

_Usage:_

"How does Laudable actually find the right customers to feature in case studies?"

"It scans your existing data sources like call recordings, NPS responses, and Salesforce logs to identify customers who are already expressing satisfaction or success. Instead of you manually digging through transcripts or spreadsheets, the system surfaces those specific advocates and drafts the case study content for you."
