Laudspeaker is an open source platform designed for mobile marketing automation and customer onboarding. It allows teams to segment users and engage them through complex, scheduled campaigns across push notifications, SMS, and email. The company positions itself as an alternative to established commercial tools like Braze, Customer.io, Iterable, Pendo, and Appcues.

Laudspeaker was acquired by PostHog, marking a notable exit for the startup. The company operated with a small team of three people, working remotely from San Francisco, California.

_Usage:_

"How does Laudspeaker differ from closed-source marketing automation tools like Braze or Appcues?"

"Laudspeaker is an open source alternative that handles mobile marketing automation and onboarding, specifically supporting complex scheduled campaigns via push, SMS, and email. Unlike the proprietary solutions it cites as alternatives, it offers an open source model, which was likely a key factor in its acquisition by PostHog."
