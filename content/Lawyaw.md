**Website:** http://www.lawyaw.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/lawyaw

Lawyaw is a vertical SaaS platform that enables the digitization of legal services by making programmable documents easy to incorporate into digital workflows. It fills a foundational gap in the market for law firms seeking to modernize their document processes.

The platform powers over 1,000 law firms, helping to make access to legal services faster, cheaper and more convenient, and is targeting the $150B consumer legal services market. Lawyaw operates with a team of 15 out of San Francisco, CA.

Lawyaw was acquired, reflecting its notable impact on the legal tech space.

_Usage:_

"How does Lawyaw’s document automation differ from generic document tools?"

"Lawyaw focuses specifically on legal workflows, offering programmable documents that can be embedded directly into a law firm’s digital processes, which is why it powers over a thousand firms and was notable enough to be acquired."
