Customers report that Leadbay can produce results in ten minutes that would otherwise take six months with traditional GTM agencies or competitors like Clay.

The company operates out of San Francisco with a team of eight. Leadbay's platform is available via leadbay.ai and is part of the Fall 2025 batch.
