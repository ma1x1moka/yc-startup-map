**Website:** https://www.leena.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/leena-ai

Leena AI provides an AI‑powered virtual assistant that answers employee questions across HR, IT, and finance, leveraging enterprise knowledge to respond like a human and even handle complex personalized queries such as salary discrepancies.
The platform integrates with common collaboration tools such as Microsoft Teams and Slack, making it easy for employees to access support. Leena AI serves 500 customers, including Coca‑Cola, Sony, Puma, Vodafone, Nestle, P&G, and Airbnb, and has raised over $40 million from investors like Bessemer and Greycroft.
With a team of 500 based in New York City and New Delhi, Leena AI has been active since Summer 2018 and continues to expand its enterprise software offerings.

_Usage:_

"How does Leena AI integrate with existing collaboration tools?"

"Leena AI can be accessed directly from MS Teams or Slack, allowing employees to ask questions within the tools they already use."
