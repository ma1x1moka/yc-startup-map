Legacy is a startup focused on fertility and family planning, offering a mail‑in kit for sperm testing and freezing, personalized improvement products, tele‑health, DNA fragmentation analysis, and supplements. It aims to change the outdated view that fertility is a women's issue by making the process accessible and affordable for men and people with sperm.

Founded at Harvard, Legacy has raised $40M from investors including FirstMark Capital, Bain Capital Ventures, Section 32, Tribe Capital, and celebrities such as Justin Bieber and The Weeknd. It has secured nationwide insurance coverage for over 10 million lives, military contracts including the Navy SEALs and 15 million veterans, and employer offerings with top fertility benefit providers.

With a team of about 30, the company emphasizes values such as customer centricity, reliability, kindness, openness, and winning, and operates out of New York City and Cambridge, Massachusetts.
