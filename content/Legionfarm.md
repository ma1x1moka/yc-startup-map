Founded in 2016, Legionfarm was backed by Y Combinator in Winter 2020 and raised $17.8M from over 20 investors. The platform has served more than 250,000 customers through two web interfaces and five bots, creating over 10,000 jobs for professional gamers worldwide. Legionfarm turns gaming hobbies into careers, delivering global entertainment and esports impact. Since 2023, its LLM‑driven automation has replaced more than 100 operations roles, and its AI workspace offers smart agents, real‑time feedback, evaluations, and community fine‑tuning to maximize pro efficiency. The company operates with a team of seven from Los Angeles, CA, and focuses on consumer and gaming industries.
