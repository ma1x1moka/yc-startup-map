Paragraph 1: LemonBox is an AI-driven personalized nutrition company that uses generative AI to analyze users' dietary habits, health goals, and biometric data. It then creates a custom daily supplement formula and delivers it as a personalized day pack via a monthly subscription.

Paragraph 2: Founded in Silicon Valley in 2016, the company has raised backing from Y Combinator, Bessemer Venture Partners, and Partech Ventures. It has served over 10 million users worldwide and operates with a team of 20 based in San Francisco.
