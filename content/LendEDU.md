**Website:** https://lendedu.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/lendedu

LendEDU operates a personal finance comparison website designed to bring transparency to consumer financial markets. The platform enables users to compare options across student lending, unsecured loans, auto loans, banking, credit cards, and various insurance products.

After participating in Y Combinator's Winter 2016 batch, LendEDU also graduated from the Iowa Startup Accelerator and received seed funding from Built By Iowa and the Iowa Startup Accelerator Fund. Its comparison tools and data were featured across major media outlets, including The Wall Street Journal, The New York Times, Bloomberg, and Forbes.

LendEDU has since been acquired.
