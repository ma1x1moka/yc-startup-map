**Website:** https://trylevel.app | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/level

Level offers fast and simple financing to lending startups, buying loans in small quantities and expanding access to capital as customers perform well. Within four weeks of launching, it purchased $1.3 million in loans across six customers, demonstrating rapid scaling. The company was acquired after its early traction and is backed by Liquid2, Y Combinator (S21) and others.

_Usage:_

"How is Level’s financing model different from traditional venture debt?"

"Level buys existing loans from startups in small batches and rewards good performance with additional capital, rather than providing a fixed non‑dilutive loan upfront like many venture‑debt firms. This approach lets it grow the credit line as the borrower proves itself."
