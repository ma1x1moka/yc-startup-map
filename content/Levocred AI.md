Levocred AI builds and deploys AI agents specifically for credit funds, fintechs, and banks. The platform automates key workflows including back-office tasks, portfolio monitoring, compliance, and underwriting.

The company is based in San Francisco and operates with a small team of two. It is currently live across multiple lending teams, processing approximately $1 billion in receivables through its system.
