**Website:** https://lifeat.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/lifeat

LifeAt is a productivity platform that combines video content with task management to help knowledge workers enter flow state. It claims users complete daily tasks twice as fast while ending the day feeling good.
LifeAt is backed by Meta, Line, Y Combinator, Goodwater, and other top VCs. It has more than a million users worldwide and is shared by over 500,000 followers on TikTok, Instagram, and Twitter. With a six-person team based in San Francisco and Seattle, LifeAt continues to grow its user base.

_Usage:_

"How does LifeAt's video-based task system differ from conventional to-do list apps?"

"Unlike typical to-do lists, LifeAt embeds short instructional videos that guide users through each task, helping them stay focused and finish twice as fast, according to the company's metrics."
