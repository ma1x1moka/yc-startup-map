**Website:** https://www.drive-thru.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/lilac-labs

Lilac Labs builds a voice AI system designed to replace the human employee taking orders at drive-thrus, targeting quick service restaurants facing labor shortages and rising wages. The company notes that previous attempts at this technology were too costly and lacked the necessary accuracy and latency, but current capabilities now allow for a voice interface that meets high customer experience standards.

The startup operates in San Francisco with a team of four and was part of the Summer 2024 YC batch. It targets a massive market, citing 200,000 drive-thrus in the United States that handle 6 billion visits annually. Lilac claims its solution delivers approximately $100,000 in value per location through labor savings, upsell revenue, and reduced training costs.

The company is currently marked as Inactive in the YC directory, indicating it has since shut down.

_Usage:_

"How does this differ from the older voice ordering systems that didn't work out?"

"Previous drive-thru voice ordering attempts were expensive to implement and failed to meet the accuracy and latency thresholds required for a good customer experience. Lilac Labs argues that current AI capabilities have finally crossed that threshold, allowing for a reliable voice interface that can handle the volume and speed of a typical drive-thru without the high implementation costs of earlier solutions."
