**Website:** https://www.lineleap.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/lineleap

LineLeap operates a mobile platform that allows customers to purchase LineSkip passes, drinks, cover, and event tickets at over 400 top nightlife venues across the country. For venues, the platform offers technology to generate new revenue streams and run marketing campaigns via push notifications, in-app messaging, and email.

Based in New York City with a team of 50, LineLeap participated in the Y Combinator Summer 2019 batch and has acquired over one million customers to date. Its broader goal is to serve as the primary platform for purchasing, venue discovery, and brand-to-consumer engagement at top events and nightlife destinations.

_Usage:_

"How do venues use LineLeap to increase revenue and engage customers?"

"Venues adopt LineLeap's technology to sell digital passes like LineSkip passes, drinks, cover, and event tickets directly through the app. They also use the platform to market to their audience using push notifications, email campaigns, and in-app messaging."
