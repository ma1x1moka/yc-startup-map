**Website:** https://www.shoplocale.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/locale

Locale creates organic high-protein meals that prioritize longevity through strict nutritional standards. Each meal contains 40-60g of protein, utilizes nutrient-dense meat sources such as grass-fed and pasture-raised options, and includes over 8g of fiber. The company avoids seed oils, processed ingredients, and refined sugars, instead cooking with extra virgin olive oil and using organic, pesticide-free produce.

The brand emphasizes clean eating by ensuring its products are microplastic-free and high in anti-inflammatories and Omega-3s. Meals are packaged in reusable glass jars to support sustainability. Since launching in 2020, Locale has delivered over 100,000 orders and is backed by investors including a16z, Y Combinator, and Goodwater.

_Usage:_

"What specific nutritional metrics does Locale guarantee per meal compared to standard meal kits?"

"Locale guarantees 40-60g of protein and at least 8g of fiber per meal, while explicitly excluding seed oils, refined sugars, and processed ingredients. It also ensures the produce is organic and pesticide-free, and the packaging is microplastic-free reusable glass, distinguishing it from conventional delivery services that often use plastic and lower-protein profiles."
