**Website:** https://www.localyze.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/localyze

Localyze reimagines how people move for work by offering a centralised platform that brings together seamless technology, transparency and service. The platform allows companies to hire talent from anywhere and supports employees in working and living wherever they choose. Localyze was acquired. With a team of 105 based in Hamburg and remote, the company serves the B2B and Human Resources markets, focusing on immigration and relocation services.

_Usage:_

"How does Localyze streamline the visa and relocation process compared to traditional methods?"

"Localyze centralises all visa, compliance and relocation steps into one SaaS platform, providing real‑time visibility and dedicated support, whereas traditional approaches rely on multiple vendors and manual paperwork."
