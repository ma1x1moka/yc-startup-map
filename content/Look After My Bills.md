Look After My Bills is an auto‑switching service that continuously checks energy tariffs and moves customers onto the best available deal without manual effort. By handling the comparison and switch process each year, it aims to keep households paying the lowest possible price for electricity and gas.

The company was acquired after its Winter 2018 Y Combinator batch, reflecting the value of its automated bill‑optimisation technology. Based in England, it operated with a two‑person team focused on the fintech and consumer‑finance markets.

_Usage:_

"How does Look After My Bills find a cheaper energy plan compared to doing it yourself?"

"It runs an automated service that monitors the market for energy tariffs and triggers a switch whenever a lower‑priced option becomes available, eliminating the need for users to manually compare rates each year."
