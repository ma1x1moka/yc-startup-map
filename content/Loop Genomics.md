Loop Genomics provides long‑read sequencing solutions across a diverse set of research and clinical applications. Its platform transforms short‑read DNA sequencers into long‑read sequencing machines, enabling long‑read data without requiring new hardware.

Based in San Francisco and San Jose with a team of seven, Loop Genomics was acquired, reflecting its impact in the healthcare and diagnostics sectors.

_Usage:_

"How does Loop Genomics achieve long‑read sequencing using existing short‑read sequencers?"

"Loop Genomics uses a platform that converts standard short‑read DNA sequencers into long‑read sequencing machines, so users can generate long‑read data on equipment they already have."
