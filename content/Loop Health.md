**Website:** http://www.loophealth.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/loop-health

Loop Health is a new type of healthcare provider that combines high‑quality primary care with employer‑funded insurance. Employees receive unlimited primary care visits at asset‑light clinics, along with traditional hospitalization coverage.
During its YC Winter 2020 batch, Loop grew from zero to $85,000 ARR with 200% month‑over‑month growth, serving 22 companies and 5,500 members in Pune. Employers pay $15 per member per year for the base plan and Loop earns an additional $25 on prescriptions, lab tests, and hospitalization referrals, yielding 70% gross margins.
The company addresses a gap in the Indian market where only 8% of employees use hospital‑only insurance benefits. By offering doctor visits and telemedicine at the same price, Loop can lower premiums while giving employees better access to care, tapping into a $8 billion, 27% CAGR employer‑funded health insurance opportunity.

_Usage:_

"How does Loop Health's pricing structure differ from traditional employer health plans?"

"Loop Health charges employers $15 per member per year for the base plan, which includes unlimited primary care visits and traditional hospitalization coverage. This structure keeps overall costs comparable to traditional plans while providing more comprehensive coverage."
