**Website:** https://www.loopsupport.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/loop-support

Loop Support builds pre‑employment assessments that accurately simulate the work applicants will actually perform on‑the‑job, helping employers predict candidate performance in entry‑level support positions. The SaaS platform is used by B2B recruiting teams to evaluate candidates on realistic tasks rather than relying solely on interviews or resumes. Loop Support was acquired, underscoring the market’s interest in data‑driven hiring tools.

_Usage:_

"How does Loop Support’s assessment differ from a traditional interview?"

"Loop Support’s assessments replace generic interview questions with simulated tasks that mirror the actual duties of an entry‑level support role, giving employers a concrete measure of how a candidate will perform on the job."
