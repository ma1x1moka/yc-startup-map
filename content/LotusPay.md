LotusPay is a recurring payments platform built for businesses in India. The company enables clients to collect both fixed and variable payments directly from their customers' bank accounts via paperless NACH Debit. Its infrastructure handles various payment types, including loan repayments, membership fees, subscriptions, recurring donations, and tuition fees.

Based in Bengaluru and launched in the Summer 2017 batch of Y Combinator, LotusPay was acquired by Juspay in January 2024.

_Usage:_

"How does LotusPay facilitate recurring bank account debits in India?"

"LotusPay leverages paperless NACH Debit to collect payments directly from customer bank accounts. It allows Indian businesses to pull fixed or variable amounts on a recurring basis for use cases such as subscriptions, loan repayments, and tuition fees."
