**Website:** https://lowkey.gg | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/lowkey

Lowkey captures, creates, and shares gaming moments, giving gamers a way to record and distribute gameplay highlights. Founded in Summer 2018, the company grew to a team of 10 in San Francisco before being acquired by Niantic on 11/30/2021.
