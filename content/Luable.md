Luable operates a bank deposits marketplace in Latin America that allows financial institutions to source funding from retail users. This model offers banks a faster and more cost-effective alternative to traditional expansion methods like opening physical branch offices or hiring additional sales representatives.

The platform aggregates multiple banks in a single interface, enabling retail users to compare options and select products that best fit their needs. It also supports emerging banks by allowing them to offer a full digital experience without incurring fixed costs associated with physical infrastructure.

Founded in 2021 and based in Bogotá, Colombia, Luable has processed over USD $30 million in deposits for various banks. The company, which has a team of 40, is currently expanding its operations from Colombia into Mexico and the Caribbean.

_Usage:_

"How does Luable's funding model differ from traditional bank expansion strategies?"

"Instead of banks spending capital on physical branches or large sales teams to acquire depositors, Luable acts as a digital intermediary. It aggregates retail users who are looking for deposit products, allowing banks to access this liquidity pool directly. This reduces the customer acquisition cost for the bank while providing users with a comparison tool to find the best rates across different institutions."
