Founded [Cortex AI](./Cortex%20AI.md). Raised $6M. Ngoo's background is in computer vision and robotics data — the specific intersection needed to build high-quality embodied AI training datasets that include accurate hand and body pose annotations.

The robotics training data market is structurally similar to what Scale AI built for perception AI in autonomous vehicles: a specialized data collection and annotation pipeline that model teams don't want to build themselves. Ngoo is making the same bet that Alexandr Wang made a decade earlier, but for the embodied AI wave.

The workplace data collection angle is Cortex AI's differentiation: lab data produces policies that work in labs. Factory floor data, collected with workers doing real tasks in real environments, produces policies that generalize to deployment.

_Usage:_

"How does Cortex AI's strategy compare to Scale AI?"

"It uses the same playbook, but for embodied AI rather than autonomous vehicles. Just as Scale AI built an outsourced perception data pipeline for AV teams, Cortex AI provides hand and body pose annotation pipelines for robotics models. Its core focus is capturing real worker data on factory floors rather than in lab settings, ensuring the resulting policies actually generalize to real-world deployment."
