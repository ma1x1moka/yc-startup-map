Lumina is a job platform based in Singapore that connects 1.5 million workers with 30,000 companies in Southeast Asia. The company has expanded its services globally through Lumibot, a bespoke automation service that combines AI with human-in-the-loop support to handle repetitive business processes.

Lumibot targets use cases such as operations support, revenue operations, web and social media management, email handling, content moderation, and AI training. The service operates on a prepaid credit system with no minimum commitment, allowing businesses to automate complex workflows while maintaining flexibility through dedicated service managers and SLAs.

_Usage:_

"How does Lumina's pricing model work compared to traditional outsourcing agencies?"

"Lumina uses a prepaid credit system where AI-powered automation starts at $13 per hour, supplemented by human-in-the-loop support at $2 to $6 per hour. Unlike traditional agencies that often require long-term contracts, Lumina operates without minimum commitments and offers a fully refundable deposit starting at $500, providing more flexibility for businesses testing automation workflows."
