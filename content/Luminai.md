Luminai is a B2B SaaS company that focuses on automating repetitive manual tasks within mission-critical business processes. The platform serves customers across the insurance, healthcare, and financial services sectors, helping them streamline operations and reduce manual effort.

Based in San Francisco, the company has a team of 31 people and was part of Y Combinator's Summer 2020 batch. Luminai has raised $20 million to date, with backing from General Catalyst, Craft Ventures, and various angel investors including founders and executives from companies like Dropbox, Notion, and Uber.

_Usage:_

"How does Luminai differ from general-purpose RPA tools?"

"Luminai specifically targets mission-critical business processes in regulated industries like insurance, healthcare, and financial services, rather than offering a generic automation layer. It focuses on removing repetitive manual work from these specific verticals, which often have complex compliance and workflow requirements that general RPA tools may not handle as effectively."
