**Website:** http://luministlabs.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/luminist-labs

Luminist Labs developed a non-invasive diagnostic test for the early detection and staging of liver fibrosis and non-alcoholic steatohepatitis (NASH). Operating in healthcare and genomics out of New York City, the company focused on medical diagnostics for critical liver conditions.

The company was part of the Y Combinator Winter 2016 batch. Luminist Labs was acquired in 2020 by DiscernDx.
