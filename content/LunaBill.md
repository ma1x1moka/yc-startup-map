Founded by Suhail Parry and David Day. Builds AI voice agents specifically for the medical billing call category — insurance authorization calls, claim follow-ups, prior authorization requests, and patient payment conversations.

Medical billing calls are a $25B+ industry in the US: hospitals employ thousands of billing staff whose job is to call insurance companies, navigate phone trees, wait on hold, and dispute denials. Each call follows predictable scripts but requires real-time adaptation when the insurance agent asks unexpected questions. LunaBill automates the routine calls and flags complex ones for human review.

The HIPAA compliance and EHR integration are the technical moat: connecting to Epic, Cerner, or Athenahealth requires security certifications and integration work that competitors can't bypass. Once a hospital system has integrated and validated the product, switching costs are high.

Positioned alongside [Beacon Health](./Beacon%20Health.md) in the healthcare AI automation space — [Beacon Health](./Beacon%20Health.md) handles clinical workflows within EHR systems, LunaBill handles the financial workflows that happen outside them.

_Usage:_

"Why can't hospitals just train staff better instead of using AI for billing calls?"

"Staff turnover in medical billing is 30-40% annually. Every new hire requires months of training. AI agents don't quit, don't need training resets, and can handle 10x the call volume of a human team."
