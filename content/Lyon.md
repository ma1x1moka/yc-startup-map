**Website:** https://lyon.so | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/lyon

Lyon builds private foundation models for banks, insurance companies, and fintechs, learning from proprietary transactions, payments, clicks, and customer interactions to predict credit, fraud, collections, income, churn, and lifetime value. The models run inside the company’s cloud, so data and intelligence remain under the customer’s control.

Lyon is already working with a major insurer and trained a model on 28 billion transactions for a fintech serving tens of millions of active users, identifying premium‑card converters four times more precisely than its existing rules. The model is now being deployed for credit.

The company is based in San Francisco, CA, and has a team of two.

_Usage:_

"How does Lyon keep data private while training foundation models?"

"Lyon trains its models inside the customer’s own cloud, ensuring that all transaction data stays within the company’s environment and is never exposed externally."
