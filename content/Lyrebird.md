**Website:** https://lyrebird.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/lyrebird

Lyrebird provides a new generation of speech synthesis technologies designed for developers. The company emerged from several months of research conducted at the MILA lab at the University of Montréal.

Based in Montreal, QC, Canada, the team of 13 engineers focused on B2B applications within the engineering and product design sectors. Lyrebird was acquired.
