MICSI is a New York City-based startup developing AI software designed to enhance magnetic resonance imaging capabilities. The technology aims to double the resolution of MRI scans while simultaneously reducing scan times by half. This improvement allows imaging centers to increase their capacity and patient throughput, with the company estimating an additional $2 million in revenue per MRI scanner.

Beyond immediate efficiency gains, MICSI views its initial offering as a step toward transforming MRI into a quantitative instrument. The goal is to provide highly reproducible data that supports more accurate diagnoses and patient management. The company operates with a small team of two and focuses on the healthcare and diagnostics sectors.
