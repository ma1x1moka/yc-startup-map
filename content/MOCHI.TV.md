Mochi.TV is a mobile‑first entertainment platform that serves vertical anime shorts designed for quick consumption. Users scroll through a feed of bite‑sized episodes and can unlock them using in‑app coins or a subscription.

The service produces new shows daily using artificial intelligence, allowing a steady stream of fresh content. Its AI‑generated shorts have amassed over 200 million organic views across all platforms.

Based in San Francisco with a three‑person team, Mochi.TV targets the consumer content market by combining AI‑driven production with a micro‑payment model.

_Usage:_

"How does Mochi.TV’s AI create new anime shorts each day compared to traditional animation pipelines?"

"Mochi.TV uses artificial intelligence to generate the visual and narrative elements of its anime shorts, enabling daily production without the lengthy manual animation process. The AI‑generated episodes are then delivered through the mobile app, where users unlock them with coins or a subscription."
