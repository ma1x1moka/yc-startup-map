Mable operates a personalized migraine clinic that utilizes DNA analysis to identify and provide appropriate medication, supported by continuous expert care through telehealth. The company is based in London and San Francisco, with a remote team of seven members.

By combining genomics with medical devices and telehealth, Mable aims to improve treatment outcomes for migraine sufferers. The platform reports that patients experience an 80% higher treatment satisfaction rate, gain one extra day per week of quality life, and achieve a 55% reduction in pain.

Mable has been acquired, marking a significant milestone for this healthcare-focused startup.

_Usage:_

"How does Mable use genetics to treat migraines differently than standard telehealth?"

"Mable uses DNA analysis to determine which medication is most likely to work for a specific patient, rather than relying on trial-and-error prescribing. This personalized approach is combined with continuous expert care via telehealth, resulting in reported outcomes like 55% pain reduction and higher satisfaction rates compared to standard treatments."
