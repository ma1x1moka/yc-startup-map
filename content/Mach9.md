**Website:** https://www.mach9.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/mach9

Mach9 develops automated feature extraction technology to accelerate how engineering teams model and understand the physical world. Its flagship platform, Digital Surveyor, is designed for transportation agencies, utility companies, and engineering firms to process miles of road corridor projects in a fraction of the time required by traditional methods.

Built on research from Carnegie Mellon University's Robotics Institute, the company applies computer vision and machine learning to create engineering-grade maps for infrastructure design and operations. The platform is used by leading survey and geospatial organizations, including Langan, Olsson, Woolpert, HDR, and major state DOTs, to tackle large-scale projects with greater speed and accuracy.

Founded in 2021 and headquartered in San Francisco, Mach9 operates with a team of 25 employees, focusing on the intersection of AI and the architecture, engineering, and construction industries.

_Usage:_

"How does Mach9's Digital Surveyor differ from standard GIS or CAD tools for infrastructure projects?"

"While traditional CAD and GIS tools often require manual digitization or extensive human input to map road corridors and infrastructure features, Mach9 uses automated feature extraction to process these datasets much faster. It generates engineering-grade maps from raw data, allowing firms to handle large-scale projects with less manual processing time while maintaining the accuracy needed for design and operations."
