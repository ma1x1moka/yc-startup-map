**YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/madeats

MadEats is a delivery-only restaurant group based in Makati, Philippines, that operates through its own network of ghost kitchens. By eliminating the costs associated with physical dining areas and wait staff, the company redirects resources toward improving food quality, growing its food brands, and enhancing the delivery experience. This model allows MadEats to maintain high margins while offering affordable options to consumers.

The company was selected for the Y Combinator Summer 2021 batch and has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does using ghost kitchens change the unit economics compared to a traditional brick-and-mortar restaurant?"

"It removes the capital and operational costs of maintaining a physical dining space and hiring front-of-house staff. This allows the company to allocate more budget directly to food production, brand development, and delivery logistics, resulting in higher margins for the same end product."
