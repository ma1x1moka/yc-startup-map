**Twitter:** @mamagnus00 | **LinkedIn:** linkedin.com/in/magnus-mueller

Co-founded [Browser Use](./Browser%20Use.md) with [Gregor Zunic](./Gregor%20Zunic.md).

**Education:** Data Science, ETH Zurich (Switzerland's top technical university, globally ranked top 10 in engineering).

**Background:** ML researcher who published research for **Cambridge CARES** (Cambridge Centre for Advanced Research and Education in Singapore). R&D in process automation.

The ETH Zurich Data Science background is the strongest technical credential in Europe for AI/ML work — ETH has produced a disproportionate share of ML researchers at top labs. The Cambridge CARES publication gives Muller peer-reviewed ML research credibility before the startup existed.

The Browser Use open-source strategy was Muller's distribution decision: release the framework free, let the community validate it, convert community trust into commercial cloud revenue. The **97,600 GitHub stars** are evidence the strategy worked faster than expected.

_Usage:_

"Why did two ETH Zurich data scientists build browser automation?"

"They saw the gap that frontier AI created: models can reason, but they can't act unless you give them the web. Browser Use was the missing action layer — and ETH's research tradition of building rigorous systems, not just demos, shows in the product reliability."
