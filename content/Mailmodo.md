**Website:** https://www.mailmodo.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/mailmodo

Mailmodo is a B2B SaaS company that provides a no-code email marketing platform designed to create app-like experiences within email clients. By allowing marketers to embed interactive elements such as forms, shopping carts, calendars, and NPS surveys directly into messages, the platform reduces the number of steps required for user engagement. This approach aims to increase email conversion rates by enabling actions like feedback collection, meeting booking, and abandoned cart recovery without leaving the inbox.

The company, which operates with a team of approximately 70 people from San Francisco and remote locations, was part of the Y Combinator Summer 2021 batch. Its focus on interactive email widgets distinguishes it from traditional static email tools by facilitating direct user interaction and lead generation within the email interface itself.

Mailmodo has since been acquired, marking a significant milestone for the company in the competitive email marketing space.

_Usage:_

"How does Mailmodo differ from standard email marketing tools like Mailchimp or HubSpot?"

"Standard tools typically send static content that requires users to click a link and navigate to a website to take action. Mailmodo allows you to embed functional widgets—like booking calendars, product carts, or feedback forms—directly inside the email body, so users can complete the entire transaction or interaction without leaving their inbox."
