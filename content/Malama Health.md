Malama Health offers holistic support during and after pregnancy at no cost to patients, focusing on doula-led care. The company operates within the healthcare sector, specifically targeting digital health and femtech solutions to improve maternal experiences.

Based in San Francisco, Malama is in-network with major insurers including Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, Anthem, and Kaiser, as well as several Medicaid Managed Care Plans. This insurance integration allows the service to be accessible without direct out-of-pocket expenses for the patients receiving care.

The company is backed by the Stanford d.school, Y Combinator (S22), and Alpine Investors. With a small team of three, Malama continues to operate actively, leveraging its YC batch status to scale its support model.
