Manicule offers AgentRel, a DevRel service tailored for AI agents that helps companies get discovered and used by agents. It owns developer documentation and technical content across social channels end-to-end. It grew 100% month-over-month over the last three months to $53K in monthly revenue, serving customers such as Supermemory, Greptile, Reducto, and Mastra. The company addresses a gap in the market where traditional SEO/GEO SaaS tools only show ranking data, and agencies lack agent expertise. Hiring a full-time DevRel who can manage all of this is difficult, with roles open for months at $300K+ salaries. Manicule solves this by providing a fractional DevRel that covers all aspects of agent discovery and promotion. With a team of seven based in San Francisco, Manicule operates in the B2B marketing space, built by a team with prior startup experience. The company remains active and continues to expand its agent-focused DevRel offering.
