**Website:** https://www.mantys.io/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/mantys

Mantys builds AI agents that automate healthcare and insurance operations.
It works with some of the largest healthcare groups in the Middle East, including Aster, NMC, Zulekha, Medcare, Thumbay and Fakeeh, automating patient access workflows such as insurance eligibility, registration and prior authorization by interfacing with hospital information systems, payer portals and unstructured documents.
The company is deployed across more than 100 healthcare facilities and processes workflows for over 40,000 patients each day. Based in Dubai and supported by a team of ten, Mantys operates as a B2B SaaS solution within the healthcare IT sector.

_Usage:_

"What systems does Mantys integrate with to automate prior authorization?"

"Mantys' AI agents connect directly to hospital information systems and payer portals, and they also parse unstructured documents to determine eligibility and prior authorization status, streamlining the workflow that would otherwise require manual data entry."
