Manufact is the MCP cloud platform that prepares products for the Claude Connectors marketplace, the ChatGPT/Codex plugins store, and any AI agent that supports MCP. It offers the fastest and most complete solution for production‑ready MCP servers, enabling developers to launch AI‑driven services at scale.

The company also maintains the open‑source mcp‑use SDK, a framework for building MCP apps that has earned 10 000 GitHub stars and more than 8 million downloads across Python and TypeScript. With a six‑person team based in San Francisco, Manufact targets B2B infrastructure developers seeking robust, ready‑to‑use cloud services.

_Usage:_

"How is Manufact's MCP cloud platform different from generic cloud providers for AI workloads?"

"Manufact focuses specifically on MCP servers and integrates directly with the Claude Connectors marketplace and the ChatGPT/Codex plugins store, whereas generic providers offer broader compute without the built‑in support for MCP‑based AI agents. Its open‑source mcp‑use SDK and the claim of being the fastest, most complete production‑ready MCP solution are unique to its offering."
