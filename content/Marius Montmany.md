Co-founded [REVER](./REVER.md) in 2022 with [Oriol Hernandez i Fajula](./Oriol%20Hernandez%20i%20Fajula.md) in Barcelona, part of YC's Summer 2022 batch, after both grew frustrated with "reverse logistics" — the difficulty of handling product returns efficiently. Montmany previously worked at Glovo from 2018 to 2020, including as Global Head of Product & Logistics. REVER makes e-commerce returns possible in two clicks with refunds in 24 hours, and has raised $15 million.

_Usage:_

"What is REVER's core promise to shoppers?"

"Returns in two clicks and refunds within 24 hours — turning a normally slow, frustrating e-commerce process into something fast and simple."
