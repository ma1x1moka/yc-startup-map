Mark Cuban Cost Plus Drug Company, PBC, is a Dallas-based consumer health and wellness company that produces low cost versions of high cost generic drugs. Founded in Summer 2018, the company operates as a public benefit corporation.

By focusing on affordability, it offers consumers more accessible medication options in the healthcare market.

_Usage:_

"How does Mark Cuban Cost Plus Drug Company keep drug prices low?"

"It produces low cost versions of high cost generic drugs, offering more affordable options for consumers."
