Mark co-founded [OpenInt](./OpenInt.md) — Open Source Embedded Product Integrations.

Mark is the co-founder and CTO of Map3. Previously he worked at Blockchain.com as Head of Crypto Engineering, where he built and ran multiple engineering teams, contributed to the non-custodial wallet across all platforms (iOS, Android and Web), and led the effort to build the custody infrastructure across both Hot and Cold systems. Originally from Munich, Germany, Mark has been an entrepreneur since high school and holds a Master's degree in Computer Science.
