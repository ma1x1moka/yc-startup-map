**LinkedIn:** linkedin.com/in/mark-pothen/

Co-founded [Beacon Health](./Beacon%20Health.md) with [Obinna Akahara](./Obinna%20Akahara.md).

Grew up in his mother's primary care practice — the literal founder-market fit origin story. The pain of watching physicians spend hours on administrative tasks (prior auths, screenings, referral coordination) rather than patient care was not a problem he discovered from a case study; it was his childhood environment.

Spent **6 months embedded in a clinic implementing AI** before founding the company — not building a product, but deeply understanding the clinical workflow, EHR quirks, and physician workflow constraints that outsider founders miss.

Prior product and go-to-market roles at early-stage healthcare startups gave Pothen the commercial framework to pair with the clinical depth.

The Accel and Sequoia scout backing pre-YC signals that institutional investors already validated the team's clinical credibility before the YC program added its check — unusual for a healthcare company where clinical due diligence is longer than most YC diligence timelines.
