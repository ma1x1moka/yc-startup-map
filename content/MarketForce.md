MarketForce is a fintech marketplace that empowers neighbourhood merchants in Africa to source, order, and pay for inventory digitally. It also provides a banking wallet with credit facilities, digital payment collection, and agency banking services, allowing merchants to resell digital financial services such as airtime, electricity tokens, and bill payments.

Since its incorporation in 2019, MarketForce has grown rapidly, operating in five Sub‑Saharan African markets and serving more than 200,000 merchants and 180 consumer brands. The company runs an asset‑light model and has built a team of 400 employees based in Nairobi, Kenya, positioning its platform as a SuperApp that blends e‑commerce, banking, and financial services into a single digital experience.

The company remains active and continues to expand its reach across Africa, positioning itself as a key player in the region’s digital commerce and financial inclusion ecosystem.

_Usage:_

"How does MarketForce's banking wallet differ from traditional merchant banking services?"

"MarketForce’s wallet offers instant credit lines and digital payment collection within the same app, letting merchants manage inventory orders and financial services in one place, unlike separate banking apps that require distinct accounts."
