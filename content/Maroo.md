**Website:** https://maroo.us/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/maroo

Maroo was a B2B SaaS company that built a customer relationship management system specifically designed for creatives. The platform operated within the finance and accounting sector, helping users manage their professional relationships and financial workflows.

The company was based in New York City with a remote-friendly team of five members. Maroo was part of the Y Combinator Summer 2021 batch.

Maroo was acquired by Helium Ventures, marking the end of its independent operation as a startup.

_Usage:_

"Why would a creative professional need a dedicated CRM instead of using a general-purpose tool like Salesforce or HubSpot?"

"Maroo tailored its CRM features specifically for the workflows and financial needs of creative professionals, rather than forcing them to adapt to a generic B2B sales tool. By focusing on this specific vertical, it aimed to provide a more intuitive experience for managing client relationships and accounting tasks relevant to that industry."
