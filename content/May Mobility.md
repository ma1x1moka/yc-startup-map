**Website:** http://maymobility.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/may-mobility

May Mobility focuses on the development and deployment of autonomous vehicle technology, specifically designing safe and efficient shuttle solutions that complement existing public transportation options. Based in Ann Arbor, MI, the company has accumulated over 300,000 autonomous rides to date, positioning itself as a leader in the field.

The company aims to make transportation more accessible and reliable while improving road safety and encouraging better land use. By fostering more green, vibrant, and livable spaces, May Mobility seeks to transform both cities and rural areas through its self-driving systems.

With a team of approximately 260 employees, the organization operates at the intersection of industrials, automotive, and robotics, leveraging artificial intelligence to drive sustainable mobility solutions.

_Usage:_

"How does May Mobility's approach to autonomous vehicles differ from consumer-focused robotaxi services?"

"May Mobility focuses on shuttle solutions designed to complement public transportation rather than replacing private cars or offering on-demand rides for individuals. Their goal is to make transit more accessible and reliable in both urban and rural settings, emphasizing safety and sustainable land use over the typical point-to-point consumer ride experience."
