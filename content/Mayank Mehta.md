Co-founded [Pulse](./Pulse.md) as CEO in 2017 with [Anand Thaker](./Anand%20Thaker.md) in San Francisco, part of YC's 2018 batch, building a crowdsourced knowledge marketplace connecting C-Suite technology executives with vendors. Companies including Salesforce, Microsoft, Okta, and Dialpad have relied on Pulse to inform their strategy, content, and marketing teams.

_Usage:_

"Who uses Pulse?"

"C-Suite technology executives share insights on the platform, while companies like Salesforce, Microsoft, Okta, and Dialpad use that data to inform their strategy and marketing."
