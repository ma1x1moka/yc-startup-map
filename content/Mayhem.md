Mayhem provides automatic game tracking, real‑time leaderboards, and global chat built into every community event, making it easy for gamers to play together. The company launched in the Winter 2018 Y Combinator batch and operates from San Francisco with a ten‑person team.

Its integrated features let community events display live scores and chat without separate tools, a capability that attracted attention from larger players. Mayhem was acquired by Niantic in 2020, highlighting the strategic value of its community‑focused gaming platform.

_Usage:_

"Does Mayhem really provide real‑time leaderboards for every community event?"

"Yes. Mayhem embeds automatic game tracking and real‑time leaderboards directly into each community event, so participants can see live scores and chat without needing any additional software."
