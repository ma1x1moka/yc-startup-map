Mecha Health creates proprietary foundation models that process medical images and generate draft radiology reports. Its first model was built in under two months and outperformed Microsoft, Google, and OpenAI on clinical accuracy metrics while being two orders of magnitude smaller and trained on only a quarter of the data.

The company partners with the largest privately owned radiology practice in the United States and a multinational tele‑radiology firm, giving them dedicated models that let radiologists read a scan every five minutes instead of one per hour. By charging per scan, Mecha Health targets a market opportunity estimated at over $40 billion. The team of four operates out of San Francisco, California.
