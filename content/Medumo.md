Medumo, founded by a team of Harvard and Yale‑trained physicians, engineers, designers, and social entrepreneurs, offers CareTours™ – a platform that sends automated, precisely‑timed text and email messages to guide patients through medical procedures. The service provides turn‑by‑turn instructions without disrupting clinical workflow, helping hospitals, ASCs, and practices across all specialties.

By delivering these messages, Medumo dramatically improves procedural completion rates, increases referral conversion, reduces staff burden, and recovers revenue loss for its healthcare customers. The company is based in Boston, employs 12 people, and operates in the Healthcare and Healthcare IT sectors.

Medumo was acquired, as noted in its YC directory entry, underscoring the market’s interest in automated patient navigation solutions.

_Usage:_

"How is Medumo’s CareTours different from a typical appointment reminder service?"

"CareTours goes beyond simple reminders by providing a sequence of timed, context‑specific messages that walk patients through each step of a procedure, rather than just notifying them of an upcoming appointment. This turn‑by‑turn guidance is designed to improve completion rates and reduce staff workload, which standard reminder tools don’t address."
