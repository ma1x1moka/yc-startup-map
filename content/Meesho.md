**Website:** http://www.meesho.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/meesho

Meesho operates as India’s only true online marketplace, designed to make e-commerce accessible for the next billion users. The platform provides sellers with access to millions of customers, a selection from over 30 categories, and essential infrastructure including pan-India logistics, payment services, and customer support.

With a vision to enable 100 million small businesses and individual entrepreneurs to succeed online, Meesho democratizes internet commerce by bringing new products and customers into the digital economy. The company is headquartered in Bengaluru, India, and employs approximately 1,450 people.

Meesho went public, marking a significant milestone in its growth from a YC Summer 2016 batch company to a major player in the B2B and retail sectors.

_Usage:_

"How does Meesho support small-scale sellers compared to traditional e-commerce platforms?"

"Meesho handles the heavy lifting for individual entrepreneurs by providing integrated logistics, payment processing, and customer support, allowing them to focus on sourcing products rather than managing complex supply chain operations."
