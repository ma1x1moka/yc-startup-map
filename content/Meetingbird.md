**Website:** https://www.meetingbird.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/meetingbird

Meetingbird is a B2B productivity platform that provides automated meeting scheduling for individuals and teams. Originating from San Francisco as part of Y Combinator's Summer 2017 batch, the company built scheduling tools to streamline calendar management.

The platform was adopted by teams at companies such as Uber, Shopify, and Yelp. Meetingbird has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"What does Meetingbird offer for team calendar management?"

"Meetingbird provides automated scheduling tools built for individuals and teams. It handles meeting coordination for users across companies like Uber, Shopify, and Yelp to streamline calendar management."
