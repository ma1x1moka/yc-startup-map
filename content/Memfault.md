**Website:** https://memfault.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/memfault

Memfault is the first IoT reliability platform that empowers teams to build more robust devices with software at scale. It offers performance monitoring, device debugging, and OTA update capabilities built into fleets of devices for proactive operations. The SaaS solution helps developers accelerate go-to-market, de-risk product launches, cut product costs, and deliver superior products. Memfault was acquired, underscoring its impact in the IoT reliability space.

_Usage:_

"How does Memfault differ from a typical IoT device management service?"

"Memfault focuses specifically on reliability engineering, providing built-in performance monitoring, debugging tools, and OTA update workflows that let teams proactively manage device health, whereas many standard device management services only offer basic provisioning and connectivity features."
