**Website:** https://memorahealth.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/memora-health

Memora Health helps healthcare organizations digitize and automate complex care workflows, turning existing clinical workflow data into cohesive messaging journeys that guide patients through their care episodes. The platform also automates simple follow‑up tasks directly in the electronic medical record.

By reducing care‑team notifications by nearly 40% and achieving an average Net Promoter Score above 70, Memora Health has improved clinical outcomes across various populations. The company was acquired after demonstrating these results.

_Usage:_

"How does Memora Health’s automation differ from a typical patient reminder system?"

"Memora Health ingests the organization’s existing workflow data and creates automated messaging journeys that not only remind patients but also complete simple follow‑up actions inside the EMR, which cuts care‑team notifications by almost 40%."
