**Website:** http://getmentum.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/mentum

Mentum helps procurement and supply‑chain teams streamline strategic sourcing with AI, turning unstructured data into actionable insights for faster decisions and cost savings. It serves industries such as consumer packaged goods, energy, automotive, and aerospace, and is backed by Google’s Gradient Ventures and Y Combinator. Mentum was acquired, highlighting the market’s interest in AI‑driven procurement solutions.

_Usage:_

"How is Mentum different from a traditional procurement platform?"

"Mentum applies AI to convert unstructured procurement data into actionable insights, enabling faster decision‑making and cost reductions, whereas conventional platforms rely on static rule‑based workflows and manual data handling."
