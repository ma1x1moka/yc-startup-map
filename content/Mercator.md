Mercator is an AI-assisted data analytics platform that writes faster, cheaper, and smarter SQL queries.

The platform was backed by Y Combinator, Tribe Capital, Soma Capital, Valor Equity Partners, Amplify Partners, and Rebel Fund, and operated with a two-person team based in San Francisco and remote. It served B2B customers and was part of the Summer 2022 YC batch.

Mercator is now inactive, having shut down after its initial run.
