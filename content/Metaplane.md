**Website:** https://metaplane.dev | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/metaplane

Metaplane is a data observability platform designed to ensure that business data is reliable and trusted. It helps data teams prevent and detect issues before they escalate into major incidents, such as incorrect revenue reports reaching executive leadership. The company was acquired, marking its exit from the YC ecosystem.

The platform utilizes machine learning for anomaly detection and provides end-to-end column-level lineage to monitor the entire data stack. Users can set up monitoring for their infrastructure within 30 minutes, allowing them to identify root causes and prevent future data incidents.

Based in New York City, Metaplane serves B2B engineering and product teams. Notable users include Bose, Ramp, and Klaviyo, who rely on the platform to maintain data integrity across their operations.

_Usage:_

"How does Metaplane differ from standard logging or monitoring tools?"

"Metaplane focuses specifically on data quality and lineage rather than just system health. It uses ML to detect anomalies in the data itself and tracks column-level lineage across the stack, which helps teams pinpoint exactly where a data issue originated before it affects downstream business metrics."
