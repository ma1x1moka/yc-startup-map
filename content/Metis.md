Metis builds infrastructure enabling AI agents to reliably execute complex tasks in production environments. The company targets B2B customers needing robust agent reliability and operates with a team of 13 engineers across San Francisco and remote locations. Metis was acquired, reflecting its relevance in the AI infrastructure space.

_Usage:_

"What makes Metis’ infrastructure different from other AI agent tooling?"

"Metis focuses specifically on the reliability layer for agents in production, offering services that ensure complex tasks are performed consistently, whereas many other tools emphasize building or deploying agents without that dedicated reliability focus."
