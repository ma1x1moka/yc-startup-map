Founded by [Karim Rahme](./Karim%20Rahme.md) and [Tobias Herber](./Tobias%20Herber.md). Vienna, Austria. Team of 4.

Flagship product **Magic MCP** connects MCP clients — Cursor, Claude Code, Claude Desktop — to 600+ integrations with enterprise-grade security, scaling, observability, and sub-second boot times.

MCP (Model Context Protocol, released by Anthropic) is the emerging standard for how AI agents connect to external tools and APIs. Metorial provides the serverless runtime layer: idle servers hibernate and resume with sub-second cold starts, connections are secured and monitored, enterprise compliance requirements are met.

The Vercel analogy is precise: Vercel didn't invent deployment, it made Next.js reliable at enterprise scale. Metorial does the same for MCP — the protocol exists and works at small scale; Metorial makes it work where enterprises require it.

Natural stack with [Multifactor](./Multifactor.md) (what agents are allowed to do) and [Browser Use](./Browser%20Use.md) (how agents control browsers). [S2](./S2.md) handles the streaming data layer beneath.
