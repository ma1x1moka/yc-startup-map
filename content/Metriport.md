**Website:** https://metriport.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/metriport

Metriport provides real-time access to medical data for 340 million individuals across the US by connecting to all major healthcare IT systems. The open-source platform manages dozens of connections across national and state HIEs, QHINs, ADT networks, pharmacies, labs, and EHRs. It matches, extracts, standardizes, deduplicates, and enriches these records into a unified data model accessible via a single API, data warehouse, or directly within an EHR.

Founded in 2022 and headquartered in San Francisco, the company processes 4.2 billion network requests a month and returns the first structured record in under 15 seconds. Beyond longitudinal clinical data, Metriport offers ambient monitoring to alert providers in real time during patient transitions of care. It also delivers AI-driven insights, including medical record summarization, condition suspecting, and surfacing care gaps.

The platform supports teams across care delivery, value-based programs, and health tech platforms, with users including Amazon One Medical, Strive Health, Color, Sollis Health, and Canvas Medical.

_Usage:_

"How does Metriport handle the fragmentation of patient data across different EHRs and HIEs?"

"It acts as a middleware layer that connects to dozens of sources, including national HIEs, QHINs, and direct EHR integrations. Instead of requiring you to build separate connectors for each system, it ingests the raw data, deduplicates it, and standardizes it into a single unified data model. You then access this clean, structured data through a single API or data warehouse, which also includes AI features like record summarization and care gap detection."
