**Website:** http://www.middesk.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/middesk

Middesk builds software that gives financial‑service firms accurate, complete business information for efficient onboarding decisions. Its Identity product supplies the data needed to verify companies quickly. The company also offers an Agent product that streams the filing process for employers with state and federal agencies, simplifying the establishment of businesses nationwide. Customers such as Affirm, Brex, Plaid, Mercury, Divvy, Rippling, and Gusto rely on these tools. Based in San Francisco and backed by Sequoia Capital, Accel Partners, and Y Combinator, Middesk serves the B2B operations market with a 130‑person team.

_Usage:_

"What exactly does Middesk’s Identity service provide that’s different from a generic business data API?"

"It delivers verified, complete business identity data specifically curated for financial‑services onboarding, allowing companies like Affirm and Brex to make efficient compliance decisions without building their own data pipelines."
