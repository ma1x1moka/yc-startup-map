Miden builds a modern infrastructure that enables businesses in Sub‑Saharan Africa to launch card programs and other financial products. By cutting integration time, reducing card fraud, and improving stability, the company quickly rose into the top five in total payment volume among card‑issuing fintechs in the region within eight months of launch. The firm aims to create the first comprehensive fintech and banking software stack for Africa, starting with its card issuer‑processor platform, and serves B2B customers through APIs from its base in Ikeja, Lagos, Nigeria.
