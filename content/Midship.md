**Website:** https://midship.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/midship

Midship is rebuilding internal audit from the ground up by deploying AI agents to handle SOX testing. These agents perform audit work end to end, allowing human auditors to shift their focus toward higher-value risk assessment and advisory tasks. The company was based in San Francisco and operated with a small team of three.

Midship was acquired, marking a significant milestone for its AI-driven approach to financial compliance.

_Usage:_

"How does Midship handle SOX testing compared to traditional manual audits?"

"Midship uses AI agents to execute the entire SOX testing process end to end, rather than just assisting with specific steps. This automation gives auditors the leverage to move away from repetitive testing tasks and concentrate on higher-value activities like risk analysis and advisory work."
