**Website:** https://mymindbase.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tradex

Mindbase is an AI avatar platform that launches digital personas, both based on real humans and entirely artificial, to operate as influencers on Instagram and YouTube. The company equips each AI influencer with dedicated communication channels, including email, phone numbers, and WhatsApp accounts, to facilitate deep relationship building with audiences similar to human creators. The platform currently generates over 5 million daily views through a network of more than 10 AI influencers.

The business model involves brands paying monthly fees for access to these AI influencers, with reported costs ranging from $1,000 to $4,000 per month per entity. Mindbase cites a case where a brand partner achieved a 15x return on ad spend, generating $45,000 in sales from a $3,000 investment. The company projects that generating $20,000 annually per influencer could scale the business to $100 million in annual recurring revenue with a network of 5,000 entities.

Based in San Francisco and operating remotely, the seven-person team consists of IIT alumni and repeat founders. They previously built TradeX, a prediction market platform that reached $40 million in monthly trading volume with 5 million users, leveraging trend tracking and influencer partnership expertise that they are now applying to the AI influencer space.

_Usage:_

"How does Mindbase differentiate its AI influencers from standard chatbots or static virtual avatars?"

"Unlike static avatars or simple chatbots, Mindbase assigns each AI influencer a full suite of communication tools, including a unique email address, phone number, and WhatsApp account. This allows the digital persona to engage in multi-channel, persistent interactions that mimic the depth of relationships formed with human creators, rather than just posting content."
