**Website:** https://mindee.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/mindee

Mindee offers a universal self-service platform designed to help software builders automate the understanding of documents through deep learning. The company focuses on providing a single API that grants applications the ability to process document content with human-like accuracy, addressing a critical step in automation across multiple industries.

The platform operates on three main pillars: a self-service environment where users can train their own models, a catalog of pre-built use cases optimized for critical markets like accounting, AP automation, expense management, and KYC, and an internal capability to develop tailor-made AI solutions for large customers with specific needs.

With a team of approximately 60 people, Mindee is based in Paris, San Francisco, and London. It serves the B2B sector by providing developer tools and APIs focused on automation and artificial intelligence.

_Usage:_

"How does Mindee differ from a standard OCR library?"

"While standard OCR libraries focus primarily on recognizing text characters, Mindee provides a higher-level API for document understanding. It uses deep learning to not just read the text but to interpret the context and structure of the document, allowing developers to extract specific data points relevant to use cases like KYC or expense management without building custom models from scratch."
