**Website:** https://mineralsoft.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/mineralsoft

MineralSoft is a data management platform built specifically for oil and gas mineral rights and royalty management. Based in Austin, Texas, and launching as part of the Y Combinator Winter 2016 batch, the company combines specialized software with professional services to help asset owners track and optimize their energy holdings.

The platform caters to a wide spectrum of clients managing oil and gas interests, including investment funds, family offices, individual owners, and financial advisors. It aims to streamline administrative oversight and help users maximize the value of their energy portfolios.

MineralSoft has since been acquired. Prior to its acquisition, the company grew to a team of 35 employees.

_Usage:_

"Who uses MineralSoft and what does the platform actually provide?"

"MineralSoft is designed for investment funds, family offices, individual owners, and advisors who manage oil and gas interests. Rather than being purely self-serve software, the platform pairs its specialized data management tools for mineral rights and royalties with professional services to assist users with asset management."
