**Website:** http://miso.kr | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/miso

Miso operates a marketplace that streamlines the process of booking home services, aiming to make the experience as straightforward as purchasing physical goods online. The platform offers a diverse catalog of over 1,000 services, ranging from cleaning and moving to interior remodeling and lessons.

The company has scaled significantly, fulfilling more than $1.5 billion in transactions for over 1.5 million households. Based in Seoul, South Korea, Miso employs a team of approximately 300 people and utilizes artificial intelligence to enhance its home services operations.

_Usage:_

"How does Miso handle the complexity of booking non-standard services like remodeling compared to simple product purchases?"

"Miso abstracts the complexity by treating service bookings like product transactions, offering a standardized interface for over 1,000 different service types including cleaning, moving, and interior remodeling. This approach has allowed them to process over $1.5 billion in transactions for more than 1.5 million households, suggesting the model effectively handles the variability of home services."
