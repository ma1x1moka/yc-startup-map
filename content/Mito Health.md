**Website:** https://mitohealth.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/mito-health

Mito Health is a San Francisco-based startup that operates an at-cost lab testing marketplace designed to incentivize users to make better health decisions rather than simply buying more tests. The platform uses a 60-second quiz to provide personalized recommendations on which tests to take, which to skip, and the exact cost, ensuring members pay the published lab price while non-members pay a 10% premium.

The service includes clinician-reviewed next steps, longitudinal tracking, and an AI Concierge that helps build personalized test panels. Mito Health positions its business model as a counterpoint to traditional health companies that profit from increased test volume, instead deriving value from helping users avoid unnecessary spending. The company is active in the Summer 2024 YC batch with a team of five.

_Usage:_

"How does Mito Health's pricing model differ from standard direct-to-consumer lab testing services?"

"Unlike most health companies that profit when you buy more tests, Mito Health profits when you make better decisions, often meaning fewer tests. It offers every lab test at its published cost, where members pay exactly that amount and non-members pay a 10% premium. The platform uses a quiz and an AI Concierge to recommend what to test and what to skip, aiming to reduce unnecessary spending rather than drive volume."
