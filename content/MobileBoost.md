MobileBoost provides AI-native mobile testing solutions designed to help engineering teams achieve higher test coverage faster. Its core product, GPT Driver, utilizes artificial intelligence to automate end-to-end mobile app testing, reducing the need for manual QA resources and tedious maintenance.

The platform aims to accelerate release cycles by providing reliable and consistent results, allowing teams to ship higher-quality applications. Notably, Duolingo has cited a partnership with MobileBoost that reduced their manual regression testing by 70%.

Based in Berlin with a remote team of 15, MobileBoost serves B2B clients in engineering and product design. The company is trusted by major industry leaders including Spotify, Netflix, Salesforce, and Groupon.

_Usage:_

"How does MobileBoost handle the flakiness issues common in traditional E2E mobile testing?"

"Instead of relying on brittle, hard-coded scripts that break with UI changes, MobileBoost uses its GPT Driver to automate testing via AI. This approach is designed to eliminate tedious maintenance and provide consistent results, as demonstrated by Duolingo’s report of reducing manual regression testing by 70%."
