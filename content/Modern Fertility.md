**Website:** https://www.modernfertility.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/modern-fertility

Modern Fertility is a women’s health company that makes personalized reproductive health information more accessible earlier in life. It offers at-home laboratory tests that were previously confined to fertility clinics, allowing women to gain powerful insights into their reproductive health from the comfort of their own homes.

The company aims to close the fertility information gap by empowering women to own the decisions impacting their bodies and futures. Based in San Francisco, the team of 25 has raised $22 million from investors including Forerunner Ventures, Maveron, Union Square Ventures, First Round Capital, and Y Combinator.

Modern Fertility was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Modern Fertility differ from traditional fertility clinic visits?"

"Modern Fertility brings the same laboratory tests typically performed in fertility clinics to the user's home. This removes the need for in-person appointments and makes personalized reproductive health data more accessible for women who may not be ready for children yet but want to understand their options earlier."
