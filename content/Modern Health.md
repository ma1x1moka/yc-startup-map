**Website:** https://www.modernhealth.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/modern-health

Modern Health is the leading mental wellness platform that combines clinically validated assessments, personalized stress management support, an international network of certified behavioral health coaches, and licensed therapists to deliver mental health support across a broad range of individual needs. It empowers employers to help employees be less stressed, more engaged, and happier through evidence‑based technology and professional support.

The company has raised over $170M from investors including Battery Ventures, Kleiner Perkins, Founders Fund, and John Doerr, and it supports more than 200 companies worldwide such as Lyft, Pixar, and Okta. With a team of 375 people operating from Boston, San Francisco, and remotely, Modern Health serves a global client base in the healthcare services sector.

_Usage:_

"How is Modern Health different from a typical employee assistance program?"

"Modern Health goes beyond standard EAPs by offering clinically validated assessments and a personalized stress‑management plan, plus access to a vetted network of certified coaches and licensed therapists, all delivered through its evidence‑based platform."
