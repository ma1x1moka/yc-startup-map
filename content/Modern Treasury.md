Modern Treasury offers tools that automate the full cycle of money movement—from payment initiation, through approvals, to reconciliation. It makes these capabilities accessible through a web application or API.

The platform serves B2B finance and accounting needs, positioning itself as a fintech solution that streamlines payment operations. With a team of 160 located in San Francisco, it supports businesses in managing payments more efficiently.

_Usage:_

"How does Modern Treasury differ from a traditional payment processor?"

"Modern Treasury not only initiates payments but also handles approvals and reconciliation, exposing the entire workflow through an API and web interface, whereas many traditional processors focus solely on transaction execution."
