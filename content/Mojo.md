**Website:** https://mojo-app.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/mojo

Mojo is a mobile application that lets creators produce pro‑quality videos for social media platforms. The app combines design tools with a subscription model to deliver a polished video‑editing experience. Mojo was acquired after graduating from Y Combinator’s Winter 2018 batch.

Based in Paris, the company grew to a team of about 20 people and operates in the consumer and content industries, focusing on video creation and design tools.

_Usage:_

"Do I need a paid subscription to export videos from Mojo?"

"Yes, Mojo operates on a subscription model, so exporting finished videos requires an active paid subscription."
