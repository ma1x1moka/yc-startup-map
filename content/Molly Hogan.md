Co-founded [Level](./Level.md) in January 2021 with [Vladimir Korshin](./Vladimir%20Korshin.md) and [Asa Schachar](./Asa%20Schachar.md), part of YC's Summer 2021 batch. Level built a tech-driven underwriting process for early-stage fintech companies raising debt. Before Level, Hogan led new and international product initiatives at Amazon for over seven years. After Level was acquired by Vouch in 2023, she became a Senior Manager of Product at Remitly.
