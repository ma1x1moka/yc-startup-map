**Website:** https://momence.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/momence

Momence provides a unified platform for scheduling and processing payments for local experiences and leisure activities. The company operates in the consumer, travel, and tourism sectors, helping providers manage bookings and transactions efficiently. With a team of 75 people based in San Francisco and remote locations, Momence streamlined the operational workflow for experience businesses. The company was acquired, marking its exit from the independent startup phase.

_Usage:_

"How does Momence handle the transaction flow for experience providers?"

"It combines scheduling and payment processing into a single system, allowing providers to manage bookings and collect payments without needing separate tools for each function."
