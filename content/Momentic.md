**Website:** https://momentic.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/momentic

Momentic is an agentic quality platform built for web and mobile applications. Operating out of San Francisco with a team of 15, the company enables engineering teams to write end-to-end tests in plain English and execute them inside their continuous integration pipelines.

To reduce testing overhead, Momentic uses AI agents to automatically triage failures, maintain test coverage, and catch regressions before software updates reach end users. Over 100 teams—including Notion, Bilt, Quora, Xero, and Webflow—use the platform to test their critical user flows.

_Usage:_

"How do engineering teams write and manage tests in Momentic?"

"Engineers author end-to-end test cases in plain English and run them directly within CI. From there, Momentic's AI agents assist with triage, maintain overall test coverage, and flag regressions before code makes it to production."
