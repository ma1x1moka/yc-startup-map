Momentus is a space infrastructure services company that positions itself as a first mover, offering foundational services that enable businesses to flourish in space.
With an experienced team of aerospace, propulsion, and robotics engineers, Momentus designs and operates cost‑effective and energy‑efficient in‑space transport and service vehicles that utilize water plasma propulsion technology.
The company has secured service agreements with numerous private satellite operators, government agencies, and research organizations, and it is a public company.

_Usage:_

"How does Momentus's water plasma propulsion compare to conventional chemical rockets for in‑space transport?"

"Momentus's water plasma propulsion uses water as propellant, generating thrust through plasma generation, which is more energy‑efficient and cost‑effective than traditional chemical rockets, allowing it to operate in‑space vehicles with lower mass and fuel requirements."
