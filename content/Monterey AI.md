**Website:** http://www.monterey.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/monterey-ai

Monterey AI helps companies improve engagement and reduce churn by analyzing both qualitative feedback and quantitative data from multiple sources. The platform sets up user feedback channels and automatically collects this information to generate specific actions for product improvement.

Based in San Francisco and operating remotely, the company focused on B2B engineering and product design workflows. Its approach combined generative AI with productivity tools to streamline how product teams interpret user sentiment and behavior.

Monterey AI was acquired, marking the end of its independent operations as a YC-backed startup.

_Usage:_

"How does Monterey AI differ from standard survey tools or raw data dashboards?"

"Unlike tools that only collect data or display metrics, Monterey AI actively analyzes the mix of qualitative feedback and quantitative signals to tell you exactly what actions to take. It automates the collection process across multiple sources and uses generative AI to turn that raw input into concrete product insights for reducing churn."
