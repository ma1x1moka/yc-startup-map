**Website:** https://www.moonvalley.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/moonvalley

Moonvalley is developing a next-generation creative studio that leverages the most capable video and image foundational models. The platform is designed to be the central hub where generative image and video experiences are built, targeting the consumer and virtual/augmented reality sectors.

Based in Toronto, Canada, the company has grown to a team of 95 people. It is backed by Y Combinator and Khosla Ventures, reflecting investor confidence in its approach to AI-driven creative tools.

As an active startup from the Winter 2021 batch, Moonvalley focuses on providing the infrastructure and tools necessary for creators to produce high-quality generative media.

_Usage:_

"How does Moonvalley differ from general-purpose image generators like Midjourney or DALL-E?"

"Moonvalley positions itself not just as a model, but as a full creative studio environment. While tools like Midjourney focus on generating individual assets, Moonvalley aims to be the place where entire generative video and image experiences are constructed, likely offering more integrated workflows for complex creative projects rather than just single-prompt generation."
