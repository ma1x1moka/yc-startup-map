Muffin Data is a turnkey data platform and reporting solution built for consumer packaged goods (CPG) brands. It combines proprietary data sets with automated ingestion from industry‑specific sources to deliver a clear, unified view of in‑store sales performance, product distribution, and supply‑chain health. The service enables CPG companies to monitor sales and distribution metrics without stitching together multiple data feeds.

Muffin Data was acquired, which highlights its value as a specialized analytics tool for the CPG sector. With a small team of two, the company has packaged complex data workflows into an easy‑to‑use platform for its B2B customers.

_Usage:_

"How does Muffin Data’s data collection differ from traditional market‑research providers?"

"Muffin Data pulls together proprietary data sets and automatically ingests industry‑specific sources, rather than relying on manual surveys or third‑party reports, so it can present a continuously updated, unified view of sales, distribution and supply‑chain health for CPG brands."
