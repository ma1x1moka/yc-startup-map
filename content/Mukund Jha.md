Co-founded [Emergent](./Emergent.md) as CEO with his twin brother [Madhav Jha](./Madhav%20Jha.md), part of YC's Summer 2024 batch, building an AI-powered platform that turns plain-English descriptions into fully functional web and mobile apps without coding. Jha dropped out of a PhD program to found the company, is a graduate of Columbia Engineering, and previously co-founded and served as CTO of Dunzo. Emergent raised $100M from Khosla Ventures, SoftBank, Google, and others, reaching $50M ARR within 7 months of public launch.
