**Website:** https://insurestack.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/mulligan

Mulligan, now operating as Insure Stack, provides an end-to-end AI automation platform specifically designed for commercial insurance brokers. The system automates core operational tasks such as quoting, policy checking, and proposal generation, claiming to reduce manual effort by up to 90%.

The company was part of Y Combinator's Winter 2023 batch and is based in San Francisco. It was acquired in 2026, marking the conclusion of its independent operation.

With a small team of five, the company focused on leveraging generative AI and workflow automation to streamline document-heavy processes within the insurance industry.

_Usage:_

"How does Mulligan handle the complexity of commercial insurance policies compared to manual review?"

"It uses generative AI to automate the entire workflow, including quoting, policy checking, and proposals. The system is designed to handle these document-intensive tasks with up to 90% automation, significantly reducing the manual labor required for commercial brokers."
