**Website:** https://www.mutinyhq.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/mutiny

Mutiny is an AI agent that creates customer-facing content in minutes, enabling teams to produce ABM campaigns, executive business cases, and ROI reports without waiting on other departments.

The company serves B2B sales teams and has customers such as Figma, Rippling, Uber, and Snowflake. It is backed by Sequoia Capital, Tiger Global, Insight Partners, and Y Combinator.

Founded in Summer 2018, Mutiny is headquartered in New York City and has a team of about 20 employees.

_Usage:_

"How does Mutiny speed up the sales cycle?"

"By generating ABM campaigns, executive business cases, and ROI reports in minutes, Mutiny eliminates the need to wait on other teams, allowing deals to move from cold to closed faster."
