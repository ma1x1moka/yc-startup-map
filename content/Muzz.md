**Website:** https://muzz.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/muzz

Muzz is a mobile application designed specifically for single Muslims seeking marriage partners, operating on the premise that the community prioritizes marriage over casual dating. As the largest platform in this niche, it claims over 10 million members and has facilitated more than 500,000 weddings, addressing a significant gap in the global Muslim demographic.

Headquartered in London, the company was the first Muslim-centric startup to be backed by Y Combinator in Summer 2017. It has since raised $9 million in funding from Silicon Valley and global investors and maintains a team of 104 people. The platform has received coverage from major media outlets including the BBC, The New York Times, and TechCrunch.

By focusing on high-quality mobile experiences for a specific cultural and religious group, Muzz distinguishes itself in the crowded social networking space, aiming to transform how Muslims meet and marry across the globe.

_Usage:_

"How does Muzz differ from generalist dating apps like Tinder or Bumble in terms of user intent and target demographic?"

"Muzz is explicitly designed for Muslims who are looking to marry rather than date casually, which aligns with the cultural and religious norms of its target audience. While generalist apps often cater to a broader range of relationship goals, Muzz focuses on facilitating long-term commitments, resulting in a user base of over 10 million members who are specifically seeking a marriage partner within the Muslim community."
