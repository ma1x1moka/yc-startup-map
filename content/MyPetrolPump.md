**Website:** https://mypetrolpump.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/mypetrolpump

India has 200 million vehicles but only 60 000 gas stations, creating long queues and congested roads while new stations are impractical due to high land prices. MyPetrolPump solves this by bringing fuel directly to doorsteps, targeting large‑volume fleet customers such as schools, cab rentals, freight logistics, tours, hospitals, hotels and real‑estate operators. The company has 5 000 registered customers, can serve 1 200 of them with its limited fleet, and has attracted 400 franchisee requests across the country.

Founded by a Shell veteran with nine years of global oil‑marketing experience, MyPetrolPump operates out of Bengaluru, Karnataka, and serves the B2B supply‑chain and logistics sector. With a team of 42, it has been acquired, marking its success in addressing a critical infrastructure gap in India.

_Usage:_

"How does MyPetrolPump manage fuel delivery logistics compared to traditional gas stations?"

"MyPetrolPump uses mobile refueling vehicles that travel directly to fleet customers, eliminating the need for vehicles to drive to fixed stations. This approach reduces dead mileage and improves productivity for large‑volume users, while the company’s founder’s experience in oil marketing ensures efficient supply‑chain management."
