**Website:** https://mystery.org | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/mystery-org

Mystery.org began by compiling the 150 most common science questions children ask teachers, forming its Mystery Science collection. The platform reached more than 4 million children in 50 % of U.S. elementary schools, and it has sold thousands of schools without employing a single salesperson. Backed by Y Combinator, Learn Capital, and Reach Capital, the company has expanded its scope to answer all other questions children have, receiving over 500 k inquiries in the last 18 months and producing video explanations for each.

The company’s mission is to cultivate a generation of better thinkers by providing clear, visual explanations—essentially a visual Wikipedia for kids. Mystery.org was acquired in 2024, marking its success in the education sector.
