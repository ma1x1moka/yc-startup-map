Nurx is a telemedicine startup that increases access to healthcare by offering prescription birth control and PrEP online.

The company issues prescriptions remotely and ships the medication directly to users’ doors, operating out of New York City and San Francisco with a team of roughly 300 employees.

Nurx was acquired, highlighting its impact in the consumer health services space.

_Usage:_

"How does Nurx’s service differ from getting birth control at a traditional pharmacy?"

"Nurx lets patients obtain a prescription online and have the medication shipped straight to their door, eliminating the need to visit a clinic or pharmacy in person."
