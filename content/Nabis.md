Nabis is the #1 licensed cannabis wholesaling platform in California, distributing over $1 billion of cannabis annually and supporting more than 500 exclusive brands. It supplies the entire network of state‑licensed dispensaries with top‑tier products.

The company provides lightning‑fast fulfillment, warehousing, payment processing, financing, data analytics, and sales and marketing services, enabling brands and retailers to innovate, launch, and scale. With a team of 700 and headquarters in New York City, Nabis combines B2B supply‑chain expertise with fintech capabilities to streamline the cannabis market.
