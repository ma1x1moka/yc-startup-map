Nabla Bio uses AI and massively parallel experimentation to design antibodies that precisely bind disease targets at the right location while minimizing manufacturability and toxicity risks. It serves pharmaceutical and biotech companies to bring more antibody therapies to patients. The company is a well‑funded, revenue‑generating, bilingual team of wet‑ and dry‑lab scientists with eight employees based in Boston, MA, USA.
