**Website:** https://naborly.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/naborly

Naborly is a credit bureau focused on the renting generation, reinventing how credit reports and scores work for the rental industry. It helps landlords assess prospective tenants and lets tenants build credit simply by paying rent.

The service leverages artificial intelligence and fintech techniques to generate rental‑specific credit data, bridging a gap between traditional credit reporting and the needs of landlords and renters. Naborly operates out of Toronto with a team of about 25 employees.

Naborly was acquired, marking its exit from the Y Combinator portfolio.

_Usage:_

"How does Naborly’s credit scoring differ from traditional credit bureaus?"

"Naborly bases its scores on rent‑payment behavior rather than the broader credit history used by traditional bureaus, so tenants can build credit simply by paying rent on time, which is a data point most conventional credit reports don’t consider."
