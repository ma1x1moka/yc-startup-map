**Website:** https://www.nasdisc.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/nasdisc

Nasdisc is a San Francisco-based marketplace designed to facilitate the buying and selling of physical music media, including vinyl, cassettes, and CDs. The company addresses a fragmented secondary market where existing platforms often lack robust transaction processing capabilities, despite facilitating hundreds of millions of dollars in annual exchanges. By taking a 12% commission on sales, Nasdisc positions itself to capture value from a rapidly expanding industry where record prices have been skyrocketing due to supply constraints and high demand.

The startup was founded by a team of four software engineers who previously worked at Google and other tech roles, leveraging their technical background to build a more user-friendly trading platform. They launched their website in late 2021, quickly amassing $50,000 in listings and generating initial weekly sales of $1,000. The company has since been acquired, marking a successful exit for this niche e-commerce venture.

_Usage:_

"How does Nasdisc handle the transaction process compared to other vinyl marketplaces?"

"Unlike other dedicated marketplaces that may not process transactions directly, Nasdisc handles the full transaction flow, taking a 12% cut of each sale. This integrated approach aims to solve the usability and payment friction found in existing platforms, making it easier for buyers and sellers to complete deals on physical media."
