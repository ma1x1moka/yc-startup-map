Co-founded [Continue](./Continue.md) as CTO with [Ty Dunn](./Ty%20Dunn.md) in June 2023, building an open-source, customizable coding autopilot that continuously learns from development data. Sesti studied math and physics at MIT (class of 2024), was the first hire at Mayan (YC W21), and previously worked on mission control software at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory.
