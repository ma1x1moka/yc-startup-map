**LinkedIn:** linkedin.com/in/naveen-qureshi

Co-founded [Sable](./Sable.md) in 2019 with Towers Wilen, Andrej Paule, and [Joseph Finlayson](./Joseph%20Finlayson.md), meeting her co-founders at Columbia Business School (Laidlaw Scholar MBA). After moving to New York and being unable to get a US credit card despite a strong Canadian credit history, Qureshi built Sable to let internationals open US bank accounts and get credit without a Social Security Number. Sable grew to a few hundred thousand accounts and was sold; she's now co-founder and CEO of Rapidfolio, AI for fintechs and banks.
