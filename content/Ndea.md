**Website:** ndea.com | **Twitter:** @ndea | **YC slug:** ndea-com

Founded by [Francois Chollet](./Francois%20Chollet.md) and [Mike Knoop](./Mike%20Knoop.md). Team of 15. YC partner: Tyler Bosmeny.

Entered YC W26 having already raised **$43M** — the largest pre-program raise in the entire batch. Goal: create a "factory for rapid scientific advancement" capable of autonomous invention and discovery across drug discovery, robotics, energy, and space.

Ndea's thesis diverges sharply from the mainstream scaling consensus. Where frontier labs bet on larger models trained on more data, Ndea bets on **program synthesis**: systems that discover and compose explicit programs to solve problems, rather than pattern-matching from training data.

Chollet and Knoop are also co-founders of the ARC Prize Foundation — offering $1M annual prizes for AI systems that pass the ARC-AGI benchmark, a test that current LLMs consistently fail. Ndea is the commercial expression of that conviction.

[Synthetic Sciences](./Synthetic%20Sciences.md) and [Origin Bio](./Origin%20Bio.md) operate in the same AI-for-science space from different angles; Ndea's contribution is the foundational model architecture rather than the application layer.

_Avoid:_ Treating Ndea as another LLM startup. They are explicitly building something orthogonal to the transformer scaling paradigm.

_Usage:_

"Is Ndea competing with OpenAI and Anthropic?"

"Not directly. They're betting the path to AGI runs through program synthesis, not scaling transformers. If they're right, the frontier labs are optimising the wrong variable."
