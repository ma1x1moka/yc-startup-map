Co-founded [Lume](./Lume.md) with Robert Ross and Nicolas Machado, whom he met in their freshman dorm at Stanford. Before Lume, Zewde brought experience from roles at Opendoor and GGV Capital. As engineers, the three co-founders had personally felt the pain of manually mapping data between systems, which became the problem Lume set out to solve.

_Usage:_

"What problem does Lume solve?"

"It automates the tedious, manual work of mapping data between different schemas, using AI to generate and maintain custom data integrations automatically."
