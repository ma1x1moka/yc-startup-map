**Website:** http://www.neowize.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/neowize

NeoWize developed a novel machine learning approach that utilizes neural networks and adaptive input to create more and better data, specifically designed to enhance predictive power when working with small datasets. By changing how deep learning algorithms handle data availability, the company aimed to increase the effectiveness of models in data-scarce environments.

The technology was applied to help e-commerce sites understand individual buyer preferences, enabling the creation of a superior user experience from the very first visit. As a B2B retail solution, NeoWize focused on personalization to drive engagement for online stores.

The company, based in Tel Aviv, Israel, was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does NeoWize handle the problem of limited user data on new e-commerce sites?"

"Instead of relying solely on the limited historical data available from a new visitor, NeoWize uses neural networks and adaptive input to generate additional synthetic data. This process creates a richer dataset that improves the predictive power of the model, allowing the site to personalize the experience more effectively from the first visit rather than waiting for enough real-world interactions to accumulate."
