Nephrogen is a Stanford biotech spin‑out focused on curative gene therapies for kidney and pancreatic diseases, conditions that affect 1 in 7 Americans and lack approved gene‑based treatments. Its platform blends AI and high‑throughput screening to design safer, more efficient, and cost‑effective gene delivery vehicles targeting the kidney and pancreas, attracting non‑dilutive investment from seven major pharmaceutical companies, including Merck KGaA, Novartis, and Bristol Myers Squibb. With a lean team of five based in New York City, Nephrogen continues to advance its pipeline toward clinical development.
