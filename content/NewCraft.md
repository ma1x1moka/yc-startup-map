**Website:** https://www.newcraft.io/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/newcraft

NewCraft provides a universal API that lets developers connect to employee platforms such as Workday, ADP, and Paylocity, standardizing data across these systems. It reduces the need for costly custom integrations. The company offers a modern developer experience, including test environments, documentation, and security features, aiming to cut the typical $10M development expense and years of business development effort. NewCraft was acquired. The team consists of two remote engineers, and the product targets B2B engineering and product design teams needing streamlined HR and payroll data access.

_Usage:_

"Does NewCraft’s API simply forward calls to each payroll provider, or does it provide additional functionality?"

"NewCraft’s API goes beyond a thin wrapper: it normalizes data from Workday, ADP, and Paylocity into a single schema, supplies test environments, comprehensive docs, and built‑in security, which together lower integration costs and development time compared with building separate integrations."
