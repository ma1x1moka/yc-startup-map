**Website:** https://www.newfront.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/newfront-insurance

Newfront builds a modern insurance brokerage that delivers transparent, real‑time data to reduce total cost of risk and provide deeper insights for its clients. It serves 20% of U.S. unicorns, hundreds of YC‑backed startups, and more than 150 public companies with business insurance and total‑rewards solutions. Headquartered in San Francisco with over 800 employees and offices across the United States, the company was acquired after establishing a sizable presence in the fintech and insurance sectors.

_Usage:_

"How does Newfront achieve lower insurance costs compared to traditional brokers?"

"Newfront leverages transparent, real‑time data to give clients clearer risk assessments, which translates into lower total cost of risk and more actionable insights, a model that differentiates it from legacy brokers that rely on slower, less visible data flows."
