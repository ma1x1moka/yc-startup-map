**Website:** https://nexa.farm/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/nexa-labs

Nexa Labs develops implantable microchips that gather comprehensive biometrics from cattle, surpassing existing market solutions.

The accompanying AI‑driven animal health engine delivers early disease detection, reproductive insights, GPS tracking, and more, enabling farmers to save time and money.

By amassing the world’s largest cattle health dataset, Nexa transforms cattle farming into a science, securing global food supply and boosting economic output. Nexa Labs was acquired in 2025.

_Usage:_

"How does Nexa’s implantable microchips differ from other livestock monitoring solutions?"

"Nexa’s chips gather more comprehensive biometrics than anything else on the market, feeding an AI engine that provides early disease detection, reproductive insights, and GPS tracking—features that set it apart from conventional GPS‑only or basic biometric tags."
