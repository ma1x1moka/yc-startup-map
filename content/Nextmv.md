**Website:** https://nextmv.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/nextmv

Nextmv provides a DecisionOps platform designed to accelerate optimization AI teams by offering tools for the deployment, testing, CI/CD, collaboration, and management of decision models. The company enables developers to create scalable, custom decision services featuring unique API endpoints and options to integrate with popular solvers and modeling solutions.

Based in Philadelphia, PA, and operating remotely, Nextmv serves the B2B engineering sector with a focus on logistics and supply chain applications. The company was acquired, marking a significant milestone in its trajectory within the optimization AI space.

_Usage:_

"How does Nextmv differ from just using a solver library directly in my application?"

"Nextmv acts as a DecisionOps layer that wraps around solvers, providing the necessary infrastructure for deployment, testing, and CI/CD that you would otherwise have to build yourself. It allows you to expose your optimization models as scalable decision services with unique API endpoints, handling the integration with popular solvers and modeling solutions so your team can focus on the logic rather than the plumbing."
