Co-founded [Lume](./Lume.md) with Robert Ross and Nebyou Zewde, whom he met during his first year at Stanford studying Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, where he also served as Class President. Before Lume, Machado worked as a Product Manager at Meta on Oculus VR and gained venture capital experience at Romulus Capital.
