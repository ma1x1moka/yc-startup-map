Co-founded [Central](./Central.md) with [Josh Wymer](./Josh%20Wymer.md) and [Pranav Kashyap](./Pranav%20Kashyap.md) in January 2023, focusing on product and design, part of YC's Summer 2024 batch. Modi is a two-time YC founder, having previously co-founded Clayboard, a virtual workshop platform backed by YC's Winter 2020 batch, before starting Central, which was later acquired by Mercury.
