Co-founded [Within](./Within.md) as CTO with [Andrew Antos](./Andrew%20Antos.md), meeting him in MIT's 15.390 Entrepreneurship class as an undergrad (CS, 2018). Before founding the company, Nadhamuni worked on AI projects at Flipkart (building fraud-detection systems), Massachusetts General Hospital, and Airware. He was named to Forbes' 30 Under 30 in AI in 2025.
