**Website:** https://www.nocap.so | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/no-cap

No Cap built the world’s first AI agent that both coaches founders and makes investments on their behalf. The platform operates in the fintech space, applying artificial intelligence to the fundraising process.

The company was acquired after its Winter 2023 YC batch, reflecting the market’s interest in AI‑driven investing tools. It maintains a small team of five people and operates from San Francisco with remote staff.

_Usage:_

"How does No Cap’s AI agent decide which founders to back compared to a traditional venture firm?"

"No Cap’s AI agent evaluates founders by using its coaching interactions to assess readiness and potential, then makes investment decisions automatically, whereas a traditional VC relies on human partners to source and judge deals."
