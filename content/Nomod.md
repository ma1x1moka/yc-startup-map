**Website:** https://nomod.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/nomod

Nomod is a Dubai-based fintech company that operates the leading payments platform for small businesses in the United Arab Emirates. The company supports over 55,000 merchants and processes more than $500 million in annualized total payment volume.

With a team of 68 people, Nomod focuses on providing essential payment infrastructure to the region's small business sector. It remains an active participant in the YC Summer 2021 batch.

_Usage:_

"How does Nomod compare to general-purpose payment processors in the UAE?"

"Nomod is specifically positioned as the leading platform for small businesses in the UAE, rather than a general-purpose processor for all enterprise sizes. It has scaled to support over 55,000 merchants and handle $500M+ in annualized transaction volume, indicating it is a primary choice for SMEs in that specific market."
