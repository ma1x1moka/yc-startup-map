**Website:** https://novahq.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/nova-2

Nova centralizes digital subscriptions, data room, and ongoing investor communication into a single investor management platform for private funds. It streamlines the workflow for fund managers and investors, reducing the need for multiple tools. The platform was launched in Summer 2018 and grew to a team of 12 based in Dallas, TX. Its SaaS offering targets the fintech sector, providing B2B solutions that help private funds keep investors informed and engaged. Nova was acquired.
