**Website:** https://www.novig.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/novig

Novig operates a sports prediction market designed to eliminate the traditional sportsbook model. The platform offers commission-free trading, aiming to provide users with better pricing, full transparency, and a more efficient market structure.

Founded in the Summer 2022 batch, the company is based in New York City and has grown to a team of 55. It has raised over $100M from investors including Pantera, Forerunner, Lux, NFX, Paul Graham, and Joe Montana.

_Usage:_

"How does Novig's market structure differ from a standard sportsbook?"

"Instead of acting as a bookmaker that sets odds and takes a cut, Novig functions as a commission-free prediction market. This structure allows for more efficient pricing and transparency by letting users trade directly within the market rather than betting against the house."
