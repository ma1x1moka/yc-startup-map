NuCypher, also known as the Threshold Network, is a B2B infrastructure company based in Austin, Texas, that specializes in decentralized threshold cryptography. Since 2017, the team has developed cryptographic primitives for secure data sharing, including Proxy Re-Encryption and Threshold Access Control (TACo), alongside novel cryptoeconomic mechanisms like Stable Yield.

The company’s flagship product, TACo, launched on mainnet in January 2024 and is currently running on over 120 nodes with approximately $80 million in associated economic security. It supports a growing ecosystem of paying developers and major infrastructure partners such as Ceramic, Irys, Textile, and Unlock, maintaining a decentralized architecture from its genesis block.

As one of the two founding teams of the Threshold Network, NuCypher hosts several key protocols including tBTC, Random Beacon, and tUSD. The organization has also implemented original network and governance architectures, including a historic on-chain merger, to support its mission of enhancing privacy and security in decentralized systems.

_Usage:_

"How does TACo handle key management differently from traditional centralized encryption services?"

"TACo uses Threshold Access Control to allow for end-to-end encrypted data sharing without relying on a single point of failure or a central authority to hold keys. It operates on a decentralized network of over 120 nodes, meaning the cryptographic operations are distributed, which provides the security benefits of threshold cryptography while maintaining the flexibility needed for developers integrating with partners like Ceramic or Unlock."
