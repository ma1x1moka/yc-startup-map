**Website:** https://nucleoresearch.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/nucleo

Nucleo helps oncologists and radiologists extract insights from CT scans to support tumor characterization and treatment.

The company works with major hospitals such as Stanford Hospital, Cedars‑Sinai, UCI Health, and Weill Cornell, providing a specialized AI solution for oncology imaging.

Nucleo is a small, active startup in the healthcare sector with a team of two, founded in the Fall 2025 batch.

_Usage:_

"How does Nucleo’s agentic platform help oncologists?"

"It extracts insights from CT scans to support tumor characterization and treatment, giving clinicians actionable information for cancer care."
