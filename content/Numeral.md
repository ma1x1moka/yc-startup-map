**Website:** https://www.numeral.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/numeral

Numeral provides an end-to-end solution for U.S. sales tax, VAT, and GST compliance, managing registrations, filings, calculations, and ongoing support for businesses selling globally. The company serves over 3,000 global clients, including Eight Sleep, Supabase, and Brex, helping them remain compliant across diverse regulatory environments.

Built with an AI-native approach, the platform covers more than 70 countries and tens of thousands of jurisdictions. Numeral is headquartered in San Francisco and employs a team of 110 people focused on delivering accuracy and scale in financial compliance.
