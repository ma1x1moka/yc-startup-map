**Website:** https://www.numero.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/numero

Numero was founded in 2019 to build a modern fundraising software suite from the ground up. Since then it has scaled its platform to serve thousands of clients ranging from city council seats to U.S. Senate races and has helped them collectively manage over $10 billion in contributions. Its all‑in‑one system combines a donor CRM, broadcast email and text tools, and payment‑processing pages that clients rely on daily. The company operates with a tight‑knit team of 15 engineers and staff in San Francisco and remotely, and it is backed by investors including 776, Y Combinator, and Initialized Capital.

_Usage:_

"How is Numero different from using a generic CRM and separate email or payment services for campaign fundraising?"

"Numero bundles a donor‑focused CRM, broadcast email/text messaging, and built‑in payment processing into a single platform, so campaigns don’t need to stitch together separate tools. The integrated design is purpose‑built for political fundraising, which streamlines daily workflows compared to generic CRMs paired with third‑party email or payment services."
