NxtCure Labs is an AI-native, full-stack CRO built to take therapeutics from discovery to market faster. Nxtcure Labs is reinventing medicine from lab to drug discovery.

About 2 million Americans are diagnosed with cancer every year. Science has never moved faster, new immunotherapies, targeted drugs and cancer vaccines arrive every year. But roughly 90% of cancer drugs fail in clinical trials, and about 80% of trials run late. The breakthroughs exist. The way it tests them is stuck in the last century.

NxtCure is building autonomous clinical trials. Before a single patient enrolls, it runs the trial thousands of times on digital twins. Each virtual patient is a system of coupled differential equations that track how a drug moves through plasma, tumor and heart, and how the tumor grows, shrinks or fights back. The parameters are fit to real trial data, so every twin behaves like real biology. Across thousands of simulations it can calculate the odds that a dose will hit its endpoints, and fix the protocol before it costs anyone time, money or hope.
