**Website:** https://observe.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/observe-ai

Observe.AI is a leader in Contact Center AI that helps top brands analyze 100% of their calls and streamline quality‑assurance workflows. The platform transcribes every call with high accuracy, applies automatic speech recognition and natural language processing, and provides agent assistance and coaching tools, giving businesses full visibility into their customer‑service operations.

The company serves more than 150 customers and partners, including Root Insurance, Alcon Laboratories, TripAdvisor, and Pearson. Backed by investors such as Menlo Ventures, Next47, and Y‑Combinator, Observe.AI operates from San Francisco with an additional office in Bangalore and employs about 240 people.

_Usage:_

"Do I need to manually tag calls for quality‑assurance when using Observe.AI?"

"No. Observe.AI automates quality‑assurance workflows by transcribing every call with high accuracy and providing built‑in analytics and coaching tools, so agents and managers can monitor performance without manual tagging."
