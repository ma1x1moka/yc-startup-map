**Website:** https://www.octifi.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/octifi

OctiFi provides a Buy Now Pay Later service tailored for the Southeast Asian market, specifically Singapore. The platform empowers commerce by offering instant 0% interest loans to responsible spenders, enabling consumers to finance larger purchases such as home renovations or online furniture without incurring extra costs.

The company operated with a team of 11 and focused on consumer finance within the fintech industry. OctiFi has since been acquired, marking the conclusion of its independent operations.

_Usage:_

"How does OctiFi's 0% interest BNPL model differ from standard credit card financing for large purchases like home renovations?"

"OctiFi offers instant loans with 0% interest specifically for responsible spenders, allowing users to pay later at no extra cost. This contrasts with standard credit card financing, which typically accrues interest on the balance if not paid in full, making OctiFi's model a cost-neutral option for extending budget on items like furniture or renovation projects."
