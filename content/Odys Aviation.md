Odys Aviation, formerly known as Craft Aero, is developing vertical takeoff and landing aircraft specifically designed for major airlines. The company aims to reduce travel times by half on high-traffic routes by utilizing existing city helipads and local airports rather than relying solely on traditional long-haul infrastructure.

Based in Los Angeles, the team consists of 11 employees with backgrounds in electric propulsion and automotive manufacturing. The company has secured two USAF Agility Prime contracts and reports pre-orders and options for more than 1,000 aircraft, signaling significant early interest in its hard-tech aviation solutions.

_Usage:_

"How does Odys Aviation's approach to eVTOL differ from typical urban air mobility startups?"

"While many eVTOL companies focus on short-distance urban point-to-point travel, Odys Aviation targets major airlines and the world's busiest routes. The strategy involves using city helipads and local airports to cut travel time in half for longer distances, rather than just solving the last-mile problem within a single metro area."
