**Website:** https://www.of.one | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ofone

OfOne builds software that automates order taking at fast-food drive-thrus using conversational AI. The system handles customer interactions by answering questions, intelligently upselling items like ice cream on hot days, and maintaining a polite tone. This approach aims to help restaurant chains operate more efficiently while enhancing the customer experience.

The company targets the significant volume of orders processed through drive-thrus, which are the largest order channel in the restaurant industry with 200,000 locations in the US. OfOne was acquired, marking its exit from the YC Winter 2023 batch.

_Usage:_

"How does OfOne handle the upselling process compared to a standard automated kiosk?"

"OfOne uses conversational AI to engage in natural dialogue rather than just processing button clicks. It can answer specific customer questions and perform contextual upsells, such as suggesting ice cream when the weather is hot, while consistently using polite language like 'please' and 'thank you' to maintain a human-centric feel."
