Okay offers dashboards and actionable insights designed for high‑performing engineering teams, helping them monitor productivity and DevOps metrics. The company operates as a B2B productivity tool, focusing on analytics for engineering workflows. Okay was acquired, reflecting its value in the engineering analytics space.

Based in San Francisco with a four‑person team, Okay served customers seeking data‑driven engineering operations before its acquisition.
