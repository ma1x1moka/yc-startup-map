**Website:** https://www.olaclick.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/olaclick

OlaClick provides a restaurant solution that allows mass-market eateries in Latin America to create digital menus and promote them instantly with a single click. The platform facilitates simple food ordering directly through WhatsApp, integrating e-commerce, POS, and CRM capabilities into a streamlined workflow for the region.

With a team of 30 based in Lima, Peru, and operating remotely, the company serves over 40,000 restaurants across Latin America. By leveraging WhatsApp as the primary interface, OlaClick claims to increase sales for its users by 40%, addressing the specific digital adoption needs of the local food and beverage sector.

_Usage:_

"How does OlaClick handle the ordering process for restaurants that don't have a dedicated app?"

"It uses WhatsApp as the primary channel for receiving orders, allowing restaurants to create and promote a digital menu with one click so customers can place simple food orders directly within the messaging app rather than downloading a new application."
