Co-founded [Fragment](./Fragment.md) as CEO, part of YC's Summer 2023 batch, helping businesses integrate AI into their workflows. Moindrot ranked first in the entrance exam at École Polytechnique and studied at Stanford, and previously created and led the AI team at MetaMap. Fragment was acquired by Bret Taylor's Sierra in April 2026, with Moindrot joining Sierra's team to strengthen its agent development in France.
