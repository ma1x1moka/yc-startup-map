**Website:** https://ollama.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ollama

Ollama is an open‑source developer tool that helps businesses get up and running with large language models. The company, a Winter 2021 Y Combinator batch participant, is based in San Francisco and operates in the B2B infrastructure space. By focusing on easy deployment, Ollama aims to lower the barrier for companies to integrate LLM capabilities into their products.

_Usage:_

"How does Ollama differ from other large‑language‑model deployment tools?"

"Ollama is positioned as an open‑source, developer‑focused platform that lets you quickly start using large language models on your own infrastructure, whereas many alternatives are cloud‑only services; the directory only notes its B2B infrastructure focus and open‑source nature."
