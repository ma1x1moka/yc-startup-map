Co-founded [refine](./refine.md) as CTO with [Civan Ozseyhan](./Civan%20Ozseyhan.md) and [Eren Erkalkan](./Eren%20Erkalkan.md), part of YC's Summer 2023 batch. Aplak started his professional software career in high school and dropped out of university after his first year. In 2018 he co-founded Pankod Yazılım ve Danışmanlık, a software development house in Istanbul, before starting refine. He is now CEO of VoltAgent, building a JavaScript framework.
