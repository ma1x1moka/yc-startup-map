**Website:** https://addomni.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/omni

Omni operates as a B2B productivity tool designed to help teams manage internal knowledge and ensure it remains current. The product functions as a Chrome extension that fits into natural workflows, integrating with existing knowledge sources to deliver bite-sized answers when needed.

The company was based in San Francisco and maintained a small team of five employees. It was ultimately acquired, marking the end of its independent operation.

_Usage:_

"How does Omni differ from a traditional search bar or documentation site for sales teams?"

"Unlike static documentation that can become outdated, Omni acts as an intelligent layer on top of your existing knowledge sources. It lives directly in the browser as a Chrome extension, so users don't have to leave their workflow to find information. Instead of searching through pages, it provides immediate, bite-sized answers that are kept up to date automatically."
