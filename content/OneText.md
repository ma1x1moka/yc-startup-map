**Website:** https://onetext.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/onetext

OneText is disrupting SMS marketing and payments by turning a simple text reply into a complete purchase, making every transaction as effortless as paying with a hotel room number.

In its first year, the company onboarded 50 fast‑growing e‑commerce brands and added up to 14% incremental revenue to their bottom lines, generating $597,000 in payments in March and $50,000 in revenue in April.

The company’s founders met at PayPal Checkout and left to build a payments platform that truly helps merchants acquire customers and win sales. A CMO of its largest customer, a $90 million clothing brand, has called OneText “the best thing that’s happened to SMS marketing in forever.”.

_Usage:_

"How does OneText turn a text reply into a purchase?"

"OneText turns a simple text reply into a complete purchase by allowing shoppers to reply to a message and complete the transaction without leaving the messaging app, eliminating the need for a separate checkout page."
