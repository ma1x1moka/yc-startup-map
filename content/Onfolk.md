**Website:** http://onfolk.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/onfolk

Onfolk provides a unified platform for payroll and human resources management, designed to replace fragmented spreadsheets and poorly integrated tools. The company enables growing teams to run payroll in one minute, onboard new starters in two minutes, and manage centralized employee information, time-off, and reporting. It also offers hands-off integrations for pensions and accounting.

Founded by software engineers who previously spent four years scaling Monzo, Onfolk was built to address the inefficiencies of traditional payroll providers. The team leveraged their experience to create a product they felt was missing from the market, aiming to streamline the administrative burden on modern companies.

Onfolk has since been acquired, marking a significant milestone for the London-based startup. It holds certification as a payroll software from the UK government, underscoring its compliance and reliability in the B2B human resources sector.

_Usage:_

"How does Onfolk differentiate itself from traditional payroll providers or spreadsheets?"

"Onfolk focuses on speed and integration, allowing teams to run payroll in one minute and onboard new employees in two.
