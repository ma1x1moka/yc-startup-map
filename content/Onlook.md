**Website:** https://onlook.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/onlook

Onlook is a San Francisco-based developer tool that functions as a visual editor for code, aiming to close the gap between design and development. The platform allows users to build, deploy, and iterate on websites and web apps by integrating AI capabilities directly into the workflow.

The project gained significant traction shortly after launch, reaching the top of Hacker News and becoming the number one trending repository on GitHub, surpassing DeepSeek in popularity. It has accumulated over 8,500 GitHub stars, reflecting strong interest in its approach to merging design and development.

Founded by Daniel Farrell and Kiet Ho, the four-person team combines design expertise with engineering backgrounds from Amazon and ServiceNow. Onlook positions itself as a comprehensive solution for anyone looking to streamline the process of creating and maintaining web applications.

_Usage:_

"How does Onlook differ from traditional low-code platforms or standard IDEs?"

"Onlook acts as a visual editor for code rather than a black-box generator, allowing designers and developers to work in the same environment. Unlike standard IDEs that are purely text-based, it integrates AI to help bridge the gap between design intent and actual code, enabling faster iteration on web apps without losing control over the underlying implementation."
