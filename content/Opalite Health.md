Opalite makes it easy for healthcare providers to communicate with non-English speaking patients in real time — without waiting for an interpreter or delaying care. Available instantly, 24/7, replacing traditional interpretation services with software that saves millions per year.

Built a voice AI system positioned as the safest, most compliant, and most accurate medical interpreter. Works inside existing clinical workflows rather than requiring separate tooling.

_Usage:_

"How does Opalite’s voice AI integrate into existing clinical workflows without requiring separate tooling?"

"Opalite embeds its voice‑AI interpreter directly into the clinician’s existing EHR or telehealth interface, exposing a simple start‑translation button that launches a real‑time, 24/7 multilingual conversation within the same screen, so providers never have to switch to a separate app or platform."
