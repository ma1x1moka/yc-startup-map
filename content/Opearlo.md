**Website:** https://www.opearlo.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/opearlo

Opearlo builds voice applications designed for platforms like Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. Based in London, England, the team participated in Y Combinator's Summer 2017 batch with a focus on consumer content for smart voice assistants.

Operating with a team of two, Opearlo was subsequently acquired.
