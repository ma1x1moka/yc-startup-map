**Website:** https://openint.dev | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/openint

OpenInt provides a platform that lets teams build and deploy product integrations in hours instead of weeks, using no‑code or low‑code approaches. It offers unified APIs for reading and writing data and can sync that data directly to a database, simplifying the integration workflow. The company, a remote‑first four‑person SaaS startup in the B2B analytics space, was acquired after its Winter 2023 batch.

_Usage:_

"Will OpenInt really cut integration development time from weeks to hours?"

"Yes, OpenInt is designed to let teams ship product integrations in hours by providing no‑code or low‑code tooling, unified read/write APIs, and direct database sync, which eliminates much of the custom code and manual effort that typically stretches integration projects over weeks."
