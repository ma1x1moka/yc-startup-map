**Website:** https://openmeter.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/openmeter

OpenMeter has built an open-source platform that processes billions of events into a usage lake within seconds, creating a standardized data source for metering. The platform serves as the backbone for revenue and efficiency teams, enabling them to track and monetize AI and API usage at scale.

The company's mission is to become the primary data source for AI and API monetization, standardizing metering across B2B infrastructure. OpenMeter was acquired, reflecting the strategic value of its technology for larger players in the space.

With a team of seven, OpenMeter operates in the B2B infrastructure sector, focusing on providing the tooling needed for efficient usage tracking and revenue optimization.

_Usage:_

"How does OpenMeter's usage lake differ from a typical analytics pipeline?"

"OpenMeter's usage lake ingests billions of events in seconds and normalizes them into a standardized metering format, whereas a typical analytics pipeline often handles raw logs and requires additional processing to derive usage metrics. This built-in standardization lets revenue and efficiency teams directly monetize AI and API consumption without building custom pipelines."
