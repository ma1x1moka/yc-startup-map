**Website:** https://opensea.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/opensea

OpenSea was founded in 2017 and pioneered the first non-custodial marketplace for digital assets, allowing users to trade tokens, culture, ideas, digital and physical assets across all major blockchains.

It has since grown into the primary destination for the on-chain economy, serving millions of users in the consumer and content sectors.

The company employs about 75 people located in New York City and remotely, and it continues to shape digital economies with a talent-dense crew.

_Usage:_

"How is OpenSea different from other NFT marketplaces?"

"OpenSea was the first to offer a non-custodial marketplace that works across all major blockchains, so users keep control of their assets while trading a wide range of digital and even physical items, unlike platforms that are custodial or limited to a single chain."
