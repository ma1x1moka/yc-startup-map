**Website:** https://opennote.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/opennote

Opennote is a notebook with an AI thinking partner built in. It edits writing, references knowledge and the web, runs background tasks, and gives feedback as you work. The company was acquired and operates as a consumer SaaS in the education and productivity space, with a small team of three based in San Francisco, CA.
