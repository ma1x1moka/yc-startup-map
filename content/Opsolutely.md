**Website:** https://www.opsolutely.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/opsolutely

Opsolutely is a San Francisco-based company that provides automated deployment tools designed to make the software shipping process safe, easy-to-use, and friendly for every member of an engineering team. By targeting the most chaotic part of software creation, the platform aims to make engineering teams more productive, inclusive, and collaborative.

The company, which operated with a team of two, was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Opsolutely handle the complexity of deployment workflows compared to manual processes?"

"Opsolutely automates the deployment process to remove the chaos and difficulty typically associated with shipping software. It is designed to be safe and easy-to-use, ensuring that the process is accessible and collaborative for all team members rather than being a bottleneck or a source of error."
