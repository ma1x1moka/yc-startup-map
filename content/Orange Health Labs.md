Orange Health Labs is India’s leading on-demand diagnostics lab for urban consumers, offering 30-minute at-home sample collection and 6-hour reports across the top cities of India.

Launched in January 2021, it has grown to a team of 1,100 employees and operates out of Bengaluru, KA, India, backed by investors such as Accel, General Catalyst, Y Combinator, Bertelsmann India, and Amazon.

_Usage:_

"How does Orange Health Labs achieve its 30-minute sample collection time?"

"Orange Health Labs uses an on-demand model with a network of mobile phlebotomists that collect samples at customers’ homes within 30 minutes of booking. The samples are then processed in centralized labs, delivering results in under six hours."
