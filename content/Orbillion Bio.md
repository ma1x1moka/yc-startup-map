**Website:** https://www.orbillion.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/orbillion-bio

Orbillion Bio develops cultivated beef at scale by leveraging advanced computational models to optimize its bioprocess. The company utilizes proprietary bioware to precisely control the microenvironment required for meat cell growth, eliminating the need for GMOs, serum, or expensive growth factors. This approach enables a production cost of $4 per kg at commercial scale.

The technology addresses the primary industry challenges of cost and scale-up through an integrated bioprocess platform. This system allows for asset-light production anywhere globally within two weeks. Orbillion Bio has formed partnerships with two global food companies and a European partner to fast-track commercialization in the ground beef market.

Orbillion Bio has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Orbillion Bio achieve such low costs for cultivated beef compared to traditional methods?"

"They use computational models to rapidly optimize the bioprocess and proprietary bioware to control the cell microenvironment. This setup supercharges cell growth without relying on GMOs, serum, or expensive growth factors, which drives the cost down to $4 per kg at commercial scale."
