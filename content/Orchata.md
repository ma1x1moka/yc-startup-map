**Website:** http://www.orchata.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/orchata

Orchata is a consumer app that provides 20-minute delivery of groceries, fresh produce, alcohol, pharmacy items, and top e-commerce brands in Latin America. The company operates an all-in-one software platform for managing darkstores located around cities, aiming to replicate the rapid growth model established by Gopuff in the United States.

Launched in Mexico, the service grew from zero to $6.3M in revenue with over 30,000 customers. It targeted the massive $451B Latin American grocery market, which was transitioning online at a rapid pace. The company maintained a team of 50 based in Monterrey, Mexico.

Orchata is currently listed as Inactive, indicating the company has since shut down.

_Usage:_

"How does Orchata's darkstore model compare to traditional grocery delivery?"

"Orchata uses an all-in-one software stack to manage a network of darkstores positioned around the city, which allows it to promise 20-minute delivery for a wide range of items including fresh meat, alcohol, and pharmacy goods. This approach mirrors the Gopuff playbook in the US, focusing on speed and a no-substitute policy at supermarket prices, rather than relying on centralized warehouses or third-party courier networks that typically have longer lead times."
