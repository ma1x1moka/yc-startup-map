The company sits in the same wave as [Synthetic Sciences](./Synthetic%20Sciences.md) — using foundation model architectures trained on biological data to accelerate scientific discovery. Where Synthetic Sciences automates research workflows, Origin Bio provides the predictive model layer for genomics.

The AlphaGenome comparison is the key credibility marker: DeepMind's genomics group is one of the best-resourced in the world. Beating their published benchmarks from a seed-stage YC company is either a strong result or a sign that the benchmark framing matters as much as the score.
