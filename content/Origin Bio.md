description: DNA foundation model that outperforms DeepMind's AlphaGenome on genomic benchmarks. YC W26.
---

Origin Bio built a foundation model trained on genomic sequences that, according to benchmark results, outperforms DeepMind's AlphaGenome on several genomic understanding tasks. The model predicts gene expression, regulatory element activity, and other molecular phenotypes from DNA sequence alone.

The company sits in the same wave as [Synthetic Sciences](./Synthetic%20Sciences.md) — using foundation model architectures trained on biological data to accelerate scientific discovery. Where Synthetic Sciences automates research workflows, Origin Bio provides the predictive model layer for genomics.

The AlphaGenome comparison is the key credibility marker: DeepMind's genomics group is one of the best-resourced in the world. Beating their published benchmarks from a seed-stage YC company is either a strong result or a sign that the benchmark framing matters as much as the score.

_Usage:_

"What does outperforming AlphaGenome actually mean?"

"It means Origin Bio's model predicts gene expression from DNA sequence more accurately on the held-out test sets the benchmark uses. Whether those test sets generalize to real biology is the open question."
