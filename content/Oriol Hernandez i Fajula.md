Co-founded [REVER](./REVER.md) with [Marius Montmany](./Marius%20Montmany.md) in Barcelona in 2022, part of YC's Summer 2022 batch, after both became frustrated with inefficient reverse logistics while working at large logistics companies. Before REVER, Hernandez i Fajula worked in management consulting at Boston Consulting Group and in investment banking at Bank of America Merrill Lynch, and has been a VC investor at BlackWood Capital Partners since 2022.
