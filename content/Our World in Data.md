**Website:** https://ourworldindata.org/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/our-world-in-data

Our World in Data publishes an open-access platform that lets users explore the state of the world through interactive data visualizations and summaries of scientific literature. It covers topics ranging from global poverty and health to climate change and education, offering 3405 charts across 297 topics.

The service is free, open-source, and positioned as a public good, attracting millions of monthly users and frequent citations in research and media worldwide. With a team of 19 located in Oxford and remote, it operates in the education sector as a nonprofit organization.

The organization remains active, continuously adding new visualizations to help people understand progress and setbacks on the world’s largest problems.

_Usage:_

"How is Our World in Data different from generic open data portals like the World Bank’s data site?"

"Our World in Data not only provides raw data but also pairs it with interactive visualizations and concise summaries of the scientific literature, all under a free, open-source license. This focus on storytelling and education, plus its breadth of 3405 charts across 297 topics, makes it a distinct public‑good resource compared with more data‑only platforms."
