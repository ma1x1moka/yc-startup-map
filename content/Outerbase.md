**Website:** https://www.outerbase.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/outerbase

Outerbase is the interface for your database, allowing companies to view, edit, and modify their data without writing SQL. It also lets users generate beautiful visual dashboards through a visual UI. The platform is positioned as a developer tool with generative AI, analytics, and data‑engineering capabilities, serving B2B teams in engineering, product, and design. Outerbase was acquired, as noted in its YC directory entry.

_Usage:_

"Can I create a dashboard in Outerbase without knowing SQL?"

"Yes. Outerbase’s visual interface lets you select tables and fields, and it automatically generates the underlying queries so you can build and customize dashboards without writing any SQL yourself."
