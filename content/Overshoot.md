**Website:** https://overshoot.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/overshoot

Overshoot makes it easy for developers to build and run real-time vision applications. 300+ developers connect live video feeds to a large collection of Vision Language Models with 3 lines of code, getting responses in under 200ms — 10x faster than existing inference platforms.

Target applications: physical security, safety, gaming, robotics, consumer products. Solves the core problem of slow inference and model unavailability that breaks real-time vision use cases at scale.

_Usage:_

"What makes Overshoot able to deliver inference 10× faster than existing platforms?"

"Overshoot’s platform lets developers hook live video streams into a curated set of Vision‑Language Models with just three lines of code, and the service returns results in under 200 ms, which is about ten times the latency of typical inference platforms; the speed comes from the tightly integrated, low‑overhead connection between the video feed and the models."
