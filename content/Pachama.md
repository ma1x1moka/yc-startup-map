Pachama is a climate‑tech company that leverages computer vision and satellite imagery to verify reforestation and conservation projects, ensuring they sequester carbon, enhance biodiversity, and benefit local communities.

Through a tech‑verified marketplace, Pachama channels capital from corporate clients such as Amazon, Airbnb, Netflix, and Nespresso into forests across Brazil, Mexico, India, and the United States. The company’s platform provides transparent, data‑driven proof of carbon removal, attracting backing from investors like Bill Gates’ BEV, Chis Sacca’s LowerCarbon, and the Amazon Climate Pledge.

Pachama was acquired in 2024, and it operates fully remote with a team of 85 based in San Francisco, CA, and worldwide.

_Usage:_

"How does Pachama verify the carbon sequestration of its reforestation projects?"

"Pachama uses computer vision algorithms on satellite imagery to monitor forest growth and calculate the amount of carbon sequestered, providing a data‑driven verification that its projects meet the claimed environmental impact."
