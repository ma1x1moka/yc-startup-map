**Website:** https://pactima.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/pactima

Pactima provides a real-time solution that allows users to view and sign documents while simultaneously hosting a secured video call. By combining digital signatures with live video, the platform enables transactions that traditionally required in-person meetings to be conducted online. The company operates as a B2B SaaS provider focused on the legal and documents sectors.

Pactima was acquired, marking a significant milestone for the venture-backed startup. The company is based in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada, with a remote-friendly team structure.

_Usage:_

"How does Pactima differ from standard e-signature tools like DocuSign?"

"Standard e-signature tools typically handle the document transmission and signature capture asynchronously, whereas Pactima integrates a secured video call directly into the signing process. This allows parties to negotiate, verify identity, and sign in real-time, effectively replicating the experience of an in-person closing or meeting without the need for separate video conferencing software."
