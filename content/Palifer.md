**Website:** https://www.palifer.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/palifer

Palifer develops AI-driven predictive maintenance models that analyze historical logs to reduce unplanned failures in trains and heavy industry.
By extracting actionable insights, the platform boosts schedule compliance, helping operators keep fleets on time.
The startup, founded in Summer 2019, was acquired and operates with a small team of two in San Francisco, CA, serving the industrial and automotive sectors.

_Usage:_

"How does Palifer’s approach to predictive maintenance differ from traditional rule-based systems?"

"Palifer’s models use machine learning to sift through historical operational logs, identifying patterns that signal impending failures, whereas rule-based systems rely on static thresholds and predefined conditions."
