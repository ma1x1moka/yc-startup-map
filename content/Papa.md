Papa is a one-stop-shop for flexible family care, offering companionship and a helping hand wherever needed. Founded in 2017 and headquartered in Miami, the company has grown to a team of 500 employees and is backed by prominent investors such as Canaan, Tiger Global Management, Comcast Ventures, SoftBank Vision Fund 2, TCG, Initialized Capital, and Seven Seven Six. Operating in the healthcare and healthcare IT sectors, Papa focuses on consumer health services and digital health, and remains an active player in the market.
