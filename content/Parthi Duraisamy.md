Parthi co-founded [Alaan](./Alaan.md) — Modern finance platform for the Middle East.

Parthi grew up in a lower-middle-class family in India. Neither of their parents had been to high school or college. The first life-changing event occurred in 2005 when they won a full scholarship to study engineering in Singapore. The drive to prove myself then sent them on a journey through prestigious institutions, including Barclays Investment Bank, McKinsey, and Dartmouth College. While they always felt like an outsider in these institutions, the journey eventually led them to build Alaan.
