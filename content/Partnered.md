**Website:** https://partnered.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/partnered-2

Partnered operates as a network that connects B2B sales teams to facilitate the sharing of customer introductions. The platform allows users to join for free and get started in less than five minutes.

The company was based in New York City and operated remotely with a small team of two. It focused on the B2B and sales industries using a SaaS model.

Partnered was acquired, marking the conclusion of its independent operations.

_Usage:_

"How does Partnered differ from standard CRM tools regarding lead generation?"

"Partnered is not a CRM but a network specifically for exchanging warm introductions between B2B sales teams. Instead of managing your own pipeline, you connect with other sales reps to share customer leads, allowing you to get started in under five minutes without a paid subscription."
