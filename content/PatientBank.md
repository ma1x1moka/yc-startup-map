PatientBank was a healthcare technology company that enabled individuals to consolidate and share their medical records online, regardless of which doctor or hospital generated the data. The startup operated out of New York City and San Francisco with a small team of two employees.

Backed by Y Combinator, General Catalyst, and Khosla Ventures, PatientBank aimed to solve the fragmentation of patient health data. The company has since shut down and is currently inactive.
