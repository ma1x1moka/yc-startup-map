Founded by Eli Bullock-Papa and Ryan Zhang ("I like history"). Team of 3. San Francisco.

**35,000 daily active users**. 4,000+ published community presets (each with 50+ plays). **100 billion+ tokens processed weekly** — one of the highest token volumes in the W26 batch.

Players choose a country and historical moment — ancient Rome, Edo Japan, the French Revolution, "What if the USSR hadn't broken apart?" — and interact with a generatively simulated world populated by AI agents with historically grounded behaviour. Every player's game is different; events emerge from agent interactions, not scripted outcomes.

Most historical games use fixed scripts. Pax Historia simulates societies, economies, political dynamics, and individual actors using large language models grounded in historical data.

The 2,334 Twitter likes at launch made it the most-engaged W26 launch on social media — ahead even of [Pocket](./Pocket.md). The 35K DAU is the real metric: people are coming back daily, not just trying it once.

[Synthetic Sciences](./Synthetic%20Sciences.md) simulates scientific research with AI agents. Pax Historia simulates historical societies. The shared architecture insight: LLMs are finally capable enough to maintain coherent, complex simulation state across long sessions.
