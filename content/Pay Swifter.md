Pay Swifter offers a suite of payment solutions, including ACH, designed for the cannabis sector.

Dispenaries, multi‑state operators, and marketplaces can use its software and APIs to accept payments, send payouts, and accelerate growth.

The company was acquired in 2020.

_Usage:_

"How does Pay Swifter handle ACH payments for cannabis dispensaries compared to traditional processors?"

"Pay Swifter’s APIs are built to meet the regulatory requirements of the cannabis industry, providing ACH processing that is compliant with state laws and enabling dispensaries to accept payments and issue payouts through a single platform, unlike generic processors that may not support cannabis transactions."
