**Website:** https://paymongo.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/paymongo

PayMongo builds products and tools that enable thousands of individuals and businesses in the Philippines to make and receive payments over the Internet. It supports payments through credit or debit cards, electronic wallets, over-the-counter payments and many more.
PayMongo offers robust APIs, plugins and other products that abstract out the complexities of payments, allowing users to get up and running quickly and focus on their business. With a team of 220 employees based in Taguig, NCR, PayMongo has attracted investors such as Founders Fund, Peter Thiel, Bedrock Capital and Stripe.
As a leading fintech player in the Philippines, PayMongo remains active and continues to expand its payment infrastructure for local merchants and consumers.
