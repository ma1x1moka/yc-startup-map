Payhippo is a fintech company that provides loans to small and medium-sized enterprises in Africa, specifically targeting the Nigerian market. The company operates with a team of 11 people based in Lagos, Nigeria, and Los Angeles, California.

The platform is designed for speed, delivering credit to small businesses in under three hours. In July 2021, Payhippo reported generating $42k in revenue in Nigeria, accompanied by a 25% month-over-month growth rate and a 97% repayment rate.

_Usage:_

"How does Payhippo handle the speed of lending compared to traditional banks?"

"Payhippo cuts the typical wait time for SME loans down to under three hours, which is significantly faster than conventional banking processes. This speed is supported by a high repayment rate of 97%, suggesting that the rapid disbursement model does not come at the cost of credit risk."
