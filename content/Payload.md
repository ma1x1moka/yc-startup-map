**Website:** https://payloadcms.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/payload

Payload is a developer-first headless CMS and application framework designed to provide engineers with a superior alternative in the crowded CMS market. Built from the ground up, it focuses on saving time and effort when constructing backends and admin panels for websites, native apps, ecommerce, and SaaS products.

The company emphasizes clean, well-structured code and comprehensive documentation to streamline the development process. By targeting specific pain points in content management, Payload aims to change the status quo of how developers build and manage digital products.

Payload was acquired, marking a significant milestone for this Grand Rapids-based team of three engineers.

_Usage:_

"Why would I choose Payload over other headless CMS options?"

"Payload is positioned as a developer-first choice that prioritizes clean, well-structured code and a better engineering experience compared to traditional CMS options. It is specifically crafted to save time when building the backend and admin panel, making it a strong fit for teams that want to build the CMS they need without fighting against the tooling."
