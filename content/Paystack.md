**Website:** https://www.paystack.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/paystack

Paystack provides payment processing infrastructure that allows businesses to accept credit card, debit card, money transfer, and mobile money payments directly from their websites or mobile applications. The company operates out of offices in Lagos and San Francisco, serving as a key financial tool for the African market.

Founded in Y Combinator’s Winter 2016 batch, Paystack has grown into a team of approximately 115 people. Its focus on integrating diverse local payment methods with global card networks addresses specific challenges in the region's financial ecosystem.

The company’s status is listed as Acquired, indicating it was subsequently bought by another entity, marking a significant milestone in its history as a YC alum.

_Usage:_

"How does Paystack handle the mix of local and international payment methods for merchants?"

"Paystack aggregates credit card, debit card, money transfer, and mobile money into a single integration, allowing merchants to accept these various forms of payment directly from their website or mobile app without needing separate processors for each method."
