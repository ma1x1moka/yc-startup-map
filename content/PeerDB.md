**Website:** https://peerdb.io/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/peerdb

PeerDB provides a fast and cost-effective method for streaming data from Postgres to data warehouses, queues, and storage engines. It supports multiple streaming modes, including log-based CDC, cursor-based, and XMIN-based approaches, while handling native Postgres features like jsonb, arrays, and postgis. The company claims its solution is 10x faster than existing tools and efficiently manages schema changes and toast columns.

PeerDB was acquired, marking a notable exit for this developer-focused data engineering tool. The company operates with a small team of two, focusing on open-source solutions for B2B analytics and enterprise software.

_Usage:_

"How does PeerDB handle complex Postgres data types compared to standard ETL tools?"

"PeerDB is designed to natively support comprehensive Postgres data types, including jsonb, arrays, and postgis, while efficiently streaming toast columns and handling schema changes.
