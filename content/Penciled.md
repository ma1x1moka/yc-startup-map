**Website:** https://penciled.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/penciled

Penciled is an AI-powered front office assistant designed specifically for physical therapy clinics. It automates administrative tasks such as scheduling plans of care, sending patient reminders, and handling last-minute cancellations. The system also utilizes an automated waitlist to fill any resulting openings in the schedule.

The company integrates directly with electronic health record (EHR) systems like WebPT to streamline operations. Penciled currently books thousands of appointments for physical therapists each month, helping practices manage their front desk workflows more efficiently.

_Usage:_

"How does Penciled handle scheduling conflicts and cancellations in a physical therapy practice?"

"Penciled processes last-minute cancellations and immediately fills those openings using an automated waitlist. It also manages the initial scheduling of plans of care and sends out reminders, all while integrating directly into existing EHRs like WebPT to ensure the data stays synchronized."
