Penny is a personal finance app that accesses users' statement history—with permission—to automatically organize income and spending. It uses conversational AI to chat with users, letting them know how they’re doing each month, forecasting next‑month balances, alerting them to upcoming bills, and even suggesting cancellations of unused services.

By simplifying explanations and delivering timely insights, Penny makes finances less intimidating and stressful compared to many feature‑heavy apps that are hard to use. It automatically organizes financial data, explains it clearly, and tells users what they need to know when they need to know it.

Penny was acquired, reflecting its notable impact in the fintech space.

_Usage:_

"How does Penny’s chat‑based interface differ from other finance apps that just show static dashboards?"

"Penny’s chat interface proactively reaches out with personalized summaries, forecasts, bill reminders, and service‑cancellation suggestions, whereas typical finance apps present data on static screens and require users to dig for insights themselves."
