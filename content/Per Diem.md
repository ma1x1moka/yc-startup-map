Per Diem serves as an AI operating system for restaurants, designed to replace the dozens of disconnected tools typically used for ordering, loyalty, marketing, and operations. The platform utilizes AI agents to build and maintain branded apps, run marketing campaigns, manage customer loyalty, and automate daily operations to help restaurants grow first-party sales.

Built natively on Square, the company supports over 800 brands that collectively power more than 500,000 monthly orders. Per Diem acts as the central operating system for commerce, customer retention, and growth within the restaurant industry.

The company is based in New York City with a remote-friendly culture and maintains a team of approximately 25 people.

_Usage:_

"How does Per Diem handle the integration of restaurant operations compared to using separate point-of-sale and marketing tools?"

"Per Diem consolidates the fragmented stack by using AI agents to manage branded apps, marketing, and loyalty directly within a single system. It is built natively on Square, which allows it to handle commerce and customer retention without requiring restaurants to stitch together multiple third-party services for ordering and operations."
