**Website:** https://www.percept.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/percept-ai

Percept.AI provides end-to-end artificial intelligence solutions to help businesses connect with and support their customers. Part of the Y Combinator Summer 2017 batch, the startup developed tools to make customer support operations more intelligent and efficient.

The platform leverages deep learning and natural language processing technology to handle customer support workflows. Based in San Francisco and Sunnyvale, California, the seven-person B2B SaaS company has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Percept.AI assist existing customer support teams?"

"Percept.AI applies deep learning and natural language processing to provide end-to-end automated support solutions. Rather than relying on simple decision trees, its technology processes natural language to help business support teams resolve inquiries more efficiently."
