**Website:** https://www.perceptive.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/perceptive-technologies

Perceptive Technologies is a stealth‑mode, venture‑backed startup that recently closed a Series A round to advance automated robotic surgery in dentistry. It combines a novel imaging modality that can see inside teeth and beneath the gingiva without the need for X‑rays with AI that diagnoses decay and plans treatment, enabling robotic systems to perform tooth cutting in about one minute. This rapid, low‑discomfort procedure saves time and cost for dentists and expands access to dental care in underserved populations. The company was a 2020 YC Winter batch graduate and received the Forsyth DenTech 2021 CareCaptial award.

_Usage:_

"How does Perceptive's imaging modality differ from traditional dental X‑rays?"

"Perceptive's imaging system can visualize inside teeth and below the gingiva without using X‑ray radiation, whereas conventional dental X‑rays rely on ionizing radiation to capture images of tooth structure."
