**Website:** https://perlstreet.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/perl-street

Perl Street operates a platform designed to democratize structured finance, specifically targeting the financing needs of sustainable infrastructure and next-generation hardware technologies. The company addresses the massive capital gap required for climate action, noting that reaching net zero emissions requires over $4 trillion per year in global clean energy investment according to IEA estimates.

By facilitating debt financing, Perl Street has helped multiple hardware startups secure over $140M in offers from reputable institutions. The platform focuses on upgrading critical physical assets such as energy storage devices, electric vehicles, charging stations, and building electrification systems.

The company is based in Sydney, Austin, and remote locations, with a small team of four. It operates within the fintech and credit sectors, emphasizing that the future of distributed and resilient infrastructure is dependent on accessible financing for new hardware.

_Usage:_

"How does Perl Street actually get hardware startups the debt financing they need when traditional banks might be hesitant about new tech?"

"Perl Street acts as a platform that structures these deals to make them accessible to reputable financial institutions, effectively unlocking capital for projects that might otherwise struggle to find traditional debt financing. They have already facilitated over $140M in debt offers for hardware startups, focusing on areas like energy storage and EV infrastructure where the capital requirements are high but the technology is essential for climate goals."
