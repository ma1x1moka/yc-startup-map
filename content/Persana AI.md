**Website:** https://www.persana.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/persana-ai

Persana AI provides an agent layer for modern go-to-market teams, replacing fragmented point solutions with a unified platform that autonomously researches, enriches, and engages prospects. The system utilizes a multi-agent architecture to track funding, hiring, and tech changes across over 100 data sources, executing complex workflows without manual triggers or platform limits.

Trained on 700 million contacts and 200 million companies, Persana employs proprietary embeddings fine-tuned for GTM use cases to continuously learn which actions convert to pipeline. It features bi-directional native integration with major CRMs like Salesforce and HubSpot, allowing teams to scale prospecting without adding headcount while reclaiming time previously spent on manual work.

Founded by LinkedIn engineering veterans and backed by Y Combinator, Persana AI was acquired, marking its exit from the YC Winter 2023 batch.

_Usage:_

"How does Persana handle prospecting differently than standard workflow automation tools?"

"Instead of relying on predefined rules and manual triggers, Persana uses autonomous agents that think, learn, and execute 24/7. It consolidates data from over 100 sources into a single agentic platform, allowing it to run complex workflows across large prospect lists without the hard limits or fragmentation typical of traditional workflow tools."
