Co-founded [Hera](./Hera.md) with [Chia-Lun Wu](./Chia-Lun%20Wu.md), building an AI tool that makes motion design radically faster and cheaper — users have created over 50,000 animations, with more than 200,000 created in the first two weeks after launch. Before Hera, Tribelhorn worked in investment banking after studying at WHU, then joined Lunar X, where he acquired and scaled YouTube channels like Economics Explained and The Game Theorists to over 30 million subscribers, repeatedly hitting the same bottleneck: slow, expensive animation production.

_Usage:_

"What inspired Peter Tribelhorn to start Hera?"

"While scaling YouTube channels with tens of millions of subscribers at Lunar X, he kept hitting the same bottleneck — animation was slow and expensive — which became the problem Hera was built to solve."
