**Website:** https://www.phiar.net | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/phiar

Phiar develops the first AI-powered AR navigation software for driving that runs on smartphones and in-vehicle displays, leveraging an ultra‑lightweight road‑understanding AI that operates entirely at the edge in real time.

The platform is designed to enhance driving safety, provide intuitive wayfinding, and connect drivers with their surrounding environments. It was founded in Summer 2018 and has a team of 12 based in San Francisco and Redwood City.

Phiar was acquired, marking its recognition as a notable player in automotive AI and augmented reality.

_Usage:_

"How does Phiar's AR navigation differ from traditional GPS apps?"

"Phiar's solution runs entirely on the device using lightweight AI that processes road data in real time, providing augmented reality overlays directly on the driver’s view without relying on cloud services."
