**Website:** https://phind.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/phind

Phind operates as an AI search engine designed to retrieve the best answers through multi-step reasoning. It employs a generative user interface to present results in an interactive and visually engaging format.

The company emerged from the Summer 2022 batch and focused on the consumer search space. Phind has since shut down.

_Usage:_

"What's the mechanism behind Phind's answer generation and presentation?"

"The system relies on multi-step reasoning to synthesize the best answer for a query, then uses a generative UI to render the response in a dynamic, interactive format rather than a static list."
