**Website:** https://phonic.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/phonic

Phonic is a San Francisco-based SaaS platform that helps businesses collect and analyze voice and video feedback. By leveraging artificial intelligence, the company processes unstructured audio and video data to provide actionable insights for marketing and B2B operations.

The startup was part of Y Combinator's Winter 2020 batch and operated with a team of eight people across San Francisco and Foster City. Phonic was acquired, marking the conclusion of its independent operations as a YC-backed company.

_Usage:_

"How does Phonic handle raw video and audio feedback differently from standard text analytics tools?"

"Instead of relying on manual transcription or simple keyword matching on text, Phonic uses AI to directly process voice and video files. This allows it to extract sentiment and insights from unstructured media that would otherwise be too time-consuming to review manually, making it suitable for large-scale feedback collection in marketing contexts."
