**Website:** https://photoroom.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/photoroom

Photoroom offers photo editing software designed to be powerful enough for professional results yet simple enough for users without technical training. By leveraging deep learning to translate pixels into objects, the platform drastically simplifies non-creative tasks such as removing backgrounds from images.

Headquartered in Paris, France, the company focuses on enabling entrepreneurs and small businesses to compose standout images for their commerce operations. With a team of approximately 100 people, Photoroom applies computer vision and generative AI to streamline the design process for content creators.

_Usage:_

"How does Photoroom handle background removal compared to traditional manual editing?"

"It uses deep learning to identify and separate objects from the background automatically, removing the need for manual masking or complex selection tools. This allows users to generate clean, professional product images quickly without requiring specific design training."
