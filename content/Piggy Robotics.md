Piggy Robotics builds humanoid robots that do your chores at iPhone prices. Just like the millions of cars generating data for self-driving, it needs millions of humanoids generating real-world data for general-purpose robotics. Humanoid companies today can only manufacture hundreds a year because motors are expensive and slow to assemble. It uses artificial muscles. Each muscle is just a tube wrapped in braided fibre, and the whole robot is powered by a single pump — which means it cans mass-produce humanoids by the millions. Its design is built for that scale from day one and is cheap enough to enter billions of homes.
