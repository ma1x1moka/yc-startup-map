**Website:** https://www.getpingpong.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/pingpong

PingPong is a video messaging application designed for remote teams operating across multiple time zones. It allows teams to collaborate by exchanging short video, voice, and screen recording clips, effectively serving as a video-centric alternative to text-based chat platforms.

The company was based in Orem, Utah, and operated with a remote-first team of eight people. PingPong was acquired, marking the conclusion of its independent operation as a YC-backed startup.

_Usage:_

"How does PingPong differ from standard video conferencing tools like Zoom or Teams?"

"PingPong is not a real-time conferencing tool; instead, it functions as an asynchronous messaging platform where teams exchange short clips of video, voice, or screen recordings. It is often described as 'Slack for video,' allowing remote teams in different time zones to collaborate through brief, recorded updates rather than scheduling live meetings."
