**Website:** https://pipekit.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/pipekit

Pipekit is a remote-based infrastructure company that provides a management layer for Argo Workflows, an open-source pipeline framework for Kubernetes. The platform enables data teams to build complex data and CI pipelines more efficiently, claiming to reduce build times by half while saving enterprises hundreds of thousands of dollars annually.

The company maintains Argo Workflows, which is used in production by major organizations including Bloomberg, Intuit, Adobe, New Relic, and NVIDIA. Pipekit operates as a B2B developer tool focused on data engineering and DevOps, helping enterprises manage their pipeline infrastructure at scale.

_Usage:_

"How does Pipekit differ from just using Argo Workflows directly?"

"Pipekit acts as a managed application on top of Argo Workflows, which is the underlying open-source framework. While you can use Argo Workflows directly, Pipekit provides the management layer that allows data teams to handle complex pipelines more easily, reportedly cutting the time required to set them up in half and reducing annual costs for enterprises."
