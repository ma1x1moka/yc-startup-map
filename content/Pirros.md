**Website:** http://pirros.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/pirros

Pirros is an AI platform that helps architecture and engineering firms manage design details, streamlining the detail management process, reducing errors, and speeding up project delivery. It is building the system of record for detail management in a $12T+ industry that has been underserved by technology for too long.

The company raised a Series A of about $17 million in 2025 from top‑tier investors including YC, Funders Club, PlanGrid, and Elephant. Pirros is profitable with roughly $6 million ARR and growing revenue at over 10% month‑over‑month. It serves more than 300 firms, growing 10% MoM, and boasts 120% net revenue retention.

Pirros is headquartered in Los Angeles, CA, with a team of 44 employees. Its co‑founders come from structural engineering, giving the platform deep industry expertise.
