**Website:** http://www.platohq.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/plato

Plato provides a mentorship platform designed to help engineering and product managers resolve challenging management situations in real time. The service connects managers directly with experienced technical leaders from companies like Google, Netflix, Lyft, and Box through video one-on-one conversations, AMAs, and group sessions.

Part of the Y Combinator Winter 2016 batch, the San Francisco-based B2B productivity company operated with a team of 26 across local and remote locations. Plato has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Plato connect engineering managers with mentors during active management challenges?"

"Plato enables real-time interaction through one-on-one video conversations and AMAs. Instead of static training courses, managers get paired directly with veteran tech leaders from companies like Google, Netflix, Lyft, and Box to address specific workplace situations as they happen."
