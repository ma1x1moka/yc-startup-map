Players' Lounge enables gamers of all skill levels to place bets on the outcome of their video game matches and win money. The platform adds a monetary return to gaming time, creating an extra layer of fun and entertainment.

The company raised $15 million from investors including Y Combinator, Samsung, Griffin Gaming Partners, Strauss Zelnick, Drake and Myles Garrett. It operates out of New York City and Brooklyn with a team of 13.

Players' Lounge remains active in the consumer gaming space, offering a novel betting experience for AAA titles.
