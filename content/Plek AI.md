**Website:** https://www.plek.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/plek-ai

Plek is the AI company brain for private equity value creation. Upload an employee census, and Plek maps every person to a taxonomy of 50,000 jobs and 250,000 tasks, down to the time each task takes. It then analyzes spans and layers, org structure, footprint, compensation, functional fragmentation and AI exposure in parallel, and reconciles overlapping levers employee by employee so every dollar is traceable.

What takes a performance improvement team 6 to 8 weeks takes Plek under a minute. At a 3,600-person company, Plek found $30M in annual EBITDA levers, matching what a global PE advisory firm found in six weeks. Funds and advisories use Plek from due diligence to exit to run org value creation scenarios, from the platform or from their own AI agents via MCP.

Plek grew out of the MIT Work Analytics Lab, founded by CEO Pierre Bouquet during his MIT PhD. Long-term, Plek becomes the Celonis of the workforce: it continuously monitors the org and flags value creation levers as they appear.
