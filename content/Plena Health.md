Plena is an AI operating system designed for specialty medical practices. It automates the administrative workflows that keep clinics running—referrals, fax intake, scheduling, procedure compliance, and collections—end to end across all the systems a practice already uses. Unlike point solutions that cover only a slice of the problem, Plena becomes the operating layer across the entire practice, connecting the systems, teams, and workflows that keep it running. In its first ten months, Plena grew 17 times and crossed seven figures in committed annual recurring revenue across six specialties and eight states. Eighty percent of that growth came through word of mouth, underscoring the product’s fit and the demand for a unified AI‑powered practice layer.

_Usage:_

"How does Plena integrate with a clinic’s existing EHR and practice management systems?"

"Plena connects to the systems a practice already uses, acting as an operating layer that automates referrals, fax intake, scheduling, procedure compliance, and collections across those systems. It does not replace the underlying software but instead orchestrates the data and workflows between them, providing a unified AI‑driven interface for the entire practice."
