Plerk offers a fintech solution that allows companies to provide flexible benefits and perks to local and remote employees regardless of geography. The platform utilizes virtual cards to enable employees to choose their preferred merchants for various perks, while companies retain total control over budgets and receive a single consolidated bill.

The company, which was acquired by Minu.mx, operated with a team of 28 people based in Guadalajara, Jal., Mexico, and remotely. It focused on B2B operations, helping organizations deploy funds for general or granular perk programs while giving employees the autonomy to decide what they truly need.

_Usage:_

"How does Plerk handle the complexity of paying for employee perks across different countries?"

"Plerk solves this by issuing virtual cards that allow employees to spend directly at local merchants, bypassing the need for centralized procurement in every region. Companies set the budget and program rules, but employees choose the specific vendors, and the company receives one bill for all transactions, simplifying accounting for distributed teams."
