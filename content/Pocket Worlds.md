Pocket Worlds is the creator of Highrise, a virtual world that is now featured in the iOS App Store and has 50 million users.

The company builds bleeding‑edge technology that allows developers and amateurs to create immersive experiences and worlds inside its virtual universe.

With a team of about 100 people based in Austin, TX, and remote, Pocket Worlds is positioned at the forefront of the consumer and social gaming industry.
