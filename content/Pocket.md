Founded by [Akshay Narisetti](./Akshay%20Narisetti.md) and [Gabriel Dymowski](./Gabriel%20Dymowski.md). San Francisco. Team of 15.

The revenue outlier of the entire W26 batch: **$27M ARR**, 30,000+ units shipped, 50% month-over-month growth — all within five months of launch. Raised $11M (June 2026).

A small clip-on device with 3 microphones and 1 button that captures, transcribes, and summarises in-person conversations, meetings, and phone calls with automatic to-do extraction. No app to open, no record button to press — it's always on.

The AI wearable graveyard is long: Google Glass, Amazon Echo Frames, Humane AI Pin. Pocket survived where others failed by solving one specific pain — forgotten conversations and untracked action items — rather than trying to replace the phone.

The 50% month-over-month retention through five months signals real behaviour change, not launch curiosity. Series A-level traction from a seed hardware company is essentially unprecedented in recent YC history.

_Avoid:_ Comparing Pocket to Otter.ai or similar phone-based transcription apps. Otter requires deliberate recording. Pocket is ambient and passive — the behaviour change is the product.

_Usage:_

"What makes Pocket different from just leaving Otter running?"

"Friction. Opening an app and pressing record means you miss spontaneous conversations, hallway decisions, and the meeting where someone forgot to hit record. Pocket's always-on form factor removes the decision entirely."
