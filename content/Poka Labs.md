**Website:** https://www.pokalabs.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/poka-labs

Poka Labs is a San Francisco-based startup building an AI platform designed to assist sales representatives at chemical manufacturers. The system analyzes product catalogs, customer purchase behaviors, and internal tribal knowledge to generate accurate pricing and quotes, enabling teams to respond to customer requests more efficiently.

The company serves industrial manufacturers ranging from those with a few million dollars in annual sales to those with billions. By automating the complex process of quoting and pricing, Poka Labs aims to help these companies win more business while maintaining optimal margins.

Founded by Malay and Andrew, who met at Harvard Business School, the five-person team operates out of San Francisco and focuses on the intersection of B2B sales and supply chain logistics.

_Usage:_

"How does the system handle the specific pricing logic for different chemical products?"

"The platform ingests the company's specific product catalog, historical customer purchase behaviors, and unstructured 'tribal knowledge' to understand the nuances of each deal. This allows the AI to generate quotes that are not just fast, but also strategically priced to win the business, rather than relying on generic rule-based systems."
