**Website:** https://www.poliglota.org/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/poliglota

Poliglota operates as an online language school focused on the Latin American market, providing personalized language sessions in small groups. The platform matches students of the same proficiency level with expert language coaches who are trained in the company's proprietary social methodology.

Based in Santiago, Chile, the company has built a team of 170 employees to support its educational services. It has served over 12,000 students who have paid to learn English, with many citing the goal of securing higher income as their primary motivation for enrollment.

_Usage:_

"How does Poliglota structure its classes compared to one-on-one tutoring?"

"Instead of individual sessions, Poliglota groups students by proficiency level and assigns them to a small class led by a single instructor. The coaches are specifically trained in a social methodology to facilitate these group interactions, which the company positions as a way to make language learning more effective for its Latin American user base."
