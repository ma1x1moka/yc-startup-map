**Website:** https://polymail.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/polymail

Polymail is an email platform designed specifically for sales communication, helping teams increase conversion rates and shorten sales cycles. The tool integrates email tracking, Salesforce synchronization, and calendar scheduling to streamline workflows for sales professionals.

Based in Los Angeles and backed by Y Combinator, the company has assisted thousands of customers in transforming their sales communication processes. It provides executives with transparency into sales activity, offering visibility into team performance and outreach efforts.

The company has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Polymail integrate with existing CRM and calendar tools for sales teams?"

"Polymail syncs directly with Salesforce and includes calendar scheduling features alongside email tracking. This allows sales teams to manage their outreach and track engagement without leaving their email client, while giving executives a clear view of sales activity and conversion metrics."
