Pop Meals is a restaurant chain that operates without traditional chefs, relying instead on a network of cloud kitchens and technology-enabled quick service restaurant outlets. The company is focused on building leading food brands across the Asian market.

Founded in the Summer 2017 Y Combinator batch, Pop Meals has raised over $28 million from Y Combinator and leading tech investors in Silicon Valley, Europe, and Asia. The company is currently expanding rapidly across the region.

With a team of 153 employees based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, Pop Meals sits at the intersection of consumer food and beverage and automated food service, utilizing ghost kitchen infrastructure to scale its operations.

_Usage:_

"How does Pop Meals differ from a traditional restaurant chain or a standard ghost kitchen operator?"

"Pop Meals combines the ghost kitchen model with a 'restaurant chain without chefs' approach, meaning it relies on tech-enabled QSR outlets and standardized recipes rather than individual culinary talent. It is funded as a tech company to build food brands across Asia, rather than just providing delivery infrastructure for existing brands."
