**Website:** https://popsql.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/popsql

PopSQL is an SQL editor for teams that addresses broken data collaboration by letting users write SQL, visualize results, and share them in a single interface. The product has attracted more than 2000 companies, including DoorDash, Shipt, Redfin, and Optimizely, and is built by a 15‑person team in San Francisco and remote. PopSQL was acquired, marking it as a notable entrant in the B2B engineering and product design space.

_Usage:_

"What makes PopSQL's collaboration features stand out compared to other SQL editors?"

"PopSQL lets teams write, visualize, and share SQL results all in one place, eliminating the need to rewrite queries or copy/paste into Slack, which streamlines data collaboration."
