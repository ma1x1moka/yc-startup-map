**Website:** https://www.positional.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/positional

Positional provides a modern SaaS platform designed to streamline inbound marketing and SEO strategies. It connects the entire workflow, linking initial keyword research with final analytics and user actions that drive business revenue. The toolset is explicitly optimized for tangible results rather than vanity metrics.

The company operated with a small team of five people across locations in Charleston, SC, and Hoboken, NJ. It focused on the B2B marketing sector, offering a comprehensive solution for scaling digital presence.

Positional was acquired, marking the end of its independent operation as a YC-backed startup.

_Usage:_

"How does Positional differ from standard SEO tools that just track rankings?"

"Positional aims to close the loop between SEO efforts and actual revenue. Instead of just showing you where you rank, it connects the workflow from keyword research through to the specific user actions that result in sales, helping you see which content actually drives business value rather than just traffic."
