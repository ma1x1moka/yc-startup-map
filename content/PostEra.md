PostEra is building a modern 21st century biopharma by using machine learning to accelerate medicinal chemistry and bring more cures to patients. The company advances small molecule programs through partnerships with biopharma while also developing its own internal pipeline.
PostEra has raised $26M from top investors and closed $1Bn in AI partnerships, signing multi‑year agreements with Amgen, Pfizer and the NIH. It also launched and led the world’s largest open‑science drug discovery effort, COVID Moonshot.
The company is headquartered in Boston, MA, and employs a team of 40 people.
