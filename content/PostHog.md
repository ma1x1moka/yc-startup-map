**Website:** https://www.posthog.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/posthog

PostHog is a single platform that gives engineers everything they need to build better products, including product analytics, session replays, feedback tools, feature flags, experimentation, LLM observability, and a data warehouse with SQL and one-click data imports. The company has a team of 150, operates out of San Francisco and remote, and serves B2B analytics customers. It has maintained an average monthly revenue growth of about 10% and remains self-sustaining without a large recent funding round.

_Usage:_

"What does PostHog's LLM observability feature do?"

"LLM observability tracks and analyzes large language model usage and performance within the product, helping teams understand model behavior."
