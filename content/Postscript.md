**Website:** https://postscript.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/postscript

Postscript provides an SMS revenue platform that lets e‑commerce merchants drive sales through text‑message conversations. Its service is used by brands such as Ruggable, Dr. Squatch and Thrive Cosmetics, as well as more than 20,000 other merchants.

The company reports that merchants generate over $2 billion in e‑commerce revenue each year through its platform, and it is growing roughly twice as fast annually. Postscript handles massive scale, supporting real‑time conversations across 100 million phones.

With a remote team of 180 employees, Postscript positions itself as the #1 revenue channel for e‑commerce, aiming to eventually enable more than $1 trillion in annual merchant revenue.
