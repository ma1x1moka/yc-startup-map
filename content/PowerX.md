**Website:** http://www.powerx.co | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/powerx

PowerX creates AI‑powered sensors that generate proprietary data, enabling corporations to reduce energy, water and emissions by 30% or more. The company’s technology combines machine learning with IoT hardware to identify inefficiencies across industrial operations.

PowerX was acquired after demonstrating its impact in the industrial and climate sectors. The acquisition highlights the market’s interest in AI‑driven sustainability solutions.

Based in New York City with a team of about 20 employees, PowerX participated in YC’s Winter 2020 batch and focuses on the Industrials and Climate industries.

_Usage:_

"How do PowerX’s sensors achieve the claimed 30% reduction in energy, water, and emissions?"

"PowerX’s sensors collect detailed usage data and feed it into machine‑learning models that spot waste patterns. By providing actionable insights, the system lets companies adjust processes and achieve at least a 30% cut in energy, water and emissions."
