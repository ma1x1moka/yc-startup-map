Presti AI provides a faster, cheaper solution for furniture companies to produce product images using generative AI.

Its core model is optimized for furniture products and includes multiple features tailored to the unique needs of the industry.

Since launch, the company has grown to 100 paying B2B customers, with a 10% week‑over‑week growth rate. It raised a $3.5M seed round in Summer 2024 and operates with a 16‑person team based in Paris, France. Presti AI’s focus on furniture and its specialized AI model set it apart from generic generative AI tools, making it a notable player in B2B retail e‑commerce.
