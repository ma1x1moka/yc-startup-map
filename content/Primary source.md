description: Material that is authoritative because it is the thing itself: the code, the schema, the actual error, the real API response.
---

Primary sources cannot be wrong about what they describe, because they are what they describe. The file's contents are the truth about the file. The stack trace is the truth about the failure. Loading these into [context](./Context.md) converts guesswork into reading.

They cost more [tokens](./Token.md) than a summary and are worth it wherever precision matters. A description of a schema invites the [model](./Model.md) to fill gaps from parametric memory; the schema itself does not.

_Usage:_

"I described the error to it."

"Paste the actual trace. Your description dropped the frame that matters."
