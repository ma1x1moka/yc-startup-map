**Website:** https://procoto.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/procoto

Procoto provides a platform that simplifies running RFPs, tracking contracts, and managing vendors for procurement teams. It is designed to help organizations move away from dense legacy systems and spreadsheets without incurring the high costs associated with enterprise solutions like SAP or Coupa.

The company, which operated with a team of four based in Saint Petersburg, FL, and remotely, focused on making procurement processes more affordable and accessible. Its status is listed as Acquired, indicating it was purchased by another entity, though specific details regarding the acquirer or transaction date are not provided in the directory data.

_Usage:_

"How does Procoto differ from enterprise procurement suites like SAP or Coupa?"

"Procoto positions itself as a more affordable and self-service alternative for teams that find enterprise systems too complex or expensive. It aims to get procurement teams out of spreadsheets and dense legacy tools without requiring a large budget commitment typically associated with major vendors."
