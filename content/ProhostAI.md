**Website:** https://www.prohost.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/prohostai

ProhostAI builds an AI‑powered property manager for short‑term rental hosts on Airbnb and other platforms. The platform streamlines tasks such as pricing optimization, guest communication, and maintenance coordination, allowing hosts to focus on growing their rental businesses.
The team is made up of an ex‑Airbnb staff data scientist and a former Dropbox senior software engineer, both of whom are active Superhosts managing nine properties valued at $15 million and generating $1.5 million per year.
Based in New York City, ProhostAI serves the B2B travel market as a SaaS solution that leverages artificial intelligence to deliver a hands‑off experience for hosts.

_Usage:_

"How does ProhostAI differ from traditional property management software?"

"ProhostAI uses AI to automate pricing, messaging, and maintenance scheduling for Airbnb hosts, drawing on data from Airbnb and other platforms, whereas traditional software typically requires manual input for these tasks."
