**Website:** https://www.prolific.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/prolific

Prolific helps dedicated research teams collect high‑quality human‑powered data at scale using a simple‑to‑use platform that targets and manages participants from a diverse, vetted participant pool.
Created by researchers for researchers, Prolific was founded to provide a better way for researchers and organisations to obtain high‑quality human data and feedback at scale for important research. More than 30,000 researchers across academia and industry use Prolific to gather definitive human data and feedback from reliable, engaged and fairly‑treated participants, with a new study launched every 3 minutes.
The company operates as a B2B marketplace from London, England, with a team of 180 employees.

_Usage:_

"How does Prolific ensure the quality of its participant pool compared to other crowdsourcing platforms?"

"Prolific uses a vetted participant pool, ensuring participants are engaged and fairly treated, and offers a simple‑to‑use platform to target and manage participants, which leads to high‑quality data for researchers."
