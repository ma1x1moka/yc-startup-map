**Website:** http://www.prometheusfuels.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/prometheus

Prometheus removes CO₂ and water from the air and uses electricity generated by solar and wind farms to synthesize electrofuels that are chemically identical to gasoline, diesel and jet fuel. The fuels can be used in existing vehicles and aircraft without any engine modifications, and they do not add new CO₂ to the atmosphere because the carbon is drawn from the air. Each gallon of Prometheus electrofuel effectively leaves a gallon of fossil oil un‑burned. Prometheus’ zero‑net‑carbon jet fuel is priced at one cent per gallon less than conventional jet fuel, making it the first renewable fuel that can compete on price. American Airlines has placed an order for 10 million gallons, which the company plans to begin shipping next year.

_Usage:_

"Why can Prometheus’ electrofuel be cheaper than traditional jet fuel despite the energy needed for CO₂ capture and synthesis?"

"Prometheus uses only solar and wind electricity, which have very low marginal costs, to power the electro‑chemical process that converts captured CO₂ and water into fuel. By avoiding the cost of extracting and refining fossil oil, the overall production cost can be lower, allowing the company to price its jet fuel a cent per gallon below conventional alternatives."
