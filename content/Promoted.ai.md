**Website:** http://www.promoted.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/promoted

Promoted.ai provides a unified platform for search, native advertising, and feed ranking designed for online marketplaces and scaled e-commerce applications. By leveraging AI, the company aims to dramatically improve relevance and revenue for its users. The team consists of twelve engineers based in Seattle, WA, with backgrounds at Google, Meta, and Pinterest.

The company serves publicly traded and late-stage marketplace companies, including Eventbrite, Upwork, and Outschool. Its mission is to connect every buyer and seller by enhancing the efficiency of these digital marketplaces.

Promoted.ai was acquired, marking the conclusion of its independent operation as a YC-backed startup.

_Usage:_

"How does Promoted.ai differ from building search and ads in-house?"

"Instead of maintaining separate systems for search, ads, and feed ranking, Promoted.ai unifies them into a single AI-driven stack. This approach is designed to improve relevance and revenue simultaneously, which is particularly useful for marketplace companies that need to balance buyer discovery with seller monetization without the overhead of building and maintaining distinct infrastructure for each function."
