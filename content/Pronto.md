description: Pronto provides a ride‑sharing and delivery platform for second‑tier cities in Latin America. YC W19.

**Website:** http://www.tuprontomx.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/pronto

Pronto is the first ride‑sharing and delivery platform built specifically for second‑tier cities in Latin America, where cash transactions dominate. Because cash payments make the Uber commission model impractical, Pronto charges drivers a monthly subscription ranging from $59 to $99 based on their revenue, which yields higher lifetime value, low customer acquisition cost, and strong driver engagement. Since launch the company has processed over 2,000,000 trips, serving more than 200,000 users, and after bootstrapping to half a million trips it joined Y Combinator’s Winter 2019 batch. It targets a market of over 14,000 second‑tier cities and 500 million people lacking access to ride‑sharing platforms. The team of 62 operates from Mexico City.

_Usage:_

"How does Pronto make money if most rides are paid in cash?"

"Pronto bypasses cash commissions by charging drivers a recurring subscription fee of $59‑$99 per month, which is tied to the driver’s monthly revenue. This subscription model creates a predictable revenue stream for the company while keeping the service free for riders who pay cash."
