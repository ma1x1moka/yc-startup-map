---
description: Having the agent build a quick, rough version when conversation is too low-fidelity and you need something real to react to.
---

Some disagreements cannot be resolved in words. A rough build makes the [design concept](./Design%20concept.md) concrete enough to have opinions about, and people are far better at critiquing something in front of them than at specifying it in advance.

The discipline is in what happens next. A prototype's value is the understanding it produces, and treating one as a foundation — because it works and throwing it away feels wasteful — is how throwaway code becomes permanent.

_Usage:_

"We've argued about this layout for an hour."

"Have it prototype both and look at them."
