**Website:** https://useprox.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/prox

Prox solves technical support for complex physical products — $20,000 industrial heaters, HVAC systems, CNC machines, car parts. These products require highly trained domain specialists to support, and there aren't enough of them.

If you're selling EV charging stations, your support person needs to be a certified electrician who understands local power grids, installation codes, and compatibility matrices. Prox provides AI that covers this domain expertise gap. Team of 7.

_Usage:_

"How does Prox’s AI replace the need for a certified electrician when supporting EV charging stations?"

"Prox’s AI is positioned as a knowledge layer that fills the domain‑expertise gap – it encodes the installation codes, power‑grid specifics and compatibility matrices that a certified electrician would normally provide, letting support staff answer complex EV‑charging queries without hiring a specialist."
