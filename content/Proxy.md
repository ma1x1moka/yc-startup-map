**Website:** https://proxy.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/proxy

Proxy is a San Francisco‑based startup that creates a mobile and wearable digital wallet delivered as a smart ring, enabling users to interact with payments, identity, and authentication in a seamless and fun way. The product merges consumer electronics with hard‑tech to make authentication empowering.

The company was acquired by OURA in 2023, underscoring its relevance in the consumer hardware space. At the time of acquisition Proxy employed about 20 people across San Francisco and remote locations.

_Usage:_

"How does Proxy’s smart ring enable payments compared to using a phone‑based wallet?"

"Proxy’s ring acts as a wearable digital wallet, so users can tap the ring to pay, authenticate, or present identity without needing to pull out a phone. The ring stores the necessary credentials and communicates via NFC, making the transaction process more seamless than opening an app on a phone."
