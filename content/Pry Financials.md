**Website:** https://www.pry.co | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/pry-financials

Pry Financials, a San Francisco-based team of seven, built software to streamline financial planning for startups. The platform allowed companies to manage their budget, hiring plans, financial models, and cash runway, aiming to replace the buggy and time-expensive process of using Excel for quarterly planning. The company was part of the Winter 2021 Y Combinator batch and focused on the B2B finance and accounting sector. Pry Financials has since been acquired and now operates as prune.com.

_Usage:_

"What was the main problem Pry solved for startup founders?"

"It replaced the manual, error-prone process of managing budgets and hiring plans in Excel with a dedicated tool that handled financial models and cash runway tracking, making the quarterly planning process less confusing and time-consuming."
