Pulpix leverages artificial intelligence to deliver a personalized video experience for online video platforms, enabling viewers to binge‑watch content seamlessly. The company operates as a B2B service focused on marketing applications and is based in New York City with a team of 15.

Pulpix was acquired, highlighting its notable impact in the AI‑driven video space.

_Usage:_

"How is Pulpix’s binge‑watching technology different from typical video recommendation systems?"

"Pulpix uses AI to tailor the entire viewing session rather than just suggesting individual videos, creating a continuous, personalized binge‑watch experience for platforms that integrate its service."
