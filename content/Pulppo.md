**Website:** https://www.pulppo.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/pulppo

Pulppo functions as a comprehensive operating system for real estate brokerages across Latin America, often described as the Shopify for the industry. The platform integrates performance analytics, automatic online property listings, valuation tools, and a customer relationship management system into a single suite. By automating non-value-added activities, the company aims to streamline workflows and provide greater transparency to both agents and clients.

The company operates out of Mexico City with a team of 29, targeting a market that generates billions in annual commissions. Its integrated approach allows agents to deliver a more efficient customer journey while maintaining visibility into key business metrics. Pulppo has since been acquired, marking a significant milestone for the proptech startup.

_Usage:_

"How does Pulppo differ from standard CRM tools used by real estate agents?"

"Unlike standalone CRMs that only track leads, Pulppo integrates the entire transaction lifecycle including automatic listing generation, property valuation, and performance analytics. It is designed specifically for the Latin American market to handle the full workflow from initial contact to closing, rather than just managing customer data."
