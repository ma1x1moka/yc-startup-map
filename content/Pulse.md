**Website:** https://www.pulse.qa | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/pulse-2

Pulse builds a crowdsourced knowledge marketplace for C‑Suite technology executives and the vendors that serve them. With nearly 15,000 executives on the platform and hundreds added daily, the service aims to change how B2B decisions are made. Companies such as Salesforce, Microsoft, Okta and Dialpad rely on Pulse to support their strategy, content and marketing teams.

Pulse was acquired, underscoring its impact on the enterprise knowledge space. The company, founded in YC’s Winter 2018 batch, operates out of San Francisco with a team of 11.

_Usage:_

"How is Pulse different from traditional analyst firms like Gartner?"

"Pulse uses a crowdsourced model where technology executives contribute insights, whereas Gartner relies on its own research analysts. This lets Pulse provide real‑time, peer‑driven advice that vendors such as Salesforce and Microsoft can tap into daily."
