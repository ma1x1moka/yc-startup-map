**Website:** http://www.revlo.co | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/pursuit

Pursuit, operating under the brand Revlo, helped eSports streamers on Twitch better connect with and monetize their fan bases. The company addressed the limitation of standard IRC chat rooms by allowing streamers to reward loyal fans with recognition, merchandise, and direct interaction opportunities. This approach aimed to drive higher repeat viewership and conversion into paid subscriptions for streamers.

The startup was based in San Francisco and focused on the consumer gaming sector, specifically within the creator economy. Pursuit was acquired, marking the end of its independent operations.

_Usage:_

"How did Revlo differ from standard Twitch chat interactions for streamers?"

"Standard Twitch interaction relied heavily on cluttered IRC chat rooms, which limited direct engagement. Revlo provided tools for streamers to actively reward fans with recognition, merchandise, and specific interaction opportunities, turning passive viewers into more engaged subscribers."
