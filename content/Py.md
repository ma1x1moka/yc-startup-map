**Website:** https://www.getpy.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/py

Py offers iOS and Android apps that deliver millions of lessons and quizzes, modernizing traditional online teaching tools.
The company launched Py for Work, a platform that lets companies assess candidates with real-world assessments instead of brain teasers, helping industry leaders such as LiveRamp, Amplitude, and Factual hire based on merit.
Py was acquired.
The team of three is based in San Francisco, CA, and the company operates in the education sector with a focus on recruiting.
