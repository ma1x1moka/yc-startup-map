Pylon is an agentic support platform designed specifically for B2B enterprises, allowing human teams and AI agents to collaborate on customer work. The system investigates, resolves, and acts on signals across various channels, distinguishing itself from pre-AI platforms by enriching every interaction with deep account-level context.

By automating low-impact tasks and surfacing answers proactively, Pylon aims to accelerate response times and improve customer satisfaction while creating a self-improving support system. The company is headquartered in San Francisco with a team of 120 employees.

Pylon is used by hundreds of fast-growing YC companies, including Retool, Hightouch, and Deel, to manage their customer operations.

_Usage:_

"How does Pylon differ from traditional helpdesk tools that just added a chatbot?"

"Traditional tools often treat AI as an add-on, whereas Pylon is built around agentic collaboration where humans and agents work together on the same tickets. It uses deep account-level context to automate low-impact work and surface answers before agents even ask, rather than just deflecting simple queries."
