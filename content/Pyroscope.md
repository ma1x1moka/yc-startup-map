**Website:** https://pyroscope.io/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/pyroscope

Pyroscope is an open source continuous profiling platform designed to help engineers debug performance issues and identify bottlenecks in their code. The tool integrates with applications using just a few lines of code, providing visibility into system performance without requiring significant architectural changes.

The company was based in San Francisco and Oakland, California, and operated as a small team of five engineers. It focused on the B2B market, specifically serving engineering and product design teams through its SaaS and open source offerings.

Pyroscope was acquired, marking the end of its independent operation as a YC-backed startup.

_Usage:_

"How does Pyroscope differ from traditional APM tools or manual profiling?"

"Pyroscope focuses on continuous profiling, which allows you to constantly monitor performance bottlenecks rather than just capturing snapshots when things break. It’s designed to be lightweight, requiring only a few lines of code to integrate, so you can find performance issues in your code without the overhead of more complex observability stacks."
