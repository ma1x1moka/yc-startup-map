Quadrant Eye is a San Francisco-based startup that set out to make eye exams fully online, enabling patients to get high-quality care from their homes. It aimed to improve eyecare access and fight preventable blindness.

The team combined surgeons, scientists, venture partners, and hackers to develop patentable deep-tech solutions. Backed by Y Combinator, Khosla Ventures, and the creator of Google Image Search, Quadrant Eye targeted an eye-health market worth $137B.

Despite its ambitious vision to change eyecare for good, the company is now inactive, having ceased operations after its Winter 2021 batch.
