**Website:** https://quantstamp.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/quantstamp

Quantstamp protects digital assets on Web3 by performing security audits and offering advisory services. The company has secured billions of dollars in assets through hundreds of audits conducted by its global team of security professionals. It has earned the trust of leading projects such as Aave, Compound, Maker, OpenSea, and Polygon, and also supports the ecosystem through strategic investments and scaling advice.

With a team of about 80 members located in San Francisco and remotely, Quantstamp positions itself as the leading blockchain security firm in the emerging industry. Its services span a range of security offerings designed to keep innovative Web3 products safe.

_Usage:_

"How does Quantstamp’s audit process differ from a typical software security review?"

"Quantstamp focuses specifically on blockchain and Web3 code, applying its expertise from hundreds of audits that have protected billions in digital assets. Rather than a generic software review, its audits address the unique attack vectors of smart contracts and decentralized applications, and the firm also provides strategic investment and advisory support to help projects scale securely."
