**Website:** https://queenly.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/queenly

Queenly was a San Francisco-based online marketplace focused on the formalwear industry, operating across iOS, Android, and web platforms. The company, which emerged from Y Combinator's Winter 2021 batch, aimed to empower women by providing cutting-edge technology and seamless logistics for purchasing formal attire. It was backed by investors including Andreessen Horowitz, NextView Ventures, and The House Fund, and its cofounders were featured on the Forbes 30 Under 30: Art and Style cover for 2022.

The company has since become inactive. At its peak, Queenly maintained a team of 13 employees and positioned itself as a leader in sustainable fashion and e-commerce within the consumer apparel sector.
