**Website:** https://qulture.rocks/en/home/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/qulture-rocks

Qulture.Rocks builds people management, OKR management, and culture management software for some of the world’s most innovative companies. The company is backed by investors such as Founders Fund, Blackstone, Sequoia, and Tiger Global.
Its product suite covers 1:1 meetings, engagement surveys, performance reviews, OKRs/goals, ongoing feedback, and peer‑to‑peer recognition, and it integrates seamlessly with existing software including Slack. Qulture.Rocks also assists customers in designing their people processes, which is especially helpful for startups and scale‑ups implementing these practices for the first time.
Qulture.Rocks was acquired, and it now operates with a team of about 70 people out of San Francisco.

_Usage:_

"Can Qulture.Rocks integrate with our current Slack workspace, or do we need a separate HR system?"

"Qulture.Rocks integrates directly with existing tools such as Slack, allowing teams to run 1:1s, surveys, OKRs, and recognition within the same environment without requiring a separate platform."
