**Website:** https://r2.co/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/r2

R2 provides embedded lending infrastructure that allows platforms to seamlessly offer capital to their small and medium-sized business customers across Latin America. The company operates out of Mexico City with a team of approximately 100 people, focusing on the fintech and credit sectors.

R2 was acquired, a move that underscores its significance in the B2B lending space for the region. By integrating directly into existing platforms, it enables non-financial businesses to extend credit without building their own lending engines from scratch.

_Usage:_

"How does R2 differ from a traditional bank loan for an SMB?"

"R2 is not a direct-to-consumer lender but rather infrastructure that lets a platform (like a marketplace or logistics provider) offer loans to its own SMB users. Instead of the SMB applying to a bank, the platform embeds R2's lending capabilities into its own workflow, allowing the SMB to access capital seamlessly within the ecosystem they already use."
