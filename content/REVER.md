REVER is a Barcelona-based fintech and logistics company that simplifies the post-sale experience in e-commerce. The platform allows shoppers to initiate returns in just two clicks and receive refunds within 24 hours, aiming to make returning items as easy as buying them. By handling over 100 million euros in yearly returns, REVER positions itself as a comprehensive solution for global online returns.

The company operates with a team of 35 people and generates more than 50 million euros in annual revenue. Its infrastructure supports frictionless and sustainable returns for merchants, addressing the logistical and financial complexities typically associated with product returns. REVER’s model focuses on reducing friction for consumers while providing a scalable backend for e-commerce businesses.

_Usage:_

"How does REVER handle the logistics and financial risk of instant refunds?"

"REVER acts as a global platform that manages the entire post-sale process, allowing merchants to offer two-click returns and 24-hour refunds. It handles over 100 million euros in annual returns, effectively absorbing the logistical and financial complexity of processing these transactions so that the checkout and return experience remains seamless for the end user."
