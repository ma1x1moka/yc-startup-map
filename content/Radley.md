**Website:** https://radley.health/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/radley

Radley operates as the first AI-native radiology practice, having acquired an existing radiology business to reach $4.3M in annualized revenue. The company is rebuilding this practice using AI agents to address a critical shortage of radiologists, a gap that currently leaves one in six medical scans uninterpreted and is projected to worsen to one in three within a decade.

Unlike point solutions that only address software needs, Radley owns the full clinical and operational stack to overcome outdated systems and inefficient operations. The company has secured $2.2M in contracts, demonstrating market demand for its end-to-end approach to modernizing radiology workflows.

Founded by operators with experience scaling Carbon Health to over 100 clinics, Radley is based in San Francisco and operates remotely. It is part of the Y Combinator Summer 2026 batch.

_Usage:_

"How does Radley differ from standard AI diagnostic tools that just help radiologists read scans?"

"Standard tools are point solutions that only assist with interpretation, but Radley argues that the bottleneck is actually outdated systems and slow operations. Instead of just selling software, Radley acquired a practice and rebuilt the entire clinical and operational stack with agents, effectively running the radiology business itself rather than just providing a tool for existing practices to use."
