Co-founded [Cohere.io](./Cohere.io.md) with [Yunyu Lin](./Yunyu%20Lin.md), a customer-support automation startup later acquired by Ramp. Sengottuvelu, a Duke University graduate, now serves as CTO of Ramp, continuing to build on the technology and team from Cohere.io.
