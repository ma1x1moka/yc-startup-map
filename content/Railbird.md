**Website:** https://www.railbirdexchange.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/railbird

Railbird built a prediction market exchange specifically designed to help the services sector hedge and mitigate financial risk. The platform targeted volatile industries such as hospitality, tourism, recreation, entertainment, and sports, where traditional risk-hedging opportunities were scarce. By opening the exchange to all traders, Railbird aimed to provide a more liquid and accessible way to manage uncertainty in these markets.

The company, based in New York City with a team of eight, operated in the fintech and marketplace space. It was notably acquired, marking the end of its independent operations as a YC-backed startup.

_Usage:_

"How does Railbird differ from general prediction markets like Polymarket or Kalshi?"

"While general prediction markets focus on broad political or cultural events, Railbird was specifically engineered for the services sector. It allowed traders to hedge against volatility in industries like hospitality and tourism, providing a financial instrument tailored to business risk rather than just speculative event outcomes."
