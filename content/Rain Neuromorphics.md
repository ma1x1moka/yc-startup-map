Rain Neuromorphics develops neuromorphic hardware that mimics the brain's structure, building a physical artificial neural network composed of spiking neurons and physical synapses. It combines electroceramic materials, nanofabrication techniques, and biologically plausible learning models to create the Memristive Nanowire Neural Network (MN3). The MN3 can host over ten million spiking neurons per square centimeter and consumes far less power than conventional GPUs and CPUs, making it attractive for industrial, manufacturing, and robotics applications that require power efficiency and continuous learning. Rain Neuromorphics was founded in Summer 2018, has a team of 12, and operates out of San Francisco and Redwood City, CA. The company was acquired.
