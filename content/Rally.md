Rally offers agentic user research infrastructure that automates end‑to‑end recruitment, from finding participants through scheduling and sending incentives.
Its infrastructure enforces enterprise guardrails such as consent management, permission controls, templated workflows, and data governance.
Product research teams at Google, Meta, Ramp, Figma, and Perplexity trust Rally, and the company employs 35 people across New York City and remote locations.

_Usage:_

"How does Rally ensure consent and data‑governance compared to traditional manual recruitment processes?"

"Rally’s agent embeds consent, permission checks, templates, and data‑governance rules directly into the recruitment workflow, so those compliance steps are automatically applied without the manual effort required by conventional processes."
