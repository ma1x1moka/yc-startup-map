**Website:** https://raycast.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/raycast

Raycast is a productivity tool that helps engineering teams reduce time spent on non‑coding tasks across common apps such as Jira, GitHub, and Slack. It targets the portion of a developer’s day—up to half—that can be consumed by coordination and administrative work.
By providing a unified command interface, Raycast lets engineers trigger actions in those services without leaving their primary workflow, which speeds up routine processes and improves overall efficiency. The company, part of the Winter 2020 batch, operates with a 27‑person team based in London and remote locations.

_Usage:_

"Does Raycast replace the native integrations in Jira, GitHub, and Slack?"

"Raycast doesn’t replace those integrations; it offers a single command palette that lets engineers invoke the same actions across the apps from one place, reducing context‑switching and saving time on non‑coding work."
