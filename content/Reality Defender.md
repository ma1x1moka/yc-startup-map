Reality Defender is the market leader in deepfake detection, offering real‑time protection for enterprises and governments against deepfake and agentic fraud. Founded in Winter 2022 and headquartered in New York City, the company has grown to a 54‑person team that delivers its solution as a SaaS platform. Its technology has earned multiple accolades, including Gartner Market Leader in 2025, a World Economic Forum Technology Pioneer award, and first place in the 2025 Global Innovation Challenge by USA DIU and Japan MOD.

_Usage:_

"How does Reality Defender’s real‑time deepfake detection work compared to traditional forensic analysis?"

"Reality Defender’s platform continuously scans incoming media streams, using AI models trained on synthetic and real content to flag deepfakes instantly, whereas traditional forensic methods typically analyze static footage after the fact."
