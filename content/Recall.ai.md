Recall.ai offers an API that pulls recordings, transcripts, and metadata from meetings. The service supports multiple capture methods, including meeting bots, a desktop recording SDK, and a mobile recording SDK, and works with platforms such as Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and in-person meetings.
Over 3,000 companies rely on the API to process millions of hours of meeting data. The infrastructure is designed to be stable and fast, allowing engineering teams to get up and running in days instead of the six months it would normally take to build a similar solution.
With a team of 30 engineers based in San Francisco, the company focuses on B2B infrastructure solutions.

_Usage:_

"How does Recall.ai integrate with Zoom?"

"Recall.ai integrates with Zoom by providing an API that can pull recordings, transcripts, and metadata directly from Zoom meetings, supporting the same data capture methods as other platforms."
