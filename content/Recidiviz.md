Recidiviz is a non‑profit technology company that creates an open‑source platform for criminal‑justice data. The platform ingests data from disparate silos, links it, standardizes it, and exposes it in usable formats for the ecosystem. It offers products and analyses that help government agencies understand how their justice systems are functioning, set and track progress toward goals, drive evidence‑based improvements to practice and policy, and ensure justice‑impacted individuals reenter their communities safely and quickly. With a team of 120 located in New York City, Recidiviz operates in the government sector as a civic‑tech and GovTech nonprofit.

_Usage:_

"How does Recidiviz ingest and standardize data from disparate criminal‑justice silos?"

"Recidiviz’s open‑source platform pulls data from across data silos, links related records, standardizes the information, and exposes it in formats that agencies can use for analysis and policy development."
