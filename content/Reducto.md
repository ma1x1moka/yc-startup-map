**Website:** https://reducto.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/reducto

Reducto provides an agentic document platform designed for AI teams building systems to automate document workflows at enterprise scale. The company offers a comprehensive toolkit that processes documents in a human-like manner, leveraging a combination of custom in-house models and leading frontier models to ensure efficiency and accuracy.

Built for enterprise workloads, Reducto supports flexible deployment options ranging from cloud environments to fully air-gapped setups. It prioritizes security and compliance with SOC II and HIPAA standards, along with a zero data retention policy.

Based in San Francisco, the 30-person team serves leading AI organizations, including Harvey, Scale AI, and Vanta, helping them manage complex document data engineering and search tasks.
