Refactor builds autonomous AI agents designed to call, click, and navigate legacy software systems within large enterprises. The company works closely with major organizations across healthcare, retail, and logistics to safely implement ambitious AI use cases in record time.

Operating out of San Francisco, the three-person team has partnered with companies serving tens of millions of customers and generating over $50 billion in annual revenue. By focusing on secure and reliable agent deployment, Refactor addresses the challenge of integrating advanced AI into complex, existing business infrastructure.
