**Website:** https://www.joinreframeapp.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/reframe

Reframe is a consumer health application designed to help individuals quit or cut back on alcohol use. It leverages principles from neuroscience and psychology to assist users in shifting their mindset regarding drinking and building sustainable habits. The company emphasizes a non-stigmatizing approach, focusing on behavioral change rather than strict restriction.

Based in Atlanta, Georgia, the company operates with a team of 35 people. It claims to be the leading app in this space, having supported over 2 million users in the past 12 months. Reframe states that it has helped more people reduce alcohol use than any other organization globally.

_Usage:_

"How does Reframe differ from traditional abstinence-based recovery programs?"

"Reframe focuses on harm reduction and habit formation using neuroscience and psychology, explicitly avoiding the stigma and rigid restrictions often associated with traditional abstinence-only models. It is designed for people who want to cut back on drinking rather than necessarily quit entirely, positioning itself as a tool for behavioral shift rather than just a support group."
