Regology is an AI‑powered regulatory intelligence platform that helps compliance, legal, risk, and operations teams navigate complex regulatory environments across U.S. federal, state, and global jurisdictions.

At its core, the Smart Law Library™ offers a curated, continuously updated repository of applicable laws and regulations, and three specialized AI agents drive the platform: the Regulatory Change Agent tracks and alerts on jurisdiction‑specific developments; the Compliance Agent maps changes to policies, controls, and risks; and the Regulatory Research Agent delivers plain‑language summaries, multi‑jurisdictional comparisons, and actionable insights.

Regology was acquired and now operates with a team of 60 in San Francisco, CA, serving B2B legal clients.

_Usage:_

"What is the difference between Regology's Regulatory Change Agent and its Compliance Agent?"

"The Regulatory Change Agent tracks and alerts on jurisdiction‑specific developments, while the Compliance Agent maps those changes to internal policies, controls, and risks."
