**Website:** https://www.reific.co/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/reific

Reific builds RL environments for superhuman reasoning over time, for the frontier labs that train agents. AI has learned to reason over text and code. But most of what matters in the real world shows up as numbers that change every second: a heartbeat, a gearbox, a GPU cluster. No human can track thousands of signals at once, but AI should be able to.

In its environments, data streams in on a clock while the agent works, and every action is graded by what actually happened. Most AI training today teaches models to match human experts. Reific treats human performance as the floor. Each environment comes from real, de-identified records, in three task families: personal agents, industry, and science & compute.

Today, even the best frontier model averages under 50% on each family. Training an open model on its environments raised its full-solve rate on unseen tasks by 54% (from 25.6% to 39.5%), with no loss on general benchmarks. Its mission is to build the infrastructure that keeps AI climbing the frontier, long past human level in the real world.
