Rejuvenation Technologies Inc. uses a Stanford‑patented mRNA platform to unlock healthspan by targeting short telomeres. In preclinical studies the therapy reversed 5‑10 years of telomere shortening with a single dose in one day and extended mouse lifespan by the equivalent of 10 human years, showing efficacy in four organ systems including the immune system.

The company has 50+ granted patents, was cleared by FDA INTERACT, and is currently raising a $10M seed extension to fund three investigator‑initiated trials with readouts expected in Q3 2027.
