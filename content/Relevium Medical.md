Relevium Medical is developing a platform injectable gel-based therapeutic designed to treat knee osteoarthritis, a progressive disease affecting approximately half the population. The company aims to address the current standard of care, which often requires repeat injections and strong oral medications that can lead to opioid addiction.

The team has secured $3.7 million in non-dilutive funding and completed preclinical testing, demonstrating that the drug is safe and selectively blocks pain-transmitting nerves. Preliminary results indicate the treatment provides pain relief that lasts four times as long as existing options.

Based in Galway, Ireland, Relevium Medical targets a $7.7 billion market opportunity in the US alone. The platform biotherapeutic is also designed to be applicable to other joints affected by osteoarthritis, such as the hip and ankle.
