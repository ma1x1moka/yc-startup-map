**Website:** https://www.remoracarbon.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/remora

Remora builds carbon capture systems specifically designed for heavy-duty vehicles like trains and trucks. The company generates revenue by extracting, purifying, and selling CO2 from vehicle exhaust, while simultaneously reducing soot, particulate matter, and NOx emissions. Remora’s technology claims to capture up to 90% of the CO2 in the exhaust stream.

The Detroit-based team, which includes 45 employees, has established partnerships with major logistics and rail companies including Union Pacific, Norfolk Southern, Ryder, and Werner. Remora has raised $117 million from investors such as Lowercarbon Capital, Valor Equity Partners, Union Square Ventures, and Y Combinator.

_Usage:_

"How does Remora make money from carbon capture on trucks and trains?"

"Remora installs capture technology on the vehicles, extracts the CO2 from the exhaust, purifies it, and then sells that captured carbon to buyers. This creates a revenue stream for the railroads and trucking companies that host the equipment, effectively turning a waste product into a commodity while also reducing other pollutants like soot and NOx."
