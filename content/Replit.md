**Website:** https://replit.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/replit

Replit is a world‑leading collaborative development environment that runs entirely in the browser, allowing users to learn, create, and deploy code without installing local tools. It powers a community of over 20 million developers and supports real‑time collaboration on projects. Based in San Francisco with a team of 65, Replit serves B2B customers in engineering, product, and design through its developer‑tools platform.

_Usage:_

"How does Replit’s in‑browser IDE differ from a traditional local development setup?"

"Replit runs code directly in the browser, so you don’t need to install compilers, runtimes, or editors on your machine, and you can share a live editing session with others instantly. All build and deployment steps happen in the same cloud environment, which contrasts with a local setup where you manage the toolchain and deployments yourself."
