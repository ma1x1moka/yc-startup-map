**Website:** https://www.useresonance.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/resonance

Resonance hyper‑personalizes MarTech campaign content and automatically refreshes and stores high‑performing assets for future reuse. It operates as a SaaS subscription platform that continuously learns which content performs best and updates the bank without manual effort. Resonance was acquired, highlighting its value in the B2B marketing space. The company is based in San Francisco and was founded by a two‑person team in the Winter 2024 YC batch.

_Usage:_

"How does Resonance differ from a typical marketing content management tool?"

"Resonance uses reinforcement‑learning‑driven AI to automatically identify high‑performing campaign content, refresh it, and store it for reuse, whereas a standard CMS just stores static assets without any self‑improving logic."
