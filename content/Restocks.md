**Website:** https://restocks.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/restocks

Restocks was a San Francisco-based mobile e-commerce platform founded in 2015 that provided real-time alerts for sneaker and streetwear drops. The application enabled consumers to track brand releases, price drops, and stock updates while allowing them to save checkout profiles and purchase items directly within the app.

Part of Y Combinator's Winter 2016 batch, the company operated with a two-person team. Restocks has since ceased operations and is listed as inactive.

_Usage:_

"How does purchasing work inside the Restocks mobile app without redirecting users?"

"Users store their checkout profiles directly within the mobile platform. When a tracked brand restocks or drops a new item, users can view live inventory status and complete the entire transaction inside the app without navigating away to external store pages."
