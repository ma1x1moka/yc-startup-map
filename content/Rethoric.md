**Website:** http://rethoric.com/ycombinator | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rethoric

Rethoric operates as a LinkedIn content consultancy and scheduling platform designed for companies seeking to maintain visibility with their ideal customer profile without dedicating internal resources to social media management. The service handles the full content lifecycle, from strategy to publishing, for a client base that includes over 100 startups and enterprises such as Checkr, ClickUp, Storylane, and Checkly.

The platform is built to replace traditional agency workflows that rely on disjointed tools like Google Docs and scattered review threads. By integrating directly with LinkedIn and Slack, Rethoric presents content that is already written, scheduled, and ready for publication, streamlining the process for its users.

_Usage:_

"How does Rethoric differ from just hiring a freelance social media manager or using a standard scheduling tool like Buffer?"

"Rethoric combines the strategic oversight of a consultancy with a unified platform that replaces the fragmented agency workflow of docs and review threads. Instead of just scheduling posts, it manages the entire operation from strategy to publishing, with content appearing in your Slack and LinkedIn already written and ready to go, which is distinct from simple scheduling tools that require you to create the content yourself."
