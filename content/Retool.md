**Website:** https://retool.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/retool

Retool is a developer‑focused platform that lets companies build internal tools quickly, positioning itself as the fast way to create B2B productivity applications. It provides pre‑built UI components and integrations so teams can assemble dashboards, admin panels, and workflows without starting from scratch.

With a team of about 300 engineers in San Francisco, Retool has become a go‑to solution for businesses that need internal software fast, fitting into the broader developer‑tools ecosystem. Its focus on speed and ease of use makes it notable among productivity‑oriented B2B services.

_Usage:_

"How is Retool different from writing a custom internal tool from the ground up?"

"Retool offers a library of ready‑made UI components and data connectors that let you assemble an internal app by dragging and dropping, rather than coding every element yourself. This speeds up development compared to a full custom build, especially for teams that need to iterate quickly on internal workflows."
