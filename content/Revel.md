Revel offers events and online communities that nourish women at midlife and beyond. The company was backed by Forerunner Ventures and Y Combinator and was named one of Fast Company’s Most Innovative Companies of 2020.

Revel launched in the Summer 2019 Y Combinator batch and grew a team of 16 based in San Francisco and remote. It focused on consumer and social industries and is now inactive.
