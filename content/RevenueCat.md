**Website:** https://www.revenuecat.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/revenuecat

RevenueCat was founded in Summer 2018 as part of YC’s S18 batch and focuses on simplifying in‑app subscription monetization for mobile applications.
The platform has become the default monetization solution for mobile, appearing in more than 40% of newly shipped subscription apps and handling over $12 B in annual purchase volume.
With a team of 160 engineers based in San Francisco and remote, RevenueCat serves a diverse developer community—from solo developers in Brazil to large teams such as OpenAI’s mobile squad—helping them understand and grow their revenue.
