**Website:** https://www.revi.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/revi

Revi helps investment banks, private equity firms, and corporate development teams source new M&A deals by automating the end‑to‑end origination workflow. It uses agentic AI workflows that replicate the full deal‑sourcing process, eliminating the usual trade‑off between precision and scalability.

The company launched its first version in September 2023 and is already used by KPMG and platforms backed by KKR and Nordic Capital. With a small team of two operating from New York City and remotely, Revi targets B2B sales functions in the finance sector.

_Usage:_

"How does Revi’s agentic workflow differ from traditional M&A sourcing tools?"

"Revi’s agentic workflow automates the entire deal‑origination pipeline rather than just providing a list of leads, so it can maintain the same precision of a manual process while scaling to handle many opportunities at once, which typical sourcing tools don’t achieve."
