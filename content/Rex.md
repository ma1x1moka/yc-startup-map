Rex is an AI-native service that replaces manual order-to-cash operations with AI agents that continuously manage invoices, customers and exceptions.

The order-to-cash function is a $60B outsourced service that is too messy for traditional automation; Rex agents run in the background and resolve work to SLA standards, keeping cash flowing. It is live with pre‑IPO tech companies, managing over $500m in receivables from Fortune 100 firms and cutting manual follow‑up cycles from weeks to hours.

Founded after building the finance ops platform Sequence, Rex won the Vercel AI Accelerator out of 2,500 teams, selected by Guillermo Rauch and leaders from OpenAI, Anthropic and Browserbase.
