Ribbon Health is a health care data platform that supplies insurers, medical providers, and digital health solutions with the infrastructure needed to build accurate provider directories, reliable referral management, and efficient care navigation.

Its API layer integrates seamlessly into existing workflows, continuously improving data accuracy by aggregating information from thousands of sources and delivering the most comprehensive data on doctors, insurance plans, and the cost and quality of care.

Ribbon Health was acquired, marking it as a notable player in the healthcare IT space.

_Usage:_

"How does Ribbon Health's API differ from other provider directory services?"

"Ribbon Health offers a single platform that provides comprehensive data on doctors, insurance plans, cost and quality of care from thousands of sources, and integrates seamlessly into existing workflows, whereas other services may provide only limited data or require separate integrations."
