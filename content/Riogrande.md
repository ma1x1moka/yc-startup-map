Riogrande operates as a holding company focused on acquiring and scaling direct-to-consumer brands within Latin America. Based in Mexico City, the firm specializes in the consumer and food and beverage sectors, leveraging e-commerce infrastructure to drive growth for its portfolio companies.

The company was a Summer 2020 Y Combinator batch participant. Its status is listed as Acquired, indicating that Riogrande has since been bought by another entity, a common trajectory for venture-backed growth platforms in the consumer space.

_Usage:_

"How does Riogrande differ from a traditional private equity firm or a standard e-commerce marketplace?"

"Riogrande functions as a roll-up or growth platform specifically for D2C brands in LatAm, rather than just providing capital like PE or acting as a neutral marketplace. It actively buys these brands and integrates them to scale their operations, focusing on consumer and food/beverage sectors in the region."
