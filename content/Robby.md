**Website:** https://tryrobby.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/robby

Robby is an AI growth engine for home services businesses. Combines customer and third-party data to generate dozens of new leads daily, and arms technicians with talking points to increase close rates. GMs get a single command center to manage and drive revenue.

On launch day, helped close $40K in new business within an hour. Paying customers are each uncovering 6 figures in additional revenue opportunities every week. The team spent months riding along with technicians to understand every role in the business.

_Usage:_

"What data does Robby combine to generate leads and talking points, and how does it turn that into actionable insights?"

"Robby pulls both the business’s own customer data and third‑party data, then runs an AI model that matches service‑need signals with the technicians’ expertise to produce a daily list of new leads and a set of talking points that technicians can use to improve close rates."
