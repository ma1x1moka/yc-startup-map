Co-founded [Lume](./Lume.md) as CEO in 2022 with Nicolas Machado and Nebyou Zewde, meeting his co-founders in their freshman dorm at Stanford. Lume (YC W23) is a no-code tool that uses AI to automatically generate and maintain custom data integrations between any start and end schema.

_Usage:_

"Where did Lume's founders meet?"

"In their freshman dorm at Stanford, where Robert Ross, Nicolas Machado, and Nebyou Zewde became close friends before founding the company together."
