Roboflow enables developers to build computer vision solutions by providing a suite of tools for dataset creation, model training, and deployment across self‑hosted, edge, and API environments. It supports image and video collection and annotation, and offers foundation and fine‑tuned small models for rapid prototyping.
Since its launch in Summer 2020, Roboflow has attracted more than 250,000 developers, including users from over half of the Fortune 100, who rely on its open‑source and hosted offerings to accelerate product and design workflows. Headquartered in San Francisco with a team of about 100, the company serves B2B engineering and product teams and positions itself at the intersection of machine learning, computer vision, and enterprise software.
