Rodeo Robotics (YC F26) automates vehicle maintenance so shops can get vehicles back to work at lower cost.

Co-founders Michael Hazard and Colby Duke met at Applied Intuition, where they helped OEMs like Toyota and General Motors deploy autonomy in the real world and saw firsthand how much hands-on work it takes to keep fleets running.

They're now applying their experience to the $600B US maintenance market. The average vehicle is getting older and needs more maintenance, but shops struggle to recruit new technicians to replace experienced workers as they retire. This leaves shops turning away work and customers waiting longer for service.
