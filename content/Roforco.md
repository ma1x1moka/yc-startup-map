**YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/roforco

Roforco is a four-person game studio based in Kristiansand, Norway, that operates as one of the top studios on the Roblox platform. The team has launched seven original titles, including Korrupt Zombies and Sunsilk City, which have collectively been played by over 100 million users.

The company reached a peak of 10.2 million monthly active users, driven by a strong focus on social virality. Roforco maintains a significant social presence with over 100,000 TikTok followers and 64,000 Discord members, generating tens of millions of views across TikTok and YouTube.

Founded by two 17-year-old high school dropouts who began building viral consumer products at age 12, the studio was accepted into the Y Combinator Summer 2022 batch.

_Usage:_

"How does Roforco drive user acquisition for its Roblox games compared to traditional ad spend?"

"Roforco relies heavily on organic social reach rather than paid advertising, leveraging over 100,000 TikTok followers and 64,000 Discord members to generate tens of millions of views. This social-first strategy helped their seven original titles reach over 100 million users and peak at 10.2 million monthly active users."
