Roin Technologies develops automated robots designed to streamline commercial concrete floor construction. The company’s technology allows a single operator to perform the work typically required by six construction workers, significantly increasing efficiency on job sites.

Based in San Francisco, the company operated with a small team of three people before being acquired. Its focus on hard tech and robotics positions it within the broader trend of automating labor-intensive industrial tasks.

_Usage:_

"How does Roin's robotic system compare to traditional manual concrete finishing in terms of labor requirements?"

"Roin's robots are designed so that one person operating the system can do the work of six construction workers, drastically reducing the labor headcount needed for concrete floor construction projects."
