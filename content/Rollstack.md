Rollstack automates the creation of data-driven slide decks and documents, cutting the hours that organizations spend on manual preparation.

The company serves mid-size to public firms such as SoFi, Zillow and Whirlpool, and is headquartered in New York City with a remote-friendly workplace.

Backed by Insight Partners and Y Combinator, Rollstack’s 30-person team focuses on rapid execution and first-principles thinking to deliver outsized impacts for its B2B analytics customers.
