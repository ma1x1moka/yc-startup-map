Rootly is a modern on-call and incident management platform designed to streamline how engineering teams handle system failures and alerts. The company positions itself as an AI-native solution within the broader infrastructure and developer tools space.

It serves a diverse customer base that includes prominent technology companies such as Dropbox, Lattice, Webflow, Faire, Figma, LinkedIn, and NVIDIA. With a team of approximately 75 people operating from San Francisco and remotely, Rootly continues to be an active player in the B2B infrastructure sector.
