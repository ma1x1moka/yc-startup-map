**Website:** https://routant.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/routant

AI agents won't stay inside the companies that build them. The most valuable agents will work across company boundaries, inside customers' private systems. In those environments, intelligence is no longer the barrier to deployment. Trust and access are. Routant is the zero-trust access layer for this new agentic world. Vendors integrate once, while customers retain governance over every deployment, deciding what agents can access and which actions need human review, with a complete audit trail of every action.
