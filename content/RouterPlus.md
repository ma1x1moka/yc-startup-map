RouterPlus is a marketplace for AI inference. RouterPlus finds the right AI model for a team's product, then get it served at the team's scale, at the best price.

Most teams that run AI in production pay for a top model on every task, when a cheaper model can do most of the work. The same model can also cost twice as much depending on which provider runs it, and many providers fail at peak traffic.

RouterPlus tests models on a team's own traffic and recommends one. Providers then compete for the workload. It checks that the chosen provider can hold the team's real peak, and it keeps a second provider ready as a backup. Teams call the model through one API.
