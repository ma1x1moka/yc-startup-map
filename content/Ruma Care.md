**Website:** https://rumacare.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ruma-care

Ruma Care automates all admin work for infusion clinics — starting with prior authorizations and copay assistance enrollment. Biologics cost $80–$150K+ per year per patient and are denied by insurers at a rate of 37% each year.

When denials happen, or workflows fail, or patients are infused before paperwork is cleared, providers don't get reimbursed for the medication and are left covering costs. Ruma automates the entire prior auth and enrollment pipeline. Team of 2.
