Robots and servers need more power and data every year, and both still get it through tangles of cables and custom PCBs. A typical robot is a pile of boards wired together by hand, and every design change means waiting on a new board. SPITA is building networking for power. SPARC is a backplane with plug-in cards: drop in a chip or module, and software sets whether each pin carries power or data. Nothing needs rewiring or a new PCB. Once the design works, it exports the schematic for production. SPITA Labs is starting with robotics teams, who can test new designs without spinning a board. Next is the server rack, where the same approach replaces today's cable-heavy backplanes.
