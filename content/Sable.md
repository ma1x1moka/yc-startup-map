Sable is a digital banking platform that provides the only full‑suite banking solution for internationals in the United States. It offers U.S. bank accounts, debit and credit cards, and lets users open an account in as little as five minutes. Newcomers can start building a U.S. credit history from day one, even without a Social Security Number or prior U.S. credit history. The Sable app is available on iOS and Android, and the company is based in New York City with a team of 12. Sable was acquired after its Summer 2019 Y Combinator batch, reflecting its notable position in the fintech and consumer‑finance space.

_Usage:_

"Can I get a credit card from Sable without having an SSN?"

"Yes. Sable issues both debit and credit cards to eligible users who don’t have a Social Security Number, allowing them to open an account and begin building U.S. credit immediately."
