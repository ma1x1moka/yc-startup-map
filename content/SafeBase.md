**Website:** https://www.safebase.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/safebase

SafeBase provides a Trust Center platform designed to help security teams proactively share and automate access to security, compliance, and privacy information. The company focuses on streamlining the process of completing security questionnaires for B2B clients.

Founded in the Summer 2020 batch, SafeBase operates out of San Francisco with a remote-friendly team of approximately 60 people. It is categorized under B2B, Security, and Cybersecurity industries.

SafeBase was acquired, marking its exit from the YC portfolio as a notable player in the security infrastructure space.

_Usage:_

"How does SafeBase handle the repetitive nature of customer security questionnaires?"

"It acts as a centralized Trust Center that allows security teams to store their compliance and privacy data once, then automatically uses that information to complete incoming security questionnaires, reducing the manual effort required for each new customer review."
