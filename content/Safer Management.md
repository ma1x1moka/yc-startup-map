Safer Management provides attendance tracking software to public school districts and colleges. The platform addresses the financial incentive for public schools to maintain high average daily attendance, which directly impacts their federal funding.

Founded in Dallas, Texas, the company was launched after the founder observed inefficiencies in the sign-in process while dropping off his children. The team has since expanded to serve 75 public schools and two colleges, generating annual recurring revenue of $621,000 with 82% margins. The company is currently profitable and operates on a pricing model that scales to $15 per student per year.

Safer Management targets a market of 70 million public school students in the United States, representing a $1.4 billion opportunity. The company utilizes artificial intelligence and machine learning tags to support its operations within the education sector.

_Usage:_

"How does Safer Management help schools secure federal funding?"

"Public schools receive federal funding based on their average daily attendance, so Safer Management’s software ensures accurate tracking to help districts capture the money they are entitled to. It streamlines the sign-in process, which was previously inefficient, allowing schools to maintain the attendance records necessary for funding eligibility."
