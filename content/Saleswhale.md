**Website:** https://www.saleswhale.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/saleswhale

Saleswhale is an AI-powered sales assistant designed to scale lead qualification by handling two-way email conversations with prospects. The platform automates the administrative aspects of sales, including data entry into CRMs, lead enrichment, and the capture of referral or rejection reasons, ensuring that sales teams only engage with qualified leads and scheduled meetings.

The company, which operated out of Singapore with a team of 23, supported clients such as Randstad, General Assembly, and Verlocal. Saleswhale was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Saleswhale handle the handoff between automated email and human sales reps?"

"It filters out the noise by conducting the initial two-way email conversations to qualify leads. It handles the grunt work like enriching prospect data and logging interactions into your CRM, so your human sales team only receives qualified leads and confirmed meetings, rather than spending time on admin or unqualified prospects."
