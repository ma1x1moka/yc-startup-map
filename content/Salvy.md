**Website:** https://www.salvy.com.br | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/salvy

Salvy is a mobile carrier for businesses in Brazil, serving over 1,800 companies and managing more than 20,000 mobile lines. Its software layer integrates with HRIS, ERP and other systems, allowing IT managers to provision thousands of lines rapidly and cut costs by over 50%. The market comprises 55 million B2B mobile lines in Brazil, representing about $5 billion in ARR, and Salvy’s 27‑person team operates from Curitiba.

_Usage:_

"How does Salvy achieve the claimed 50% cost reduction for corporate mobile lines?"

"Salvy provides a unified software layer that streamlines provisioning and management of mobile lines, integrates with existing HR and ERP systems, and consolidates usage under a single carrier, which reduces administrative overhead and enables bulk pricing that leads to the reported savings."
