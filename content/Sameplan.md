**Website:** https://www.sameplan.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sameplan

Sameplan serves as a central collaboration tool for sales teams and their prospects, designed to streamline the management of high-value enterprise relationships. By acting as the single source of truth for deal plans, it helps teams align on objectives and increase efficiency compared to traditional email-based workflows.

The San Francisco-based startup, which operated with a small team of three, focused on the B2B sales sector. It was ultimately acquired, marking the end of its independent operations.

_Usage:_

"How does Sameplan differ from just using email or a generic project management tool for enterprise deals?"

"Sameplan acts as a dedicated central hub specifically for the deal itself, allowing sales teams and prospects to collaborate directly on the plan rather than scattering communication across email threads. It is built to handle the complexity of high-value enterprise transactions by keeping all alignment and efficiency efforts in one customer-facing interface."
