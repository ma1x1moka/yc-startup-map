**Website:** https://samplehc.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sample

Sample Healthcare enables users to build copilots for processes such as patient intake and reimbursement, turning operational burdens into strategic assets. The platform captures ground-truth data from these interactions to continuously improve the underlying models.

The company, based in San Francisco with a team of five, works with leading national providers and has already secured data from 76% of US oncology clinics. Its network is rapidly expanding across the nation to cover more healthcare operations.

Sample was acquired, marking a significant milestone for this Winter 2024 batch company focused on healthcare IT.

_Usage:_

"How does Sample's approach to data collection differ from standard AI deployments in healthcare?"

"Unlike static models, Sample's copilots capture valuable ground-truth data during real-world tasks like patient intake. This feedback loop allows the models to become smarter over time, creating a data moat that improves accuracy and utility for the provider."
