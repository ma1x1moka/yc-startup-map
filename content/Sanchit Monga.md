Co-founded [RunAnywhere](./RunAnywhere.md) with [Shubham Malhotra](./Shubham%20Malhotra.md).

**Former Intuit engineer** — built mobile SDKs and platform tooling used by **50M+ active users**. Multiple prior products: consumer discovery, agentic docs, mobile testing.

The SDK distribution experience from Intuit is the go-to-market muscle RunAnywhere needs: building a developer SDK that 50M users depend on means understanding versioning discipline, backward compatibility guarantees, documentation quality, and the developer trust required for infrastructure adoption. These are exactly the challenges RunAnywhere faces as it scales.

Monga's role at RunAnywhere is the distribution and market layer: making sure developers can adopt the SDK easily, building the control plane product, and selling the enterprise version. [Shubham Malhotra](./Shubham%20Malhotra.md)'s MetalRT work provides the technical foundation; Monga provides the distribution experience.
