**Website:** https://www.satsuma.xyz/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/satsuma

Satsuma is a developer tool that enables teams to build applications on top of real-time blockchain data. It allows developers to ingest decoded data from multiple chains, customize it for specific use cases, and access it via API endpoints. The company operates on the premise that blockchains function as distributed databases for web3 products, but the current friction in accessing and querying this data is a significant blocker for mass adoption.

Founded in the Summer 2021 batch, Satsuma was built by a team of engineers with prior experience at Airtable, Heap, and Y Combinator. The company is headquartered in San Francisco with a remote-friendly culture and a small team of five. Satsuma was acquired, marking a notable exit for its infrastructure-focused approach to web3 data engineering.

_Usage:_

"How does Satsuma handle data from multiple blockchains for my application?"

"Satsuma acts as an indexing layer that takes decoded data from various chains, lets you customize the schema for your specific needs, and exposes it through standard API endpoints. This removes the complexity of managing raw blockchain queries directly, allowing your backend to treat on-chain data like a traditional database."
