Savvy is a health benefits product that gives employees payment cards to purchase their own health insurance, providing a flexible stipend model. The company grew to tens of millions in volume and expanded into all 50 U.S. state health insurance markets, becoming one of the first to market after new regulation. In 2022, Savvy was acquired by Take Command, the leading incumbent in the health insurance HRA space, after receiving backing from Y Combinator, Marc Andreessen, and the founders of One Medical and Indeed.

Savvy operates with a team of 12 in San Francisco and focuses on fintech and insurance solutions.

_Usage:_

"How does Savvy’s stipend card model differ from traditional employer-sponsored insurance?"

"Savvy’s model gives employees a payment card that they can use to buy their own health insurance plans, instead of the employer paying premiums directly; this allows employees to choose plans that best fit their needs and gives them more control over their benefits."
