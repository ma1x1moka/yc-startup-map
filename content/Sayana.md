**Website:** https://sayana.app/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sayana

Sayana is a mental wellness app that helps users track thoughts and feelings daily, receive relevant tips on coping with emotions, and connect with others in similar life situations.

The startup was acquired by Headspace Health, reflecting its value in the consumer health and wellness space. With a small team of two, Sayana operated out of San Francisco, CA.

_Usage:_

"How does Sayana personalize coping tips compared to generic wellness apps?"

"Sayana collects daily self-reported thoughts and feelings, then uses that data to deliver tailored coping suggestions and match users with peers in similar life situations."
