**Website:** http://scale.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/scale-ai

Scale provides data‑centric infrastructure that accelerates AI development in organizations of any size, delivering critical business insights and operational efficiency.

The platform leverages Reinforced Learning with Human Feedback (RLHF) to help companies build the strongest AI models that supercharge their operations.

Scale’s customer base spans major tech firms, defense agencies, automotive research institutes, and fintech and logistics companies, including Meta, Microsoft, the U.S. Army, the DoD’s Defense Innovation Unit, OpenAI, General Motors, Toyota Research Institute, Brex, Instacart, and Flexport. With a team of 500 employees based in San Francisco, the company has positioned itself as a key player in AI infrastructure.

_Usage:_

"How does Scale's RLHF approach differ from traditional supervised learning?"

"Scale’s RLHF uses human feedback to iteratively refine model outputs, enabling stronger AI models for business tasks compared to standard supervised learning that relies solely on labeled data."
