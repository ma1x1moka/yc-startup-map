Scanwell Health makes healthcare more accessible through smartphone-enabled, at-home diagnostics. People can test from anywhere, skip waiting rooms, and receive same-day results.

By quickly diagnosing acute illnesses, chronic diseases, and rare conditions, Scanwell accelerates the path to treatment. Its first product is the only FDA-cleared over-the-counter app that provides testing and treatment for urinary tract infections (UTIs).

Founded in Summer 2018, the company had a team of 13 and operated out of Los Angeles, CA, with remote employees. Scanwell Health was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Scanwell Health's UTI app provide same-day results compared to traditional clinic visits?"

"Scanwell Health's app lets users collect a urine sample at home, upload a photo, and receive a diagnosis and treatment recommendation within the same day, eliminating the need to wait for lab results."
