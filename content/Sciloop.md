**Website:** https://sciloop.dev | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sciloop

Sciloop creates expert-level math and physics problems that frontier AI models can't solve, then sells the data to AI labs for training and evaluation. Problems are created by IPhO and IMO medalists — the top 0.01% of STEM talent globally.

On their benchmark, models like GPT 5.4 Pro and Gemini 3.1 Pro score 0–5% on the hardest problems. Founded by Bilal and Osman, International Physics Olympiad medalists from MIT with ML research experience at MIT CSAIL.
