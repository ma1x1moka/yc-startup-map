**Website:** https://www.getscopeai.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/scopeai

ScopeAI automates the process of analyzing customer conversations and communicating user insights to product and operations teams. The platform leverages natural language processing to automatically tag and analyze support interactions for emerging trends in customer feedback.

The software integrates directly into existing customer support channels, including Zendesk, Intercom, and Desk.com (Salesforce). Part of the Y Combinator Winter 2017 batch, ScopeAI has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does ScopeAI extract customer insights without manual tagging?"

"ScopeAI connects directly with existing support tools like Zendesk, Intercom, and Desk.com. Using natural language processing, it automatically tags customer conversations and analyzes them to surface feedback trends directly for product and operations teams."
