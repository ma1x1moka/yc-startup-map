**Website:** https://www.searchlight.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/searchlight

Searchlight is a San Francisco‑based SaaS platform that applies machine learning to the recruiting process, helping B2B companies evaluate candidates more effectively. Its talent AI analyzes applicant data to surface higher‑quality hires, aiming to reduce hiring mistakes and streamline talent acquisition. The company, founded in YC’s Winter 2019 batch, grew to a team of about 20 engineers before being acquired, underscoring the market’s interest in AI‑enhanced recruiting solutions.

_Usage:_

"How does Searchlight’s AI differ from a traditional applicant tracking system?"

"Searchlight’s AI goes beyond basic resume parsing by using machine‑learning models to assess candidate fit and predict hiring outcomes, whereas a typical ATS mainly stores and routes applications without predictive insight."
