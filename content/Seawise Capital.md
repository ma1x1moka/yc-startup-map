**Website:** https://www.seawisecapital.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/seawise-capital

Seawise Capital is a trade finance company that serves Indian exporters, offering working capital to small and medium enterprises based on the creditworthiness of their international customers. It provides collateral‑free financing by discounting invoices from large overseas buyers, allowing SMEs to access funds without pledging assets. The company operates out of New Delhi with a three‑person team and was part of Y Combinator’s Winter 2019 batch. Seawise Capital was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Seawise Capital give SMEs financing without requiring collateral?"

"It discounts the exporters’ invoices from their large overseas customers, using the strength of those customers as security, so the SME receives working capital without having to pledge any assets."
