---
description: Material about the thing rather than the thing itself: summaries, notes, docs, a previous session's write-up.
---

Secondary sources are compressed and therefore cheap, which is exactly what makes them the right medium for a [handoff](./Handoff.md). A good summary of yesterday's [session](./Session.md) costs a fraction of the transcript and carries most of the value.

Compression is lossy, though, and the loss is invisible from inside the summary. When the [agent](./Agent.md)'s behaviour suggests it is missing something specific, that is the moment to go back to the [primary source](./Primary%20source.md) rather than rewriting the summary.

_Usage:_

"The notes say the migration is done."

"Check the actual migration table before we build on that."
