**Website:** https://segments.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/segments-ai

Segments.ai helps robotics and automotive companies label their multi-sensor data for AI training and validation. The platform enables customers to efficiently annotate point cloud and image data, accelerating their path to autonomy.

The remote‑first YC startup has a lean team of eight and a healthy runway, and its tools are used by both large organizations and innovative startups building autonomous drones, delivery robots, self‑driving cars, and more. Segments.ai was acquired, marking it as a notable entrant in the AI infrastructure space.

With a focus on deep learning, developer tools, and computer vision, the company operates from Brussels and remote locations, serving B2B, engineering, product, and design industries.

_Usage:_

"What types of data can Segments.ai annotate?"

"Segments.ai can annotate point cloud and image data from multi‑sensor setups, enabling faster AI training and validation for autonomous drones, delivery robots, and self‑driving cars."
