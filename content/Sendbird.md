Sendbird is a B2B AI SaaS platform that powers over 300 million conversations each month, enabling businesses to connect with customers through intelligent agents. Founded in Winter 2016 and headquartered in San Francisco, the company has raised more than $220 million from investors such as ICONIQ, Tiger Global, and SoftBank Vision Fund, and serves clients including DoorDash, Match Group, Yahoo!, PayPal, and Walgreens.
