**Website:** https://www.senecagov.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/seneca-systems

Seneca Systems developed Romulus, a Constituent Services Platform designed to centralize communications and organize service requests for government teams. The software addresses the challenges of managing constituent interactions across multiple channels by providing visibility into public opinions and the work performed by government employees.

The company aimed to replace outdated private-sector software with a solution built specifically for government workflows, enabling workers to handle requests with less overhead. Seneca Systems was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Romulus differ from standard private-sector CRM tools for government use?"

"Romulus is built from the ground up for government workers rather than being a repurposed private-sector tool. It specifically centralizes constituent communications and organizes service requests to address the unique maze-like processes and lack of visibility that government teams face, aiming to reduce implementation and purchase friction compared to outdated legacy solutions."
