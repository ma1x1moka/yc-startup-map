**Website:** https://senpai.gg/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/senpai-gg

SenpAI.gg operated as a desktop application designed to assist the global PC gaming community by providing personalized in-game recommendations and post-game feedback. The platform utilized computer vision overlays and a voice assistant to guide players before and during matches, aiming to accelerate skill development through data-driven insights. Based in Boston, the company was part of Y Combinator’s Summer 2021 batch and raised $3M in seed funding from ACT Venture Partners. The company has since become inactive.

_Usage:_

"How does SenpAI.gg actually analyze gameplay to provide coaching?"

"It runs as a desktop app that uses computer vision to read the screen in real-time, displaying an overlay with recommendations while you play. It also offers a voice assistant for hands-free guidance and generates post-game feedback to highlight areas for improvement, essentially acting as an automated coach that watches your screen rather than just analyzing logs."
