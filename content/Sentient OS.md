Sentient OS (YC F26) is the personal agent with zero inference cost.

Personal agents are the hottest thing in AI right now, but they're unsustainably expensive to run (Elon just said so about Grok Bot). Sentient runs entirely on your own chip instead, so it's free and private.

Every night, Sentient's on-device models process your emails, messages, Granola transcripts, files, and more, building a rich personal knowledge base that lives only on your Mac. Sentient OS's custom inference stack runs on Macs with just 8 GB of RAM.
