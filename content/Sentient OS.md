Truly personal AI requires running inference on your life, every day. In the cloud, that's expensive and a privacy nightmare. On your own chip, it's free and private.

Sentient OS's custom inference stack, built on a LiteRT-LM fork, runs on Macs with just 8 GB of RAM. Every night, Sentient's on-device models process your emails, messages, Granola transcripts, files, and more, building a rich personal knowledge base on your Mac.

Your projects, conversations, commitments, and preferences. Context you'd never have the time to type into a chatbot.
