Sepal AI is a data research company that partners with leading AI labs and enterprises to improve model performance on real-world tasks. It operates a Cloud-Native Agent Dataset Factory designed to transform the creation of evaluation and training data from a manual, labor-intensive process into an automated and scalable workflow.

Founded in 2024 by engineers and operators from Vercel and Turing, the San Francisco-based company went through Y Combinator and raised several million dollars. Sepal AI serves multiple Fortune 500 companies and top AI research labs, focusing on making data generation standardized and consistent.

The company has since been acquired, marking a significant milestone for this 15-person team focused on B2B data infrastructure.

_Usage:_

"How does Sepal AI handle the creation of training data compared to traditional manual labeling?"

"Sepal AI uses a Cloud-Native Agent Dataset Factory to automate the generation of evaluation and training data. This approach replaces manual, inconsistent, and labor-intensive labeling processes with a system that is standardized and scalable, allowing AI labs to produce the specific data needed to improve model performance on targeted tasks."
