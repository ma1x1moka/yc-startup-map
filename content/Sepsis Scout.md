**Website:** https://sepsisscout.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sepsis-scout

Sepsis Scout builds wearable devices that continuously monitor patients and applies deep‑learning models to forecast the onset of sepsis before it becomes clinically apparent. By alerting caregivers early, the system aims to enable preventive interventions outside the hospital setting.

The company was acquired by Cytovale, reflecting its relevance in the healthcare‑IT space. Based in San Francisco with a five‑person team, Sepsis Scout operates at the intersection of medical devices and health‑tech AI.

_Usage:_

"How does Sepsis Scout’s wearable predict sepsis earlier than traditional hospital monitoring?"

"The wearable streams physiological data to an AI model trained on sepsis cases; the deep‑learning algorithm detects subtle patterns that precede clinical signs, generating alerts that let clinicians act before the patient deteriorates."
