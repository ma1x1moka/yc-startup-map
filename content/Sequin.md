**Website:** https://sequinai.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sequin

Sequin develops AI voice agents designed to automate the intensive process of collections calls for consumer lenders and servicers. The company focuses on a compliance-first approach, leveraging generative AI to handle tasks that traditionally require significant human agent involvement. Based in San Francisco, the team includes former product and engineering leaders from major financial institutions like Visa and PayPal.

Backed by Y Combinator, Sequin has raised $5.7M from investors including executives at OpenAI, the Schwab family, and venture firms such as Matrix, Commerce Ventures, and Thomvest. The company aims to dramatically reduce the cost of collections operations for its clients through automated, scalable voice technology.
