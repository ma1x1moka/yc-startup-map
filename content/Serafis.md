**Website:** https://serafis.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/serafis

Serafis is a San Francisco-based startup building what it calls super-intelligence for financial markets. The company uses artificial intelligence to analyze petabytes of unstructured data, aiming to detect shifts in market narratives before they reach broad consensus. Its approach is modeled on the early conviction seen in major assets like Bitcoin, Tesla, and Nvidia during their respective breakout periods.

The platform is already live with 12 organizations that manage over $70 billion in capital. By focusing on narrative intelligence, Serafis seeks to provide investors with an edge in identifying emerging trends within the financial sector.

_Usage:_

"How does Serafis differentiate itself from standard financial data providers that also track market sentiment?"

"Serafis focuses specifically on identifying narrative inflections before they become consensus, rather than just reporting on existing sentiment. It mines petabytes of unstructured data to spot early shifts in how the market views specific assets or sectors, aiming to generate conviction similar to early positions in Bitcoin or Tesla."
