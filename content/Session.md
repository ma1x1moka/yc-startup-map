---
description: One continuous run of work with an agent, from first message to clear. The unit that accumulates context.
---

A session is the lifetime of a [context window](./Context%20window.md). It begins fresh, grows with every [turn](./Turn.md), and ends when you clear it or start somewhere new. Its whole arc is one of accumulation — and accumulation is what eventually degrades it.

Scoping sessions deliberately is one of the highest-leverage habits available. One session per coherent piece of work, ended before quality falls off, beats a marathon session that has drifted through four unrelated tasks and can no longer tell which decisions still apply.

_Usage:_

"It's been sharp all morning and now it's flailing."

"That's one session across four tasks. Hand off and start clean."
