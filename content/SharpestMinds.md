**Website:** http://sharpestminds.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sharpestminds

SharpestMinds operates a mentorship program for aspiring data scientists that pairs them with experienced engineers. The mentors, drawn from leading AI groups at Apple, Uber, Tesla, Facebook and Amazon, guide mentees through real-world projects and interview preparation. Mentees join the program at no upfront cost and agree to pay a percentage of their first-year salary after they are hired, aligning the program's revenue with the success of its graduates. The model functions as a talent-matching service that blends education with recruiting. SharpestMinds was acquired after its Winter 2018 Y Combinator batch. The company, based in Toronto with remote staff and a four-person team, operates in the B2B recruiting and talent space.

_Usage:_

"Is SharpestMinds' income-share agreement similar to a traditional coding bootcamp's tuition model?"

"No, SharpestMinds only charges a share of the graduate's first-year salary after they secure a job, whereas bootcamps typically require upfront tuition regardless of outcome. The mentorship is delivered by engineers from top AI groups, and payment is contingent on employment, aligning incentives."
