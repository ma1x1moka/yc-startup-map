**Website:** https://www.shef.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/shef

Shef is a marketplace that connects consumers with talented chefs in their community, allowing users to order homemade meals delivered to their door. By applying a sharing‑economy model to the dinner table, the platform creates economic opportunities for chefs and makes homemade food more accessible and affordable, with chefs crafting small‑batch menus based on their expertise. The company has attracted over $100 million in funding from investors such as Andreessen Horowitz, Y Combinator, Craft Ventures and M13, and employs about 70 people in San Francisco.

_Usage:_

"How does Shef’s model differ from typical restaurant delivery services?"

"Shef doesn’t operate its own kitchens; instead it matches customers with independent chefs who prepare meals in small batches at home and ship them, so the platform focuses on a sharing‑economy marketplace rather than restaurant‑owned delivery."
