Shelf Engine helps grocery retailers cut waste and boost sales by using machine-learning models to forecast the exact quantity of perishable items to stock.\n\nFounded in Seattle, the company grew to a 40-person team before being acquired, a move that underscored the value of its data-driven inventory solution.
