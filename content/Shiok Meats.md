Shiok Meats is a cell‑based clean meat company that focuses exclusively on seafood. Based in Singapore, it uses cellular agriculture to grow shrimp and other marine protein in a laboratory from animal cells, eliminating the need for animal farming. The company describes its products as animal‑, health‑ and environment‑friendly, offering the same taste, texture and nutrients as conventional shrimp while avoiding cruelty.

Shiok Meats is the first cell‑based meat company in Singapore and Southeast Asia, positioning it as a regional pioneer in cultivated seafood. The company has a nine‑person team operating out of Singapore and was acquired, reflecting investor confidence in its approach to climate‑friendly protein production.
