**Website:** https://shipamax.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/shipamax

Shipamax builds automation software that can “understand” any logistics document and turn it into a business‑ready, machine‑readable format. Its core technology uses natural‑language processing to extract structured data from the myriad forms, invoices, and shipping manifests that move through supply chains.

By converting these documents automatically, the platform removes the manual work that traditionally ties together up to twenty companies in a single shipment, making the behind‑the‑scenes logistics process simpler and more scalable. Customers in the B2B supply‑chain and logistics space value the ability to streamline operations and adapt to today’s on‑demand economy.

Shipamax was acquired after demonstrating this capability, and it now operates from London with a team of 32 engineers and product specialists.
