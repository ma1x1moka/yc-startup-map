**Website:** https://getshogun.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/shogun

Shogun is an ecommerce experience platform that helps brands drive higher conversions, revenue, and brand loyalty. Its two main products, Shogun Page Builder and Shogun Frontend, let teams build and optimize online stores. The platform integrates with major ecommerce services such as Shopify and BigCommerce, allowing customers to use familiar back‑ends while enhancing the front‑end experience.

The company serves more than 20,000 customers, including brands like Leesa, MVMT, Timbuk2, and Chubbies. Shogun operates as a remote‑first, inclusive organization with about 90 employees spread across San Francisco, Palo Alto, and other remote locations. It is part of the Winter 2018 Y Combinator batch and remains active in the B2B SaaS e‑commerce space.

_Usage:_

"Does Shogun replace the native page‑builder features in Shopify?"

"Shogun provides its own Page Builder and Frontend that integrate with Shopify, so teams can use Shogun’s tools to design and optimize their stores while still running on Shopify’s platform."
