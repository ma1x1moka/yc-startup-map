Co-founded [Verse Therapy](./Verse%20Therapy.md) as CTO with Adam Steinle and Danielle Yoesep. Zhang has a background spanning biomedical engineering and software, having worked as a software engineer at Google and Amazon before the startup. Verse Therapy was later acquired by Beaming Health.
