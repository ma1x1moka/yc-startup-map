**Website:** https://textsidekick.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/textsidekick

Sidekick is an AI agent designed to streamline frontline operations by interacting with workers through standard text messages. It allows employees to submit problems, photos, or voice memos in any language, after which the system answers queries using the company's internal documents, creates and routes work orders, and syncs with existing operational software. The tool requires no app installation or login, functioning on any phone to lower the barrier to entry for field teams.

The platform also monitors connected machine data to proactively alert workers when equipment performance begins to drift, aiming to prevent downtime before it occurs. Additionally, Sidekick builds a searchable knowledge base from every interaction, ensuring that institutional knowledge is retained and accessible rather than lost when experienced staff retire. The company is based in San Francisco and operates in the B2B and manufacturing sectors.

_Usage:_

"How does Sidekick handle the lack of a dedicated mobile app for field workers?"

"It bypasses the need for a specific app or login by operating entirely over standard SMS. Workers can send text messages, photos, or voice memos in any language, and the AI agent processes these inputs to answer questions from company documents, create work orders, and sync with existing software, making it accessible on any phone."
