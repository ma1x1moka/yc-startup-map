Co-founded [Proxy](./Proxy.md) as CTO with [Denis Mars](./Denis%20Mars.md) in YC's Summer 2016 batch. Ratner holds a Master's in Electrical Engineering from Stanford and dual Bachelor's degrees in Software Engineering and Biochemistry from the University of Sydney. He previously co-founded Omnisio (acquired by Google) and worked as an engineer at Jive Software and YouTube. Proxy was later acquired by Oura, where Ratner now heads engineering for future technologies.
