**Website:** http://www.simplecitizen.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/simplecitizen

SimpleCitizen streamlines the complex process of applying for US citizenship by guiding applicants through a streamlined online experience. The platform allows users to answer just 20 questions in approximately 20 minutes, aiming to make the procedure as straightforward as filling out a job application. By automating this step, the service helps users avoid significant legal fees associated with traditional application methods.

Based in Salt Lake City, Utah, the company operated with a team of 23 people before being acquired. It focused on the consumer and civic tech sectors, specifically targeting individuals navigating the US immigration system. The acquisition marks the end of its independent operation, consolidating its approach to simplifying government-related applications.

_Usage:_

"How does SimpleCitizen replace the need for a lawyer in the citizenship process?"

"It replaces the initial intake and form-filling phase by using a 20-question online survey that takes about 20 minutes to complete. This automated guidance is designed to handle the application setup in a way that saves users thousands of dollars in legal fees, effectively making the process as simple as a standard online job application."
