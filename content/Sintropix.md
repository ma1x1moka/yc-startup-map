Sintropix is a financial system of record that closes your books every day and files your taxes in every country you operate in.

Sintropix connects to your banks, invoicing and payroll, and handle the accounting end-to-end. Founders get one place to do their finance work: track KPIs, see consolidated P&L and ARR reports, and forecast runway.
