**Website:** https://sivo.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sivo

Sivo provides a debt-as-a-service platform that allows fintechs, neobanks, and gig platforms to lend money to their users at scale. By offering an API and risk protocol, the company helps these businesses avoid the lengthy process of raising debt, which can take up to two years. Sivo positions itself as the "Stripe for Debt" in this space.

The company is active and has secured a $100m debt capital partnership, with plans to launch its API in April. It reports having over 100 companies in its customer pipeline, including Uber. Sivo is a 15-person team based in Vancouver, San Francisco, and remote locations, with members drawing on experience from firms like Goldman Sachs, Revolut, and Citigroup.

_Usage:_

"How does Sivo handle the capital requirements for lending if the client platform doesn't have its own balance sheet?"

"Sivo acts as the intermediary that provides the debt capital, allowing the client to lend without raising funds themselves. The company has signed a $100m debt capital partnership to support this model, effectively offloading the capital-raising burden from the fintech or gig platform to Sivo's infrastructure."
