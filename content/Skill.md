---
description: A teachable capability packaged as a unit, kept out of the context window until a pointer pulls it in for the task at hand.
---

A skill bundles instructions, and often supporting scripts or reference files, for doing one kind of work properly. It sits on disk with only a short description visible to the [agent](./Agent.md); when a task matches, the full contents load.

This is [progressive disclosure](./Progressive%20disclosure.md) applied to expertise. Twenty skills cost twenty short descriptions in [context](./Context.md), not twenty full playbooks — so you can accumulate specialised know-how without every [session](./Session.md) paying for all of it.

_Usage:_

"Where do I put our release checklist?"

"Make it a skill. Then it loads when it's releasing and stays out of the way otherwise."
