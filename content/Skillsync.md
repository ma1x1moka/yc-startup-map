description: Hiring platform that evaluates engineers on shipped work rather than resumes and interviews. YC W26.
---

Reached 1,854 Twitter likes at launch. Builds an alternative hiring pipeline for engineers: instead of resumes, cover letters, and whiteboard interviews, candidates are evaluated on their actual shipped work — GitHub contributions, deployed projects, open-source impact, production code they can demonstrate.

The founding frustration: the resume + technical interview system selects for people who are good at passing technical interviews, which correlates imperfectly with people who are good at shipping software. Whiteboard coding in 45 minutes on an artificial problem is a poor proxy for the skills that matter in a real engineering role.

Skillsync's alternative: structured portfolios of real work, async technical demonstrations on actual problems, and employer-side tools to evaluate authentic engineering output.

The supply side (engineers) find it attractive because their actual work is being evaluated. The demand side (employers) find it attractive if they've been burned by interview-passing engineers who can't ship in production.

Skillsync and [Cardboard](./Cardboard.md) are both betting on work product over process — [Cardboard](./Cardboard.md) evaluates video through the actual editor experience, Skillsync evaluates engineers through their actual shipped code. [Pocket](./Pocket.md) produces the transcripts and notes that make work product visible after the fact.

_Usage:_

"Won't candidates just clean up their GitHub to look better?"

"Yes, and that's fine — curating your best work is a legitimate signal. It's still more predictive than optimizing for a specific LeetCode style in a 45-minute window."
