**Website:** https://skio.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/skio

Skio provides subscription management infrastructure specifically designed for brands operating on the Shopify platform. The company enables e-commerce businesses to handle recurring billing and customer retention within the Shopify ecosystem. Skio was acquired with a reported cash exit of $105 million, having reached $32 million in annual recurring revenue after raising $8 million in funding.

Based in New York City with a remote-friendly team of 70, Skio operated at the intersection of B2B marketing and fintech. Its acquisition highlights the significant value placed on specialized subscription tooling for major e-commerce platforms.

_Usage:_

"How does Skio differ from generic subscription apps on the Shopify App Store?"

"Skio is built exclusively for Shopify, meaning it integrates deeply with the platform's specific data structures and checkout flows rather than trying to support multiple e-commerce backends. This focus allows it to handle the nuances of Shopify's subscription models more natively than multi-platform tools, which is likely why it reached $32M ARR before its acquisition."
