**Website:** https://www.slash.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/slash

Slash is America’s leading business banking platform, serving over 5,000 mid‑market companies and processing $30 billion of transactions each year across all payment rails. Backed by NEA, Menlo Ventures, Stanford University and the founders of Tinder and Plaid, the company offers deposit accounts with free outgoing wire and ACH transfers, unlimited virtual cards that earn up to 2 % cash back, and integrations with major accounting platforms. It also supplies free invoicing, expense management, bill‑pay software, stablecoin payments, and a powerful card‑creation and payments API, all available to businesses in more than 100 countries. With a team of about 70 people in San Francisco, Slash positions itself as the “Costco” of business banking, delivering high cash‑back rewards and high‑yield treasury accounts while keeping its software stack free for finance teams.
