Sleek builds AI agents designed to automate critical eCommerce workflows for online shoppers. The company partners with consumer businesses to power these automated experiences across both mobile and desktop platforms via the browser. Notably, Sleek was acquired, marking a significant exit for the Toronto-based team.

_Usage:_

"How does Sleek handle the difference between mobile and desktop browser environments for its automation agents?"

"Sleek’s agents are built to operate across both mobile and desktop browsers, allowing consumer businesses to provide a consistent automated experience regardless of the device the shopper is using."
