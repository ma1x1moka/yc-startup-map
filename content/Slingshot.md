**Website:** https://slingshotml.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/slingshot

Slingshot is an AI tool that analyzes medical records to determine whether a diagnosis is supported by the underlying clinical evidence. The company addresses frustrations with inflated and erroneous medical bills, aiming to help save patients money with less manual work.

Slingshot was acquired, marking its exit from the YC Summer 2022 batch. The team is based in San Francisco, California, and focuses specifically on the healthcare industry.

_Usage:_

"How does Slingshot determine if a diagnosis is valid?"

"It uses AI to analyze medical records and check if the stated diagnosis is actually supported by the clinical evidence present in those records, effectively automating the verification process to catch errors or unsupported claims."
