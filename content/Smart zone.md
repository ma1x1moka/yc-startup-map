---
description: The stretch of a session where the agent works well — enough context to be useful, not so much that quality has slipped.
---

[Sessions](./Session.md) have an arc. Early on the [agent](./Agent.md) lacks the [context](./Context.md) to be effective; in the middle it has what it needs and the window is still clean; late on, degradation sets in. The middle stretch is where the good work happens.

The zone does not track the window limit. A session can be well past its smart zone with most of the window still free — the limit is where the [harness](./Harness.md) refuses to continue, but quality falls off long before that. Plan around the zone, not the ceiling: the real budget for a task is the [tokens](./Token.md) the agent works well within.

_Usage:_

"We're only at forty percent of the window."

"And well out of the smart zone. Those aren't the same number."
