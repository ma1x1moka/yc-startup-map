**Website:** https://solarmente.es | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/solarmente

SolarMente enabled homeowners in Southern Europe to install solar panels, energy storage, and heat pumps with zero upfront cost. The company operated on a model where users paid through predictable monthly savings rather than a large initial capital expenditure. Based in Barcelona with a team of 65, SolarMente raised $5M from investors including Y Combinator, Climate Capital, and Leonardo DiCaprio. The company has since become inactive.

_Usage:_

"How did SolarMente handle the capital intensity of home electrification for consumers?"

"They removed the upfront barrier entirely by covering the installation costs for solar, storage, and heat pumps. Homeowners then repaid the investment through the predictable monthly savings generated by the new energy system, effectively turning a capital expenditure into a subscription-like cash flow model."
