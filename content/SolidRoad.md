Companies deploying AI agents for customer service have no systematic way to test them. Human QA doesn't scale — you'd need as many reviewers as conversations. SolidRoad automates the QA layer: synthetic adversarial test conversations, failure mode identification, performance scoring, and automatic training data generation from regressions.

The loop: deploy agent → SolidRoad finds failures → failures become training examples → agent improves → repeat.

The $25M Series A in April 2026 — less than a year after a $6.5M seed — is one of the fastest institutional re-ups in the F25/W25 cohort. It reflects real enterprise traction, not just Demo Day momentum.

Sits in a natural stack with [Multifactor](./Multifactor.md) (agent security) and [Metorial](./Metorial.md) (agent connections). Where [Bolna](./Bolna.md) and [Beacon Health](./Beacon%20Health.md) deploy agents into verticals, SolidRoad is the horizontal QA layer any deployment needs.

_Usage:_

"Can't I just monitor customer satisfaction scores?"

"CSAT is lagging — you find out the agent failed after the customer had a bad experience. SolidRoad catches failures before real users hit them by running adversarial test cases against the agent in simulation."
