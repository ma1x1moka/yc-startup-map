Solve Intelligence provides an AI platform designed to assist legal professionals with every stage of the patent process, including application drafting, office action responses, invention harvesting, and litigation support. The company combines a team of patent attorneys, AI PhDs, and software developers to build tools that handle complex IP workflows.

Backed by $55M from investors including Y Combinator, Microsoft, and Thomson Reuters, Solve Intelligence serves over 700 IP firms and in-house teams across the US, Europe, and Asia. The platform is marketed as 100% secure and confidential, with customers reporting 60-90% improvements in efficiency and quality.
