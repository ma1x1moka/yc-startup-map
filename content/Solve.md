**Website:** https://www.solve.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/solve

Solve (Y Combinator Summer 2017) provides airport fast‑track, customs, and concierge services in more than 470 airports worldwide, covering departure, arrival, and transit. The service saves travelers hours by meeting them at their arrival gate and handling logistics, operating from its Las Vegas, NV base with a two‑person team. Solve was acquired, highlighting the market’s interest in streamlined travel logistics.

_Usage:_

"What does Solve actually do for a traveler arriving at an airport?"

"Solve meets the traveler at the arrival gate, fast‑tracks them through customs, and provides concierge assistance for any departure, arrival, or transit needs, leveraging its network across hundreds of airports."
