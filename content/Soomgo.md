Soomgo is a local services marketplace that connects providers with new customers across more than 1,000 services, including moving, piano lessons, wedding planning, and others. It operates out of Seoul, South Korea, and employs roughly 125 people serving the consumer and home‑personal sectors. Soomgo was acquired, marking its exit from the Y Combinator portfolio.
