**Website:** https://soundry.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/soundry-ai

Soundry AI builds generative AI tools that enhance fan engagement by allowing listeners to remix songs into any musical style. Its flagship product, Groove, can transform a track like Taylor Swift into the heavy‑metal style of Slayer, demonstrating the platform’s flexibility.

The Seattle‑based startup was part of Y Combinator’s Winter 2024 batch and has a two‑person team. Soundry AI operates in the consumer and content industries and focuses on social‑media‑driven music experiences. The company has been acquired, underscoring the market interest in AI‑powered music remixing.

_Usage:_

"How does Soundry AI’s Groove remix feature differ from generic AI music generators?"

"Groove lets users specify both the source track and a target genre, producing a remix that matches the chosen style—e.g., turning a pop song into a metal version—whereas many generic AI music tools only generate new compositions without a direct style‑transfer capability."
