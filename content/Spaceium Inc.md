Spaceium builds fully automated space stations to refuel and repair spacecraft. It has secured $86.1 million in binding commercial contracts, $230 million in the pipeline, and $1B in letters of intent, and has successfully tested hardware that will launch next year. The stations store and transfer both cryogenic and non-cryogenic fuels with zero loss, using a proprietary modular robotic arm that can transfer fuel to any spacecraft design. Customers include launch vehicles, orbital transfer vehicles, moon landers, and other spacecraft that benefit from increased payload capacity and extended travel capabilities. Spaceium's long-term vision is to build service hubs along the space superhighway connecting Earth to Moon and Mars, helping humanity become multi-planetary. The company is based in Ottawa, Canada, and operates in the industrial, aviation, and space sectors.
