Spark AI offers workflow software that helps solar, storage, and data center developers navigate zoning, permitting, and other project-related requirements. The platform has reviewed and identified over 1 million documents across nearly every U.S. jurisdiction, allowing users to instantly see local sentiment, project requirements, and fatal flaws. It is trusted by dozens of industry‑leading developers who collectively manage more than 90 GW of projects and $30 billion in financing. The five‑person team draws leadership experience from AI and energy backgrounds at Microsoft, Apple, the Department of Energy, and Brex.

_Usage:_

"How does Spark’s AI workflow speed up permitting compared to doing it manually?"

"Spark leverages a curated database of over a million zoning and permitting documents, so instead of searching through paperwork, developers get instant insights into local requirements, sentiment, and potential deal‑breakers, cutting the research time from weeks to seconds."
