---
description: A written statement of what is being built and what counts as done. A handoff artifact aimed forward rather than backward.
---

A spec fixes the target before implementation starts, which gives every subsequent [session](./Session.md) the same definition of success and gives you something concrete to review that is far cheaper to change than code.

It is also the most effective place to catch a misunderstanding. Disagreements about scope surface in a paragraph in seconds; the same disagreement discovered in a finished implementation costs the implementation.

_Usage:_

"Can it just start building?"

"Get the spec agreed first. Rewriting a paragraph beats rewriting the module."
