**Website:** https://speck.sh | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/speck

Speck helps professionals in back‑to‑back calls on their meeting tasks. It automates pre‑call research, listens to every word, learns, and creates meeting notes, follow‑ups, and action items based on how the user works.

The company was acquired after its Winter 2024 batch. Based in San Francisco, it operates with a three‑person team and focuses on B2B productivity and sales enablement.

_Usage:_

"How is Speck different from a standard transcription tool like Otter.ai?"

"Speck not only records and transcribes the conversation, it also performs pre‑call research, learns the user’s style, and automatically generates structured notes, follow‑ups, and action items, turning raw audio into actionable outcomes."
