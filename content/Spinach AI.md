**Website:** https://spinach.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/spinach-ai

Spinach AI is a Y Combinator-backed company that serves as the system of action for conversation data. It captures and transforms raw conversations into structured knowledge that powers people, agents, and LLMs across the enterprise.

The platform aims to drive productivity for workers and provide strategic insight for leaders. It is backed by Zoom and Atlassian and is used by industry leaders such as Abnormal AI and eToro.

Based in Nashville, TN, and operating remotely, Spinach AI focuses on B2B productivity and conversational AI solutions.

_Usage:_

"How does Spinach AI differ from standard CRM or chat tools?"

"Spinach AI acts as a system of action specifically for conversation data, meaning it doesn't just store chats but transforms them into structured knowledge. This structured output is designed to power downstream applications like agents and LLMs, rather than just providing a log of interactions for humans to read."
