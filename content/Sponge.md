**Website:** https://paysponge.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sponge

Sponge is the easiest way for AI agents to hold and spend money, and for businesses to sell directly to them. Agents can hold funds and transact autonomously with other agents and businesses using bank accounts, cards, and crypto — purchasing services like email, browser use, and premium data without human involvement.

Addresses the missing financial primitive in the agentic stack: agents need to be economic actors, not just task executors.
