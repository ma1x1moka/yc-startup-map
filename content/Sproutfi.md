**YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sproutfi

Sproutfi is a fintech platform based in São Paulo, Brazil, that serves as the region's first investing social network. It allows users in Latin America to invest in thousands of US stocks and ETFs with no account minimums, no fees, and a minimum investment of just $1 USD. The platform integrates social features that facilitate the sharing of investment ideas and learning among investors.

The company was founded by a team of 17 experienced builders with backgrounds at major financial institutions including Goldman Sachs, E*Trade Financial, Nubank, ING, and Wise. Sproutfi was acquired, marking its exit from the YC Summer 2021 batch.

_Usage:_

"How does Sproutfi handle the complexity of US market access for Latin American investors compared to traditional brokers?"

"Sproutfi removes traditional barriers by eliminating account minimums and fees, allowing users to start investing with as little as $1 USD. It also adds a social layer that lets users share ideas and learn from each other, which is distinct from standard brokerage apps that focus solely on transaction execution."
