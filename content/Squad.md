**Website:** http://squadapp.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/squad

Squad is a consumer social app that lets groups share their screens during video calls, enabling friends to use any application together in real time. By integrating screen sharing directly into the chat experience, it creates shared, interactive moments for live communities. Squad was acquired, reflecting its appeal in the social video space.

The team of ten operates from San Francisco and remotely, focusing on enhancing social interactions through seamless shared experiences.

_Usage:_

"Can Squad’s screen sharing be used with any app during a video call, or is it limited to specific software?"

"Squad’s core feature is adding screen sharing to video chats so users can open and use any app with friends while staying on the call, so it is not restricted to a predefined list of applications."
