**Website:** https://www.stackai.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/stackai

StackAI is an enterprise AI transformation platform that partners with large organizations to deploy custom AI agents for automating complex back-office workflows. The company provides a no-code interface that allows teams to build and manage agents handling tasks such as compliance audits, legal intake, due diligence research, and insurance claim underwriting.

Notable clients include BAE Systems, Monzo, Morgan & Morgan, and Goodwill, which use the platform to integrate AI into their core operations. With a team of 62 based in San Francisco, StackAI focuses on the B2B sector, specifically targeting high-stakes operational processes that require precision and scale.

The company was acquired by Asana in May 2026, marking a significant consolidation in the enterprise AI agent space.

_Usage:_

"How does StackAI handle complex, multi-step back-office tasks like compliance audits without requiring engineering resources?"

"StackAI uses a no-code platform to let business teams define and deploy custom AI agents that can execute multi-step workflows. Instead of writing code, users configure agents to handle specific tasks like legal intake or due diligence, allowing the system to automate these processes directly within existing enterprise operations."
