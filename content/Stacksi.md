**Website:** https://www.stacksi.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/stacksi

Stacksi enables fast-growing enterprise startups to automate the process of answering security questionnaires, freeing up engineering time for strategic projects. By allowing sales teams and executives to upload existing security policies and incoming questionnaires, the platform parses these documents to identify gaps against industry standards and helps establish a robust security program. This approach allows companies to quickly navigate enterprise security audits and improve their overall security posture while closing deals faster.

The company, which operated with a small remote team based in Los Angeles, was acquired, marking a significant milestone for this B2B security tool.

_Usage:_

"How does Stacksi handle the initial setup for a company that already has written security policies?"

"You upload your existing written security policies along with the new security questionnaire into the system. Stacksi then parses both sets of documents to identify any gaps between your current policies and the standards required by the questionnaire, helping you automate the completion process and address those gaps."
