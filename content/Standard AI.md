**Website:** https://standard.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/standard-ai

Standard AI is an artificial intelligence platform designed for the retail sector, enabling a checkout-free shopping experience. The company provides technology that allows buyers to simply take what they want and leave, eliminating the traditional step of visiting a cashier.

With a team of approximately 150 people, the company operates out of San Francisco, Milan, and remotely. It focuses on B2B retail tech, applying AI to streamline the point-of-sale process for retailers.

_Usage:_

"How does this differ from standard self-checkout kiosks?"

"Unlike self-checkout kiosks where the customer still has to scan items and process payment, Standard AI's platform allows buyers to grab items and leave without any interaction with a terminal or cashier. It is a fully automated, checkout-free system rather than a manual self-service point."
