StarJar provides AI‑powered automation for inbound document processing in hospitals, handling patient referrals, claim files, audit files, medical images and more. The platform streamlines these workflows so that hospitals can ingest and route documents without manual effort.

StarJar works with many of the top healthcare systems and hospitals in the United States, automating millions of inbound documents for them each year. As a SaaS enterprise solution, it delivers scalable processing across a range of document types.

The company was acquired after building a team of 11 engineers and staff, and it continues to serve the B2B healthcare market under its new ownership.

_Usage:_

"How is StarJar different from a generic document management system?"

"StarJar is focused specifically on inbound document processing for hospitals, using AI to automatically extract and route patient referrals, claim files, audit files and medical images. Unlike a generic DMS that stores documents, StarJar automates the entire intake workflow for healthcare providers, handling millions of documents each year for top US hospital systems."
