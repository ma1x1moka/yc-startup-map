**Website:** https://starcity.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/starcity

Starcity operated as one of the largest co-living brands in the real estate sector. The company focused on providing housing solutions within the proptech space, based in San Francisco, California.

Starcity was acquired by Common in 2021, marking its exit from the market. This acquisition consolidated its position within the co-living industry during that period.

_Usage:_

"What happened to Starcity after it was a major player in co-living?"
