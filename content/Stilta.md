**Website:** https://stilta.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/stilta

Stilta builds agentic AI software for intellectual property, starting with patent litigation. Leading IP law firms and in-house IP teams use Stilta for source-backed, auditable analysis — whether challenging or enforcing a patent.

The key differentiation: every output is source-backed, referenced, and auditable. AI helps with patent drafting, but doesn't deliver the trust and transparency that legal-grade work demands. Stilta runs search and analysis at scale with full traceability. Stockholm-based, W26.

_Usage:_

"How does Stilta ensure its AI outputs are source‑backed and auditable compared to other AI patent‑drafting tools?"

"Stilta ties every piece of analysis to the underlying documents it retrieved, storing the exact search queries, the prior‑art excerpts, and the citations that support each conclusion, so users can trace any recommendation back to a verifiable source, unlike typical AI drafting tools that generate text without exposing the data that generated it."
