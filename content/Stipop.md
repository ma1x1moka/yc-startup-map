**Website:** https://studio.stipop.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/stipop

Stipop operates as a monetization platform for independent character IP owners, often described as a Patreon for character creators. The company provides fun, dynamic features that allow creators to generate revenue directly from their audience. It is a team of eight based in Los Angeles and Santa Monica, California.

The platform evolved from a sticker service that hosted 5,000 creators and 250,000 stickers. During this earlier phase, Stipop supplied stickers to major platforms including Canva, Microsoft Teams, Google, and Adobe. It also offers a sticker API and SDK designed to help chat apps improve user engagement and revenue.

Stipop is an active company from the Summer 2021 YC batch. It has received investment from Snap through the Yellow Accelerator, Y Combinator, Translink Investment, Strong Ventures, and Next Ventures.

_Usage:_

"How does Stipop differ from general creator platforms like Patreon, and what infrastructure do they offer to third-party apps?"

"Stipop is specifically tailored for character IP owners rather than general content creators, focusing on dynamic features for character-based revenue. Beyond the creator platform, it provides a sticker API and SDK that chat apps can integrate to boost their own user engagement and monetization, having previously supplied stickers to major platforms like Canva and Adobe."
