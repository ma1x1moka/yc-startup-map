**Website:** https://storyboarder.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/storyboarder

Storyboarder is a Y Combinator W23 company that provides a tool and platform for anyone to create, share, and sell webcomics. The company aims to address the limitations of the existing webcomic market, where content variety is constrained by the need for creators to possess drawing skills, despite high reader demand for diverse stories.

Leveraging years of experience building storyboarding software for filmmakers, which has reached 700K users, Storyboarder is developing next-generation tools that utilize video game interfaces, ubiquitous 3D compute, and AI. This approach allows users to easily create scenes and pose characters, effectively democratizing visual storytelling for a broader audience.

The company is based in New York City and operates remotely with a small team of two. It is backed by Y Combinator, David Karp, Andrew Kortina, and other product-focused investors.

_Usage:_

"How does this differ from traditional digital art tools for comics?"

"Unlike traditional tools that require users to draw from scratch, Storyboarder uses 3D compute and AI to let users create scenes and pose characters without needing drawing skills, similar to how video game interfaces work."
