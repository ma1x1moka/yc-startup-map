Stralis Aircraft designs and manufactures hydrogen‑electric airplanes that fly ten times farther than comparable battery‑electric models while offering lower operating costs than conventional fossil‑fuel aircraft. Its first product is a fifteen‑seat aircraft with an 800 km range slated to enter service in 2026 with launch customer Skytrans. The company’s team of four in Brisbane brings over 85 years of aerospace experience, having developed, flight‑tested and certified aircraft at organisations such as Google X, magniX, Heart Aerospace, Ampaire, Airbus, Boeing, Bombardier and Gulfstream. Stralis Aircraft has secured $145 million in letters of intent from seven airlines across the United States, Europe and Australia, underscoring market interest in its zero‑emission solution.

_Usage:_

"How does Stralis achieve a range ten times longer than battery‑electric aircraft?"

"Stralis uses hydrogen fuel‑cell technology to generate electricity for propulsion, which provides a much higher energy‑density than batteries; this allows its aircraft to travel farther on a single refuel while remaining emission‑free and keeping operating costs below those of fossil‑fuel planes."
