Streamline Systems is building the replacement for GPS.

Satellite positioning has become the single point of failure for almost every modern system, both military and civil. Streamline Systems has built the replacement. A drop-in module that integrates with existing Ardupilot, INAV and PX4 flight controllers, with demonstrated satellite-independent autonomous flight and GPS handoff.

It haves secured over $80k in contracted revenue to supply its navigation modules for the Pentagon's $1.1bn Drone Dominance Program.
