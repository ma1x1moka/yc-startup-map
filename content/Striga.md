**Website:** https://striga.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/striga

Striga provides infrastructure for digital assets and financial services, consolidating security and regulatory compliance into a single set of APIs. The platform serves early-stage FinTechs, Web3 innovators, banking institutions, and crypto exchanges with services spanning crypto custody, trading, card issuing, and banking.

By addressing the high barrier to entry created by complex regulatory requirements, Striga allows businesses to operate across 30 countries under a single contract. The company was based in Tallinn, Estonia, and had a team of 14 people before being acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Striga handle the regulatory complexity for a startup launching in multiple countries?"

"Instead of building separate compliance programs for each jurisdiction, you integrate with Striga's unified API suite. It handles the heavy lifting of security and regulatory compliance, allowing you to deliver crypto custody, trading, and banking services across 30 countries with just a single contract."
