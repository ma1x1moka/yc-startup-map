**Website:** https://strive.school | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/strive-school

Strive School operates a six-month online program designed to transform STEM students into professional-grade software engineers. The company addresses the gap between theoretical university curricula and industry requirements by providing practical, job-ready training for those pursuing science and technology fields in Europe.

The platform utilizes an income share agreement model, where students pay a portion of their future salary in exchange for access to the curriculum. This approach allows candidates to defer costs until they are employed, aligning the company's success with the career outcomes of its graduates.

Strive School was acquired, marking a significant milestone for the Berlin-based team. The company, which maintains a small remote-first team, focused on the intersection of education and fintech to modernize software engineering training in the European market.

_Usage:_

"How does the payment model for Strive School differ from traditional bootcamps or university tuition?"

"Instead of requiring upfront tuition, Strive School uses an income share agreement where students pay a percentage of their future salary after becoming employed. This shifts the financial risk from the student to the provider and ensures that the cost of training is directly tied to the student's success in landing a software engineering role, which is a distinct model from the standard flat-fee structures common in most coding bootcamps."
