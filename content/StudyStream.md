**Website:** https://studystream.live | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/studystream

StudyStream operates a video platform and online community designed to help students study collaboratively in real time, available around the clock. By connecting learners from over 180 countries, the platform aims to reduce isolation and boost productivity through peer accountability and motivation.

The company has supported more than 3 million students in becoming more productive and motivated. Based in London with a remote-friendly team of eight, StudyStream focuses on building a global community where students can encourage one another to succeed.

_Usage:_

"How does StudyStream differ from standard video conferencing tools like Zoom for studying?"

"While Zoom is a general-purpose communication tool, StudyStream is specifically built as a 24/7 community platform for study sessions. It emphasizes continuous availability and a global peer network to provide ongoing accountability, rather than just facilitating a one-off video call."
