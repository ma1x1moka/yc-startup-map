**Website:** https://www.subsets.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/subsets

Subsets is the leading lifecycle growth platform for consumer subscription teams. It builds an AI decisioning layer that unifies and powers existing growth stacks, enabling teams to automate and scale lifecycle experimentation, orchestrate personalized user journeys, and measure impact across retention, engagement, and LTV without engineering support.
Trusted by major consumer subscription businesses such as The Atlantic, NBCUniversal, Daily Mail, Matas Group, and Hearst, Subsets raised $6.1 million and launched out of Y Combinator’s Summer 2023 batch in San Francisco before relocating to Copenhagen.
With a team of seven, Subsets operates in the B2B enterprise software space, focusing on machine learning-driven retention automation for subscription media businesses.
