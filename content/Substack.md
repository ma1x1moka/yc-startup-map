**Website:** https://substack.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/substack

Substack is a platform that lets independent writers start newsletters, build a community, and earn money from subscriptions. Publishing on Substack is free and unlimited, and the company only takes a cut when writers generate revenue. The service, founded in YC’s Winter 2018 batch, operates as a B2B marketing marketplace with a team of about 90 people based in San Francisco.

_Usage:_

"How does Substack generate revenue if publishing is free?"

"Substack makes money by taking a share of the subscription revenue that writers earn; it does not charge writers any upfront fees for publishing."
