**Website:** https://sunflowerclinic.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sunflower

Sunflower is an AI-native telemedicine clinic focused on addiction care, addressing a significant gap in the market where over 18,750 patients are currently on waitlists for therapy or medication. The company is building clinical services to serve Medicaid, which spends over $30 billion annually on substance use disorder services, leveraging a substantial addressable market of $153 million in pending demand.

The company’s initial product, Sunflower Sober, was the first AI Sponsor for addiction, which reached 100 monthly active users in under six months and generates approximately $1.5 million in annual subscription revenue. Sunflower Sober is pacing to add over 800,000 new users per year, a growth rate the company notes is faster than that of Alcoholics Anonymous.

Based in San Francisco, the 15-person team includes clinical leaders from the Mayo Clinic, Choose Your Horizon, and Charlie Health. The company aims to combine its established brand in the sobriety community with robust clinical infrastructure to better serve patients.

"How does Sunflower differentiate its AI approach from traditional addiction support groups like AA?"
