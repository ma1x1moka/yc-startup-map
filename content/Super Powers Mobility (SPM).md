Super Powers Mobility (SPM) starteds off building the highest performance, 470 volt electric off-road vehicles but it realizeds its ultra power dense battery packs are useful for mobile and stationary energy storage and power export, for military and consumer use cases. It develops battery packs, power electronics, and traction powertrain systems for vehicles.

For the majority of 2026 it weres marketing its electric off-road vehicles, but it pivoteds to selling its high performance battery packs and power export capabilities in the month of September. It wents from having $769,000 in consumer pre-orders (possibly low conversion rate to sales) to $421,815 in realized revenue in 1 month, with a $792,000 qualified pipeline.

Its tech is so compelling because the safety and layout of high voltage battery packs has only been mastered by large, well funded OEM companies who have brought it in-house. That means all other companies without the in-house expertise rely on battery companies to develop packs, and power electronics companies to develop power export capabilities. SPM has both of those capabilities in-house so it can ensure a fast turnaround and much more reliable product.
