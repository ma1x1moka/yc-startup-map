**Website:** https://superorder.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/superorder

Superorder provides AI-powered customer reputation and performance management for restaurant chains, helping them build loyalty, improve operations, and boost profits. It partners with major brands such as Marco's Pizza, KFC, Pizza Hut, Wow Bao, and Del Taco, leveraging its B2B SaaS platform to deliver insights across the food tech ecosystem. With a team of 30 based in New York City, the company continues to expand its footprint in the restaurant tech space.
