**Website:** https://superwall.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/staffbar

Superwall provides a standalone subscription platform for consumer mobile and web applications, combining backend infrastructure with a no-code paywall and experimentation engine. The platform manages over $1.6 billion in annual subscription revenue across more than 10,000 apps, handling entitlements, purchase APIs with server-side receipt validation, and real-time revenue analytics. Notably, the infrastructure layer is free at any scale, with billing applied only to revenue generated through Superwall-rendered paywalls.

The company distinguishes itself by allowing high-revenue apps to run their entire subscription stack on Superwall alone, eliminating the need for a second backend. For example, the largest single app on the platform runs $43.7 million in annual recurring revenue entirely on Superwall. The platform supports Apple App Store, Google Play, and Stripe, covering various monetization models including auto-renewing subscriptions, free trials, and one-time purchases.

Superwall also offers extensive experimentation tools, having analyzed over 100 million paywall views and conducted more than 4,500 A/B tests. Its research indicates that multi-page onboarding paywalls can convert 37% better than single-page alternatives. Additionally, the company provides first-class tooling for AI coding agents, including open-source agent skills and an editor MCP server for building live paywalls from the terminal.

_Usage:_

"How does Superwall's pricing model differ from traditional subscription platforms that charge a percentage of total revenue?"

"Superwall charges 1% only on revenue attributed to its own paywalls, whereas traditional platforms often take a cut of all subscription revenue. This means if an app generates $50k/month in subscriptions but doesn't use Superwall's paywalls, it pays $0 for the platform. The infrastructure layer, including entitlements and receipt validation, is free at any scale, so the cost is strictly tied to the incremental revenue Superwall helps earn."
