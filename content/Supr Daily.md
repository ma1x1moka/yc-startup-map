**Website:** http://www.suprdaily.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/supr-daily

Supr Daily is a daily grocery delivery service operating in India. It uses a daily milk subscription as a “railroad” to bundle deliveries, cutting last‑mile costs by tenfold and enabling a “Trojan Cow” approach that brings all groceries to the doorstep by 7 am each morning.

At its peak the company generated a $100 million annual revenue run rate, operated in six cities and fulfilled more than 200 k orders per day. Supr Daily was part of Y Combinator’s Winter 2017 batch and was later acquired by Swiggy.

_Usage:_

"How does Supr Daily’s milk‑subscription model lower delivery costs compared to standard grocery delivery services?"

"Supr Daily bundles grocery trips with a daily milk subscription, treating the milk delivery route as a fixed “railroad”. By piggybacking other items onto that route, it spreads the last‑mile expense across many orders, which the company says reduces those costs by about ten times."
