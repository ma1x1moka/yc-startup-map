**Website:** https://www.surematics.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/surematics

Surematics built an online platform designed to help commercial insurance brokers structure complicated insurance deals. While standard consumer coverage is straightforward, the company targeted complex, high-value cases involving expensive assets such as oil rigs.

By leveraging blockchain technology, Surematics enabled multiple organizations to collaborate digitally to produce legally enforceable contracts. Based in San Francisco as part of the Y Combinator Summer 2017 cohort, the company has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Surematics facilitate collaboration on complex insurance policies?"

"Surematics provides a collaborative online workspace that uses blockchain technology to allow multiple parties to structure high-value commercial insurance deals—such as coverage for oil rigs—and generate enforceable contracts."
