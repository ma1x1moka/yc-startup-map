**Website:** https://www.sutrix.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sutrix

Sutrix is the data infrastructure helping healthcare providers prepare and license clinical data to pharma companies and AI labs. Sutrix has reached $20k MRR within 2 months helping research teams continuously prepare patient data for their studies. Now, it is extending that software to help providers turn their own records into longitudinal datasets capturing patient journeys, treatment decisions, and outcomes. It is brothers from Georgia Tech who built HSS's injury surveillance system (700+ teams), with experience in clinical research at Emory and engineering at Amazon.
