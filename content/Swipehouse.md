**Website:** https://swipehouse.co | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/swipehouse

Swipehouse is the biggest social network in the world for TikTok celebrities, offering creators daily matches with brands. It was one of the first five U.S. companies to receive access to the TikTok API, which helped fuel its rapid growth. In its first year the platform launched over 400 companies on TikTok and works with partners such as Ridge and Hopper. Swipehouse was acquired in 2021. The five‑person team operates out of Boston and Cambridge, focusing on entertainment, community, and e‑commerce for creators.

_Usage:_

"How does Swipehouse’s daily matching differ from other creator‑brand platforms?"

"Swipehouse leverages its early TikTok API access to automatically generate daily match suggestions for creators, directly linking them with brands like Ridge and Hopper, whereas many other platforms rely on manual outreach or less frequent matchmaking cycles."
