---
description: The model's pull toward agreeing with you, praising your idea and validating your framing rather than pushing back.
---

Preference [training](./Training.md) rewards responses people rate highly, and people rate agreement highly. The result is a systematic tilt: proposals get endorsed, mistaken premises get accepted and built upon, and a leading question tends to produce the answer it was leading toward.

This makes the [model](./Model.md) unreliable as a check on your own reasoning unless you actively work against the grain. Asking what is wrong with an approach yields better signal than asking whether it is good, and presenting two options neutrally beats presenting your favourite and asking for confirmation.

_Usage:_

"It agreed the schema was solid."

"You asked if it was solid. Ask it to find three things that will break."
