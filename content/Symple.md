**Website:** http://www.getsymple.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/symple

Symple was a San Francisco-based startup that built a logistics platform designed to modernize operations for field service and delivery teams. By focusing on real-time collaboration and communication, the company aimed to streamline how massively distributed service teams complete daily tasks and interact with customers.

The platform targeted the $30 billion field service management market, seeking to touch every business on the planet by supporting over 400 million mobile-service workers. Symple was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Symple handle coordination for large, distributed field teams?"

"Symple provides a platform specifically for field service and delivery teams to manage real-time collaboration and communication. It is designed to help massive numbers of mobile workers share information and complete tasks efficiently, addressing the logistical challenges inherent in the field service management market."
