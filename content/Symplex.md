**YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/symplex

Symplex is an applied reinforcement learning research laboratory that builds AI assistants and data engineering solutions. Founded in Summer 2019, it operates out of New York City and focuses on reinforcement learning to improve AI assistant performance.

The company was acquired by Scale AI, signaling the value of its research. With a team of eight, Symplex delivers B2B AI solutions that leverage reinforcement learning for data engineering tasks.
