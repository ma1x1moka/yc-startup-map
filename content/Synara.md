Security cameras don't stop crime. They film it. Traditional monitoring does one job: watching an empty site after hours. It misses anything more complex, and anything that happens during the day. When something does happen, it sends an alert and leaves the response to you.

Synara is the AI monitoring station for the cameras that are already installed. It needs no new hardware and watches day and night. It understands far more than intruders: falls, guns, fights. And it owns the response. Synara verifies incidents in seconds, talks intruders down on site or sends police a verified alarm, then coordinates with officers on who's on site and where people are safe, until the insurance claim is resolved.

Because AI does the watching, monitoring becomes drastically cheaper and more accurate. Synara sells through security integrators under their own brand, so every camera they've installed becomes recurring revenue.
