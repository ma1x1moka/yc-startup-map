**Website:** https://syncly.app/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/syncly

Syncly develops agentic AI tools designed to automate video marketing workflows for large consumer packaged goods (CPG) brands. The company targets a market where brands currently spend hundreds of billions of dollars on agencies, offering an alternative to traditional manual processes. Notable clients include Tiffany and Clorox.

The startup is backed by Y Combinator, SoftBank, and John Kim, the CEO of Sendbird. Syncly has achieved $2.1M in annual recurring revenue, with growth rates of 2x in six months and 4x in twelve months, alongside a 142% net dollar retention rate. The team of 15 is based in San Francisco, California.

_Usage:_

"How does Syncly differ from standard social listening tools?"

"While traditional social listening tools focus on monitoring and analyzing content, Syncly uses agentic AI to actively automate the creation and management of video marketing. It is built specifically for CPG brands looking to reduce reliance on expensive agency spend for video production and distribution."
