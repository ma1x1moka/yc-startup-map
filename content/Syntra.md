**Website:** https://www.syntra.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/syntra

Syntra is a San Francisco-based startup focused on making healthcare faster by automating the financial operations of medical practices. Its flagship product, Syntra Autonomous, handles pre-bill chart review, claim review, and submission, streamlining the revenue cycle for providers.

Since its launch, the platform has processed over one million patient encounters and facilitated more than $200 million in insurance collections across hundreds of practice locations nationwide. The company operates with a team of ten employees and is categorized within the healthcare IT and fintech sectors.
