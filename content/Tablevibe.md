**Website:** https://www.tablevibe.co | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tablevibe-co

Tablevibe provides software that enables restaurants to launch their own online ordering stores, allowing them to sell directly to customers rather than relying on third-party platforms. The company claims this approach can reduce costs by up to 80% compared to traditional delivery apps while giving restaurants full ownership of their customer data.

The platform includes automated rider booking for on-demand delivery at low, distance-based rates. Founded in Summer 2021, the team is based in Singapore and operates remotely. Tablevibe was acquired, marking the end of its independent operation.

_Usage:_

"How does Tablevibe handle delivery logistics compared to using a standard third-party app?"

"Instead of paying high commissions to a delivery app, Tablevibe lets the restaurant run its own store and automatically books riders for on-demand delivery. It uses low, distance-based rates for these rides, which the company says can cut costs by up to 80% compared to traditional delivery apps while keeping customer data in-house."
