Taktile empowers financial institutions to unlock AI‑driven efficiencies across the customer lifecycle, reducing manual work while optimizing decisions and enabling seamless end‑user experiences.

Built for highly regulated environments, its agentic decision platform merges the speed of AI automation with human oversight, enabling safe customer approvals, fraud detection, and compliance.

With offices in NYC, London, Berlin, and Iasi, the company has raised $80M from investors such as Index Ventures, Tiger Global, Balderton Capital, and Y Combinator, and serves over 200 leading financial institutions across onboarding, credit, AML, compliance, and fraud.
