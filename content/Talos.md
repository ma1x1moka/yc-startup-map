Talos builds an early-warning system for power grid and utility infrastructure by putting retrofittable sensors on critical equipment to detect catastrophic failures early.

Power outages cost Americans over $120 billion last year. With the rising necessity of compute, data centers are being added to the grid at record pace, doubling their electricity demand in the past five years. The additional demand is overstressing a grid operating past its intended life span.

Utilities need insights not just for specific assets, but across their entire plant. Power plants especially are built around an intertwined chain of assets: any issue in one could affect all others. Talos is an all-in-one system built to monitor a variety of equipment such as transformers, turbines, pumps, generators, poles, and more. Talos's system tracks changes throughout a plant, allowing us to correlate issues between assets and providing engineers with more insights into an issue.
