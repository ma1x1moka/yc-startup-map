Talyn Air develops long-range electric vertical take-off and landing (eVTOL) aircraft designed specifically for cargo logistics. The company achieves fixed-wing performance with VTOL capability by using an electric fixed-wing aircraft that is caught mid-air by a custom winged drone, allowing for vertical take-off and landing.

Based in Los Angeles, California, the company operates in the industrials and aviation sectors with a team of 18. Talyn Air was acquired, marking a significant milestone for its innovative approach to autonomous cargo transport.

_Usage:_

"How does Talyn Air achieve vertical take-off and landing if it's a fixed-wing aircraft?"

"It uses a two-part system where a custom winged drone catches the electric fixed-wing aircraft mid-air, enabling it to take off and land vertically while maintaining the efficiency of fixed-wing flight for the long-range portion of the journey."
