Tarjimly is a YC‑backed nonprofit that builds language‑access infrastructure for navigating critical life moments across healthcare, legal, and social services. The platform connects users with translation resources to overcome language barriers in essential services. In 2025, Tarjimly was acquired by CLEAR Global, highlighting its impact and growth within the nonprofit and social‑impact space.

The organization operates with a team of about 20 people located in San Francisco and remotely, and it continues to serve as a marketplace for language assistance in critical contexts.

_Usage:_

"How is Tarjimly different from a generic translation app like Google Translate?"

"Tarjimly focuses on providing language‑access infrastructure for critical moments in healthcare, legal, and social services, rather than offering general‑purpose machine translation. Its nonprofit model and integration with service providers aim to ensure reliable, context‑specific translation where it matters most."
