**Website:** https://joinTaro.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/taro

Taro operates as a community of over 150,000 software engineers focused on career growth, serving as a talent pipeline for high-growth technology companies. The platform combines an education component, featuring online courses and discussions with leaders at top tech firms, with direct hiring support that includes sourcing and feedback on candidates.

Based in San Francisco, Taro was acquired, marking a significant milestone for this YC Summer 2022 startup. Its approach distinguishes itself by leveraging educational content to attract engineers who are actively seeking their next career move, thereby helping startups expand their engineering teams quickly without compromising on quality.

_Usage:_

"How does Taro differ from traditional recruiting agencies or job boards?"

"Taro functions less like a passive job board and more like an active talent community. It uses an education platform with courses and discussions led by tech leaders to engage and retain a large pool of software engineers. This engagement allows the company to provide sourcing and specific feedback on candidates, helping high-growth tech companies identify engineers who are not just available, but actively looking to grow their careers and ready to contribute immediately."
