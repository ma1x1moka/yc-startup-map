**Website:** https://www.tarsal.co/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tarsal

Tarsal built a data pipeline custom-designed for security teams, addressing the rapid growth of security data which expands by 25% year over year. The company aimed to bridge the gap between the modern data stack and security operations, pioneering what it called the modern security data stack.

Founded in Summer 2021, Tarsal operated as a B2B cybersecurity company with a team of roughly 10 people based in New York City and San Francisco. The startup focused on providing best-in-class data infrastructure to help security teams handle their expanding data requirements.

Tarsal has since been acquired, marking the end of its independent operations as a YC-backed startup.

_Usage:_

"Why would a security team need a specialized data pipeline instead of just using a standard big data tool?"

"Security data grows at a rate of 25% year over year, which creates a specific infrastructure challenge that general-purpose tools may not address optimally. Tarsal was built to bridge the gap between the modern data stack and security teams, providing a custom pipeline designed to handle this specific volume and type of data."
