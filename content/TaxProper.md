**Website:** https://taxproper.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/taxproper

TaxProper is an enterprise SaaS platform that acquires real-time property tax data, encodes local tax rules, and bundles the operational workflows required for property tax management.

Its core products automate tax payments, forecast future liability, and support large real-estate operators in managing appeals.

The company, based in Chicago, had a team of eight and was acquired, underscoring its value to the real-estate and construction sectors.

_Usage:_

"How does TaxProper handle local tax rule variations across different jurisdictions?"

"TaxProper encodes local tax rules into its system, allowing the platform to automatically apply the correct regulations when processing payments, forecasting, and appeals for each property."
