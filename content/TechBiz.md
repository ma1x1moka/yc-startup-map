**Website:** https://www.techbiz.app | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/techbiz

TechBiz provides an app-first Management Information System (MIS) and Business Intelligence solution designed specifically for small and medium enterprises in India. The platform addresses the challenge of business data being trapped in hard-to-access accounting software by allowing users to track essential metrics such as debtors, creditors, sales, and outstanding balances directly on mobile devices.

The company operates as a B2B SaaS provider with a small team of three members based in New Delhi and remote locations. By simplifying access to financial data, TechBiz helps business owners make quicker and more informed decisions. The company has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does TechBiz handle the data integration problem for Indian SMEs who rely on legacy accounting software?"

"It acts as an app-first layer on top of existing accounting systems, pulling out key metrics like debtors, creditors, and sales into a mobile interface. Instead of forcing users to dig through complex desktop accounting tools, it surfaces the essential business data needed for quick decision-making directly on their phones."
