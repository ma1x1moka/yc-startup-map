**Website:** https://www.techmate.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/techmate

Techmate operates as a technology concierge, offering on-demand tech support for small businesses, startups, and consumers. The service focuses on removing the frustration from technical issues by providing friendly, trusted technicians who can be booked quickly with transparent pricing.

Users can access either onsite or virtual assistance, with support available in as little as two hours. The company is tagged with IoT, Customer Support, and Home Automation, indicating a focus on managing connected devices and household technology.

Techmate was based in New York City and had a team of 11 members before being acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Techmate differ from traditional IT help desks or general consumer electronics stores?"

"Techmate acts as a specialized concierge rather than a generic repair shop, focusing on rapid deployment of trusted technicians for both virtual and onsite issues. It targets the specific pain point of scheduling and trust by guaranteeing support in as little as two hours with transparent pricing, which is distinct from the often opaque or slow processes of traditional IT support or retail repair counters."
