**Website:** http://www.teleo.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/teleo

Teleo converts heavy equipment such as dozers, trucks, and loaders into supervised autonomous robots. The company enables a single operator to remotely manage multiple machines from a remote control center, a model designed to unlock productivity gains for general contractors. Based in San Francisco and Palo Alto, the company operated with a team of 30 and focused on the industrials, manufacturing, and robotics sectors. Teleo has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Teleo's supervised autonomy model differ from fully autonomous systems in construction?"

"Instead of requiring each machine to operate independently without human oversight, Teleo’s system allows one operator to remotely control multiple heavy equipment units from a central location. This setup is intended to increase productivity for general contractors by maximizing the utilization of both the machinery and the operator."
