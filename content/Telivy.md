**Website:** https://www.telivy.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/telivy

Telivy is a San Francisco-based startup that helps small and medium businesses navigate the complex landscape of cyber insurance. It utilizes a proprietary machine learning platform to assess insurability gaps and generate specific remediation plans for companies looking to improve their security posture.

By analyzing these risks, the platform brokers insurance quotes from A+ rated carriers, streamlining the purchasing process for SMBs. The company was acquired, marking a significant exit for this security-focused venture from the Summer 2021 batch.

_Usage:_

"How does Telivy determine which insurance quotes are actually viable for a specific SMB?"

"It uses a proprietary ML-based risk platform to assess the company's current security posture and identify insurability gaps. Instead of just listing policies, it pairs these assessments with remediation plans to address weaknesses, then brokers quotes specifically from A+ rated carriers that align with the improved risk profile."
