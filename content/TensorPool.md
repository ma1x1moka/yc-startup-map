**Website:** https://tensorpool.dev | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tensorpool

TensorPool is a San Francisco‑based B2B SaaS that lets developers run ML training jobs via a simple CLI. Users describe their job and the service handles GPU orchestration and execution, aiming to cut costs. The platform positions itself as a Vercel for GPUs, targeting infrastructure and DevOps workflows. Its focus on AIOps and cloud computing lets teams manage GPU workloads without manual provisioning. TensorPool was acquired after its Winter 2025 batch, highlighting its perceived value in the AI infrastructure space.

_Usage:_

"How does TensorPool’s CLI reduce GPU training costs compared to using a major cloud provider?"

"The CLI abstracts GPU orchestration and execution, automatically provisioning and pooling GPU resources so teams avoid the overhead and pricing premiums of individual cloud instances, which the company says results in roughly half the cost."
