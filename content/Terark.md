**Website:** http://www.terark.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/terark

Terark developed a storage engine for databases and data systems designed to handle big data workloads. Its technology enables direct search capabilities on heavily compressed data, delivering up to 200X faster read performance and over 10X storage savings compared to engines like Google's LevelDB and Facebook's RocksDB.

Based in Beijing, the company targeted large-scale data applications looking to lower costs while increasing performance. Terark participated in the Winter 2017 Y Combinator batch, counted Alibaba among its paying customers, and has since been acquired.
