Termii is an AI-native reliability infrastructure that stops transaction failures across logins, payments, checkouts, fraud alerts, and OTPs. It uses AI to detect and correct issues before they reach users.

The platform processes over 3B+ transactions and serves more than 16M end-users in banks, fintechs, e-commerce and digital platforms worldwide.

With a team of 63 engineers based in Lagos, LA, and Nigeria, Termii operates as a B2B infrastructure provider, offering developer tools and SMS integration.

_Usage:_

"How does Termii's AI-native reliability infrastructure differ from traditional transaction monitoring tools?"

"Unlike rule‑based monitoring that only flags issues after they occur, Termii uses AI to proactively prevent failures in real‑time across logins, payments, checkouts, fraud alerts and OTPs, processing billions of transactions and serving millions of end‑users."
