**Website:** https://velostics.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/terusama

Terusama provides a scheduling platform that enables warehouses to coordinate truck deliveries efficiently, focusing on B2B supply chain and logistics operations. The service streamlines the process of assigning trucks to loading docks and managing delivery windows.

Based in San Francisco with a two‑person team, Terusama was acquired after its Winter 2020 Y Combinator batch, highlighting the market interest in its logistics scheduling solution.

_Usage:_

"How is Terusama’s truck‑scheduling tool different from using a generic spreadsheet or existing logistics software?"

"Terusama offers a purpose‑built platform specifically for warehouse truck scheduling, so it includes features like real‑time dock availability and automated slot assignment that generic spreadsheets lack, and it’s tailored to the logistics workflow rather than being a broad‑scope logistics suite."
