**Website:** https://asktetra.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tetra

Tetra uses AI to automatically generate notes from phone calls, helping users stay focused, remember details, and keep their teams in sync. The service captures conversation content and produces concise summaries that can be shared across B2B workflows.

Tetra was acquired, highlighting its impact in the AI‑driven productivity space.

_Usage:_

"How is Tetra different from a regular call transcription tool?"

"Tetra not only transcribes the call but also distills the conversation into concise notes that highlight key details and can be instantly shared with a team, whereas a standard transcription service only provides raw text."
