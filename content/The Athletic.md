**Website:** https://theathletic.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/the-athletic

The Athletic is a subscription-based digital sports media company that produces in-depth articles, podcasts, and premium video content. Founded in 2016 and headquartered in San Francisco, it covers more than 250 professional sports and collegiate teams across the US, Canada, and the UK. The company operates with over 600 full-time employees and maintains a remote-friendly culture with offices in San Francisco, Los Angeles, London, and Melbourne.

The Athletic was acquired, marking a significant milestone for its business model. It has built a substantial library of content, including thousands of long-form articles and over 120 podcasts, focusing on deep storytelling for its subscriber base.

_Usage:_

"What is the core business model of The Athletic?"

"The Athletic operates on a direct-to-consumer subscription model, selling access to its digital sports media platform. It generates revenue by providing subscribers with in-depth articles, podcasts, and video content covering a wide range of professional and collegiate teams, rather than relying on traditional advertising or syndication."
