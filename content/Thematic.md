Thematic provides a customer intelligence layer designed specifically for enterprise customer experience teams. It focuses on delivering trustworthy and traceable reporting that allows organizations to make high-stakes decisions based on solid data. The platform also serves as a grounded data foundation, enabling AI agents to scale safely within existing business operations.

The company reports significant performance improvements for its users, including NPS gains of 5 to 35 points. In one specific case, a customer attributed $4.8M in revenue to a six-month pilot. Independent analysis by Forrester measured a three-year ROI of 543% for the platform.

Thematic is a B2B SaaS company with a team of 20 people, operating remotely and out of Auckland, New Zealand. It is trusted by major enterprise clients such as DoorDash, Woolworths, BNY, and Mitsubishi.

"How does Thematic differ from standard analytics dashboards when it comes to integrating with AI agents?"

"Unlike traditional dashboards that often provide opaque metrics, Thematic emphasizes traceability and grounding. It acts as a data foundation specifically structured so that AI agents can operate safely and reliably, ensuring that the insights generated are trustworthy enough for enterprise decision-making."
