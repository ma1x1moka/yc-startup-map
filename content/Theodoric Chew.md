Co-founded [Intellect](./Intellect.md) as CEO in 2020 with [Anurag Chutani](./Anurag%20Chutani.md), building a mental healthcare system for global workforces and payors that went through YC's Summer 2021 batch. Intellect has grown into the global leader in hyperlocalized mental healthcare, serving 4 million lives across enterprise employers, insurers, healthcare systems, and governments in over 50 languages and 100 countries. Chew was named to the World Economic Forum's Young Global Leaders Class of 2026.
