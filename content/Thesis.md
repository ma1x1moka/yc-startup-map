Thesis is an AI research lab automating AI R&D itself. Building Darwin, a hill-climbing machine that searches vast combinatorial spaces of experiments. By using the outcome of every experiment to optimize the next, Darwin turns ML research into a compounding system rather than a sequence of manual attempts.

Directed toward the grand challenges of biology and materials science. Mission: build the machine that discovers the next Transformer, the next AlphaFold, and the breakthroughs that push science and engineering to the absolute frontier.
