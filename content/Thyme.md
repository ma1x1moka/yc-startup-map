**Website:** https://www.meetthyme.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/thyme

Thyme serves as the operating system for financial advisors, replacing legacy CRMs and billing solutions with a unified platform. It automatically captures notes from in-person, online, and phone meetings while using AI to manage client records and provide rich context to advisors.

The company also offers a client portal that allows clients to track assets, manage tasks, and pay for services directly. Thyme was acquired, marking a significant validation of its approach to modernizing the financial advisory workflow.

_Usage:_

"How does Thyme handle the administrative overhead for financial advisors compared to traditional CRMs?"

"Thyme automates the manual process of taking notes during client meetings across all channels and uses AI to organize client records, effectively replacing separate CRM and billing tools with a single system that also includes a client-facing portal."
