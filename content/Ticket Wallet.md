**Website:** https://www.ticketwallet.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ticket-wallet

Ticket Wallet is an AI-powered resale platform that automates pricing, listing, and distribution for season ticket holders and brokers across all major marketplaces. By replacing spreadsheets and manual guesswork, it streamlines the resale process and reduces the need for separate listings on each site.
Ticket Wallet has onboarded more than $3 million in ticket inventory, signed up over 100 paying sellers, and begun working with professional teams. It charges a 10 % fee per ticket sold, positioning it as a competitive alternative to other platforms that impose higher costs.

_Usage:_

"How does Ticket Wallet's AI pricing work compared to traditional spreadsheet methods?"

"Ticket Wallet replaces manual spreadsheets and guesswork by automatically setting optimal prices for each ticket, then listing and distributing them across all major marketplaces in a single workflow, giving sellers a more efficient and cost-effective resale experience."
