**Website:** http://tigereye.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tigereye

TigerEye provides an AI-powered SaaS platform designed to streamline operations within the construction industry. By automating and simplifying complex workflows, the company helps construction professionals focus on building essential infrastructure rather than getting bogged down in administrative tasks.

The company was founded in the Summer 2022 batch and operated with a team of approximately 30 people, split between San Francisco and remote locations. It was notably recognized for its application of artificial intelligence to real estate and construction sectors.

TigerEye has since been acquired, marking a significant exit for the startup.

_Usage:_

"How does TigerEye handle the specific pain points of construction project management compared to traditional project management software?"

"TigerEye uses AI to eliminate the administrative 'bullsh*t' that typically distracts construction professionals, allowing them to focus on the actual building process. Unlike generic tools, it is specifically tailored to the Real Estate and Construction industry to reduce friction in daily operations."
