Tire Swing (YC F26) is an AI-powered leasing and eligibility platform for Multifamily rentals, starting with Affordable Housing. Tire Swing enables increased revenues through faster move-ins and decreased labor costs, which are especially high in Affordable Housing.

Tire Swing signeds five customers in the last 3 months growing from zero to 100k ARR. Two of these are among the largest affordable housing developers in the country, and it is actively rolling out to their combined 35k units. Upon fully ramping to all its customers, it would be at $1M ARR.

Tire Swing is co-founded by two college friends who were both Princeton CS engineers. Lucas drove $100M+ GTV through optimizing the home page of Instacart, Paul led cybersecurity investigations into attacks perpetrated by Russian and Chinese state actors.
