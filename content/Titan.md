Titan is an award-winning wealth manager that aims to boost the next generation’s compound growth rate. It puts a human wealth advisor in every pocket, making wealth management accessible beyond the ultra‑wealthy.

The company leverages frontier technologies such as artificial intelligence to power a platform that gives advisors superpowers, allowing them to serve clients they historically never could.

Backed by a16z, General Catalyst, Sam Altman, and other investors, Titan manages over $1 billion in assets for clients nationwide and employs a 40‑person team based in New York City.
