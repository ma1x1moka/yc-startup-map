Chose open source as the distribution strategy — the Metorial runtime is open-source, with the hosted enterprise platform as the revenue layer. The Vercel analogy he uses is precise: Vercel didn't invent deployment, it made Next.js reliable at enterprise scale. Metorial does the same for MCP.

Austria-based founding team in a Valley-dominated ecosystem: the geographic distance from the YC bubble is sometimes cited as a source of product focus rather than distraction.
