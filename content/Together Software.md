**Website:** https://togetherplatform.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/together-software

Together targets companies with 500+ employees, enabling Learning and Development teams to run best-practice mentoring programs. The platform captures each employee’s development areas and goals, matches them with the most relevant mentor within the organization, and guides and tracks the mentoring relationships. 
Together integrates with Outlook, Gsuite and other enterprise tools to streamline communication and scheduling for professional users. The company was acquired, marking its recognition as a valuable tool for large enterprises.

_Usage:_

"How does Together match employees with mentors?"

"Together uses the employee’s captured development areas and goals to find the most relevant mentor within the company, then guides and tracks the relationship, integrating with Outlook and Gsuite for scheduling and communication."
