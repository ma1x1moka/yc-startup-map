**Website:** https://www.togethervideoapp.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/together-video-chat

Together Video Chat transforms standard video calls for children and adults by integrating educational activities, books, and interactive games directly into the conversation. The platform allows users to play together while chatting, enhancing the social experience with structured engagement.

The company, based in Barcelona and operating remotely, was acquired after its Summer 2020 Y Combinator batch. It focused on the consumer and home sectors, targeting the education and entertainment markets with its interactive video solution.

_Usage:_

"How does Together differ from standard video conferencing tools like Zoom or FaceTime for family use?"

"Unlike standard tools that only provide a video feed, Together embeds educational activities, books, and interactive games directly into the call interface. This allows kids and grown-ups to play and learn collaboratively during the chat rather than just talking, making it a more engaging environment for family interactions."
