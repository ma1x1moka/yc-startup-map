Co-founded [Sable](./Sable.md) in 2019 with [Naveen Qureshi](./Naveen%20Qureshi.md), Andrej Paule, and [Joseph Finlayson](./Joseph%20Finlayson.md), meeting his co-founders at Columbia Business School. Wilen studied at the University of Texas at Austin before earning his MBA at Columbia, and helped build Sable's banking product for internationals without US credit history.
