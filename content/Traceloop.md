**Website:** https://www.traceloop.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/traceloop

Traceloop offers full visibility into Large Language Model (LLM) operations by tracking every prompt, response, and failure. The platform allows developers to monitor quality, detect drift, and prevent hallucinations from reaching end users. It is powered by OpenTelemetry and integrates into existing stacks with a single line of code.

Founded in Winter 2023, the company operates as a B2B SaaS provider with a team of nine based in Tel Aviv, Israel. Traceloop was acquired, marking its exit from the independent startup phase.

_Usage:_

"How does Traceloop handle observability for LLM apps compared to standard APM tools?"

"It leverages OpenTelemetry to provide specific visibility into LLM interactions, such as prompts and responses, rather than just general application metrics. This allows you to track quality and detect drift or hallucinations directly within the LLM workflow, integrating via a single line of code."
