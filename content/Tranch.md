**Website:** https://tranch.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tranch

Tranch is an invoice-to-payments platform designed to help enterprises optimize cash flow and accelerate accounts receivable. Operating out of London and New York City, the company provides automated payment workflows tailored for services firms, software providers, and marketplaces.

Its platform supports a variety of payment methods, including Pay Now via FedNow, Pay by Card, Pay by Crypto, and Pay Later options ranging from 2 to 12 months for amounts up to $1 million. Tranch participated in the Y Combinator Summer 2022 batch and raised backing from investors including Global Founders Capital, TracVC, UpHonest Capital, Soma Capital, and FoundersX.

Tranch has since been acquired.
