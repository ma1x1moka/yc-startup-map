TransAstra is a Los Angeles‑based company that has issued five patents and has more than a dozen pending, generating over $5 million in revenue.
Its flagship Omnivore propulsion system powers spacecraft such as the Worker Bee commercial OTV using concentrated sunlight, making it faster, cheaper, and more practical than electric propulsion while being safer and more operationally flexible than legacy chemical systems.
The Sutter Telescope System locates resource‑rich asteroids and orbital debris that existing technologies cannot find, and it can identify dark, fast‑moving objects between Earth and the Moon—an issue that national defense and the Space Force consider critical. Ground‑based observatories already use Sutter, and the company will begin commercial sales of Sutter SDA data next month after tracking dozens of faint moving objects. Once launched, the telescope will be 300,000 times more cost‑effective than any other known design, revolutionizing sense‑making in space.
