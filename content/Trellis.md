**Website:** https://trellistech.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/trellistech

Trellis is building a hospitality platform where AI agents operate short-term rental properties rather than just helping guests find them. The company targets property managers by replacing fragmented tools for guest messaging, tasks, cleaning, maintenance, and owner updates with a unified agent-driven system.

Based in San Francisco, the five-person team has grown to $500k ARR across 35 customers and 3,000 listings in two months. The company reports 15% week-over-week growth and notes that at least one customer is already running day-to-day operations with no human involvement.

_Usage:_

"How does Trellis handle the operational complexity of short-term rentals compared to traditional property management software?"

"Instead of providing a dashboard for humans to manage tasks, Trellis uses agents to execute the operations end-to-end. This means the system directly handles guest messages, coordinates cleaners and maintenance vendors, and updates owners, effectively replacing the fragmented stack of manual tools with autonomous workflows."
