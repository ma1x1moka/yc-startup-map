**Website:** http://trendpop.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/trendpop

Trendpop is a data-first analytics and insights platform designed to help users understand and leverage the recommendation algorithms that drive virality on short-form video platforms. The company’s core engine has collected and analyzed over a billion social video posts using state-of-the-art algorithms to identify emerging trends as they happen.

Founded in August 2020, Trendpop grew to profitability within twelve months of its YC W21 batch. In January 2022, the company was acquired by Collab, a veteran digital creator platform and official TikTok creative and marketing partner.

Following the acquisition, Trendpop powers TikTok marketing programs for major North American record labels and is expanding into the film, television, beauty, and DTC eCommerce industries. It is currently used by marketing and A&R professionals in the music industry, as well as hundreds of brands and creators, to consistently win on social video.

_Usage:_

"How does Trendpop actually predict what will go viral instead of just showing past data?"

"It doesn't just look at historical metrics; its core engine ingests over a billion social video posts and applies state-of-the-art algorithms to reverse-engineer the recommendation systems that power virality. This allows it to surface trends in real-time, helping users like music labels and brands understand the specific mechanics driving current engagement on platforms like TikTok."
