**Website:** https://trigodata.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/trigo

Trigo aggregates consumer rent history to help landlords approve better tenants and lenders write more loans. As a Consumer Reporting Agency, it delivers this missing rent dataset through a real‑time API and web application, filling a gap that no existing solution consistently furnishes. The team, with experience from Harvard, Citigroup, Experian and Amazon, built the platform in New York City and remotely, and the company was acquired after its Winter 2023 batch.

_Usage:_

"How does Trigo’s rent‑verification API improve coverage compared to the largest existing database?"

"Trigo’s API aggregates consumer rent history to provide real‑time access, addressing the lack of consistent rent verification solutions; the largest database covers only 3% of rent data, so Trigo’s coverage is substantially higher."
