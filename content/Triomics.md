**Website:** https://triomics.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/triomics

Triomics builds AI infrastructure for modern oncology by replacing manual staff work with task-driven AI agents embedded directly into clinical workflows. The platform automatically processes unstructured patient records, including pathology reports, clinical notes, genomic panels, and scanned faxes, in real time to support clinical trial matching, visit preparation, and quality reporting.

The New York City-based company serves a team of 50 and is trusted by four of the top 10 Best Hospitals for Cancer ranked by U.S. News, as well as several of the largest community oncology practices in the United States.

_Usage:_

"How does Triomics handle unstructured data like scanned faxes and clinical notes in oncology workflows?"

"It uses task-driven AI agents embedded directly into the EHR to automatically process these unstructured records in real time, eliminating the need for highly trained staff to manually review them for tasks like clinical trial matching and quality reporting."
