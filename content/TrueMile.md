Drivers who own their trucks join its company and earn 30% more profit, while agents run its back office so one person can now manage 9x the amount of trucks.

Companies that ship goods don't sign contracts with one-truck businesses. So these drivers contact brokers one by one, taking jobs as they become available. That's the trap. A job that looks great can strand you in a city where the next jobs pay less. TrueMile's AI negotiates brokers across 10 markets at once to weigh profit across the whole week, not just one job at a time.

Trucking is insanely hard to scale because more trucks always meant hiring more people. Now it is finally able to scale truck count without spending time, resources, and dollars hiring for the back-office.
