**Website:** https://TrueMile.AI/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/truemile

TrueMile is building an AI-native trucking company. It runs its own fleet, using agents to find freight, plan loads, and coordinate drivers and brokers.

Its goal is to reduce empty miles, improve truck utilization, and lower the cost of moving goods. Operating the fleet ourselves lets us connect each decision to its outcome on the road and improve how it works.

TrueMile is starting with human drivers. As autonomous trucks become available on its routes, it intends to put them to work within an established freight network.
