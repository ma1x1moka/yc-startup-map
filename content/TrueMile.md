**Website:** https://TrueMile.AI/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/truemile

TrueMile is building a fully autonomous logistics company, starting with trucking. Delayed pickups, empty miles and poor planning eat into a carrier's profits. Its agents coordinate loads, routes, driver schedules and fuel stops to earn more from each truck and lower the cost of moving goods.

TrueMile is starting with its own fleet so it controls execution, learn from every delivery and keep the gains. Human drivers come first. As autonomous trucks become available on its routes, it will have the customers, freight and infrastructure ready for them.

As it drives toward AGI, people will still need groceries, clothes and materials to build their homes. Someone has to move all of it. It wants every leap in AI to show up in what people pay for everyday goods.
