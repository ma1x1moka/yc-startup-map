Rocket Money, formerly known as Truebill, is a personal finance application designed to analyze user spending habits and identify financial inefficiencies. The platform offers tools to optimize spending, manage subscriptions, lower bills, and automatically set aside funds for savings goals. Since its launch in 2016, the service has saved members more than $100 million.

Headquartered in Silver Spring, Maryland, with additional offices in San Francisco, the company has grown to a team of 225 people. It was backed by Accel, Bessemer Venture Partners, Eldridge Industries, and Y Combinator. Truebill was acquired, marking a significant milestone for the YC W16 company.

_Usage:_

"How does Rocket Money actually help users save money compared to just tracking expenses?"

"It goes beyond passive tracking by actively identifying inefficiencies in your financial life. The app analyzes your spending habits to find areas where you can cut costs, such as managing recurring subscriptions or negotiating lower bills. It also automates the process of setting aside money for specific savings goals, giving you a more hands-on approach to improving your financial health rather than just viewing a dashboard of past transactions."
