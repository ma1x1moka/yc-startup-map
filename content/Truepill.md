**Website:** https://truepill.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/truepill

Truepill develops API-connected healthcare infrastructure designed to help partner organizations deliver digital patient experiences. Its platform handles the background operations for prescribing services, diagnostic lab testing, and pharmacy order fulfillment.

Part of the Y Combinator Summer 2017 batch, the company grew to a team of 1,300 employees across San Francisco, San Mateo, and remote locations. Truepill has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"What core healthcare operations can be integrated using Truepill's API?"

"Truepill provides API endpoints and infrastructure to handle three main clinical and fulfillment functions: electronic prescribing, lab testing management, and pharmacy order fulfillment."
