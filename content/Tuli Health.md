**Website:** https://tuli.health | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tuli-health

Tuli Health is a London-based startup that deploys software to turn independent pharmacies into diagnostic centers. The platform enables these locations to sell and fulfill diagnostic tests, starting with COVID-19 and expanding to other types, allowing customers to receive results within 24 hours without long travel or wait times.

By integrating with the existing pharmacy network, Tuli Health helps these businesses generate incremental revenue while improving local access to healthcare. The company aims to make every local pharmacy a diagnostic center powered by its technology.

_Usage:_

"How does Tuli Health actually enable a standard pharmacy to handle diagnostics without major infrastructure changes?"

"Tuli Health provides the software layer that allows pharmacies to sell and fulfill diagnostic tests directly. Instead of building new clinics, pharmacies use the existing Tuli platform to manage the workflow, ensuring customers get results guaranteed within 24 hours, which turns the pharmacy into a functional diagnostic center."
