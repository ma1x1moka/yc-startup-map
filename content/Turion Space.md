**Website:** https://turionspace.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/turion-space

Turion Space is developing dual-use spacecraft technology designed to move objects in space and image them when they are not being moved. The company, which operates out of Los Angeles and Irvine with a team of 15, focuses on creating a sustainable future in space by advancing affordable solutions for orbital debris removal.

The founding team, which originates from SpaceX, participated in the Y Combinator accelerator in Summer 2021. Following the program, Turion Space raised a $6.2M seed round to support its mission.

The company aims to establish a foundation by selling space domain awareness imagery data while progressing toward asteroid mining capabilities. Its first DROID satellite was scheduled to launch in early 2023.
