**Website:** https://usetusk.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tusk

Tusk is an AI agent that helps developers generate unit and integration tests with codebase context. It sits in CI and automatically provides coverage whenever a PR is raised. Engineering teams use Tusk to prevent critical regressions while increasing test coverage. The tool leverages the codebase context to produce relevant tests, reducing manual effort. Tusk was acquired in 2024. The company had a small team of four based in San Francisco and focused on B2B engineering and product design.

_Usage:_

"How does Tusk integrate with existing CI pipelines?"

"Tusk sits in CI and automatically provides coverage whenever a PR is raised, hooking into the pull request workflow to run its test generation during the CI run."
