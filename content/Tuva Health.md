**Website:** https://tuvahealth.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tuva-health

Tuva Health is a Y Combinator W22 company based in Salt Lake City that develops open source software to clean and transform messy healthcare data. The company operates on the premise that data transformation and quality are fundamental barriers preventing the healthcare industry from building scalable data products.

With a small team of three, Tuva Health aims to become the open standard for healthcare data transformation. Beyond simple data cleaning, the company seeks to build a data network for multi-site benchmarking, research, and machine learning on top of this open standard.

_Usage:_

"How does Tuva Health approach the problem of messy healthcare data compared to proprietary ETL tools?"

"Tuva Health uses an open source model to standardize the cleaning and transformation of healthcare data, aiming to create an industry-wide open standard rather than a proprietary silo. This approach is designed to facilitate multi-site benchmarking and machine learning by ensuring data quality across different healthcare organizations."
