Founded [TensorPool](./TensorPool.md) with [Joshua Martinez](./Joshua%20Martinez.md) and Hlumelo Notshe, whom he met freshman year at Stanford on the same dorm floor — all three were still students when they started. TensorPool lets developers describe ML training jobs via CLI, and it manages GPU allocation across multiple cloud providers automatically.

_Usage:_

"What does TensorPool actually do?"

"It's a command-line tool that takes a description of an ML training job and automatically allocates the right GPU resources across multiple cloud providers, so developers don't have to shop for compute manually."
