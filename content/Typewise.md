**Website:** https://typewise.app | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/typewise

Typewise is an AI-first customer service platform designed to boost productivity by allowing orchestrated agents to resolve requests end-to-end. Instead of relying on flowcharts or code, teams describe desired outcomes in natural language, which the platform compiles into working automations. This approach enables users to safely simulate changes and roll them out step by step.

The company is notable for its ability to cut support effort by over 50% while increasing CSAT and NPS for Fortune 500 brands such as Unilever and DPD. With more than 200 integrations, Typewise reads from and writes to CRM, ERP, and other systems to fully resolve cases, handing off to humans only where they add the most value. Hybrid intelligence features allow for partial hand-offs, review-before-send, and context-preserving resumption.

Based in Zürich, Switzerland, with a team of 12, Typewise operates natively within its own ticketing system or integrates with existing tools like Zendesk, Salesforce, and Freshdesk. The platform includes a company-specific AI language model to keep interactions on-brand and is EU-hosted and ISO 27001 certified.

_Usage:_

"How does Typewise handle the transition between AI automation and human support?"

"Typewise uses hybrid intelligence to manage this transition, allowing for partial hand-offs and review-before-send workflows. It can resume context-preserving interactions so that human agents stay in control, ensuring that cases are only escalated to humans when they add the most value rather than for every single ticket."
