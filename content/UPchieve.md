UPchieve is an edtech nonprofit that connects low-income high school students with free, 24/7 online tutoring. The platform addresses a significant gap in academic support by pairing students with live volunteers in under five minutes through its mobile application.

The organization targets the approximately 8 million low-income high school students in the US who lack in-home help with schoolwork. By democratizing access to academic support, UPchieve aims to provide equal opportunities for college attendance and upward mobility. To date, the platform has matched over 100,000 tutoring requests.

Based in New York City with a remote-friendly team of 15, UPchieve continues to operate as an active nonprofit focused on educational equity.

_Usage:_

"How does UPchieve handle the logistics of providing 24/7 tutoring without a massive paid staff?"

"UPchieve relies on a network of volunteers rather than paid tutors. When a student requests help through the app, the system matches them with an available live volunteer in under five minutes, allowing the nonprofit to scale its 24/7 coverage using community resources instead of a large payroll."
