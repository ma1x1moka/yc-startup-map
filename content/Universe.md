**Website:** http://onuniverse.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/universe

Universe is a website builder that works primarily on mobile devices. Its app lets anyone create a great‑looking site and start selling online in minutes from an iPhone, iPad, or Mac, and the experience is described as fun as playing with Legos. The company positions itself as the first website builder designed for phones, and it showcases examples of sites built on its platform.

Universe is a remote‑first company of about 25 employees spread across the United States. It graduated from Y Combinator’s Winter 2018 batch and has raised $17.2 million from investors including Google Ventures, General Catalyst, Javelin Venture Partners, and Box Group. The team emphasizes individual agency, freedom to work from anywhere, and a culture of rapid learning.

_Usage:_

"Can I create a full e‑commerce website using only the Universe iPhone app?"

"Yes. Universe’s mobile app lets you design a site, add products, and start selling online directly from an iPhone or iPad, so you can launch an e‑commerce store without needing a desktop computer."
