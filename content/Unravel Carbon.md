**Website:** http://unravelcarbon.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/unravel-carbon

Unravel Carbon is a leading sustainability data management platform that operates in more than 48 countries. It leverages AI and machine learning to convert any operational data into comprehensive carbon supply chain data, allowing enterprises to measure, track, and report their sustainability performance.

The platform is already used by some of the world’s largest companies and is backed by Sequoia Capital and Y Combinator.

With a team of 42 employees based in Singapore, Unravel Carbon continues to expand its reach in the B2B climate space.

_Usage:_

"How does Unravel Carbon convert operational data into carbon supply chain data?"

"Unravel Carbon uses AI and machine learning to ingest operational data, map it to carbon accounting metrics, and produce a full carbon supply chain profile that enterprises can use for measurement, tracking, and reporting."
