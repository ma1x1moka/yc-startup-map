**Website:** https://untetherlabs.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/untether-labs

Untether Labs provides scheduling software tailored for digital health clinics, aiming to improve margins and operational efficiency. After building its own clinic and identifying the technology needed to fix margin pressures, the company is now delivering that technology to other clinics.

For its first customer, Untether Labs projects $2.5M in revenue, illustrating the potential financial impact of its solution. Based in San Francisco with a six‑person team, the company focuses on helping clinics operate more effectively with limited resources.

_Usage:_

"Is Untether Labs' scheduling platform just another generic calendar app?"

"No. Untether Labs' software is purpose‑built for digital health clinics, targeting the specific margin and efficiency challenges those clinics face rather than offering a generic scheduling tool."
