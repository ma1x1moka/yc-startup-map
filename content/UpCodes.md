**Website:** https://up.codes/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/upcodes

UpCodes simplifies code compliance for architects, engineers, and construction professionals by providing a centralized, searchable library of over 6,000 state and city building codes. The company uses natural language processing to parse complex code adoptions across U.S. jurisdictions, enabling teams to navigate construction regulations more efficiently.

The platform powers the first AI-driven code-to-spec workflow, connecting regulations directly to specifications and a growing product library. It also offers an AI solution for building product manufacturers, allowing them to showcase compliant products and reach design professionals at the moment of specification.

With 800,000 monthly active users, UpCodes has demonstrated strong product-led growth in transforming how construction teams handle regulatory compliance. The company is based in Austin, Texas, and operates with a remote-friendly team of 35.
