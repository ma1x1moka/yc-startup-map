**Website:** https://www.upkeep.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/upkeep

UpKeep is a mobile-first computerized maintenance management system (CMMS) and enterprise asset management platform designed to replace traditional desktop software and paper work orders. The software enables field technicians to create and assign work orders, track lifetime asset costs, scan UPC labels, and view analytical performance graphs directly from their mobile devices.

Serving operations ranging from small businesses to large enterprises, the platform has accumulated over 2,000,000 registered users. UpKeep raised $50 million in total funding from investors including Y Combinator, Battery Ventures, Bain Capital, Emergence Capital, and Insight Partners.

Based in Los Angeles, the Winter 2017 Y Combinator company has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does UpKeep replace traditional desktop CMMS software for field technicians?"

"UpKeep provides a mobile-first system designed for technicians working directly in the field. Instead of managing paper work orders or returning to a desktop software installation, technicians can handle task assignments, scan UPC labels, track lifetime asset costs, and preview analytical graphs on mobile devices."
