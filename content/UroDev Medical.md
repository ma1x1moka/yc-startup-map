UroDev Medical, operating under the brand Spinal Singularity, develops connected medical devices designed to improve the quality of life for individuals with Spinal Cord Injury and Disease (SCI/D). The company focuses on addressing specific medical needs through technology that integrates connectivity with clinical care.

Its first product, the Connected Catheter, aims to revolutionize the management of Neurogenic Bladder and Urinary Incontinence. By targeting these conditions, the company sought to provide a more effective solution for patients dealing with the complications of spinal cord injuries.

UroDev Medical was based in San Clemente, California, and has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does the Connected Catheter differ from standard catheters in terms of functionality for spinal cord injury patients?"

"The Connected Catheter is a connected medical device specifically designed to manage Neurogenic Bladder and Urinary Incontinence in people with Spinal Cord Injury and Disease, whereas standard catheters are typically passive tools without integrated connectivity features."
