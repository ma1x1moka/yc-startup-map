Userlens is an AI customer success manager designed to help lean, AI-native companies manage enterprise deals. Its agents research accounts, catch churn before it happens, and drive renewals and upsells like a top‑tier CSM would, breaking the traditional ceiling where every $1‑5M of new revenue requires a new CSM hire.

The startup has $307K ARR and is growing 127% month over month, serving 30 customers including Smartly, a $300M ARR company. Over 300 CSMs use Userlens, and 76% of them use it weekly. The team, based in San Francisco, consists of four people and was founded by second‑time founders who have lived the problem at their last startup.
