**Website:** http://www.shopvida.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/vida-and-co

VIDA & Co. operates as a marketplace and manufacturing platform that produces unique consumer goods only after they have been purchased. By creating an interconnected network of designers, producers, and consumers, the company aims to offer an alternative to homogeneous, disposable retail items. It focuses on bringing quality and ethical production standards back into the supply chain while ensuring that only wanted items are brought into the world.

The company was based in San Francisco and worked within the consumer, apparel, and cosmetics industries. It utilized innovations in design and technology to make high-quality products more accessible. VIDA & Co. has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does VIDA's on-demand model differ from traditional retail inventory management?"

"VIDA produces items only after they are purchased, which eliminates the risk of overstocking and reduces waste compared to traditional models where goods are manufactured in bulk before demand is confirmed. This approach connects global creators directly with consumers to ensure that production aligns strictly with actual demand."
