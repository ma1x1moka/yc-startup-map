**Website:** https://vapi.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/vapi

Vapi builds voice AI APIs that let developers embed speech capabilities into their applications. The platform targets B2B engineering, product and design teams, enabling them to add voice interactions without building the underlying models.

Founded in YC’s Winter 2021 batch, Vapi operates out of San Francisco with a team of about 50 engineers. Its focus on developer-friendly APIs makes it a notable voice AI option for product teams seeking to integrate speech features quickly.

_Usage:_

"Can Vapi be used to add real-time speech transcription to a web application?"

"Yes. Vapi offers a voice AI API that developers can call from a web app to capture audio, process it in the cloud, and return transcribed text, allowing real-time speech-to-text functionality without needing to train or host their own models."
