**Website:** https://www.vectrix.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/vectrix

Vectrix provides security scanning for SaaS applications, helping organizations identify risks such as public document sharing, missing two-factor authentication, and weak password policies across platforms like Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, and Zoom. The tool allows IT and security teams to monitor their entire SaaS suite and receive real-time alerts when new security issues arise.

Founded by former security professionals from Autodesk, Vimeo, and PlanGrid, the company was based in San Francisco with a team of seven. Vectrix was acquired, marking the end of its independent operation.

_Usage:_

"How does Vectrix differ from traditional endpoint security tools?"

"Vectrix focuses specifically on the configuration and usage of cloud-based SaaS apps rather than local endpoints. It scans for issues like who has access to sensitive documents or if 2FA is disabled in Zoom, providing a view of security posture across the entire SaaS stack that traditional antivirus or endpoint detection tools do not cover."
