Vena Medical has developed a miniature camera designed for physicians to use during neurovascular procedures, positioning it as the world’s smallest of its kind. The technology’s pre‑clinical results were published in the Journal of Neuro‑Interventional Surgery and presented at the World Live Neurovascular Conference, underscoring its clinical relevance.

The company was named a top‑3 finalist for Innovator of the Year by the Congress of Neurological Surgeons and has raised a Series A round to fund commercialization, including FDA clearance, Health Canada approval, ISO 13485 certification, first‑in‑human trials, and early revenue. Vena Medical operates out of Kitchener, ON with a team of seven.

_Usage:_

"How does Vena Medical’s camera differ from the larger imaging tools typically used in neurovascular surgery?"

"Vena’s camera is marketed as the world’s smallest, allowing it to be inserted directly into vessels where larger scopes cannot reach, which can provide higher‑resolution images from inside the vasculature. The company’s pre‑clinical data, published in a peer‑reviewed journal, and its regulatory clearances support its claim of delivering minimally invasive imaging that standard larger devices cannot achieve."
