**Website:** http://www.vendr.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/vendr

Vendr built an AI negotiation agent that helps companies stop overpaying for software by using real contract data to benchmark prices and secure better deals automatically.

The platform leverages contract data to benchmark SaaS pricing and automatically negotiate better terms, reducing costs for B2B operations and procurement teams.

Vendr was acquired and operates with a team of 50 in Boston, MA, USA.
