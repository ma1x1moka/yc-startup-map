**Website:** http://verak.in | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/verak

Verak was a Bengaluru-based startup that offered business insurance to micro, small, and medium enterprises in India. The company provided next-day coverage starting at ₹8 per day, protecting facilities such as shops, warehouses, and factories against more than 15 natural and man-made perils.

The team operated across the entire insurance value chain, utilizing satellite-based underwriting and machine learning for pricing. They streamlined the user experience with instant quotations and a WhatsApp-based claims process.

Verak was acquired by InsuranceDekho in April 2023.

_Usage:_

"How did Verak handle underwriting and claims for small businesses?"

"They used satellite imagery for underwriting and machine learning to price policies, which allowed them to offer instant quotations. For claims, they relied on a WhatsApp-based process, making it accessible for MSMEs who might not have traditional insurance infrastructure."
