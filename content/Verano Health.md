**Website:** https://www.veranohealth.org/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/verano-health

Verano Health operates as a nonprofit organization building a digital diabetes platform specifically for underserved Americans on Medicaid. The company addresses a significant gap in care where less than 1% of patients with diabetes receive necessary training, despite 30% or more of the 80 million Medicaid enrollees having chronic diseases.

The company utilizes a proven 'Healthy at Home' program that relies on two-way SMS interactions, weekly coaching visits, and evidence-based health education to support patients remotely. By billing health insurance for telehealth visits, Verano Health aims to achieve financial sustainability while expanding access to at-home chronic disease management.

Verano Health was acquired, marking the end of its independent operation as a startup entity.

_Usage:_

"How does Verano Health deliver care without a traditional app or video interface?"

"It uses a 2-way SMS platform to conduct weekly coaching visits and deliver health education, which lowers the barrier to entry for Medicaid patients who may lack consistent access to smartphones or broadband, relying instead on basic text messaging infrastructure."
