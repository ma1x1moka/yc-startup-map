**Website:** https://www.verdexai.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/verdex

Verdex is an AI insurance adjuster that leverages data streams such as satellite imagery to verify claims. By automating verification, insurers can process claims immediately, which the company says helps save billions of dollars.

The service currently supports insurance for more than 11% of all American farmland, demonstrating a significant foothold in the agricultural insurance market. Verdex operates out of San Francisco with a two‑person team.

_Usage:_

"How does Verdex verify insurance claims faster than a traditional adjuster?"

"Verdex runs AI models on real‑time satellite imagery and other data streams to assess damage directly, eliminating the need for on‑site adjusters to inspect each claim. This lets insurers approve payouts instantly, cutting the processing time dramatically."
