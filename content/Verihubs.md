**Website:** https://verihubs.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/verihubs

Verihubs is an Indonesian AI company specializing in automated and instant verification platforms, with a focus on deepfake detection and face recognition technology. The company has served over 300 clients in Southeast Asia, including major financial institutions such as BCA, IFG, Commonwealth Bank, and Maybank, as well as the Ministry of Finance.

Ranked #1 in Indonesia by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) for performance and accuracy, Verihubs positions its technology as a staple for fraud prevention in the region. The company operates out of Jakarta with a team of 63 employees, aiming to address the gap in verification solutions amid the rapid digital acceleration that the pandemic accelerated by 4-7 years.
