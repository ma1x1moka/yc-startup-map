Vernius Systems, Inc. makes mass-deployable radars for unmanned systems. Its first product is Archimedes, a radar seeker that finds and locks onto targets, enabling autonomous intercept on any drone. It haves over $580M in LOIs for Archimedes, across some of the largest defense primes in Ukraine, the EU, and the Middle East.

Seekers are the most critical and costly component in every autonomous interceptor. Developing a radar seeker takes a 200+ person team at Raytheon and hundreds of millions of dollars. Archimedes is 100x cheaper than existing radar seekers on the market - and is the first product to make it feasible to deploy radars on every drone.
