Every refinery, chip fab and power plant is built from thousands of engineering documents that are supposed to agree, and engineers still keep them in sync by hand. Verno is AI for how the physical world gets built. An engineer describes a change, and Verno edits the P&ID in the native CAD file, carries the change through line lists and other downstream documents, and checks every one against engineering rules before the engineer approves it. On drawings already issued for construction, it has caught errors like one tank with three different design pressures across three documents. Verno is starting with process EPCs in oil and gas, chemicals, semiconductors, pharma, and food and beverage, then expanding into water and power.
