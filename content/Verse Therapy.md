Verse Therapy is a platform that helps speech‑language pathologists launch and grow independent practices, handling administrative tasks so therapists can focus on compassionate, personalized care. It provides thoughtful marketing, partnerships with rehab centers, and connects SLPs with individuals who need their expertise.

Its AI‑enabled back office streamlines insurance processing, scheduling, and documentation, reducing non‑therapy overhead. The service is in‑network with leading insurance providers, operates in ten states, and supports more than 35 dedicated therapists.

Verse Therapy was acquired, and it remains based in San Francisco with a team of three people.

_Usage:_

"How does Verse Therapy’s AI back‑office differ from generic practice‑management software for therapists?"

"Verse Therapy’s back office is built specifically for speech‑language pathologists and automates insurance claims, appointment scheduling, and documentation in one AI‑driven workflow, which reduces the non‑clinical workload that often leads to burnout, whereas generic tools typically require manual handling of those tasks."
