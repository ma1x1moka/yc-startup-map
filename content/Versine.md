**Website:** https://versine.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/versine

Versine lets your agents send and receive money. Versine is Venmo for agents.

Connect it to ChatGPT, Claude, Muse or your own agent through its MCP. Then send your agent a dinner receipt and it splits the bill. Tell it “if I don't run 10k tomorrow, pay Jake $10” and it checks Strava and pays out if you miss. It can also pay invoices that land in your inbox, or split rent and utilities with your roommates every month.

Recipients don't need an account: they get a link to cash out to their bank.
