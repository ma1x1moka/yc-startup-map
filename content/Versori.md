**Website:** https://www.versori.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/versori

Versori builds a platform that lets product, operations, and GTM teams create custom integrations, workflows, and agents quickly. It offers a low-code SDK and full-code options, enabling teams to deliver integrations in days instead of months. The platform includes proactive, self-healing agents that monitor connected apps for changes and automatically fix them. Versori targets B2B infrastructure customers and emphasizes limitless customization compared to embedded IPaaS solutions that lock into connector limits. Its speed to value can cut typical deployment fees by half or more, and its managed custom-builds allow sales teams to satisfy any prospect’s integration request without delays. Versori was acquired in 2023, reflecting its traction in the enterprise integration space. The company is based in Manchester, England, and employs 16 people.

_Usage:_

"How does Versori differ from traditional low-code integration platforms?"

"Versori offers proactive, self-healing agents that monitor and fix endpoint or schema changes automatically, unlike most low-code platforms that require manual updates. It also provides a flexible SDK for embedding custom integrations and full-code control, giving teams more customization than typical IPaaS solutions."
