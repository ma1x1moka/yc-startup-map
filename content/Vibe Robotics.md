Vibe Robotics builds robots for the work nobody should have to repeat.

Vibe Robotics is starting in hotels. Every day, housekeeping teams move linens between floors, restock carts, and fold stacks of laundry before the next guests arrive. It's physical work that has to get done every shift.

Its first robot, Vibe 01, folds a hand towel fully autonomously in 23.8 seconds. It learned from just a few hundred human demonstrations. It is piloting Vibe 01 at a Hilton property and working with hotels under the Marriott, Hilton, and IHG brands to bring it to more locations.
