Vibe Robotics builds robots for the work nobody should have to repeat.

Every day in every hotel, towels go up to rooms, linens move between floors, and stacks of laundry need to be folded before the next guests arrive. It's hard, physical, and hotels can't hire enough people to do it.

Vibe 01 is built for exactly that. It carries, delivers and folds. Two arms, a lift column, and a camera head on a base that drives itself through back-of-house hallways and service elevators. It learns by watching people do the job, then does it on its own. Vibe 01 can already fold a towel fully autonomously in 23.8 seconds, a skill it learned from just a few hundred human demonstrations.
