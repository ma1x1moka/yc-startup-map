**Website:** http://vidcode.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/vidcode

Vidcode provides computer science courses for tweens and teens, featuring more than three hundred self‑guided tutorials that teach web development fundamentals. The curricular approach lets students build video filters, simulations, augmented reality, and other creative projects while learning core programming concepts. Founded by female developers and artists, the company was originally built with girls in mind and focuses on increasing participation from young women and underrepresented minorities. Vidcode was acquired.

_Usage:_

"What makes Vidcode’s coding tutorials unique compared to other teen coding platforms?"

"Vidcode offers a large library of over 300 self‑guided tutorials that let teens build web‑development skills and create video filters, simulations, augmented reality, and other creative projects, all while learning fundamentals. Its curriculum is designed with girls and underrepresented minorities in mind, setting it apart from more generic coding sites."
