**Website:** https://www.viddyapp.io/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/viddy

Viddy is a New York-based B2B startup that offers a video-native approach to building landing pages. By relying on video content to handle the heavy lifting of development and design, the platform aims to reduce the cost and time associated with traditional page creation. This method allows brands to experiment more frequently with their marketing assets.

The company claims its landing pages convert 15-30% better across channels like paid ads, email, and SMS, while taking less than 20 minutes to create. This efficiency reportedly enables brands to test over 10 times more variations than they currently do. Viddy was acquired, marking the end of its independent operation.
