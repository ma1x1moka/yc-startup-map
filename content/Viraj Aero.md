Viraj Aero builds gas turbines that deliver both power and cooling water for data centers. It needs no grid connection and no water pipeline.

Burning natural gas already produces water. Its patented system captures that water from the exhaust. Part of it cools the data center. The rest is turned into steam and injected back into the turbine. That gives up to 40% more power from the same fuel (cheaper compute) and cuts NOx emissions by up to 99%, which cuts permit approvals from years to weeks.

Viraj Aero is building a genset around its first-of-its-kind gas turbine. It fits in a 10-foot container and runs anywhere, even in the desert. That matters because two-thirds of new data centers are being built in water-stressed regions.
