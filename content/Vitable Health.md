Vitable Health rethinks healthcare for startups and SMBs by offering an ICHRA platform that bundles direct primary care, free virtual visits, more than 1,000 prescriptions, and over 40 lab tests. Contributions are made with tax‑free dollars, and the company handles everything from onboarding to ongoing care so founders can focus on building.

The company is backed by world‑class investors including First Round Capital, Cherryrock, Y Combinator, and SoftBank, and has attracted talent from leaders such as Michael Seibel, Immad Akhund, Allison Pickens, Jack Altman, and Dan Folkman. With a team of 60 engineers, clinicians, and operators based in Philadelphia and remote, Vitable Health is actively expanding its digital health services.

_Usage:_

"How does Vitable Health's ICHRA integration differ from traditional health plans?"

"Vitable Health's ICHRA integration includes built‑in direct primary care with free virtual visits, prescriptions, and lab tests, and it handles onboarding and ongoing care, whereas traditional ICHRA plans typically require separate arrangements for primary care and lab services."
