**Website:** http://www.viyaliving.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/viya

Viya operated a mobile-first platform for apartment rentals in Latin America, focusing on making city-center living more affordable and convenient. The company offered room and apartment leases paired with seamless services such as cleaning, maintenance, laundry, and dry cleaning to reduce the burden on tenants.

Based in Monterrey, Mexico, the team of 22 had backgrounds from MIT and real estate, aiming to improve the rental experience for both tenants and owners through technology. At its peak, the company reported a monthly recurring revenue of $60k growing at 20% weekly with 40% margins.

Viya is now listed as inactive in the YC directory, indicating that the company has since shut down.

_Usage:_

"How did Viya differentiate itself from standard rental listings in the LatAm market?"

"Instead of just listing properties, Viya bundled the lease with essential services like cleaning, maintenance, and laundry. This approach was designed to make living in city centers more affordable and less hectic for tenants by handling the operational friction of renting, rather than just providing a space."
