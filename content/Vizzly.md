**Website:** https://vizzly.co/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/vizzly

Vizzly provides customer-facing analytics specifically designed for modern SaaS companies. The platform aims to offer the same level of flexibility as an in-house development process while requiring minimal technical overhead.

Based in London and operating remotely, the company was part of the YC Summer 2022 batch. It has since been acquired, marking a significant milestone for this small team of four focused on B2B analytics and developer tools.

_Usage:_

"How does Vizzly compare to building our own analytics dashboard from scratch?"

"Vizzly positions itself as a middle ground between rigid off-the-shelf tools and fully custom in-house development. It claims to provide the flexibility of building your own system but with significantly less technical overhead, making it a viable option for SaaS teams that need custom customer-facing data views without maintaining a dedicated engineering team for that specific feature."
