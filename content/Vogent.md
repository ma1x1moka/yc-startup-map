**Website:** https://vogent.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/vogent

Vogent provides an all-in-one platform for constructing voice agents, utilizing intelligent building blocks such as IVR detection models and custom phone-ready LLMs. The platform also features drag-and-drop conversational flow builders to streamline the development process. Based in San Francisco, the company operates in the B2B sector with a focus on sales and operations.

Vogent was acquired, marking a significant milestone in its trajectory as a developer tool for voice AI infrastructure. The acquisition highlights the growing demand for specialized tools that enable businesses to deploy sophisticated voice interactions without building the underlying infrastructure from scratch.

_Usage:_

"How does Vogent differ from building a voice agent from scratch using raw LLM APIs?"

"Vogent abstracts away the complex plumbing of voice AI by providing pre-built components like IVR detection and phone-ready LLMs, along with a drag-and-drop interface for conversational flows. Instead of engineering the entire stack from raw APIs, developers can assemble these intelligent building blocks to deploy agents faster, specifically targeting B2B sales and operations use cases."
