Volansi builds and operates high‑payload, long‑range unmanned aerial vehicles that enable B2B customers to ship goods point‑to‑point, reducing downtime costs. Its VTOL drones can carry loads up to 200 lb over distances of up to 1,000 mi, serving defense, commercial, and medical supply missions worldwide. Volansi was founded in 2015 in San Francisco and has grown to a team of 85 before being acquired, highlighting its commercial traction in the industrial and drone sectors.
