Voltic is developing electric cargo ships designed to be four times more profitable than their diesel counterparts. The company’s patented battery and solar technology stack was spun out of MIT’s Ocean Engineering department, supporting a mission to modernize maritime logistics with cleaner, more efficient vessels.

With over $100 million in sales secured from major industry players, including the largest US shipping company and the largest global shipper by volume, Voltic is positioned for its first commercial deployment in early 2026. The small team, based in New York City, brings decades of maritime innovation experience, having previously started two shipping lines.
