**Website:** http://vouch.us | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/vouch

Vouch is a technology-first insurance company that provides fast, responsive coverage for high-growth startups and innovative businesses. Backed with $185M in funding from world-class investors, it builds new technology to replace printed PDF applications and week-long wait times, offering policies that cover relevant startup scenarios and a simple user experience.

With a team of 190 employees located in Chicago and San Francisco, Vouch positions itself as the go-to business insurance for growth-stage companies, positioning itself alongside Stripe for payments and Brex for credit cards.

The company remains active, continuing to expand its offerings to meet the evolving needs of the fintech and insurance sectors.

_Usage:_

"How does Vouch differ from traditional insurance providers?"

"Vouch replaces the slow, paper‑based process of traditional insurers with a digital platform that writes policies tailored to startup scenarios, delivering coverage quickly and with a user experience designed for high‑growth companies."
