The best grid connection is no grid connection. Voxel builds vertically integrated energy generation and storage systems for data centers that bypass utility connection delays — which can take 5–10 years.

Leverages the vast supply of second-life EV batteries and a novel DC microgrid architecture to generate datacenter power on-site, drastically reducing system cost and time-to-power. Directly addresses the power bottleneck blocking AI infrastructure buildout.
