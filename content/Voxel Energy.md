**Website:** https://voxelenergy.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/voxel-energy

The best grid connection is no grid connection. Voxel builds vertically integrated energy generation and storage systems for data centers that bypass utility connection delays — which can take 5–10 years.

Leverages the vast supply of second-life EV batteries and a novel DC microgrid architecture to generate datacenter power on-site, drastically reducing system cost and time-to-power. Directly addresses the power bottleneck blocking AI infrastructure buildout.

_Usage:_

"How does Voxel’s on‑site DC microgrid using second‑life EV batteries differ from the traditional diesel generator/UPS approach for powering data centers?"

"Voxel replaces diesel generators and conventional UPS stacks with a vertically integrated system that combines on‑site generation and storage built from second‑life EV batteries wired into a DC microgrid, delivering power directly to the data center; this eliminates the 5–10 year utility connection delay and lowers overall system cost while still meeting the datacenter’s power needs."
