Voyage Biomedical developed a cooling-based technology to stop brain damage during a stroke. The company was founded in San Francisco and Berkeley and operated with a team of three.
The device is a hardware medical product in the neurotechnology space. It was acquired by Penumbra (NYSE:PEN) after its Summer 2019 batch, underscoring the value of its technology.
