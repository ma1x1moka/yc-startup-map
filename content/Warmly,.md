**Website:** https://www.warmly.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/warmly

Warmly, is an autonomous sales orchestration platform designed to identify and engage warm leads. It utilizes data from sources like 6sense, Clearbit, and Bombora to find contacts and companies showing interest in a user's website or researching them online but who have not yet converted. Once these leads are identified, the platform automatically follows up via chat, email, or LinkedIn, allowing users to set up specific workflows for AI-driven interactions while retaining the ability for human verification before messages are sent.

The company claims its autonomous approach yields ten times more outbound activity than a human SDR and three times higher reply rates due to the warmth of the leads. Warmly, also offers options for human-in-the-loop engagement, such as one-click warm intro requests or live video calls. The San Francisco-based team, which includes remote workers, served over 100 paying customers before being acquired by HubSpot in 2026.
