**Website:** https://www.warp.co/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/warp

Warp provides a single platform that runs payroll, HR, compliance, benefits, and IT for companies ranging from five to ten thousand employees, using AI agents to complete work that legacy systems leave to people.
The service is aimed at high‑growth companies that want to reduce HR and admin overhead so they can build faster. Warp counts fast‑growing startups such as Serval, Bland, Greptile, Reducto, Corgi and Campfire among its customers, as well as mid‑size and enterprise firms.

The company is backed by investors including Battery Ventures, Peak XV, Sound Ventures, Sapphire, A‑Star, SV Angel and Homebrew Capital, and has support from notable tech leaders such as Shopify CEO Tobi Lütke and Dropbox co‑founders. Warp employs about 80 people and is headquartered in New York City.

_Usage:_

"How does Warp’s AI‑agent approach differ from using separate payroll or HR SaaS tools?"

"Warp consolidates payroll, HR, compliance, benefits and IT into one platform and uses AI agents to automate the tasks that traditional SaaS tools leave for manual handling, so companies can cut admin overhead without stitching together multiple products."
