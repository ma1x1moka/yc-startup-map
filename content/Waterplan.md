**Website:** http://waterplan.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/waterplan

Waterplan operates a climate platform designed to help companies measure, respond to, and monitor their changing water risks. By combining internal operational data with local water satellite imagery, the platform provides a continuously updated financial assessment of water exposure. This approach allows multinational clients such as Coca-Cola, Diageo, Colgate, and AB InBev to visualize their specific vulnerabilities in real-time.

The company offers tailored mitigation and adaptation strategies based on these assessments, ranging from conventional infrastructure upgrades to nature-based solutions. Waterplan was founded by a team of second-time entrepreneurs with nearly a decade of experience working on water projects for Fortune 500 companies, bringing both technical and domain expertise to the challenge.

Based in San Francisco with a remote-capable team of 20, Waterplan has attracted investment from Y Combinator, Giant Ventures, and high-profile individuals including Leonardo DiCaprio and Richard Branson's family. The company is currently active and continues to serve the B2B climate tech sector.

_Usage:_

"How does Waterplan calculate water risk differently from standard environmental compliance tools?"

"Instead of relying solely on static regulatory reports, Waterplan merges a company's own operational data with live local water satellite imagery. This allows for a dynamic, continuously updated financial assessment of water risk rather than a one-time audit, enabling companies to see how their exposure changes over time and to identify specific adaptation opportunities like nature-based solutions."
