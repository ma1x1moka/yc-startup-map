Hardware teams burn weeks on procurement. An engineer needs a part. Someone emails five distributors, chases quotes, gets an approval, wires the money, tracks a shipment across three carriers, clears customs, and then nobody knows where the box is or what's actually on the shelf.

Waybill runs that entire loop. Send us a part, a spec, or a BOM. Waybill sources it, pull quotes, route approvals, pay the supplier, handle freight and customs, track it to your dock, and keep your stock count updated.
