WaystoCap is a technology‑driven trading platform focused on Africa. It helps businesses find international and regional partners, source competitive prices, secure better payment terms, and supports the entire trade process.

The company operates as a B2B marketplace backed by investors such as Battery Ventures, 4DX Ventures, and Y Combinator, and it was acquired.

WaystoCap employs 38 people and is headquartered in Casablanca, Morocco.

_Usage:_

"How is WaystoCap different from a generic B2B marketplace?"

"WaystoCap not only lists suppliers but also handles end‑to‑end trade support for African businesses, including partner discovery, price sourcing, and improved payment terms, all within a single platform."
