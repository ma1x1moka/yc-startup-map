**Website:** https://weav.co | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/weav

Weav builds digital infrastructure that links small businesses with banks and other financial institutions. Its universal API lets commerce platforms integrate payments and other financial services without building custom connections. The company was acquired after demonstrating its B2B payments API capability.

Based in New York City, Weav operated with a team of nine engineers and product designers focused on the payments and API space.

_Usage:_

"What types of businesses can use Weav’s universal API?"

"Weav’s API is designed for small businesses that need to connect their commerce platforms to financial institutions, enabling them to handle payments and related services through a single integration."
