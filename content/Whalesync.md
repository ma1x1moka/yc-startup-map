**Website:** https://www.whalesync.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/whalesync

Whalesync is an automation platform that simplifies data management by syncing content between website builders such as Webflow, Wix, and WordPress, and collaboration tools like Airtable, Notion, and Google Sheets. By enabling two-way, real-time synchronization, the company allows marketing teams to manage their websites directly from the tools they already use for daily operations.

Launched during Y Combinator’s Summer 2021 cohort, Whalesync has grown to a team of eight based in St. Petersburg, Florida, and remote locations. The platform is trusted by hundreds of companies, including Ramp, Webflow, and Alchemy, and processes millions of transactions daily to keep website content aligned with business data.

_Usage:_

"How does Whalesync differ from standard webhooks or manual content updates?"

"Whalesync provides two-way, real-time synchronization, meaning changes made in Airtable or Notion automatically update the website, and vice versa. This eliminates the need for manual copy-pasting or building custom webhook integrations for each specific tool combination, allowing marketing teams to manage site content directly within their existing collaboration workflows."
