**Website:** https://www.whatnot.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/whatnot

Whatnot is the largest livestream shopping platform in the U.S., connecting buyers and sellers in real-time across any category – from collectibles like trading cards to comics, fashion, sneakers, and more. It operates as a marketplace and e-commerce platform.

Founded in Winter 2020, Whatnot has grown to a team of 731 employees and operates out of Los Angeles, CA, with remote work. It serves a consumer market and focuses on livestream shopping.

The platform enables real-time interaction and facilitates transactions across a wide range of categories, making it a unique player in the livestream commerce space.
