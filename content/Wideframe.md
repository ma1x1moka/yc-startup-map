**Website:** https://wideframe.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/wideframe

Wideframe is an AI coworker for video editors that automates the searching, labeling, organizing, and sequencing of footage. Editors spend 3x more time preparing footage than actually editing — Wideframe's agent acts like an Assistant Editor to handle that work.

One agency reported saving editors 10 hours per week. Another organized an entire documentary — 2,500+ videos, 500+ audio clips, >2.5 TB — in hours rather than weeks. Supports Adobe Premiere Pro (.prproj) roundtrip.
