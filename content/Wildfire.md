**Website:** http://getwildfire.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/wildfire

Wildfire began as a campus safety alert app at UC Berkeley before expanding into a broader social news platform. The app allowed users to quickly share local updates and notify people in their immediate community whenever significant events happened nearby.

Over six years of operation, the platform delivered 65 million alerts to nearly 1 million users. The company was part of the Y Combinator Summer 2017 batch and has since been acquired.
