**Website:** https://windsor.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/windsor

Windsor uses AI and deepfake technology to produce personalized video messages. A single recorded video can be transformed into millions of variants that address individual recipients by name, company, and location, making each clip appear as if the speaker recorded it specifically for that viewer. The service is delivered as a SaaS platform targeting B2B customers, allowing businesses to automate large‑scale video outreach without manual production. Windsor operated with a five‑person team based in San Francisco and remote locations. The company was acquired after its Winter 2019 batch, reflecting market interest in scalable AI video personalization.

_Usage:_

"How does Windsor create millions of unique videos from a single recording?"

"It leverages AI and deepfake technology to synthesize the speaker’s appearance and voice while inserting personalized details such as the recipient’s name, company, and location, producing videos that look and sound like a bespoke recording."
