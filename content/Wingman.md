**Website:** https://www.trywingman.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/wingman

Wingman is a SaaS platform for inside sales teams that listens to calls and surfaces real‑time coaching suggestions. It transcribes conversations and feeds them into analytics for post‑mortem reviews and coaching sessions. The company was founded in summer 2019 and grew to a team of ten based in Bengaluru, India. Wingman was acquired, marking it as a notable player in B2B sales tech.

_Usage:_

"How does Wingman provide real‑time coaching suggestions during a call?"

"Wingman captures audio from inside sales calls, runs it through its NLP engine, and pushes actionable suggestions to the rep's screen while the call is in progress. After the call, the transcript is analyzed to generate post‑mortem insights for coaching."
