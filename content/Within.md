**Website:** http://within.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/within

Within, formerly known as Klarity, operates as a Company Brain designed to help organizations manage the transition to AI-driven operations. The platform is used by major enterprises including OpenAI, DoorDash, ServiceNow, Salesforce, JLL, and McKesson to facilitate AI transformation by mapping how work is actually performed.

The product functions through a web-based application that requires no installation, allowing employees to share their screens while working normally. Within captures and anonymizes every task, sending signals back to users to highlight their strengths. These individual activities are then consolidated by Within agents into a comprehensive enterprise map that details decision-making processes, approval flows, and control enforcement.

Based on this structural data, the company identifies opportunities to deploy AI agents against specific business objectives. It builds these agents and redesigns the surrounding people, processes, and rituals to ensure harmonious collaboration between human employees and automated systems.

_Usage:_

"How does Within capture workflow data without disrupting employee productivity or requiring heavy IT deployment?"

"It uses a lightweight web app where employees simply log in and share their screens while working as usual. The system maps and anonymizes tasks in real-time to build a structural model of the enterprise, eliminating the need for software installations or manual process documentation."
