**Website:** https://wordware.ai/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/wordware

Wordware is a San Francisco-based startup developing AI agents designed to be trusted by users. The company’s product, known as Sauna, operates as a digital coworker that learns an individual's workflows and gradually earns autonomy task by task. This approach allows the agent to handle hours of work independently while the user retains final review and decision-making power.

The team, which includes approximately 15 to 20 members, secured a $30 million seed round backed by Spark Capital, Felicis, and Y Combinator. This funding round is noted as the largest in Y Combinator history. The company is currently active and focused on building infrastructure for dependable AI collaboration.

_Usage:_

"How does Wordware's agent differ from other AI tools that try to act as immediate coworkers?"

"Most AI products attempt to function as a fully capable coworker from the start, relying on user trust. Wordware's agent, Sauna, takes a different approach by starting with limited access and learning the user's specific work patterns. It earns more autonomy incrementally as it proves its reliability on individual tasks, ensuring the user always maintains the final say rather than handing over control blindly on day one."
