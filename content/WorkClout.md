WorkClout offers a SaaS platform that helps industrial Operations and HR teams make better decisions to improve production efficiency. The tool centralizes on‑the‑job employee training, troubleshooting, safety and quality procedures, knowledge building, and performance tracking in a single system. WorkClout was acquired after its Winter 2019 batch, and it serves B2B customers in supply chain and logistics from its Los Angeles office with a team of six.

_Usage:_

"How is WorkClout different from a standard learning management system for industrial workers?"

"WorkClout combines learning management with real‑time safety, quality, and performance data, so it not only delivers training but also tracks troubleshooting steps and key performance metrics that Ops and HR teams use to boost efficiency, which typical LMS platforms don’t provide."
