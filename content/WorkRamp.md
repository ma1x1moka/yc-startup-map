**Website:** https://www.workramp.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/workramp

WorkRamp is an all-in-one learning platform designed to serve as a growth engine for businesses by empowering teams to develop talent and exceed revenue targets. The company redefines the traditional Learning Management System (LMS) for the modern workplace, positioning learning as a strategic investment that helps executives recognize its impact on organizational scale and customer advocacy.

Notable for its focus on business outcomes, WorkRamp is trusted by top learning teams at companies such as Box, Outreach, and Reddit. The San Francisco-based company, which has a remote-friendly team of 106, operates in the B2B and Human Resources sectors with a focus on sales enablement and customer success.

WorkRamp was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does WorkRamp differ from a standard LMS in terms of business impact?"

"WorkRamp positions itself not just as a content delivery tool but as a growth engine, specifically targeting revenue targets and customer advocacy rather than just compliance or training completion. It is used by teams at companies like Box and Outreach to drive specific business outcomes, distinguishing it from generic LMSs that may focus solely on course management."
