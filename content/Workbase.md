**YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/workbase

Workbase is a San Francisco-based platform designed to help B2B companies reduce customer churn and increase spending. It provides account managers with centralized data dashboards and priority checklists, enabling them to proactively save at-risk customers and upsell them to more profitable products.

The company addresses the fragmentation of customer data, which is often scattered across numerous disparate systems. By integrating these sources with one click and customizing the interface for specific business needs, Workbase creates a single hub for prioritizing customer actions. The platform was developed based on insights from internal tools built at companies like Segment, where proactive journey management was shown to significantly boost net retained revenue.

Workbase has since been acquired, marking its exit from the startup ecosystem.

_Usage:_

"How does Workbase handle the problem of customer data being siloed across different tools?"

"It acts as a central layer that one-click integrates various existing data sources, consolidating them into a single interface. This allows account managers to see a unified view of their portfolio and receive customized priority checklists for specific actions, rather than manually cross-referencing multiple systems."
