**Website:** https://www.worksphere.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/worksphere

Worksphere provides a platform for companies to manage hybrid work environments where staff split time between home and the office. It enables employees to reserve specific desks, view which colleagues are present, and coordinate in-person meetings more effectively.

The solution allows organizations to optimize their real estate usage by identifying and releasing unused office space, thereby reducing overhead costs. The company, based in Seattle, operated as a small team of six before being acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does Worksphere actually help a company save money on office space if it's just a desk booking tool?"

"It helps by providing visibility into actual desk utilization and who is in the office. This data allows companies to downsize their physical footprint by letting go of unused space, rather than paying for empty desks that are only needed on specific days."
