**Website:** https://writesonic.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/writesonic

Writesonic operates as a Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) platform designed to monitor brand visibility across more than ten AI search engines, including ChatGPT and Gemini. The tool provides specific actionable insights, suggesting strategies such as creating new content, refreshing existing pages, or identifying sites that mention competitors but exclude the user's brand.

Based in San Francisco with a remote-friendly team of 35, the company focuses on the B2B marketing sector. It has accumulated over 15,000 five-star reviews across platforms like G2, Trustpilot, and Capterra, reflecting its adoption in the evolving landscape of AI-driven search.

_Usage:_

"How does this differ from standard SEO tools?"

"Standard SEO tools track rankings on traditional search engines like Google, whereas Writesonic specifically tracks how often your brand is cited or mentioned within generative AI responses from platforms like ChatGPT and Gemini. It shifts the focus from keyword ranking to visibility within AI-generated answers, providing actions to improve that specific metric."
