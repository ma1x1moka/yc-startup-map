Xeol is a security startup that helps enterprises and Fortune 500s manage end‑of‑life software and outdated dependencies. It offers a proactive EOL management program that improves vulnerability management and reduces cyber insurance costs.

The company was founded in Summer 2023 and is based in New York City. It has a small team of three and focuses on B2B security, leveraging artificial intelligence to identify and mitigate dependency attack vectors.

Xeol was acquired.
