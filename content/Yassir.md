Yassir is the leading super app in the Maghreb, operating in 45 cities across Algeria, Morocco, and Tunisia while expanding into France, Canada, and Sub‑Saharan Africa. It offers on‑demand ride‑hailing and last‑mile delivery services.

The company is extending its platform to include digital financial services, enabling users to pay, save, and borrow within the app and helping drive the continent toward a digital economy.

Backed by venture capital from Silicon Valley, Europe, and other regions, Yassir employs around 200 people and remains active in the consumer and transportation services sectors.

_Usage:_

"How does Yassir integrate financial services into its existing ride‑hailing and delivery platform?"

"Yassir adds digital payment, savings, and borrowing features directly into its app, letting users transact while booking rides or deliveries, thereby creating a unified marketplace."
