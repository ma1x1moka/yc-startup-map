**Website:** https://yezza.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/yezza

Yezza enables small businesses in Southeast Asia to create and manage storefronts directly within WhatsApp. The platform supports various commerce use cases beyond simple product sales, including social media influencer collaborations and table reservations. Yezza is based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, and operates as a B2B SaaS provider.

The company emerged from a previous tech-enabled travel business that utilized WhatsApp to sell packages, which was spun off during the pandemic. Yezza has achieved profitability with a monthly recurring revenue of $40K and generates $3M in monthly GMV from approximately 1,500 daily orders. Merchants pay a monthly fee starting at $12 plus up to $0.25 per order.

Yezza targets a market estimated at $7.2B, driven by 50M WhatsApp Business downloads in the region. The company is backed by Y Combinator and angels with experience at Facebook and WhatsApp, and it notes that similar buying behaviors via WhatsApp exist in Latin America and Africa.

_Usage:_

"How does Yezza handle the logistics of selling physical goods if the entire transaction happens inside WhatsApp?"

"Yezza acts as the storefront layer within the WhatsApp interface, allowing merchants to list products and process orders without forcing customers to leave the chat app. It handles the e-commerce mechanics like order tracking and payment collection, charging merchants a monthly subscription and a small per-order fee, while leveraging the existing user base of WhatsApp Business in Southeast Asia."
