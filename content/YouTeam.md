**Website:** https://youteam.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/youteam

YouTeam provides a platform that connects startups with select offshore developers seeking full‑time contracts, sourcing talent that isn’t listed on freelance portals or job boards. It is the only service accessing this previously untapped pool, allowing it to surface the “crème de la crème” of engineers.

The marketplace has helped more than 60 YC startups hire top‑notch engineers they couldn’t find elsewhere, establishing it as a trusted recruiting partner in the B2B talent space.

YouTeam was acquired, reflecting its value to the market. The company operates from San Francisco and Mountain View with a team of 34 employees.

_Usage:_

"How does YouTeam find engineers that aren’t on typical freelance sites?"

"YouTeam taps into a curated offshore talent pool that isn’t posted on public freelance portals or job boards, then selects the highest‑quality candidates for full‑time contracts, giving startups access to engineers they otherwise couldn’t reach."
