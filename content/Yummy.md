**Website:** https://www.yummysuperapp.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/yummy-inc

Yummy operates as the leading ridesharing and delivery platform in Venezuela, processing over 16 million orders annually. The company functions as a comprehensive super-app that connects users with essential transportation and logistics services within the region.

Beyond core consumer services, Yummy provides a suite of merchant-enablement tools through its delivery hub. These services include e-commerce store capabilities and WhatsApp sales automation designed to help small and medium-sized businesses expand their digital reach. The company maintains a team of 260 employees based in Los Angeles, California.

_Usage:_

"How does Yummy differentiate itself from standard delivery apps for local businesses?"

"Yummy acts as a super-app that bundles ridesharing and delivery with specific merchant tools. For SMBs, it offers e-commerce store setup and WhatsApp sales automation, allowing businesses to manage sales channels directly through the platform's delivery hub rather than just using it as a passive order intake source."
