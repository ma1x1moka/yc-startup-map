Co-founded [Cohere.io](./Cohere.io.md) (unrelated to the AI lab of the same name), building tools to automate customer support workflows. Cohere.io was later acquired by Ramp, where the technology now powers parts of Ramp's own product.

"What happened to Cohere.io after YC?"
