**Website:** https://zaraz.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/zaraz

Zaraz is a third-party script manager designed to optimize how websites load external tools. By implementing a single line of code, the platform claims to make any website 40% faster, which in turn leads to double the conversion rates and improved SEO rankings.

The company operates as a B2B SaaS product based in San Francisco with a remote-friendly team of two. Zaraz was acquired, marking its exit from the YC ecosystem.

_Usage:_

"How does Zaraz actually speed up a site without breaking existing integrations?"

"It acts as a manager for third-party scripts, optimizing their load order and execution to prevent them from blocking the main page render. You replace multiple individual script tags with a single Zaraz tag, which handles the rest. The result is a 40% speed increase and a 2x lift in conversion rates, effectively acting as a performance layer for your existing analytics and marketing tools."
