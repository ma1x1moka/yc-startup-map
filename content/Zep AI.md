Zep assembles the right context from chat history, business data, and user behavior so agents are personalized, accurate, and fast. Its open source Graphiti project reached 20,000 GitHub stars in under a year, demonstrating strong community engagement. The system delivers sub‑200‑millisecond retrieval and is SOC 2 Type 2/HIPAA certified, and it is used by teams ranging from startups to Fortune 500s.

Based in San Francisco, the company has a lean team of five developers focused on building infrastructure for conversational AI.
