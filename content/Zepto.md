Zepto operates a grocery delivery service in India that promises 10-minute delivery times by leveraging a network of optimized micro-warehouses, also known as dark stores, built across various cities. The company focuses on efficient logistics infrastructure to support rapid consumer fulfillment.

With a team of approximately 1,300 employees based in Maharashtra, India, Zepto has achieved significant scale, reporting hundreds of millions of dollars in annual sales. The company cites best-in-class unit economics as a key metric of its operational success in the competitive food and beverage sector.

_Usage:_

"How does Zepto actually manage to deliver groceries in 10 minutes without the logistics costs destroying their margins?"

"They use a network of optimized micro-warehouses or 'dark stores' located close to consumers across Indian cities, which allows for rapid fulfillment. Despite this intensive infrastructure, the company reports best-in-class unit economics alongside hundreds of millions in annual sales, suggesting the model is economically viable at scale."
