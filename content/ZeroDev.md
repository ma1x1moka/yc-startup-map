**Website:** https://zerodev.app/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/zerodev

ZeroDev offers self‑custody wallets powered by account abstraction, enabling gas‑free transactions, transaction bundling, and programmable security policies. The service is designed for Web 2.5 apps, wallet apps, onramps, marketplaces, and more, making non‑custodial wallets easier to use and more powerful than custodial alternatives. With a team of five in Los Angeles, the company focuses on infrastructure for the crypto/Web3 space.

_Usage:_

"How does ZeroDev enable gas‑free transactions for users?"

"ZeroDev uses account abstraction to bundle transactions, allowing users to perform actions without paying gas directly; the service handles bundling and security policies, providing a seamless experience."
