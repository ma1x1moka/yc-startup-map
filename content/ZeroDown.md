---
description: ZeroDown lets renters become owners by buying homes and letting tenants build equity while renting. YC W19.
---

**Website:** https://zerodown.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/zerodown

ZeroDown offers an alternative to the rent-or-own dilemma by purchasing a home outright on behalf of a professional renter and allowing them to pay rent that builds purchase credits. A portion of each payment accrues toward ownership, and tenants can decide when to use those credits to buy the home from ZeroDown. The company, based in San Francisco with a remote team of 13, was acquired, highlighting its impact on the housing market for young professionals.

_Usage:_

"How does ZeroDown’s model differ from a traditional rent‑to‑own agreement?"

"ZeroDown actually buys the home with cash and then rents it to the tenant, with part of each rent payment credited toward eventual purchase, whereas traditional rent‑to‑own typically involves the tenant leasing from the seller and may not provide a clear path to equity buildup."
