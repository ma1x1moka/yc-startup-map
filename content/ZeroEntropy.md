**Website:** https://www.zeroentropy.dev | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/zeroentropy

ZeroEntropy trains small, task-specific models designed to replace frontier LLMs across production AI pipelines. Most AI applications rely on general-purpose models for narrow, repeatable pipeline steps like reranking, embedding, classification, routing, query rewriting, and context compression. Running these discrete tasks on frontier models creates unnecessary latency and high operating costs.

By focusing on specialized models for these intermediate workloads, the platform helps developers deploy AI systems that run faster, cheaper, and more accurately. Based in San Francisco, ZeroEntropy participated in the Y Combinator Winter 2025 batch with a team of six.

ZeroEntropy has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"Why swap out a frontier LLM for task-specific models in an AI pipeline?"

"Frontier LLMs are often overkill for narrow, repeatable steps like routing, classification, context compression, or reranking. Relying on general-purpose models for every stage of a pipeline makes production AI slow and expensive, whereas small task-specific models handle those specific workloads faster, cheaper, and more accurately."
