ZeroStorefront is a data platform that connects the myriad applications used by restaurants, consolidating data to improve analytics, marketing, operations, and finance. It helps restaurants move beyond the fragmented ecosystem of over two dozen apps by providing a unified data warehouse and routing layer.

The company, founded in the Winter 2019 batch, has a team of seven based in San Francisco, San Mateo, and remote. It was acquired by Thanx, a company that likely values its ability to streamline restaurant operations. Notable clients include Frank Pepe’s, Halal Guys, and Wings Over.

_Usage:_

"How does ZeroStorefront help restaurants manage their data?"

"ZeroStorefront connects the dozens of applications a restaurant uses, stores the data in a unified warehouse, and routes it to improve analytics, marketing, operations, and finance."
