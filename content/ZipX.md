**Website:** https://zipx.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/zipx

ZipX provides an e-commerce platform designed to give consumers and expats in Hong Kong and Southeast Asia easy, affordable access to products from their home countries. The platform focuses on items such as international snacks, foods, health products, and daily essentials that are otherwise hard to find or heavily marked up locally.

The service was created to address the drawbacks of existing alternatives, such as overpriced imported grocery stores and expensive package-forwarding companies. By leveraging technology, ZipX set out to make international commerce seamless and accessible.

ZipX participated in the Y Combinator Winter 2017 batch and has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does ZipX improve upon traditional package forwarding for cross-border goods?"

"Standard package forwarding companies are often expensive and cumbersome, while local specialty grocery stores charge steep markups on imported goods. ZipX streamlines the international shopping process into an integrated service to make ordering foreign food and daily essentials more direct and affordable."
