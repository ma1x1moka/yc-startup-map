**Website:** http://www.zuddl.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/zuddl

Zuddl is a modular platform that lets event marketers plan and execute events and webinars that drive growth. Its modular approach allows B2B marketers and conference organizers to pick only the components they need to build the perfect event and scale their program.
The platform is used by teams at Microsoft, Google, ServiceNow, Zylo, Postman, TransPerfect, and the United Nations, who trust Zuddl for its outcome-oriented focus and flexibility. Zuddl positions itself as a partner rather than a vendor, emphasizing collaboration with its users.
Zuddl raised $13.35 million in Series A funding led by Alpha Wave Incubation and Qualcomm Ventures, with participation from GrowX Ventures and Waveform Ventures. The company, founded in the Summer 2020 Y Combinator batch, employs 120 people and operates from San Francisco, CA, TS, India, and remote locations.
