**Website:** https://zynq.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/zynq

Zynq makes company offices smart with an all-in-one workspace management platform that redefines the relationship between people, space, and time. It helps companies fully embrace the hybrid office by giving employees a quick way to find the best time and space to collaborate, meet with guests, and more.
Founded in Winter 2020, the San Francisco-based startup serves B2B productivity markets with workflow automation and remote work tools. With a team of seven, Zynq offers a single solution to streamline office operations and improve employee experience.
The company was acquired, marking its success in the hybrid workplace space.

_Usage:_

"How does Zynq help employees find meeting spaces compared to traditional booking systems?"

"Zynq provides a real-time view of office space availability and allows employees to book rooms and desks instantly, eliminating the back-and-forth of email or legacy systems."
