**Website:** https://zyper.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/zyper

Zyper is a machine‑learning‑led community marketing tool that helps brands identify the top 1 % of their social followers and engage them as active advocates. These brand fans generate authentic, emotive content that scales peer‑to‑peer advertising, giving brands the reach of a single influencer while delivering repeat content, higher authenticity and engagement. Zyper was acquired, reflecting market interest in its unique approach to social‑driven marketing.

_Usage:_

"How is Zyper’s community‑marketing approach different from hiring micro‑influencers?"

"Zyper doesn’t rely on external influencers; it uses machine learning to surface a brand’s own top‑1 % fans, who already trust the brand and can repeatedly create authentic content, resulting in higher engagement than one‑off influencer posts."
