**Website:** http://www.bxblue.com.br | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/bxblue

bxblue is a Brazilian fintech company that built an online marketplace for personal loans. The platform focuses specifically on pensioners and government workers, a segment characterized by guaranteed income that represents an estimated $80 billion market in Brazil.

Participating in the Y Combinator Summer 2017 batch, the company built a remote team based out of DF, Brazil, scaling to 28 employees. bxblue has since been acquired.

_Usage:_

"Who is eligible to borrow through bxblue's loan marketplace?"

"The platform is tailored specifically for Brazilian pensioners and government employees, leveraging their guaranteed income to connect them with personal loan options online."
