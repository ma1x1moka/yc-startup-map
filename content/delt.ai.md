**Website:** https://delt.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/delt-ai

delt.ai is building a specialized digital financial service for businesses in Latam, aiming to tackle the more than 450 B market of business payments in Mexico. The company is a natively digital alternative to a bank, built from the ground up with payment gateways and API integrations. Its fully digital, KYC‑compliant onboarding enabled it to become the first to open branchless accounts in the country, targeting poorly served startups and SMEs and focusing on the roughly 40 B market opportunity that is profitable when served fully online. delt.ai was acquired.

The startup operates out of Mexico City with a team of 26 employees and focuses on the fintech and banking and exchange industries.
