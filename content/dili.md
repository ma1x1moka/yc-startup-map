**Website:** https://www.dili.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/dili

Dili (YC S23) is creating a reliable AI system of record that helps physical‑industry companies track compliance, analyze labor and spend, and run payroll. It has already run prevailing‑wage, apprenticeship, and other labor‑compliance programs across energy, infrastructure, datacenters, advanced manufacturing, and construction, enabling customers to secure more than $1 billion in federal funding.

The company positions its AI platform as the most dependable automation for compliance in real‑world industries, aiming to replace manual processes with a continuously updated, data‑driven solution. With a team of 15 engineers in New York City, Dili serves B2B customers in finance and accounting, energy, and related sectors.

_Usage:_

"How does Dili’s AI compliance platform differ from traditional compliance software?"

"Traditional compliance tools often require manual data entry and periodic audits, whereas Dili’s AI continuously records compliance actions, automatically runs prevailing‑wage and apprenticeship checks, and integrates payroll and spend analysis, allowing customers to maintain real‑time compliance and qualify for large federal funding programs."
