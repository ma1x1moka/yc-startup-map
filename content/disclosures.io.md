**Website:** https://disclosures.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/disclosures-io

Disclosures.io builds a complete listing toolkit designed to modernize the residential real estate transaction process. By focusing on the disclosure phase, the platform helps sellers get their homes to market faster while enabling buyers to make more informed offers. The company operates in San Francisco with a team of ten, addressing a sector where digital adoption has historically lagged behind other industries.

The company was founded on the belief that lean, pragmatic product development could improve transparency and efficiency in a high-volume market. Its approach targets the pain points of traditional home buying and selling, aiming to streamline the exchange of critical property information. Disclosures.io was acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does this toolkit actually change the workflow for a seller compared to using a standard MLS template?"

"It streamlines the disclosure process by providing a dedicated toolkit that helps sellers get their home to market faster. Instead of manually managing these documents, the platform enables a more efficient exchange of information, which in turn allows buyers to make more informed offers without the typical delays associated with traditional real estate paperwork."
