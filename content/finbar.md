Finbar is an AI investment analyst used by several top‑20 hedge funds by assets under management. It automates financial modeling, research, and data analysis, enabling multi‑million‑dollar decisions to be made up to 100× faster.

The service streamlines workflow automation in finance, allowing hedge funds to accelerate their investment processes. Finbar operates out of London with a team of four.
