**Website:** https://garten.co/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/garten

Garten is a workplace wellbeing company that provides nutrition and wellness services to create healthy and happy employees. It operates as a Top YCombinator S16 and YCG F19 company, leveraging a sophisticated tech stack that utilizes AI and IoT to enable a flexible supply chain. The company maintains a completely asset-lite model while offering full wellbeing analytics.

Garten has worked with some of the world’s top companies, including Apple, Google, Amazon, Kaiser, Stanford, Stripe, Nike, and Lyft. The company was based in San Francisco and Burlingame, California, with a team of over 200 people. Garten was acquired.
