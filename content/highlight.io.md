**Website:** https://www.highlight.io/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/highlight-io

Highlight.io is an open source, full‑stack monitoring platform that, with just a few lines of code, lets developers understand what’s slowing down users and swiftly address errors and regressions on their web app. It can playback a customer’s exact interactions to reproduce bugs and notifies developers of future errors that could occur. Highlight.io was acquired and plans to go deeper into the observability stack to give teams full visibility. The company has a team of eight, based in Seattle and remote.

_Usage:_

"How does Highlight.io replay user sessions compared to other monitoring tools?"

"Highlight.io captures the exact user interactions, allowing developers to replay them to reproduce bugs, and it also notifies of future errors that could happen."
