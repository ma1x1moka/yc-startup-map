Hypcloud is a tech-enabled commercial mortgage broker that streamlines the mortgage process for commercial real estate. It operates in Berlin, Germany, and serves the real estate and construction, housing and real estate industries. It was founded in Summer 2018 and has a team of 14.

The company was acquired, marking its success in the fintech and real estate space. Its focus on simplifying commercial mortgage brokerage through technology has positioned it as a notable player in the intersection of finance and property.
