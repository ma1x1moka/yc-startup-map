**Website:** https://infeedo.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/infeedo-ai

In 2016, inFeedo AI set out to make 100 million employees heard and valued. Today it is Asia's leading employee experience platform that engages employees, predicts attrition, and answers FAQs with conversational AI that people love.

The platform is backed by Y Combinator, Bling Capital and founders of Gainsight, Freshworks, Zeta, Lenskart, innov8. Its AI bot Amber serves as a Chief Listening Officer, helping leaders uncover hidden gaps in culture, management and wellbeing in real time.

inFeedo serves more than 60 countries, with customers that include MNCs such as Samsung, Xiaomi, Lenovo and conglomerates like TATA, Godrej and Bharti. The company employs 150 people and operates from Dubai and New York.
