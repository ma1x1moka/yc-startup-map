**Website:** https://www.inai.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/inai

inai provides a unified integration layer that allows companies to accept digital payments globally through a single API. It connects merchants to a wide range of payment infrastructure, including gateways like Stripe, Adyen, and Braintree, as well as wallets such as PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay, and buy-now-pay-later services like Klarna and Affirm.

The platform features a no-code dashboard that enables merchants to customize checkout experiences by region, displaying specific payment methods and local currency pricing for different markets. A key differentiator is that merchant data remains with inai rather than being locked into a specific payment service provider, which simplifies the process of switching between providers.

Founded in Summer 2021, the company operated with a team of 14 across London, Delaware, and remote locations before being acquired.

_Usage:_

"How does inai handle data ownership compared to using Stripe directly?"

"With inai, your data stays with inai rather than being siloed inside a specific PSP like Stripe. This architecture makes it significantly easier to switch between underlying providers, such as moving from Stripe to Adyen or Braintree, because you are not locked into a single provider's data ecosystem."
