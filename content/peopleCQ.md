**Website:** https://www.peopleCQ.com | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/peoplecq

peopleCQ, operating under the brand NowPay, is a fintech startup based in Cairo that builds a financial-wellness platform for employees in emerging markets. The company focuses on four core pillars: saving, spending, budgeting, and borrowing, aiming to improve each aspect through specialized products.

The platform allows employees to receive their salaries in advance at any point during the month and pay bills instantly. Founded by ex-employees of major tech and telecom firms, the company has raised $3.7 million from investors including 500 Startups, Beco Capital, and Plug and Play.

With a team of 85, peopleCQ positions itself as a comprehensive solution for employee financial health in the Egyptian market.

_Usage:_

"How does the salary advance feature work for employees?"

"Employees can access their earned salaries in advance at any time during the month, rather than waiting for the standard payroll date. This is part of a broader suite that also includes instant bill payment, designed to give workers more control over their cash flow."
