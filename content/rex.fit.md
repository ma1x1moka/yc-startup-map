**Website:** https://www.rex.fit | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rex-fit

Rex.Fit’s DROP is an AI wearable camera that uses computer‑vision to automatically track users’ nutrition and provide recommendations. Since its launch last month the device has secured more than $93 k in pre‑orders, roughly 450 units, demonstrating early consumer interest. The underlying nutrition‑estimation technology is also offered as an API to fitness chains, which have reported hundreds of thousands of dollars in annual revenue from its use. The product is built by two brothers who are sports nutritionists and computer‑vision engineers from ETH Zurich, and the company operates out of San Francisco with a two‑person team.

_Usage:_

"How does DROP’s computer‑vision nutrition tracking compare to manually logging meals in a food diary?"

"DROP captures images of food and applies its computer‑vision model to estimate nutritional content, eliminating the need for users to manually enter each item. While the exact accuracy isn’t disclosed, the fact that fitness chains have integrated the API and generated significant revenue suggests it provides a usable level of estimation for real‑world fitness applications."
