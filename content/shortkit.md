ShortKit lets any consumer app launch a TikTok-grade media expereinces.

Michael, spent 6 years at YouTube building the infrastructure that powers YouTube Shorts. Neil, prev. scaled a SaaS company from 0 to $6M ARR as employee #1.

shortkit handles the full media stack, starting with short form video - client SDKs, video infrastructure, rec systems, and monetization - so product teams don't have to.

_Usage:_

"How does ShortKit’s recommendation system compare to TikTok’s built‑in recommendation engine?"

"ShortKit builds on the same infrastructure that powers YouTube Shorts—thanks to its co‑founder’s six years on that team—so its recommendation stack is designed to deliver TikTok‑grade personalization, but it’s offered as a turnkey service rather than a proprietary TikTok algorithm."
