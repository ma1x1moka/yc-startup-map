**Website:** https://getstoic.com/ | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/stoic

Stoic is a consumer mental‑health app that teaches users to understand what influences their feelings and what they can do to feel better. With more than 4 million downloads and over 100 000 reviews averaging 4.8 /5, the app has built a strong reputation in the healthcare and wellness space.

Founded in Kraków and operating remotely, Stoic’s small, focused team of nine delivers its services through a mobile platform available on iOS. The company’s mission is to empower individuals with practical tools for emotional well‑being, positioning it as a notable player in the consumer health tech sector.

_Usage:_

"What makes Stoic’s approach to mental health distinct from other wellness apps?"

"Stoic focuses on helping users identify the specific factors that influence their emotions and provides actionable steps to improve mood, rather than offering generic meditation or relaxation techniques. This emphasis on causal understanding and practical action sets it apart within the mental‑health app market."
