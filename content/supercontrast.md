Supercontrast was an AI copilot for design that empowered designers to create and refine high‑quality designs and assets. Inspired by Hive's Gencraft, an AI art generator that grew to $1M ARR in six months, the two‑person team based in San Francisco and remote built Supercontrast for B2B clients in engineering, product, and design.

The startup is now inactive.
