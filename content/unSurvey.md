**Website:** http://www.unsurvey.ai | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/unsurvey

unSurvey offered AI-led market research interviews that remove the need for scheduling and provide instant insights. It served B2B customers in engineering, product, and design, positioning itself as a feedback and market research tool. The company operated with a team of eight from San Francisco and remote locations and was acquired, as noted in its YC directory entry.

_Usage:_

"How does unSurvey’s AI interview platform differ from traditional market research tools?"

"unSurvey’s platform uses AI to conduct the interview itself, so there’s no need to coordinate schedules, and it returns insights immediately, whereas conventional market research typically requires manual scheduling of respondents and a longer analysis phase."
