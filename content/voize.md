Voize is a Berlin-based startup that provides an AI companion for nurses, using machine learning and speech recognition to automate administrative tasks and free up time for patient care. The solution has already been adopted by more than 1,500 care facilities and supports over 100,000 nurses, delivering up to 30% time savings. It is backed by a $50M Series A led by Balderton Capital, with HV Capital, Y Combinator and other VCs, and has a team of 140 focused on improving productivity in healthcare.
