**Website:** https://webapp.io | **YC:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/webapp-io

webapp.io simplifies the development workflow for web applications by making CI/CD, E2E testing, and preview environments as straightforward as writing a Dockerfile. It achieves this through a custom hypervisor that streamlines infrastructure management for engineering teams.

The company, based in Toronto and operating remotely, focused on B2B developer tools to help product and design teams ship code more efficiently. It was acquired, marking the conclusion of its independent operation.

_Usage:_

"How does webapp.io handle the complexity of setting up preview environments compared to standard containerization?"

"It uses a custom hypervisor to abstract away the underlying infrastructure, allowing developers to define their environment simply by writing a Dockerfile. This makes setting up CI/CD pipelines and E2E testing significantly faster and less complex than managing traditional container orchestration manually."
