yBANQ builds a collections and reconciliation platform for MSMEs in India. In the country, reconciliation is largely manual and error‑prone, requiring manual matching of bank statements with invoices.
The company was acquired, signaling its traction in the fintech and payments space. With a team of nine based in Bengaluru, yBANQ focuses on B2B solutions that streamline financial operations for small businesses.

_Usage:_

"How does yBANQ's platform streamline collections compared to traditional methods?"

"yBANQ automates the matching of bank statements with invoices, cutting down manual effort and reducing errors that are common in India’s current manual reconciliation process."
